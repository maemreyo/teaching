**Video Script Prompt**

**Describe the show structure:**
"Start with the central question: 'How does the **biological clock** **tick**? Our **life span** is **restricted**. We think of **artificially** made objects, but is their decay comparable to a living **organism**?'"

"Next, explain the difference: 'Dead products are **static** systems. The material that **constitutes** them is **worn down**. This **ageing** follows the laws of **thermodynamics** and is **inexorable**. But an organism is a **dynamic** system. The **destruction** of old material and creation of new is in **permanent** **equilibrium**. The **material** itself, the **molecules**, are always changing. So, ageing is not **inevitable**, as organisms **possess** repair mechanisms.'"

"End with a discussion question: 'What is the key difference between the **ageing** of a car and the **ageing** of an **organism**?'"
