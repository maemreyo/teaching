**Describe the show structure:**

"Next, discuss the cost: 'We **marvel** at the **manifestation** of skills so **superior to** our own. But their **hard-won** discoveries become **commonplace**. "End with a discussion question: 'Besides **fame**, what are some of the **unpalatable** costs of being a genius?'"
