## Vocabulary Learning Plan: The Case for Zoos (4 Videos)

This is a detailed plan to learn all vocabulary words from the reading passage, divided into 4 manageable parts. Each part corresponds to a video/learning session and builds the argument for the positive role of modern zoos.

*   **Describe the show structure:**

    *   "Next, detail the dangers of the wild: 'They don't **suffer from** the **threat** of **predators**, or the **irritation** of **parasites**. Many **nasty** things happen to wild animals that don't happen in good zoos.'"

    *   "End with a debate question: 'Do you agree that a **somewhat** **restricted** but safe life for a **captive** animal is better than a free but **nasty** one?'"
