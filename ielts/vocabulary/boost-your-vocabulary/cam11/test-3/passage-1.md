## Vocabulary Learning Plan: The Story of Silk (3 Videos)

This is a detailed plan to learn all vocabulary words from the reading passage, divided into 3 manageable parts. Each part corresponds to a video/learning session and traces the history of silk from its legendary discovery to its global journey.

*   **Describe the show structure:**
    *   "Start with the material itself: 'Silk is the world's most **luxurious** **fabric**. It is a **fine**, **smooth** material. **Legend** has it that an empress in ancient China discovered it.'"
    *   "Next, tell the story of discovery: 'She found silkworms were **responsible for** the **destruction** of mulberry trees. Later, a cocoon **landed in** her hot tea and began to **unravel into** a fine **thread**. She found she could **wind** this thread around her fingers. She **persuaded** her husband to let her **rear** silkworms on a **grove** of mulberry trees. She also **devised** a special **reel** to draw the **fibres** from the cocoon.'"
