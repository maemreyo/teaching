## Vocabulary Learning Plan: Geo-engineering (4 Videos)

This is a detailed plan to learn all vocabulary words from the reading passage, divided into 4 manageable parts. Each part corresponds to a video/learning session and explores the controversial field of geo-engineering.

### VIDEO 1: The Need for a Plan B (18 words)

**Video Script Prompt**

*   **Target a specific use case:** "Introduce the concept of geo-engineering as a potential Plan B for climate change, given the difficulties in reducing carbon emissions."
*   **Focus on a specific source:** "Focus on the first 18 vocabulary words. For each, provide a short English meaning and a simple example sentence from the text."
    1.  ambitious
    2.  geo-engineering
    3.  dependence on
    4.  fossil fuels
    5.  volume
    6.  atmosphere
    7.  inevitable
    8.  reasonable
    9.  emissions
    10. strides
    11. stemming
    12. reversing
    13. alternative
    14. intentional
    15. manipulation
    16. proponents
    17. equivalent
    18. backup
*   **Describe the show structure:**
    *   "Start with the problem: 'Scientists are exploring increasingly **ambitious** **geo-engineering** projects. Such is our **dependence on** **fossil fuels** and the **volume** of carbon dioxide in the **atmosphere**, that global warming is now **inevitable**. The best we can do is keep it at a **reasonable** level by cutting carbon **emissions**. But while some countries are making major **strides**, most are having difficulty even **stemming** the rate of increase, let alone **reversing** it.'"
    *   "Next, introduce Plan B: 'Consequently, scientists are exploring the **alternative** of geo-engineering - the **intentional**, large-scale **manipulation** of the environment. Its **proponents** say it is the **equivalent** of a **backup** generator if reducing our dependency on fossil fuels fails.'"
    *   "Then, create a review screen for the 18 words."
    *   "End with a question: 'Do you think geo-engineering is a necessary **backup** plan, or a dangerous **alternative**?'"
