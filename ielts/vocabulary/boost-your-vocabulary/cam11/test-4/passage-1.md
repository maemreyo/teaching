## Vocabulary Learning Plan: The Twin Revolution (3 Videos)

This is a detailed plan to learn all vocabulary words from the reading passage, divided into 3 manageable parts. Each part corresponds to a video/learning session and explores the fascinating world of twin studies and epigenetics.

**Video Script Prompt**

*   **Target a specific use case:** "Introduce the use of twins in biomedical research to distinguish between the influences of genetics (nature) and environment (nurture)."

    15. be rooted in

    20. interplay
*   **Describe the show structure:**
    *   "Start with the basics: 'To **biomedical** researchers, **twins** offer a **precious** opportunity to **untangle** the influence of nature and **nurture**. Because **identical twins** come from a single **fertilized** egg that **splits** in two, they share **virtually** the same genetic code. Any differences must be due to environment.'"
    *   "Next, explain the two lines of research: 'By comparing identical twins with **fraternal** twins, researchers can **quantify** the **extent** to which genes affect our lives. If identical twins are more similar with respect to an **ailment**, then **vulnerability to** the disease must **be rooted in** **heredity**. These two lines of research - studying identical twins to **pinpoint** environmental influence, and comparing them with fraternal twins to measure **inheritance** - have been **crucial** to understanding the **interplay** of nature and nurture.'"
    *   "Then, create a review screen for the 20 words."
    *   "End with a question: 'Why is it so **crucial** to **untangle** the **interplay** of nature and **nurture**?'"
