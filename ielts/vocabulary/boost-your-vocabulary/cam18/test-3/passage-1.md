### **Vocabulary Learning Plan: Beyond Concrete (3-Part Video Series)**

---

VIDEO 1: The "Unbeatable" Villain (16 words)

**Video Script Prompt**

**Objective:**
"To tell the story of _why_ we are in a desperate **scramble** for alternatives, introducing **concrete** as a 'villain' with 'superpowers' that are hard to beat."

**Target Vocabulary:**
"This video introduces the villain (concrete) and its amazing properties."

1.  **concrete**
2.  **aggravating**
3.  **scramble**
4.  **commodity**
5.  **underpinned**
6.  **extent**
7.  **sheer**
8.  **manufacture**
9.  **relatively**
10. **in abundance**
11. **marvellous**
12. **properties**
13. **Portland**
14. **mouldable**
15. **pourable**
16. **sets**

**Suggested Script Structure (REVISED):**
"**Start by setting the scene:** 'Our story begins with **concrete**, the **commodity** that has **underpinned** modern life. But it's **aggravating** the climate crisis, forcing a desperate **scramble** for alternatives.'

**Next, describe the 'villain's' strengths:** 'But this villain is hard to beat. The **sheer** **scale** of its **manufacture** is huge, and we must consider the **extent** of its use. Its ingredients are **relatively** cheap and found **in abundance**.'

**Then, reveal its 'superpowers':** 'The stuff itself has **marvellous** **properties**. **Portland** cement is **mouldable** and **pourable**, meaning you can shape it into anything. Then, it **sets** incredibly hard.'

**Review:** Create a summary screen displaying all 16 words.

**Engagement Question:** 'Which of concrete's **marvellous** **properties** (like being **mouldable** or found **in abundance**) makes it hardest to replace?'"

VIDEO 2: The First Challenger (15 words)

**Video Script Prompt**

**Objective:**
"To introduce the _first hero_ in our story: Wood. This video tells the story of its 'fatal flaw' and the 'secret weapon' engineers created to overcome it."

**Target Vocabulary:**
"This video introduces wood and Cross-Laminated Timber (CLT)."

1.  **thermal expansion**
2.  **reinforce**
3.  **rather**
4.  **medieval**
5.  **treated**
6.  **emergence**
7.  **absorbs**
8.  **moisture**
9.  **susceptible**
10. **pests**
11. **not to mention**
12. **cross-laminated**
13. **adhesive**
14. **sawn**
15. **crosswise**

**Suggested Script Structure (REVISED):**
"**Start with concrete's 'sidekick':** 'Concrete has one more trick. It has a similar **thermal expansion** to steel, which allows steel to **reinforce** it. So, how can anything compete? By using a **rather** **medieval** idea: **treated** wood. We are seeing the **emergence** of tall timber buildings.'

**Next, describe the 'hero's flaw':** 'But wood has a weakness. It **absorbs** **moisture**, it's **susceptible** to **pests**, **not to mention** fire.'

**Then, reveal the 'secret weapon':** 'The solution is **cross-laminated** timber. An **adhesive** is used to stick layers of solid **sawn** timber together **crosswise**, making it super strong.'

**Review:** Create a summary screen displaying all 15 words.

**Engagement Question:** 'How does sticking **sawn** timber **crosswise** help solve wood's problem of being **susceptible** to absorbing **moisture**?'"

VIDEO 3: The "Waste" Heroes & The Hard Truth (15 words)

**Video Script Prompt**

**Objective:**
"To introduce the _second_ set of heroes—industrial waste—and tell the sobering _conclusion_ to our story, explaining why the 'villain' hasn't been beaten yet."

**Target Vocabulary:**
"This video introduces waste materials and the real-world barriers."

1.  **leading the way**
2.  **locks away**
3.  **Fly ash**
4.  **slag**
5.  **ore**
6.  **byproduct**
7.  **incorporated**
8.  **make up**
9.  **smelting**
10. **incorporation**
11. **fossil fuels**
12. **promising**
13. **unproven**
14. **abundant**
15. **shortages**

**Suggested Script Structure (REVISED):**
"**Start with the 'other heroes':** 'While wood **locks away** carbon, with Finland **leading the way**, our story has other heroes: **Fly ash** and **slag**. These are just a **byproduct** from burning coal or from iron **ore** **smelting**.'

**Next, describe their 'power':** 'These waste materials can be **incorporated** into the concrete mix. This **incorporation** means they **make up** a large part of the cement.'

**Then, tell the 'sobering conclusion':** 'But the story ends on a hard truth. These **promising** ideas are still **unproven**. The materials are not **abundant**, and they face **shortages**. Plus, if they must be shipped using **fossil fuels**, it may not make sense.'

**Review:** Create a summary screen displaying all 15 words.

**Engagement Question:** 'What is the bigger problem for new materials: being **unproven**, or facing material **shortages**?'"
