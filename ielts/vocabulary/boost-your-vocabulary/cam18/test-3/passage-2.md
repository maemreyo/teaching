### **Vocabulary Learning Plan: The Steam Car (4-Part Video Series)**

---

VIDEO 1: The Rise and Fall of Steam (15 words)

**Video Script Prompt**

**Objective:**
"To tell the story of the _original_ **primitive** **automobiles**, their **shortcomings**, and how they were **phased out** by a new 'villain': the **Internal Combustion Engine**."

**Target Vocabulary:**
"This video sets the scene for the first steam cars and their replacement."

1.  **primitive**
2.  **automobiles**
3.  **engines**
4.  **evolved**
5.  **miniaturized**
6.  **lit**
7.  **reservoirs**
8.  **replenishment**
9.  **shortcomings**
10. **self-propelled**
11. **shuttling**
12. **Internal Combustion Engine**
13. **hand-crank**
14. **starters**
15. **phased out**

**Suggested Script Structure (REVISED):**
"**Start by setting the scene:** 'Imagine the first **primitive** **automobiles** **shuttling** people around. Their **engines** were **miniaturized** versions that **evolved** from steam trains. They were truly **self-propelled** machines.'

**Next, describe the 'fatal flaw':** 'But these cars had major **shortcomings**. The boilers had to be **lit** by hand and took 20 minutes to heat up. Their water **reservoirs** only lasted 30 miles before needing **replenishment**.'

**Then, describe the 'villain's' arrival:** 'A new "villain" arrived: the **Internal Combustion Engine**. At first, its **hand-crank** **starters** were difficult, but the electric starter fixed that. Soon, steam power was **phased out**.'

**Review:** Create a summary screen displaying all 15 words.

**Engagement Question:** 'What do you think was the biggest of the steam car's **shortcomings**: the small **reservoirs** or the time it took to get the boiler **lit**?'"
