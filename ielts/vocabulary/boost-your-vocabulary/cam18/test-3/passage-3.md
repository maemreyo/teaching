VIDEO 1: The Classroom Dilemma (15 words)

**Video Script Prompt**

**Objective:**
"To tell the story of a typical **literature** lesson, showing the _problem_ of the huge learning **gap** and introducing the 'solution' of 'streaming' (separating students)."

**Target Vocabulary:**
"This video sets the scene for the classroom challenge and the 'streaming' solution."

1.  **literature**
2.  **literacy**
3.  **tentative**
4.  **textual**
5.  **advocates**
6.  **moderation**
7.  **engagement**
8.  **gap**
9.  **virtue**
10. **diagnosed**
11. **attainment**
12. **macro**
13. **academically**
14. **brightest**
15. **subject-specific**

**Suggested Script Structure (REVISED):**
"**Start by setting the scene:** 'Imagine a **literature** lesson. The **gap** in **literacy** skills is huge. Some students are making **tentative** plot summaries, while others are using **textual** evidence to debate if Shakespeare **advocates** for **moderation** or **passionate** **engagement**.'

**Next, introduce the 'problem':** 'Wouldn't it be better to separate them? To put the students discussing **virtue** in one class, and the others in another?'

**Then, describe the 'solution':** 'This idea is called "streaming." At a **macro** level, it means **academically** selective schools for the **brightest**. Within schools, it means "sets" based on **diagnosed** **attainment** in **subject-specific** areas.'

**Review:** Create a summary screen displaying all 15 words.

**Engagement Question:** 'What do you think are the benefits of separating students by their **diagnosed** **attainment**?'"

**Suggested Script Structure (REVISED):**
"**Start with the 'discovery' from an expert:** 'Professor John Hattie's **influential** **meta-analysis** from the Melbourne Education Research **Institute** shows streaming has minimal effects. Worse, it harms students in the lowest sets, who are often from a low **socioeconomic** background, further **entrenching** the social divide.'

**Next, reveal the 'true villain':** 'The problem is the teacher's expectation. When students are grouped by **diagnosed** ability, it limits what the teacher believes they can do.'

**Then, introduce the 'hero' theory:** 'The current **pedagogical** **paradigm** is **arguably** **constructivism**, which **emerged** from Vygotsky. He argued for teaching in the 'zone of **proximal** development' using **'scaffolding'**. This means teaching should be flexible, **regardless** of a student's fixed **aptitude**.'
