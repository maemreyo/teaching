VIDEO 1: The Theory and The Experiment (16 words)

**Video Script Prompt**

**Objective:**
"To tell the story of _how_ the **'Growth mindset theory'** was born, **supplanting** the old idea of **innate** ability, and to introduce the _key experiment_ Dweck used to prove it."

**Target Vocabulary:**
"This video covers the origin of the idea and the setup of the famous experiment."

1.  **taken root**
2.  **innate**
3.  **supplanted**
4.  **sought to**
5.  **progressive**
6.  **revolted against**
7.  **inherent**
8.  **destiny**
9.  **'Growth mindset theory'**
10. **hallways**
11. **motivational**
12. **coaxing**
13. **failure**
14. **indication**
15. **educationalist**
16. **praised**

**Suggested Script Structure (REVISED):**
"**Start with the 'war of ideas':** 'A **progressive** idea has **taken root**, **supplanting** an old one. The old idea, which scientists **sought to** quantify, was that intelligence is **innate** and **inherent**—that ability is **destiny**. **Progressive** thinkers **revolted against** this. Now, the **'Growth mindset theory'** is on **motivational** posters in school **hallways**.'

**Next, introduce the 'origin story':** 'The theory's goal is **coaxing** students to see **failure** not as an **indication** of their ability, but as a chance to grow. As one **educationalist** said: "Smart is something you can get." In a key experiment, one group of students was **praised** for their effort.'

**Review:** Create a summary screen displaying all 16 words.

**Engagement Question:** 'Why do you think the **progressive** idea that ability is not **destiny** is more popular than the old idea of **innate** intelligence?'"
