### **Vocabulary Learning Plan: Green Roofs (3-Part Video Series)**

---

VIDEO 1: The "Green" Calling (14 words)

**Video Script Prompt**

**Objective:**
"To tell the story of _why_ cities are **drawn to** green roofs, framing the **monumental** urban problems as a 'call to action' that **paved the way** for this new idea."

**Target Vocabulary:**
"This video covers the problem and the initial concept of green roofs."

1.  **lush foliage**
2.  **drawn to**
3.  **mitigating**
4.  **tackling**
5.  **monumental**
6.  **involvement**
7.  **forward-thinking**
8.  **paved the way**
9.  **Ongoing**
10. **showcasing**
11. **partially**
12. **greenery**
13. **drainage**
14. **norm**

**Suggested Script Structure (REVISED):**
"**Start by setting the scene:** 'Our cities face **monumental** problems, from floods to pollution. Now, leaders are **drawn to** a new solution: green roofs. They see them as key for **mitigating** floods and **tackling** air pollution.'

**Next, describe the 'discovery':** 'We now see **lush foliage** on rooftops. This required the **involvement** of **forward-thinking** leaders, who **paved the way** with new policies. **Ongoing** research is **showcasing** even more ideas, like "living walls" **partially** covered in **greenery**.'

**Then, describe the 'goal':** 'For this to work, good **drainage** systems are crucial. The goal is to make these green spaces the new **norm** for all city buildings.'

**Review:** Create a summary screen displaying all 14 words.

**Engagement Question:** 'What do you think is the biggest benefit of having **lush foliage** and **greenery** in a dense city?'"

VIDEO 2: The Mainstream Revolution (10 words)

**Video Script Prompt**

**Objective:**
"To tell the story of _how_ green roofs became **mainstream** in North America, focusing on the **expansive** **array** of **edible** farms appearing on rooftops."

**Target Vocabulary:**
"This video covers the successful implementation of green roofs."

1.  **prescribing**
2.  **depression**
3.  **dementia**
4.  **obesity**
5.  **mainstream**
6.  **array**
7.  **expansive**
8.  **date from**
9.  **edible**
10. **retro-fit**

**Suggested Script Structure (REVISED):**
"**Start with the 'success story':** 'In North America, green roofs are now **mainstream**. This revolution isn't new; some policies **date from** the 1990s.'

**Next, describe the 'outcome':** 'The result is a whole **array** of **expansive** new green spaces, including **edible** roofs for farming. This is much easier to build on new buildings than to **retro-fit** old ones.'

**Then, describe the 'human impact':** 'The social value is huge. Doctors are now **prescribing** time in these gardens for patients dealing with **depression** or **dementia**, and to help fight **obesity**.'

**Review:** Create a summary screen displaying all 10 words.

**Engagement Question:** 'Why is it so much harder to **retro-fit** an old building with an **edible** roof than to build a new one?'"
