## Vocabulary Learning Plan: Saving the Soil (4 Videos)

This is a detailed plan to learn all vocabulary words from the reading passage, divided into 4 manageable parts. Each part corresponds to a video/learning session and explores the critical issue of soil degradation and the efforts to save this precious resource.

### VIDEO 1: The Silent Crisis (20 words)

**Video Script Prompt**

*   **Target a specific use case:** "Introduce the global soil crisis, its importance to human survival, and the complex microbial life within healthy soil."
*   **Focus on a specific source:** "Focus on the first 20 vocabulary words. For each, provide a short English meaning and a simple example sentence from the text."
    1.  at risk
    2.  precious
    3.  resource
    4.  endangered
    5.  sustains
    6.  degradation
    7.  microorganisms
    8.  fungi
    9.  decomposing
    10. minerals
    11. antibiotics
    12. antibiotic-resistant
    13. ally
    14. digest
    15. properties
    16. recover
    17. remove
    18. nutrients
    19. decay
    20. harvested
*   **Describe the show structure:**
    *   "Start with the alarming fact: 'More than a third of the Earth's top layer is **at risk**. Is there hope for this **precious** **resource**? The world's soil is **endangered**. Soil **sustains** human life in many ways.'"
    *   "Next, explain the science of soil: 'Scientists have warned about soil **degradation** for decades. A gram of healthy soil contains millions of bacteria and other **microorganisms** like **fungi**, living amid **decomposing** plants and various **minerals**. Soils are the source of nearly all our **antibiotics** and could be our best hope against **antibiotic-resistant** bacteria. Soil is also an **ally** against climate change: as microorganisms **digest** dead matter, they lock in carbon.'"
    *   "Finally, introduce the problem: 'If soil loses its special **properties**, it could take thousands of years to **recover**. When plants grow, they **remove** **nutrients**. When they die and **decay**, these are returned. But when we use **harvested** crops, this cycle is broken.'"
    *   "Then, create a review screen for the 20 words."
    *   "End with a question: 'Besides food, what are some of the other surprising ways soil **sustains** human life?'"
