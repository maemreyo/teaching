This is a detailed plan to learn all vocabulary words from the reading passage, divided into 3 manageable parts. Each part corresponds to a video/learning session and explores the fascinating and controversial world of AI-generated art.

- **Describe the show structure:**
  - "Start with the big question: 'Can computers create **artificial** art? Some programs claim to **possess** creative talents. Their music has had audiences **enraptured**, and even **tricked** them **into** thinking a human was behind the **score**. Their paintings have hung in **prestigious** galleries.'"
  - "Next, explore the philosophical debate: 'Humans perform **sophisticated** creative acts. If we can **break** this **down** into code, what does that mean for human creativity?   - "Finally, introduce the Painting Fool: 'To avoid this **criticism**, the Painting Fool needs only **minimal** direction. It finds its own **material** as it **trawls through** social media, creating pictures **from scratch**, like fuzzy landscapes **depicting** trees. Some say they have a **mechanical** look, but such reactions **arise from** double standards.'"
  - "Then, create a review screen for the 20 words."
  - "End with a question: 'Do you think **computerised art** can ever be more than a tool to **realise** a human's idea?'"

### VIDEO 3: The Human Prejudice (20 words)

**Video Script Prompt**

- **Target a specific use case:** "Analyze the psychological reasons behind our prejudice against AI art and explore how future AI artists might overcome this by tapping into human culture."
- **Focus on a specific source:** "Focus on the final 20 vocabulary words. - **Describe the show structure:**
