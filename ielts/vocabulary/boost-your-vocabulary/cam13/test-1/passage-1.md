- **Describe the show structure:**
  - "Start with the context: 'New Zealand has four million **inhabitants** and is a **long-haul** flight from major markets. Tourism is 9% of its **gross domestic product**. In 1999, the country **launched** a campaign focusing on its **scenic** beauty, **exhilarating** activities, and **authentic** Maori culture.'"
  - "Next, introduce the website: 'The campaign's key feature was a website providing **potential** visitors a single gateway. Any **tourism-related business** could get listed, gaining **access to** a huge audience. Businesses updated their details **on a regular basis**. A **scheme** was organized where businesses **underwent** an independent **evaluation**, and their environmental impact was **considered**. The site also had **interactive** journeys through locations from **blockbuster films**, which used the **stunning** **scenery** as a **backdrop**.'"
  - "Then, create a review screen for the 20 words."
  - "End with a question: 'What do you think is more important for a tourism campaign: **scenic** beauty or **authentic** culture?'"
