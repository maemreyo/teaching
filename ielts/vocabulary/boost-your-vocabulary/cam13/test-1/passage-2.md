## Vocabulary Learning Plan: The Science of Boredom (4 Videos)

This is a detailed plan to learn all vocabulary words from the reading passage, divided into 4 manageable parts. Each part corresponds to a video/learning session and explores the surprisingly complex and useful nature of boredom.

- **Describe the show structure:**
  - "Start with the feeling: 'Being bored isn't always seen as **stimulating**. It's impossible to **keep your mind on** anything. Defining it for study in the **lab** is hard. It can include many **mental states**, like **frustration**, **apathy**, **depression**, and **indifference**. Is it a low-energy state, or does being **agitated** and **restless** count? One researcher compares it to **disgust**, an emotion that protects us from **infection**.'"
  - "Next, introduce the five types: 'Researchers have **identified** five **distinct** types of boredom. These can be **plotted** on two **axes**, one measuring low to high **arousal**. **Intriguingly**, while we experience all types, we tend to **specialise in** one.'"
  - "Then, create a review screen for the 19 words."
  - "End with a question: 'Which of the five **distinct** types of boredom do you think you **specialise in**?'"
