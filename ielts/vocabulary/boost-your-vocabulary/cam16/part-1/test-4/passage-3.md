#### **VIDEO 1: The AI Paradox (16 words)**

**Video Script Prompt**

**Objective:**
"To introduce the powerful capabilities of AI and the central paradox of human trust through the real-world case study of IBM's Watson. This video explains _what_ the problem is."

**Target Vocabulary:**
"This video will cover the first 16 words, establishing the core conflict of the passage."

1.  **Artificial intelligence**
2.  **police forces**
3.  **map**
4.  **stroke**
5.  **reluctant**
6.  **accounted for**
7.  **coincided**
8.  **contradicted**
9.  **typically**
10. **competent**
11. **plausible**
12. **Consequently**
13. **suspicion**
14. **disbelief**
15. **seemingly**
16. **outlandish**

**Suggested Script Structure:**
"**Introduction:** Start with AI's power. '**Artificial intelligence** can predict the future. **Police forces** use it to **map** crime, and doctors use it to predict when a patient might have a **stroke**. Yet, humans are **reluctant** to trust it.'

**The Watson Dilemma:** 'IBM's Watson AI **accounted for** 80% of cancer cases. But if its advice **coincided** with a doctor's opinion, it was useless. If it **contradicted** them, doctors **typically** concluded the AI wasn't **competent**. The AI couldn't explain why its advice was **plausible**.'

**The Result:** '**Consequently**, this created **suspicion** and **disbelief**, leading doctors to ignore its **seemingly** **outlandish** advice.'

**Review:** Create a summary screen displaying all 16 words and their simple meanings.

**Engagement Question:** 'If an AI is only seen as **competent** when it agrees with us, how can it ever teach us something new?'"

**Suggested Script Structure:**

**The Fear of the Unknown:** 'AI is still **fairly** new. Its decision-making process is hard to **comprehend**, which causes **anxiety**. We don't hear about the many **instances** where AI works; instead, we are **acutely** aware of its failures.'
