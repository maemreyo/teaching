## Vocabulary Learning Plan: The Geography of Health (4 Videos)

This is a detailed plan to learn all vocabulary words from the reading passage, divided into 4 manageable parts. Each part corresponds to a video/learning session and explores the intersection of health sciences and geography.

**Video Script Prompt**

*   **Target a specific use case:** "Introduce the concept of health geography, explaining how geographical location can significantly impact health issues and concerns."
*   **Focus on a specific source:** "Focus on the first 16 vocabulary words. For each, provide a short English meaning and a simple example sentence from the text."

    3.  prevalent

    6.  resistant to
    7.  role
    8.  obvious
    9.  tropical
    10. foster
    11. damp
    12. deserts
    13. factors
    14. well-being
    15. smog
    16. massive
*   **Describe the show structure:**
    *   "Start with the global picture: 'Many diseases have been **eradicated** due to **vaccinations**, but in some areas, certain health issues are more **prevalent**. In a globalised world, people come into **contact** more often, leading to the spread of **infections** that are **resistant to** antibiotics.'"
    *   "Next, focus on geography's role: 'Geography plays a large **role** in health. The most **obvious** example is malaria in **tropical** regions that **foster** a warm and **damp** environment for mosquitos. It's less of a problem in high-altitude **deserts**. In other countries, geographical **factors** influence the **well-being** of the population. In large cities, **smog** from the **massive** number of cars can cause health problems.'"
    *   "Then, create a review screen for the 16 words."
    *   "End with a question: 'What other geographical **factors** can you think of that influence public **well-being**?'"
