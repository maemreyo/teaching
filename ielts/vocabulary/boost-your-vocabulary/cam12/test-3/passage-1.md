## Vocabulary Learning Plan: Flying Tortoises (4 Videos)

This is a detailed plan to learn all vocabulary words from the reading passage, divided into 4 manageable parts. Each part corresponds to a video/learning session and tells the story of the Galapagos tortoise conservation effort.

### VIDEO 1: An Inhospitable Home (17 words)

**Video Script Prompt**

*   **Target a specific use case:** "Introduce the Galapagos tortoise and its unique, challenging environment, explaining how it evolved into the largest tortoise on the planet."
*   **Focus on a specific source:** "Focus on the first 17 vocabulary words. For each, provide a short English meaning and a simple example sentence from the text."
    1.  reintroduction
    2.  significant
    3.  spiny
    4.  cacti
    5.  uneven
    6.  lava
    7.  interior
    8.  resembles
    9.  lunar
    10. respite
    11. vegetation
    12. inhospitable
    13. ancestral
    14. subspecies
    15. significant_predators
    16. exceeding
    17. pirates
*   **Describe the show structure:**
    *   "Start with the big picture: 'An airborne **reintroduction** programme has taken **significant** steps to protect the Galapagos tortoise. Forests of **spiny** **cacti** cover the **uneven** **lava** plains of the island's **interior**. The island **resembles** a **lunar** landscape. Only the thick **vegetation** offers **respite** from this **inhospitable** environment.'"
    *   "Next, introduce the tortoise: 'Some time after the islands were born, **ancestral** tortoises from the mainland colonised them, evolving into at least 14 different **subspecies**. In the absence of **significant** **predators**, they grew to become the largest tortoises on the planet, occasionally **exceeding** 1.8 metres in length. But from the 17th century, **pirates** began to hunt them.'"
    *   "Then, create a review screen for the 17 words."
    *   "End with a question: 'How does an **inhospitable** environment with no **significant predators** lead to animals like the tortoise **exceeding** their normal size?'"
