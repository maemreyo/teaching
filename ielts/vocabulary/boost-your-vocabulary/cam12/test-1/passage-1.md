## Vocabulary Learning Plan: The Story of Cork (3 Videos)

This is a detailed plan to learn all vocabulary words from the reading passage, divided into 3 manageable parts. Each part corresponds to a video/learning session and explores the history, properties, and uses of cork.

### VIDEO 1: The Remarkable Material (13 words)

**Video Script Prompt**

*   **Target a specific use case:** "Introduce cork as a material, highlighting its unique properties and its long history of use by ancient civilizations."
*   **Focus on a specific source:** "Focus on the first 13 vocabulary words. For each, provide a short English meaning and a simple example sentence from the text."

    2.  tough
    3.  elastic
    4.  buoyant
    5.  fire-resistant
    6.  millennia
    7.  sealed
    8.  extraordinary
    9.  insulating
    10. constant
    11. particular
    12. replicating
    13. release the pressure
*   **Describe the show structure:**
    *   "Start with its properties: 'Cork is a **remarkable** material. It's **tough**, **elastic**, **buoyant**, and **fire-resistant**. It has been used for **millennia**; the ancient Egyptians **sealed** their stone coffins with it.'"
    *   "Next, describe the tree itself: 'The cork oak is an **extraordinary** tree. Its thick bark is excellent at **insulating** the tree, keeping the inside at a **constant** temperature. The bark has a **particular** cellular structure that technology has never succeeded in **replicating**. It's full of air, which makes it so buoyant and elastic. You can squash it, and it springs back when you **release the pressure**.'"
    *   "Then, create a review screen for the 13 words."
    *   "End with a question: 'What other **remarkable** uses can you think of for a material that is **tough**, **elastic**, and **buoyant**?'"
