## Vocabulary Learning Plan: The Risks of Agriculture (4 Videos)

This is a detailed plan to learn all vocabulary words from the reading passage, divided into 4 manageable parts. Each part corresponds to a video/learning session and explores the challenges facing agriculture in developing countries.

*   **Describe the show structure:**
    *   "Start with a **synthesis** of an online debate. Two things **distinguish** food production: everyone needs it, and it is hugely **dependent on** nature. This makes it highly **vulnerable**. Cultural values are also deeply **entrenched** in agricultural systems.'"
    *   "Next, detail the risks: 'Farmers face **major** risks, including price **volatility**. Smallholders in developing countries also deal with **adverse** environments and poor **infrastructure**.
