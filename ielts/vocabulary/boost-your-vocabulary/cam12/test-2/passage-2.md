## Vocabulary Learning Plan: The Lost City (4 Videos)

This is a detailed plan to learn all vocabulary words from the reading passage, divided into 4 manageable parts. Each part corresponds to a video/learning session and follows Hiram Bingham's journey to Machu Picchu.

### VIDEO 1: The Explorer's Goal (15 words)

**Video Script Prompt**

*   **Target a specific use case:** "Introduce the explorer Hiram Bingham and his ambitious quest to find the lost city of Vitcos in the remote Andes."
*   **Focus on a specific source:** "Focus on the first 15 vocabulary words. For each, provide a short English meaning and a simple example sentence from the text."

    2.  civilization
    3.  exploration
    4.  remote
    5.  empire
    6.  locate
    7.  plateau
    8.  elevation
    9.  descend
    10. canyons
    11. set off
    12. preceded
    13. blasted down
    14. enable
    15. substantial
*   **Describe the show structure:**
    *   "Start with the mystery: 'This is the story of an encounter with the **ruined** city of Machu Picchu, an icon of the Inca **civilization**. In 1911, the explorer Hiram Bingham began his **exploration** of the **remote** hinterland of the old Inca **empire**. His goal was to **locate** the remains of a lost city.'"
    *   "Next, describe the journey: 'Cusco lies on a high **plateau** at an **elevation** of over 3,000 metres. Bingham's plan was to **descend** through an area of dramatic **canyons**. When he **set off**, he had an advantage over travellers who had **preceded** him: a track had recently been **blasted down** the canyon to **enable** rubber to be brought up. This allowed him to avoid cutting a **substantial** corner.'"
    *   "Then, create a review screen for the 15 words."
    *   "End with a question: 'What do you think was the biggest challenge of an **exploration** in such a **remote** and high-**elevation** area?'"
