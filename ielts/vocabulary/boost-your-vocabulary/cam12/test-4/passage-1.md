## Vocabulary Learning Plan: The History of Glass (3 Videos)

This is a detailed plan to learn all vocabulary words from the reading passage, divided into 3 manageable parts. Each part corresponds to a video/learning session and tells the story of glass from its natural origins to a modern-day commodity.

### VIDEO 1: From Volcanoes to the Roman Empire (12 words)

**Video Script Prompt**

*   **Target a specific use case:** "Trace the earliest history of glass, from its natural formation in volcanoes to its development and spread by the Romans."
*   **Focus on a specific source:** "Focus on the first 12 vocabulary words. For each, provide a short English meaning and a simple example sentence from the text."
    1.  eruption
    2.  spears
    3.  evidence
    4.  glazes
    5.  container
    6.  impurities
    7.  raw
    8.  tinted
    9.  guarded
    10. collapsed
    11. reputation
    12. counter
*   **Describe the show structure:**
    *   "Start with the origins: 'Natural glass formed from the intense heat of a volcanic **eruption** and was first used as tips for **spears**. Archaeologists have found **evidence** of man-made glass from 4000 BC in the form of **glazes** on stone beads. The first hollow glass **container** was made around 1500 BC.'"
    *   "Next, explain the Roman influence: 'Early glass was highly coloured due to **impurities** in the **raw** material. In the first century AD, methods for creating colourless glass were developed, which was then **tinted** with other materials. The Romans **guarded** the secret of glass making closely. It was not until their empire **collapsed** that the knowledge became widespread. From the 10th century, the Venetians gained a **reputation** for their skill. An English glass maker later attempted to **counter** the effect of clouding in blown glass.'"
    *   "Then, create a review screen for the 12 words."
    *   "End with a question: 'Why do you think the Romans **guarded** the secrets of glassmaking so closely?'"
