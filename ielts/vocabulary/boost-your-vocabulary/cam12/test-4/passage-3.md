## Vocabulary Learning Plan: The Boardroom in Crisis (4 Videos)

This is a detailed plan to learn all vocabulary words from the reading passage, divided into 4 manageable parts. Each part corresponds to a video/learning session and explores the challenges facing corporate boards of directors.

### VIDEO 1: The Pressure Cooker (13 words)

**Video Script Prompt**

*   **Target a specific use case:** "Introduce the increased pressures and scrutiny on corporate boards following the 2008 financial crisis."
*   **Focus on a specific source:** "Focus on the first 13 vocabulary words. For each, provide a short English meaning and a simple example sentence from the text."
    1.  governance
    2.  meltdown
    3.  resulted in
    4.  prolonged
    5.  downturn
    6.  crisis
    7.  auditors
    8.  picked over
    9.  inquiries
    10. scrutiny
    11. fulfilling
    12. significantly
    13. classic
*   **Describe the show structure:**
    *   "Start with the problem: 'There have been serious failures of corporate **governance**. Following the 2008 financial **meltdown**, which **resulted in** a **prolonged** economic **downturn**, the search for explanations for the **crisis** began. Governments, regulators, and **auditors** were all in the frame.'"
    *   "Next, explain the consequences: 'The failures of bank directors have been extensively **picked over** in reports and **inquiries**. The knock-on effect of this **scrutiny** is that the time involved in **fulfilling** the demands of a directorship has increased **significantly**, calling into question the **classic** model of corporate governance.'"
    *   "Then, create a review screen for the 13 words."
    *   "End with a question: 'Do you think the increased **scrutiny** on corporate **governance** is a positive or negative development?'"
