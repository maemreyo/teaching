## Vocabulary Learning Plan: Rewilding Britain (4 Videos)

This is a detailed plan to learn all vocabulary words from the reading passage, divided into 4 manageable parts. Each part corresponds to a video/learning session and explores the concept of rewilding and the potential return of the lynx to Britain.

### VIDEO 1: The Ghost Cat (12 words)

**Video Script Prompt**

*   **Target a specific use case:** "Introduce the mystery of the llewyn, the historical evidence for the lynx in Britain, and the concept of rewilding."
*   **Focus on a specific source:** "Focus on the first 12 vocabulary words. For each, provide a short English meaning and a simple example sentence from the text."
    1.  vanished
    2.  mystery
    3.  presumed
    4.  died out
    5.  inhabitants
    6.  took up
    7.  compelling
    8.  evidence
    9.  extinction
    10. roughly
    11. glimpse
    12. unmistakable
*   **Describe the show structure:**
    *   "Start with the puzzle: 'It's time to return **vanished** native animals to Britain. An ancient poem describes hunting a **mystery** animal. What was it? The lynx was **presumed** to have **died out** 6,000 years ago, before the **inhabitants** of the islands **took up** farming.'"
    *   "Next, reveal the discovery: 'But a 2006 bone find is **compelling** **evidence** that the lynx and the llewyn were the same. This would bring forward its **extinction** date by **roughly** 5,000 years. This isn't the last **glimpse** of the animal in British culture; a 9th-century stone cross shows an **unmistakable** image of the cat. The lynx is now the symbol of the rewilding movement.'"
    *   "Then, create a review screen for the 12 words."
    *   "End with a question: 'Why is finding this **compelling** **evidence** so important for the rewilding movement?'"
