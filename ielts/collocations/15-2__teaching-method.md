### 👨‍🏫 **Phương pháp Truyền thống & Vai trò Giáo viên (Traditional Methods & The Teacher's Role)**

| Collocation                                | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                                        | Short English Meaning                                                                                   | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                        |
| :----------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------ | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **A teacher-centered / didactic approach** | Một phương pháp lấy giáo viên làm trung tâm / giáo huấn | A teaching method where the teacher is the main source of knowledge and instruction.                    | In a **teacher-centered approach**, the teacher is the primary authority figure.                        |
| **Rote learning / memorization**           | Học vẹt / học thuộc lòng                                | A memorization technique based on repetition, often without a deep understanding of the subject matter. | Critics argue that **rote learning** stifles creativity and critical thinking.                          |
| **Passive learning**                       | Học tập thụ động                                        | A learning method where students receive information from the instructor without active participation.  | Traditional lectures can sometimes lead to **passive learning**, where student engagement is minimal.   |
| **To deliver a lecture**                   | Giảng bài                                               | To give a formal talk on an academic subject to a group of students.                                    | The professor will **deliver a lecture** on the causes of the First World War.                          |
| **To impart knowledge**                    | Truyền đạt kiến thức                                    | To transmit information and knowledge to someone.                                                       | The primary role of a teacher was once seen as simply **to impart knowledge**.                          |
| **The "chalk and talk" method**            | Phương pháp "phấn và lời giảng"                         | A traditional teaching method involving the teacher writing on a blackboard and talking to the class.   | The **"chalk and talk" method** is being replaced by more interactive and technology-driven techniques. |
| **To act as a facilitator of learning**    | Đóng vai trò là người hỗ trợ, điều phối việc học        | To guide and assist students in their own learning process, rather than just providing answers.         | In modern classrooms, the teacher's role is **to act as a facilitator of learning**.                    |
| **To provide guidance and mentorship**     | Cung cấp sự hướng dẫn và cố vấn                         | To give help, advice, and support based on one's experience.                                            | A good teacher does more than teach; they **provide guidance and mentorship**.                          |
| **Direct instruction**                     | Giảng dạy trực tiếp                                     | A teaching method where the teacher explicitly teaches a skill or concept.                              | **Direct instruction** is effective for teaching foundational knowledge and skills.                     |
| **Curriculum-centered teaching**           | Giảng dạy lấy chương trình làm trung tâm                | An approach that prioritizes covering the content of the established curriculum.                        | **Curriculum-centered teaching** can sometimes neglect the individual needs of students.                |

### 💻 **Phương pháp Hiện đại & Công nghệ (Modern Methods & Technology)**

| Collocation                                        | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                                   | Short English Meaning                                                                                                        | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                 |
| :------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A student-centered / learner-centered approach** | Phương pháp lấy học sinh làm trung tâm             | A teaching method that shifts the focus from the teacher to the individual student and their learning needs.                 | A **student-centered approach** promotes autonomy and active participation.                      |
| **Active and interactive learning**                | Học tập chủ động và tương tác                      | Learning methods where students are actively involved in the learning process through discussion and activities.             | Group discussions, debates, and projects are forms of **interactive learning**.                  |
| **Collaborative learning / Peer learning**         | Học tập hợp tác / Học tập đồng đẳng                | An educational approach involving groups of students working together to solve a problem or complete a task.                 | **Collaborative learning** helps students develop teamwork and communication skills.             |
| **Hands-on activities and experiential learning**  | Các hoạt động thực hành và học tập qua trải nghiệm | Learning by doing things oneself and reflecting on the experience.                                                           | Science subjects are best taught through **hands-on activities and experiential learning**.      |
| **Problem-based learning (PBL)**                   | Học tập dựa trên giải quyết vấn đề                 | A student-centered pedagogy in which students learn about a subject through the experience of solving an open-ended problem. | **Problem-based learning** encourages students to apply their knowledge to real-world scenarios. |
| **Inquiry-based learning**                         | Học tập dựa trên truy vấn                          | A form of active learning that starts by posing questions, problems or scenarios.                                            | **Inquiry-based learning** fosters curiosity and develops research skills.                       |
| **To integrate technology into the curriculum**    | Tích hợp công nghệ vào chương trình giảng dạy      | To incorporate digital tools and resources as a core part of the teaching and learning process.                              | Many schools are finding new ways **to integrate technology into the curriculum**.               |
| **E-learning / Online learning**                   | Học trực tuyến                                     | Education that takes place over the internet.                                                                                | **E-learning** offers flexibility for those who cannot attend traditional, campus-based classes. |
| **Distance learning / education**                  | Đào tạo từ xa                                      | A mode of delivering education to students who are not physically present in a traditional classroom.                        | **Distance learning** has made it possible to get a degree from a university in another country. |
| **Blended learning / A hybrid model**              | Học tập kết hợp / Mô hình lai                      | An educational model that combines online digital media with traditional classroom methods.                                  | **Blended learning** combines the benefits of online resources with face-to-face interaction.    |

### 🌐 **Tài nguyên & Môi trường Học tập (Resources & Learning Environments)**

| Collocation                                        | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                                 | Short English Meaning                                                                                          | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                 |
| :------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Instant access to online resources**             | Tiếp cận tức thì các nguồn tài nguyên trực tuyến | The ability to immediately use a vast range of learning materials on the internet.                             | The internet provides students with **instant access to a vast range of online resources**.      |
| **Educational software and applications**          | Phần mềm và ứng dụng giáo dục                    | Computer programs or apps specifically designed to teach or facilitate learning.                               | There are many **educational apps** designed to help children learn languages in a fun way.      |
| **Digital literacy skills**                        | Các kỹ năng về trình độ kỹ thuật số              | The ability to find, evaluate, utilize, share, and create content using information technologies.              | **Digital literacy skills** are now considered as essential as traditional literacy.             |
| **To bridge geographical barriers**                | Xóa bỏ các rào cản địa lý                        | To connect people and provide opportunities regardless of their physical location.                             | Online learning can **bridge geographical barriers**, connecting students and teachers globally. |
| **Face-to-face instruction / interaction**         | Giảng dạy / tương tác trực tiếp                  | Teaching or communication that happens in person, in a shared physical space.                                  | Some students prefer **face-to-face interaction** with their teachers and peers.                 |
| **A positive and supportive learning environment** | Một môi trường học tập tích cực và hỗ trợ        | A classroom atmosphere that is safe, respectful, and encouraging for all students.                             | It is the teacher's responsibility to create a **positive and supportive learning environment**. |
| **Self-paced learning**                            | Học theo nhịp độ cá nhân                         | A learning method where the learner has control over the amount of material and duration of learning.          | One of the advantages of online courses is that they allow for **self-paced learning**.          |
| **To study at one's own pace**                     | Học theo tốc độ của riêng mình                   | To learn at a speed that is comfortable for the individual.                                                    | Flexible learning models permit students **to study at their own pace**.                         |
| **A virtual learning environment (VLE)**           | Môi trường học tập ảo                            | An online platform that provides students with digital resources, communication tools, and assessment systems. | Students can download materials and submit assignments through the university's **VLE**.         |
| **Gamification of learning**                       | Trò chơi hóa việc học                            | The application of game-design elements and principles in educational contexts.                                | The **gamification of learning** can increase student engagement and motivation.                 |

### 📊 **Đánh giá & Phản hồi (Assessment & Feedback)**

| Collocation                                     | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                                  | Short English Meaning                                                                                   | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                                |
| :---------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------ | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **To assess student learning and performance**  | Đánh giá việc học và kết quả học tập của học sinh | To judge the quality and progress of a student's work.                                                  | Teachers use a variety of methods **to assess student learning and performance**.                               |
| **To provide timely and constructive feedback** | Cung cấp phản hồi kịp thời và mang tính xây dựng  | To give helpful comments and suggestions for improvement in a prompt manner.                            | **Providing timely and constructive feedback** is more effective for learning than simply giving a grade.       |
| **Continuous assessment**                       | Đánh giá liên tục                                 | A system of evaluating students' work throughout a course, rather than solely at the end.               | Many educators believe **continuous assessment** is a fairer and more comprehensive way to evaluate students.   |
| **Formative versus summative assessment**       | Đánh giá quá trình và đánh giá tổng kết           | Assessment for learning (formative) versus assessment of learning (summative).                          | **Formative assessment** helps guide the learning process, while **summative assessment** measures the outcome. |
| **Standardized testing**                        | Thi cử theo chuẩn hóa                             | A test that requires all test takers to answer the same questions and is scored in a consistent manner. | The effectiveness and fairness of **standardized testing** is a topic of intense debate in education.           |
| **Peer assessment / evaluation**                | Đánh giá / thẩm định đồng đẳng                    | The process of students evaluating the work of their peers.                                             | **Peer assessment** can help students develop their critical judgment skills.                                   |
| **Self-assessment**                             | Tự đánh giá                                       | The process of a student evaluating their own work and learning progress.                               | **Self-assessment** encourages students to take responsibility for their own learning.                          |
| **An over-emphasis on exam results**            | Sự quá chú trọng vào kết quả thi cử               | Placing too much importance on grades from examinations.                                                | Critics argue that an **over-emphasis on exam results** leads to teaching to the test.                          |
| **To measure academic achievement**             | Đo lường thành tích học tập                       | To quantify how well a student has performed in their academic studies.                                 | Standardized tests are one way **to measure academic achievement** across a large population.                   |
| **Alternative forms of assessment**             | Các hình thức đánh giá thay thế                   | Methods of evaluation other than traditional tests, such as portfolios, projects, and presentations.    | **Alternative forms of assessment** can provide a more holistic view of a student's abilities.                  |
