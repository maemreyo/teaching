### 👨‍🏫 **Phương pháp Truyền thống & Vai trò Giáo viên (Traditional Methods & The Teacher's Role)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A teacher-centered approach** | Phương pháp lấy giáo viên làm trung tâm | A teaching method where the teacher is the main focus. | In a **teacher-centered approach**, the teacher is the main source of knowledge. |
| **Rote learning / memorization** | Học vẹt / học thuộc lòng | Learning by repetition, not by understanding. | Critics argue that **rote learning** does not encourage understanding or critical thinking. |
| **Passive learning** | Học tập thụ động | Learning by listening and receiving information without active participation. | Lectures can sometimes lead to **passive learning**, where students simply receive information. |
| **To deliver a lecture** | Giảng bài | To give a formal talk to a group of students. | The professor will **deliver a lecture** on modern history. |
| **To impart knowledge** | Truyền đạt kiến thức | To give knowledge to someone. | The primary role of a teacher was once simply **to impart knowledge** to students. |
| **The chalk and talk method** | Phương pháp "phấn và bảng" (giảng dạy truyền thống) | A traditional teaching method using a blackboard and talking. | The **chalk and talk method** is being replaced by more interactive techniques. |
| **To act as a facilitator** | Đóng vai trò là người hỗ trợ, điều phối | To help a group of learners to learn, rather than just teaching them. | In modern classrooms, the teacher's role is **to act as a facilitator** of learning. |
| **To provide guidance** | Cung cấp sự hướng dẫn | To give help and advice. | A good teacher does more than teach; they **provide guidance** and support. |

### 💻 **Phương pháp Hiện đại & Công nghệ (Modern Methods & Technology)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A student-centered approach** | Phương pháp lấy học sinh làm trung tâm | A teaching method that focuses on the students' needs. | A **student-centered approach** focuses on the needs and interests of the individual learner. |
| **Active / Interactive learning** | Học tập chủ động / tương tác | Learning by being actively involved in the process. | Group discussions and projects are forms of **interactive learning**. |
| **Collaborative learning** | Học tập hợp tác | Learning by working together in a group. | **Collaborative learning** helps students develop teamwork and communication skills. |
| **Hands-on activities / experience** | Các hoạt động / kinh nghiệm thực hành | Learning by doing things yourself. | Science subjects are best taught through **hands-on activities**. |
| **Problem-based learning** | Học tập dựa trên giải quyết vấn đề | Learning by working to solve a real problem. | **Problem-based learning** encourages students to apply their knowledge to real-world scenarios. |
| **To integrate technology into the classroom** | Tích hợp công nghệ vào lớp học | To use technology as part of teaching. | Many schools are finding new ways **to integrate technology into the classroom**. |
| **E-learning / Online learning** | Học trực tuyến | Learning done via the internet. | **E-learning** offers flexibility for those who cannot attend traditional classes. |
| **Distance learning** | Đào tạo từ xa | Studying remotely, not in a physical classroom. | **Distance learning** has made it possible to get a degree from a university in another country. |
| **Blended learning** | Học tập kết hợp (trực tuyến và trực tiếp) | A mix of online and in-person learning. | **Blended learning** combines the benefits of online resources with face-to-face interaction. |
| **A virtual learning environment (VLE)** | Môi trường học tập ảo | An online system for teaching and learning. | Students can download materials and submit assignments through the university's **VLE**. |

### 📊 **Đánh giá & Phản hồi (Assessment & Feedback)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **To assess student performance** | Đánh giá kết quả học tập của học sinh | To judge how well a student is doing. | Teachers use a variety of methods **to assess student performance**. |
| **To provide constructive feedback** | Cung cấp phản hồi mang tính xây dựng | To give helpful comments and suggestions for improvement. | **Providing constructive feedback** is more helpful than simply giving a grade. |
| **Continuous assessment** | Đánh giá liên tục | Evaluating students' work throughout a course. | Many educators believe **continuous assessment** is a fairer way to evaluate students than a single final exam. |
| **Standardized testing** | Thi cử theo chuẩn hóa | Using the same test for all students to compare results. | The effectiveness of **standardized testing** is a topic of intense debate in education. |
