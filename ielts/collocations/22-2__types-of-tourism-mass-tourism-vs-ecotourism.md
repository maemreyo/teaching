### ✈️ **Du lịch Đại chúng (Mass Tourism)**

Phần này tập trung vào các cụm từ mô tả hình thức du lịch quy mô lớn, truyền thống.

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mass tourism** | Du lịch đại chúng | Tourism involving large numbers of people. | **Mass tourism** often involves thousands of people visiting popular destinations during peak season. |
| **Conventional / Mainstream tourism** | Du lịch thông thường / chủ đạo | Traditional tourism. | **Conventional tourism** often prioritizes profit over environmental protection. |
| **Large-scale development** | Sự phát triển quy mô lớn | The construction of large buildings and facilities. | The coastline has been transformed by the **large-scale development** of hotels and resorts. |
| **A huge influx of tourists** | Một dòng khách du lịch khổng lồ | A very large number of tourists arriving. | **A huge influx of tourists** can overwhelm a small town's infrastructure. |
| **All-inclusive resorts** | Các khu nghỉ dưỡng trọn gói | Resorts where everything is included in the price. | **All-inclusive resorts** often prevent tourists from spending money in the local community. |
| **A package holiday** | Một kỳ nghỉ trọn gói | A holiday where flights, hotel, and sometimes food are all included in the price. | Many people prefer the convenience of a **package holiday**, which includes flights, hotels, and tours. |
| **Standardized tourist experiences** | Những trải nghiệm du lịch được tiêu chuẩn hóa | Tourist experiences that are the same for everyone. | Mass tourism can lead to **standardized tourist experiences** that lack authenticity. |
| **To cater to the masses** | Phục vụ cho đại chúng | To provide what a large number of people want. | The entertainment and food options are designed **to cater to the masses**. |
| **High-volume, low-cost travel** | Du lịch số lượng lớn, chi phí thấp | Travel that involves a lot of people and is cheap. | Budget airlines have fueled the growth of **high-volume, low-cost travel**. |
| **To focus on profit maximization** | Tập trung vào việc tối đa hóa lợi nhuận | To make as much profit as possible. | The business model of many large tour operators is **to focus on profit maximization**. |

### 🌱 **Du lịch Sinh thái & Bền vững (Ecotourism & Sustainable Tourism)**

Phần này tập trung vào các cụm từ mô tả hình thức du lịch có trách nhiệm với môi trường và cộng đồng.

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ecotourism / Sustainable tourism** | Du lịch sinh thái / Du lịch bền vững | Tourism that is environmentally friendly and benefits local people. | The main principle of **ecotourism** is to travel responsibly to natural areas. |
| **Responsible travel** | Du lịch có trách nhiệm | Travelling in a way that is ethical and does not harm the environment or local people. | **Responsible travel** involves respecting local cultures and minimizing your environmental footprint. |
| **Low-impact tourism** | Du lịch ít tác động | Tourism that has a small effect on the environment. | Activities like hiking and bird-watching are considered forms of **low-impact tourism**. |
| **An eco-friendly tour / hotel** | Một tour / khách sạn thân thiện với môi trường | A tour or hotel that is environmentally friendly. | We stayed in an **eco-friendly hotel** that used solar power and recycled water. |
| **To travel off the beaten track** | Đi du lịch đến những nơi ít người lui tới | To go to places that are not common for tourists. | Ecotourists often prefer **to travel off the beaten track** to experience unspoiled nature. |
| **An authentic cultural experience** | Một trải nghiệm văn hóa đích thực | A real and genuine cultural experience. | Staying with a local family can provide a more **authentic cultural experience**. |
| **Locally-owned businesses** | Các doanh nghiệp do người địa phương sở hữu | Businesses that are owned by local people. | Supporting **locally-owned businesses** ensures that money stays within the community. |
| **Small-scale operations** | Các hoạt động quy mô nhỏ | Businesses or activities that are small in size. | Ecotourism lodges are typically **small-scale operations** that blend in with the environment. |
| **To have an educational component** | Có yếu tố giáo dục | To include an element of learning. | A key feature of ecotourism is that it should **have an educational component**. |

### ⚖️ **Nguyên tắc & Tác động So sánh (Principles & Comparative Impacts)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **To minimize the environmental impact** | Giảm thiểu tác động môi trường | To reduce the harm done to the environment. | The primary goal of sustainable tourism is **to minimize the environmental impact**. |
| **To promote conservation efforts** | Thúc đẩy các nỗ lực bảo tồn | To encourage actions to protect the environment. | Revenue from ecotourism can be used **to promote local conservation efforts**. |
| **To raise environmental awareness** | Nâng cao nhận thức về môi trường | To make people more knowledgeable about environmental issues. | Guided tours in national parks can **raise environmental awareness** among tourists. |
| **To respect the local culture** | Tôn trọng văn hóa địa phương | To show respect for the culture of a place. | Responsible tourists should always make an effort **to respect the local culture**. |
| **To empower local communities** | Trao quyền cho cộng đồng địa phương | To give local people more control over their own lives. | Ecotourism can **empower local communities** by providing alternative sources of income. |
| **To preserve fragile ecosystems** | Gìn giữ các hệ sinh thái mỏng manh | To protect natural environments that are easily damaged. | Strict regulations are needed **to preserve fragile ecosystems** from the impacts of tourism. |
| **To have a large / small carbon footprint** | Có dấu chân carbon lớn / nhỏ | To produce a large / small amount of carbon dioxide. | Air travel, a key part of mass tourism, has a **large carbon footprint**. |
| **To provide a more meaningful experience** | Cung cấp một trải nghiệm ý nghĩa hơn | To provide an experience that is more significant and valuable. | Many travelers find that ecotourism **provides a more meaningful travel experience**. |
| **To generate significant waste** | Tạo ra lượng rác thải đáng kể | To produce a large amount of waste. | Large cruise ships and all-inclusive resorts can **generate significant amounts of waste**. |
