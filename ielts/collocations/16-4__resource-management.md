### 💎 **Các loại Tài nguyên (Types of Resources)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Natural resources** | Tài nguyên thiên nhiên | Materials or substances such as minerals, forests, water, and fertile land that occur in nature. | A country's economic wealth often depends on its **natural resources**. |
| **Finite / Infinite resources** | Tài nguyên hữu hạn / vô hạn | Resources that are limited / unlimited. | We must remember that many resources, like fossil fuels, are **finite**. |
| **Renewable resources** | Tài nguyên tái tạo | Resources that can be replaced naturally. | Solar and wind power are examples of **renewable resources**. |
| **Non-renewable resources** | Tài nguyên không tái tạo | Resources that cannot be replaced once used. | Our society is currently too dependent on **non-renewable resources**. |
| **Raw materials** | Nguyên liệu thô | The basic material from which a product is made. | The factory imports **raw materials** from several different countries. |
| **Water resources** | Nguồn tài nguyên nước | Natural sources of water, such as rivers and lakes. | Effective management of **water resources** is crucial in arid regions. |

### 📉 **Sử dụng & Khai thác (Use & Exploitation)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The depletion of natural resources** | Sự cạn kiệt tài nguyên thiên nhiên | The reduction in the amount of natural resources. | **The depletion of natural resources** is a direct consequence of overconsumption. |
| **To exploit resources** | Khai thác tài nguyên | To use resources, often for profit. | It is unethical **to exploit resources** without considering the impact on local communities. |
| **Unsustainable exploitation** | Sự khai thác không bền vững | Using resources in a way that cannot be maintained. | **Unsustainable exploitation** of forests has led to widespread deforestation. |
| **Resource management** | Quản lý tài nguyên | The control and planning of the use of resources. | Proper **resource management** is key to achieving sustainable development. |
| **To consume resources at an alarming rate** | Tiêu thụ tài nguyên ở mức báo động | To use resources very quickly in a worrying way. | The modern world is **consuming resources at an alarming rate**. |
| **Over-exploitation of resources** | Khai thác tài nguyên quá mức | Using resources too much. | **Over-exploitation of fish stocks** has led to the collapse of some fisheries. |
| **Resource scarcity** | Sự khan hiếm tài nguyên | A lack of sufficient resources. | **Resource scarcity** can lead to economic instability and conflict. |
| **To put a strain on resources** | Gây áp lực lên tài nguyên | To place a heavy burden on resources. | A rapidly growing population will **put a strain on** a country's water and food **resources**. |

### 🌱 **Sự Bền vững & Các Hoạt động Bền vững (Sustainability & Sustainable Practices)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sustainable development** | Phát triển bền vững | Development that meets the needs of the present without compromising the future. | The goal of **sustainable development** is to meet present needs without compromising the future. |
| **Sustainable agriculture / forestry** | Nông nghiệp / Lâm nghiệp bền vững | Farming or forestry that is environmentally friendly. | **Sustainable agriculture** aims to produce food while protecting the environment. |
| **To live a sustainable lifestyle** | Sống một lối sống bền vững | To live in a way that does not harm the environment. | **Living a sustainable lifestyle** involves reducing consumption and waste. |
| **To adopt sustainable practices** | Áp dụng các phương pháp bền vững | To start using methods that are environmentally friendly. | More companies are beginning **to adopt sustainable practices** in their operations. |
| **A circular economy** | Nền kinh tế tuần hoàn | An economy where waste is eliminated by reusing and recycling materials. | **A circular economy** aims to eliminate waste by reusing and recycling materials. |
| **Corporate social responsibility** | Trách nhiệm xã hội của doanh nghiệp | The idea that companies should be responsible for their impact on society and the environment. | **Corporate social responsibility** includes minimizing a company's environmental impact. |
| **Environmentally friendly products** | Các sản phẩm thân thiện với môi trường | Products that do not harm the environment. | There is a growing demand for **environmentally friendly products**. |

### ⚡ **Năng lượng & Tiêu dùng (Energy & Consumption)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Energy consumption** | Mức tiêu thụ năng lượng | The amount of energy used. | We can reduce our **energy consumption** by using more efficient appliances. |
| **To conserve energy** | Tiết kiệm, bảo tồn năng lượng | To save or use energy carefully. | Simple actions like turning off lights can help **to conserve energy**. |
| **Energy efficiency** | Hiệu quả năng lượng | Using less energy to do the same job. | Improving the **energy efficiency** of buildings is a key climate goal. |
| **To be heavily reliant on fossil fuels** | Phụ thuộc nặng nề vào nhiên liệu hóa thạch | To depend very much on coal, oil, or gas. | Many economies are still **heavily reliant on fossil fuels**. |
| **A consumer society** | Một xã hội tiêu dùng | A society where buying and selling goods is very important. | We live in a **consumer society** that values material possessions. |
| **A throw-away culture** | Văn hóa "dùng một lần rồi vứt" | A culture where people throw things away after using them once. | Our **throw-away culture** is responsible for the massive amount of landfill waste. |
| **Sustainable consumption** | Tiêu dùng bền vững | Buying and using products in a way that is environmentally friendly. | The concept of **sustainable consumption** encourages people to buy less but better quality. |
