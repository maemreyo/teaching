### 🏛️ **Kiến trúc & Các Tòa nhà (Architecture & Buildings)**

| Collocation                           | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                     | Short English Meaning                                                                             | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                       |
| :------------------------------------ | :----------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Architectural heritage**            | Di sản kiến trúc                     | The legacy of historic buildings and structures that are of cultural and historical significance. | It is our collective responsibility to protect our nation's rich **architectural heritage**.           |
| **To preserve historic buildings**    | Gìn giữ, bảo tồn các tòa nhà lịch sử | To maintain and protect old and culturally significant buildings from demolition or decay.        | Many people believe it is vital **to preserve historic buildings** as they are part of our identity.   |
| **To demolish a building**            | Phá dỡ một tòa nhà                   | To completely knock down or destroy a building.                                                   | The council controversially decided **to demolish the old theater** to make way for a new development. |
| **An iconic landmark**                | Một công trình mang tính biểu tượng  | A famous and easily recognizable building or structure that represents a city or country.         | The Eiffel Tower is an **iconic landmark** that is recognized all over the world.                      |
| **An architectural masterpiece**      | Một kiệt tác kiến trúc               | A building that is considered a work of art due to its exceptional design and construction.       | The Sydney Opera House is widely regarded as an **architectural masterpiece**.                         |
| **An eyesore**                        | Một thứ chướng tai gai mắt           | A building or object that is extremely ugly and spoils the appearance of a place.                 | The abandoned, derelict factory has become an **eyesore** in the neighborhood.                         |
| **To blend in with the surroundings** | Hòa hợp với cảnh quan xung quanh     | To have a design that harmonizes with the natural or built environment around it.                 | Modern architecture should be designed **to blend in with its natural surroundings**.                  |
| **Aesthetically pleasing**            | Đẹp, có tính thẩm mỹ cao             | Beautiful, attractive, or pleasing to the eye.                                                    | While the building is functional, it is not particularly **aesthetically pleasing**.                   |
| **Utilitarian / Functional design**   | Thiết kế vị lợi / công năng          | A design that is focused on being practical and useful rather than attractive.                    | The architect prioritized a **utilitarian design** that maximized space and efficiency.                |
| **State-of-the-art architecture**     | Kiến trúc hiện đại bậc nhất          | Architecture that incorporates the very latest ideas, technologies, and features.                 | The new museum is a stunning example of **state-of-the-art architecture**.                             |

### 🏡 **Nhà ở & Nơi ở (Housing & Accommodation)**

| Collocation                                  | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                               | Short English Meaning                                                                               | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                        |
| :------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **A chronic shortage of affordable housing** | Sự thiếu hụt nhà ở giá cả phải chăng kinh niên | A long-term and persistent lack of housing that is affordable for people on low or average incomes. | There is a **chronic shortage of affordable housing** in many of the world's major cities.              |
| **High-rise apartment buildings**            | Các tòa nhà chung cư cao tầng                  | Tall buildings containing multiple floors of apartments.                                            | To cope with population density, cities are building more **high-rise apartment buildings**.            |
| **Single-family homes**                      | Nhà ở cho một gia đình                         | Detached or semi-detached houses designed for one family to live in.                                | The suburbs are characterized by sprawling developments of **single-family homes**.                     |
| **Residential areas / neighborhoods**        | Các khu dân cư                                 | Areas of a town or city where people live, rather than where they work.                             | The new development will create a new **residential area** with over 500 homes.                         |
| **Substandard housing conditions**           | Điều kiện nhà ở dưới tiêu chuẩn                | Living in a place that is unsafe, unsanitary, or in a poor state of repair.                         | Living in **substandard housing conditions** can have a severe negative impact on a person's health.    |
| **To get on the property ladder**            | Mua được căn nhà đầu tiên                      | To buy one's first home, seen as the first step towards owning more valuable property.              | Soaring house prices make it extremely difficult for young people **to get on the property ladder**.    |
| **A housing crisis**                         | Một cuộc khủng hoảng nhà ở                     | A situation in which there is a severe lack of affordable and adequate housing.                     | The city is facing a severe **housing crisis**, with demand far outstripping supply.                    |
| **To provide adequate housing**              | Cung cấp đủ nhà ở                              | To ensure that there is enough housing of a good standard for all citizens.                         | It is the government's responsibility **to provide adequate housing** for its population.               |
| **Urban slums**                              | Các khu ổ chuột đô thị                         | Densely populated urban areas characterized by substandard housing and poverty.                     | Millions of people worldwide live in overcrowded **urban slums** with limited access to basic services. |
| **Homelessness**                             | Tình trạng vô gia cư                           | The state of having no home.                                                                        | **Homelessness** is a complex social problem that is on the rise in many wealthy nations.               |

### 🌳 **Không gian Công cộng (Public Spaces)**

| Collocation                        | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                  | Short English Meaning                                                                                                | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                           |
| :--------------------------------- | :-------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Public spaces**                  | Các không gian công cộng          | Areas such as parks, squares, and streets that are open and accessible to all people.                                | Well-designed **public spaces** are the heart of a vibrant city.                                           |
| **Green spaces**                   | Các không gian xanh               | Areas of grass, trees, or other vegetation set apart for recreational or aesthetic purposes in an urban environment. | Access to **green spaces** can improve the mental and physical health of city residents.                   |
| **A pedestrianized zone / street** | Khu vực / đường phố đi bộ         | An area where vehicles are not allowed, reserved for people on foot.                                                 | The city center has been converted into a large **pedestrianized zone** to reduce pollution.               |
| **Recreational facilities**        | Các cơ sở vật chất giải trí       | Places and equipment provided for the public to do sports and leisure activities.                                    | The local park has excellent **recreational facilities**, including tennis courts and a playground.        |
| **To foster a sense of community** | Nuôi dưỡng tinh thần cộng đồng    | To encourage a feeling of fellowship, connection, and mutual support among people in an area.                        | Public spaces play a vital role in **fostering a sense of community**.                                     |
| **A vibrant public realm**         | Một không gian công cộng sôi động | The lively and energetic public spaces of a town or city, including streets, squares, and parks.                     | The renovated waterfront has become a **vibrant public realm** with cafes, markets, and street performers. |
| **To be accessible to the public** | Mọi người dân đều có thể tiếp cận | To be open and available for everyone to use, regardless of ability or income.                                       | All national museums should be free and fully **accessible to the public**.                                |
| **A venue for social interaction** | Một địa điểm để tương tác xã hội  | A place where people can meet, gather, and interact with each other.                                                 | Community centers provide a safe **venue for social interaction** among residents.                         |
| **Civic spaces**                   | Các không gian công dân           | Public places where people can gather and participate in community and political life.                               | Town squares are traditional **civic spaces** for protests, celebrations, and public debate.               |
| **The public realm**               | Không gian công cộng (nói chung)  | All the spaces between buildings that are accessible to the public, such as streets, squares, and parks.             | Improving the quality of **the public realm** is a key goal of modern urban design.                        |

### 🗺️ **Quy hoạch & Thiết kế (Planning & Design)**

| Collocation                             | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                           | Short English Meaning                                                                                       | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                                        |
| :-------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Urban planning and design**           | Quy hoạch và thiết kế đô thị               | The process of designing and managing the physical growth and development of cities.                        | Good **urban planning and design** should prioritize the well-being of residents.                                       |
| **A well-designed public space**        | Một không gian công cộng được thiết kế tốt | A public area that is planned and designed in an effective, attractive, and user-friendly way.              | A **well-designed public space** can encourage social interaction and improve public health.                            |
| **A mixed-use neighborhood**            | Một khu dân cư đa chức năng                | A neighborhood that combines residential, commercial, cultural, and institutional uses.                     | **Mixed-use neighborhoods** reduce car dependency by allowing people to live, work, and shop in the same area.          |
| **To enhance the urban environment**    | Nâng cao môi trường đô thị                 | To improve the quality of the physical and social environment in a city.                                    | Planting more trees is a simple yet effective way **to enhance the urban environment**.                                 |
| **A livable city**                      | Một thành phố đáng sống                    | A city that provides a good quality of life for its inhabitants.                                            | Access to public transport, green spaces, and affordable housing are key features of a **livable city**.                |
| **To meet the needs of residents**      | Đáp ứng nhu cầu của cư dân                 | To provide the services and facilities that the people living in a place require.                           | Urban planners must **meet the needs of all residents**, including children, the elderly, and people with disabilities. |
| **To prioritize pedestrians over cars** | Ưu tiên người đi bộ hơn xe ô tô            | To design streets and public spaces in a way that gives more importance to people on foot than to vehicles. | Many modern cities are redesigning their centers **to prioritize pedestrians over cars**.                               |
| **Sustainable architecture**            | Kiến trúc bền vững                         | The practice of designing buildings to be environmentally friendly and resource-efficient.                  | **Sustainable architecture** often incorporates features like solar panels and green roofs.                             |
| **Human-centered design**               | Thiết kế lấy con người làm trung tâm       | An approach to design that focuses on the needs, experiences, and perspectives of people.                   | **Human-centered design** is crucial for creating public spaces that are truly inclusive.                               |
| **Urban regeneration**                  | Tái thiết đô thị                           | The process of improving a deprived urban area by rebuilding and investing in it.                           | The **urban regeneration** of the docklands area transformed it into a desirable place to live.                         |
