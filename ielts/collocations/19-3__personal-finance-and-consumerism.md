| **To manage one's finances** | Quản lý tài chính cá nhân | To control your own money. | Schools should teach students how **to manage their finances** effectively. |

### 💳 **Chi tiêu & Nợ nần (Spending & Debt)**

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **To make ends meet** | Kiếm đủ sống (trang trải chi phí) | To have just enough money for your basic needs. | With rising inflation, many families are struggling **to make ends meet**. |
| **To be deep in debt / heavily in debt** | Nợ nần chồng chất | To owe a large amount of money. | After years of overspending, he found himself **deep in debt**. |
| **To get into / fall into debt** | Mắc nợ | To start to owe money. | It is easy **to fall into debt** with the widespread availability of credit cards. |

| **To be burdened with debt** | Bị gánh nặng nợ nần | To have a lot of debt that causes you problems. | Many young graduates **are burdened with** significant student **debt**. |

| **To live beyond one's means** | Sống vượt quá khả năng tài chính | To spend more money than you earn. | **Living beyond one's means** is a primary cause of personal debt. |

### 🛍️ **Chủ nghĩa Tiêu dùng & Chủ nghĩa Vật chất (Consumerism & Materialism)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A consumer society** | Một xã hội tiêu dùng | A society where buying and selling goods is very important. | We live in a **consumer society** that constantly encourages us to buy more. |
| **A throw-away culture** | Văn hóa "dùng một lần rồi vứt" | A culture where people throw things away after using them once. | Our **throw-away culture** is a major contributor to environmental problems. |
| **Material possessions / Worldly goods** | Của cải, tài sản vật chất | The things that you own. | Some people argue that happiness cannot be found in **material possessions**. |
| **To keep up with the Joneses** | Đua đòi, cố gắng bằng bạn bằng bè | To try to have the same new things as your neighbors or friends. | The pressure **to "keep up with the Joneses"** can lead people to spend money they don't have. |
| **Brand-name goods / Designer labels** | Hàng hiệu | Products made by well-known, expensive brands. | Young people are often attracted to expensive **brand-name goods**. |
| **To be materialistic** | Là người coi trọng vật chất | To believe that money and possessions are the most important things. | A person who is **materialistic** judges success by the things they own. |
| **A status symbol** | Một biểu tượng của địa vị | A possession that is a sign of a person's high social status. | In some cultures, a luxury car is a powerful **status symbol**. |
| **Advertising campaigns** | Các chiến dịch quảng cáo | Organized series of advertisements. | Sophisticated **advertising campaigns** can create artificial needs and desires. |

### 🎓 **Kiến thức & Các vấn đề Tài chính (Financial Literacy & Issues)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Financial literacy / education** | Kiến thức / giáo dục tài chính | Knowledge about how to manage money. | A lack of **financial literacy** is a widespread problem. |
| **To make informed financial decisions** | Đưa ra các quyết định tài chính có hiểu biết | To make money decisions based on good information. | The goal of financial education is to empower people **to make informed financial decisions**. |
| **Financial hardship** | Khó khăn tài chính | A situation of having serious money problems. | Losing a job can cause immense **financial hardship** for a family. |
| **To live from paycheck to paycheck** | Sống kiểu "làm đồng nào xào đồng nấy" | To have only enough money to last until your next payday. | Many low-wage workers have **to live from paycheck to paycheck**, with no savings. |
| **A source of income** | Nguồn thu nhập | Where your money comes from. | The rent from the apartment provides a steady **source of income** for her. |
| **Disposable income** | Thu nhập khả dụng (sau khi đã trừ thuế, chi phí thiết yếu) | Money left to spend after paying for essential things. | The amount of **disposable income** a family has affects their spending habits. |
| **To achieve financial security / independence** | Đạt được sự an toàn / độc lập về tài chính | To have enough money to feel safe and not have to work. | The ultimate goal for many is **to achieve financial independence**. |
