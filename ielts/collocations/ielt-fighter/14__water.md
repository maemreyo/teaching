### 💧 **Nguồn nước & Các loại hình (Water Sources & Types)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Freshwater resources** | Nguồn nước ngọt | Natural sources of fresh, non-salty water. | **Freshwater resources** are becoming increasingly scarce in many parts of the world. |
| **Groundwater supplies** | Nguồn cung nước ngầm | Water stored underground. | Over-extraction is depleting **groundwater supplies** at an alarming rate. |
| **Surface water** | Nước bề mặt (sông, hồ) | Water in rivers, lakes on the Earth's surface. | **Surface water** is often contaminated by industrial runoff. |
| **Coastal waters** | Vùng biển ven bờ | The sea water near a coast. | Pollution from cities threatens the health of our **coastal waters**. |
| **Marine ecosystem** | Hệ sinh thái biển | The community of living things in the ocean. | Oil spills can cause irreversible damage to the **marine ecosystem**. |
| **River basin** | Lưu vực sông | The area of land drained by a river. | The entire **river basin** was affected by the prolonged drought. |
| **Potable water / Drinking water** | Nước uống được | Water that is safe to drink. | Access to clean **potable water** is a fundamental human right. |
| **Tap water** | Nước máy | Water supplied through pipes to a building. | In many developed countries, **tap water** is safe to drink. |

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **Oil spill** | Sự cố tràn dầu | An accident where oil is released into the sea. | The **oil spill** had a devastating impact on local wildlife. |
| **Waterborne diseases** | Các bệnh lây truyền qua đường nước | Illnesses spread by contaminated water. | Contaminated wells were the source of an outbreak of **waterborne diseases**. |
| **To deplete resources** | Làm cạn kiệt tài nguyên | To use up the available supply of something. | Unsustainable practices continue **to deplete** our precious water **resources**. |
