### 🏙️ **Cuộc sống Thành thị (Urban Life)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The hustle and bustle of the city** | Sự hối hả và nhộn nhịp của thành phố | The busy and noisy activity of a city. | Some people thrive on **the hustle and bustle of city life**. |
| **A fast-paced lifestyle** | Lối sống nhịp độ nhanh | A life that is very busy and active. | A **fast-paced lifestyle** in a major city can be both exciting and stressful. |
| **The anonymity of city life** | Sự ẩn danh của cuộc sống thành thị | The state of being unknown in a city. | While some find it lonely, others enjoy **the anonymity of city life**. |
| **A vibrant, multicultural environment** | Một môi trường đa văn hóa, sôi động | An exciting environment with many different cultures. | Cities offer a **vibrant, multicultural environment** with diverse food and entertainment. |
| **To be surrounded by modern amenities** | Được bao quanh bởi các tiện nghi hiện đại | To have modern facilities like shops and restaurants all around you. | Urban dwellers are **surrounded by modern amenities** such as shopping malls and cinemas. |
| **Round-the-clock activity / services** | Hoạt động / dịch vụ 24/7 | Activity or services that are available 24 hours a day. | In a megacity, there is **round-the-clock activity**, and many services are available 24/7. |
| **A concrete jungle** | Một "rừng bê tông" (ám chỉ thành phố toàn nhà cửa) | A city with a lot of modern buildings and no nature. | Some people feel that large cities are just a **concrete jungle** with no connection to nature. |
| **A wider range of...** | Nhiều lựa chọn hơn về... | A larger variety of something. | Cities provide a **wider range of** entertainment options and job opportunities. |

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A slower pace of life** | Nhịp sống chậm hơn | A less busy and more relaxed way of living. | Many people move to the countryside in search of a **slower pace of life**. |
| **A tranquil / peaceful environment** | Một môi trường yên tĩnh / thanh bình | A calm and quiet environment. | The countryside offers a **tranquil environment**, free from the noise of the city. |
| **A close-knit community** | Một cộng đồng gắn bó, khăng khít | A community where people are very close and supportive. | In a **close-knit community**, neighbors often know and support each other. |
| **To be in tune with nature** | Gần gũi, hòa mình với thiên nhiên | To be connected to and understand nature. | Living in a rural area allows you **to be more in tune with nature** and the changing seasons. |

### ⚖️ **So sánh & Các khía cạnh chung (Comparisons & General Aspects)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A stark contrast between...** | Một sự tương phản rõ rệt giữa... | A very clear difference between two things. | There is a **stark contrast between** the cost of living in the city and the countryside. |
| **A matter of personal preference** | Một vấn đề về sở thích cá nhân | Something that depends on what you like. | Whether you choose to live in the city or the country is a **matter of personal preference**. |
| **The quality of life** | Chất lượng cuộc sống | The standard of health, comfort, and happiness experienced by someone. | The **quality of life** can be measured by factors such as health, safety, and well-being. |
| **The cost of living** | Chi phí sinh hoạt | The amount of money needed for basic necessities. | **The cost of living** is significantly higher in urban centers. |
| **A sense of belonging** | Cảm giác thân thuộc, thuộc về | A feeling of being part of a group. | Some people find a greater **sense of belonging** in a small rural community. |
| **To get back to nature** | Trở về với thiên nhiên | To return to a more natural way of life. | After years in the city, they decided **to get back to nature** and bought a small farm. |
| **Job / Employment opportunities** | Các cơ hội việc làm | Chances to get a job. | Major cities typically offer far more diverse **job opportunities** than rural areas. |
| **Access to services (e.g., healthcare, education)** | Tiếp cận các dịch vụ (ví dụ: y tế, giáo dục) | The ability to use services like healthcare and education. | **Access to high-quality healthcare and education** is often better in urban areas. |
| **To commute long distances** | Đi lại quãng đường dài | To travel a long way to and from work. | People living in the suburbs often have **to commute long distances** to work in the city. |
| **To enjoy peace and quiet** | Tận hưởng sự yên bình và tĩnh lặng | To enjoy a calm and quiet situation. | If you want **to enjoy peace and quiet**, the countryside is the ideal place to live. |
