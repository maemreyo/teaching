### 🌍 **Khái niệm & Mô tả Xã hội Đa văn hóa (Describing a Multicultural Society)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A multicultural / A pluralistic society** | Một xã hội đa văn hóa / đa nguyên | A society with many different cultures. | London is a prime example of a thriving **multicultural society**. |
| **A melting pot of cultures** | Một "nồi lẩu" văn hóa (nơi các văn hóa hòa quyện) | A place where different cultures mix together. | New York City is often described as a great **melting pot of cultures**. |
| **A rich tapestry of cultures** | Một "tấm thảm" văn hóa phong phú | A complex and varied mix of cultures. | Immigration has contributed to the **rich tapestry of cultures** that defines the nation. |
| **Ethnic / Cultural diversity** | Sự đa dạng về dân tộc / văn hóa | The existence of a variety of ethnic or cultural groups. | **Cultural diversity** is one of the most positive aspects of a multicultural society. |
| **People from all walks of life** | Mọi người từ mọi tầng lớp xã hội | People from all social and economic backgrounds. | A truly multicultural city is home to **people from all walks of life** and all corners of the globe. |
| **A cosmopolitan city** | Một thành phố quốc tế, đa văn hóa | A city with people from many different countries and cultures. | Living in a **cosmopolitan city** exposes you to a wide range of languages and traditions. |
| **Minority groups / communities** | Các nhóm / cộng đồng thiểu số | Groups of people who are smaller in number than the main group. | The government has a responsibility to protect the rights of **minority groups**. |
| **An inclusive society** | Một xã hội hòa nhập (không loại trừ ai) | A society where everyone feels they belong. | The aim is to create a tolerant and **inclusive society** for everyone. |

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **A vibrant cultural life** | Một đời sống văn hóa sôi động | A cultural life that is full of energy and excitement. | Multiculturalism contributes to a **vibrant cultural life** with diverse music, art, and food. |

### ⚠️ **Thách thức & Vấn đề (Challenges & Problems)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A clash of cultures / values** | Một sự xung đột văn hóa / giá trị | A conflict between different cultures or beliefs. | A **clash of cultures** can arise from different beliefs about social norms and behavior. |
| **Social tension / friction** | Căng thẳng / xung đột xã hội | Disagreement or conflict in society. | **Social tension** can occur if different groups feel they are competing for the same resources. |
| **To face racial discrimination / prejudice** | Đối mặt với sự phân biệt chủng tộc / định kiến | To experience unfair treatment because of your race. | Immigrants and minority groups often **face racial discrimination** in the job market. |
| **Language barriers** | Rào cản ngôn ngữ | Difficulties in communication because of different languages. | **Language barriers** can make it difficult for new immigrants to integrate into society. |
| **A lack of social integration** | Sự thiếu hòa nhập xã hội | A situation where some groups are not fully part of society. | A **lack of social integration** can lead to the formation of isolated communities. |
| **To form isolated communities / enclaves** | Hình thành các cộng đồng / khu vực biệt lập | To form separate communities. | Without proper integration policies, immigrant groups may **form their own isolated enclaves**. |
| **A threat to the national identity** | Một mối đe dọa đối với bản sắc dân tộc | A danger to a country's unique character. | Some people believe that multiculturalism is a **threat to their national identity**. |
| **Misunderstanding and mistrust** | Sự hiểu lầm và thiếu tin tưởng | A lack of understanding and trust. | A lack of communication between groups can breed **misunderstanding and mistrust**. |
| **Social unrest** | Bất ổn xã hội | Protests and dissatisfaction in society. | Extreme social tension can sometimes boil over into **social unrest**. |

### 🤝 **Hòa nhập & Gắn kết Xã hội (Integration & Social Cohesion)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **To integrate into a new society** | Hòa nhập vào một xã hội mới | To become part of a new society. | The process of **integrating into a new society** can be long and challenging. |
| **The process of assimilation** | Quá trình đồng hóa | The process of a minority group adopting the culture of a larger one. | Some argue that immigrants should assimilate, while others advocate for multiculturalism. |
| **To live in harmony** | Sống hòa thuận | To live together peacefully. | The goal is to create a society where all ethnic groups can **live in harmony**. |
| **To promote social cohesion** | Thúc đẩy sự gắn kết xã hội | To help society stick together harmoniously. | Community projects and festivals can help **to promote social cohesion**. |
| **To build a cohesive society** | Xây dựng một xã hội gắn kết | To create a society where people are united. | Education plays a key role in **building a cohesive society** based on shared values. |
| **To respect cultural differences** | Tôn trọng sự khác biệt văn hóa | To accept and value cultural differences. | To live peacefully together, it is essential **to respect cultural differences**. |
| **To maintain one's cultural identity** | Duy trì bản sắc văn hóa của mình | To keep your own cultural identity. | Immigrants should be encouraged **to maintain their cultural identity** while also integrating. |
| **A shared sense of belonging** | Cảm thức chung về sự thuộc về | A feeling of being part of a group. | A successful multicultural society fosters a **shared sense of belonging** among all its citizens. |
| **Intercultural communication** | Giao tiếp liên văn hóa | Communication between people from different cultures. | Strong **intercultural communication** skills are vital in a globalized world. |
