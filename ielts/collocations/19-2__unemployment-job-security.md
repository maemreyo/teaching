### 📉 **Tình trạng Thất nghiệp & Nguyên nhân (Unemployment & Its Causes)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **To be unemployed / out of work** | Bị thất nghiệp / không có việc làm | To not have a job. | He has been **out of work** for six months since the factory closed. |
| **The unemployment rate** | Tỷ lệ thất nghiệp | The percentage of people who are unemployed. | The government's main economic goal is to reduce the **unemployment rate**. |
| **Mass unemployment** | Thất nghiệp hàng loạt | A situation where a very large number of people are unemployed. | An economic recession can lead to **mass unemployment**. |
| **Youth unemployment** | Thất nghiệp ở người trẻ | Unemployment among young people. | **Youth unemployment** is a pressing issue in many European countries. |
| **Long-term unemployment** | Thất nghiệp dài hạn | Being unemployed for a long time. | **Long-term unemployment** can have serious psychological effects. |
| **An economic downturn / recession** | Một cuộc suy thoái kinh tế | A period of reduced economic activity. | During an **economic downturn**, companies are often forced to lay off staff. |
| **To be made redundant / laid off** | Bị cho thôi việc, sa thải (do thừa nhân sự) | To lose your job because your employer no longer needs you. | Hundreds of workers **were made redundant** when the company relocated its factory. |
| **A decline in traditional industries** | Sự suy giảm của các ngành công nghiệp truyền thống | A reduction in the importance of old industries. | **A decline in traditional industries** like coal mining has led to high unemployment in some regions. |

### 🛡️ **Sự đảm bảo Việc làm (Job Security)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Job security** | Sự đảm bảo, an toàn trong công việc | The state of having a job that is secure and likely to continue. | People in government jobs often enjoy a high degree of **job security**. |
| **A permanent / stable job** | Một công việc lâu dài / ổn định | A job that is long-term and secure. | Many young people today prefer flexibility over a **stable job**. |
| **A temporary / short-term contract** | Hợp đồng tạm thời / ngắn hạn | A contract for a job that lasts for a limited time. | Working on a series of **short-term contracts** can be stressful and financially precarious. |
| **The gig economy** | Nền kinh tế tự do (gig) | A labor market characterized by short-term contracts or freelance work. | The rise of **the gig economy** has led to a decrease in traditional employment. |
| **Freelance work** | Công việc tự do (freelance) | Work done by a self-employed person for various companies. | **Freelance work** offers independence but lacks the benefits of a permanent job. |
| **A precarious employment situation** | Tình trạng việc làm bấp bênh | A job situation that is not secure or certain. | Many workers in the gig economy face a **precarious employment situation**. |
| **A lack of job security** | Sự thiếu đảm bảo công việc | Not having a secure job. | **A lack of job security** is a major source of anxiety for many freelancers. |
