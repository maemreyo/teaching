### 🛍️ **Khái niệm & Văn hóa Tiêu dùng (Core Concepts & Consumer Culture)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A consumer society / culture** | Một xã hội / văn hóa tiêu dùng | A society where buying and selling goods is very important. | We live in a **consumer culture** that equates happiness with purchasing new products. |
| **Materialism / To be materialistic** | Chủ nghĩa vật chất / Coi trọng vật chất | The belief that money and possessions are the most important things / To be a person who believes this. | **Materialism** is the belief that having money and possessions is the most important thing in life. |
| **Material possessions / Worldly goods** | Của cải, tài sản vật chất | The things that you own. | Some people argue that focusing on **material possessions** can lead to an unfulfilled life. |
| **A throw-away society** | Một xã hội "vứt bỏ" (dùng rồi vứt) | A society where people throw things away after using them once. | The problem of plastic waste is a direct result of our **throw-away society**. |
| **Planned obsolescence** | Lỗi thời có kế hoạch (sản phẩm được thiết kế để nhanh hỏng) | The practice of designing products to have a limited lifespan. | **Planned obsolescence** in electronics encourages consumers to upgrade their devices frequently. |
| **To keep up with the latest trends** | Chạy theo những xu hướng mới nhất | To do or wear what is currently popular. | The fashion industry thrives on people's desire **to keep up with the latest trends**. |
| **To be brand-conscious** | Chú trọng đến thương hiệu | To be very aware of and interested in brands. | **Brand-conscious** consumers are willing to pay more for designer labels. |
| **Brand-name goods / Designer labels** | Hàng hiệu | Products made by well-known, expensive brands. | For some, owning **brand-name goods** is a way to display their social status. |
| **A status symbol** | Một biểu tượng của địa vị | A possession that is a sign of a person's high social status. | A luxury watch or a sports car can be seen as a **status symbol**. |

### 💸 **Nguyên nhân & Động lực (Causes & Drivers)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mass production** | Sản xuất hàng loạt | The production of large quantities of a standardized article. | **Mass production** has made a wide variety of goods affordable for the average person. |
| **Advertising and marketing campaigns** | Các chiến dịch quảng cáo và tiếp thị | Organized series of advertisements and promotions. | Sophisticated **advertising campaigns** are designed to persuade us to buy things we do not need. |
| **To be bombarded with advertisements** | Bị "dội bom" bởi quảng cáo | To be shown a very large number of ads. | Every day, we **are bombarded with advertisements** on TV, social media, and billboards. |
| **To create artificial needs / desires** | Tạo ra các nhu cầu / mong muốn nhân tạo | To make people want things they don't really need. | The role of marketing is often **to create artificial needs** for new products. |
| **Peer pressure** | Áp lực từ bạn bè | The influence from friends to behave in a certain way. | **Peer pressure** can drive young people to demand the latest gadgets and clothes. |
| **To keep up with the Joneses** | Đua đòi, cố gắng bằng bạn bằng bè | To try to have the same new things as your neighbors or friends. | The desire **to "keep up with the Joneses"** fuels a cycle of competitive consumption. |
| **An increase in disposable income** | Sự gia tăng thu nhập khả dụng | An increase in the money you have left after paying for essential things. | An **increase in disposable income** has allowed people to spend more on non-essential items. |

### 💔 **Tác động lên Cá nhân & Xã hội (Individual & Social Impacts)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **To equate success with material wealth** | Đánh đồng thành công với sự giàu có vật chất | To believe that success is the same as having a lot of money and possessions. | Our society often makes the mistake of **equating success with material wealth**. |
| **A constant desire for more** | Ham muốn không ngừng | Always wanting more things. | Consumerism fosters a **constant desire for more**, leading to a cycle of dissatisfaction. |
| **To live beyond one's means** | Sống vượt quá khả năng tài chính | To spend more money than you earn. | Credit cards make it easy for people **to live beyond their means** and fall into debt. |
| **To get into debt** | Mắc nợ | To start to owe money. | Many people **get into debt** trying to maintain a lifestyle they cannot afford. |
| **A superficial sense of happiness** | Một cảm giác hạnh phúc hời hợt | A feeling of happiness that is not deep or real. | Buying new things may provide a **superficial sense of happiness**, but it is often short-lived. |
| **A loss of traditional values** | Sự mất mát các giá trị truyền thống | The disappearance of old, established beliefs. | Critics argue that materialism has led to a **loss of traditional values** like community and family. |
| **To place value on possessions** | Đặt nặng giá trị vào của cải | To think that owning things is important. | A materialistic person tends **to place more value on possessions** than on relationships. |
| **To neglect interpersonal relationships** | Sao nhãng các mối quan hệ cá nhân | To not give enough attention to your relationships with people. | An obsession with acquiring wealth can cause people **to neglect their interpersonal relationships**. |
| **A source of social competition** | Một nguồn gốc của sự cạnh tranh xã hội | Something that causes people to compete with each other in society. | The accumulation of possessions has become a **source of social competition**. |

### 🌍 **Tác động lên Môi trường (Environmental Impacts)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Overconsumption of natural resources** | Tiêu thụ quá mức tài nguyên thiên nhiên | Using too many natural resources. | Our consumer lifestyle leads to the **overconsumption of the world's natural resources**. |
| **To generate a huge amount of waste** | Tạo ra một lượng rác thải khổng lồ | To produce a very large amount of rubbish. | A throw-away society **generates a huge amount of** household **waste**. |
| **The depletion of finite resources** | Sự cạn kiệt các tài nguyên hữu hạn | The reduction of resources that are limited. | Mass production contributes to the **depletion of finite resources** like oil and minerals. |
| **To contribute to landfill problems** | Góp phần vào các vấn đề bãi rác | To add to the problems of rubbish disposal sites. | Discarded electronics and single-use plastics **contribute significantly to our landfill problems**. |
| **To have a large carbon footprint** | Có dấu chân carbon lớn | To produce a large amount of carbon dioxide. | A lifestyle based on high consumption inevitably **has a large carbon footprint**. |
| **Unsustainable consumption patterns** | Các mô hình tiêu dùng không bền vững | Ways of using resources that cannot be continued. | We must change our **unsustainable consumption patterns** to protect the planet. |
| **Environmental degradation** | Sự suy thoái môi trường | The process of the environment becoming worse. | Consumerism is a major driver of **environmental degradation**. |
