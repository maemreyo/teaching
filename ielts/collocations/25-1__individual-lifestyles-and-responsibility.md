### 👍 **Thói quen & Lối sống Lành mạnh (Healthy Habits & Lifestyle)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **To lead a healthy and active lifestyle** | Sống một lối sống lành mạnh và năng động | To live in a way that is beneficial for one's physical and mental health. | **Leading a healthy and active lifestyle** involves a balanced diet and regular physical activity. |
| **A well-balanced / nutritious diet** | Một chế độ ăn uống cân bằng / bổ dưỡng | A diet that contains a healthy variety of foods in the right proportions. | A **well-balanced diet** is essential for providing the body with the necessary nutrients for growth and repair. |
| **To eat in moderation** | Ăn uống điều độ | To consume food and drink in reasonable quantities, avoiding excess. | It is acceptable to eat junk food occasionally, as long as you **eat it in moderation**. |
| **To adhere to a fitness regimen** | Tuân thủ một chế độ tập luyện | To consistently follow a planned program of exercise. | Individuals who **adhere to a fitness regimen** are less likely to suffer from heart disease. |
| **To engage in regular physical activity** | Tham gia vào hoạt động thể chất đều đặn | To consistently participate in activities that involve bodily movement. | People of all ages should be encouraged **to engage in regular physical activity**. |
| **To maintain a healthy weight** | Duy trì cân nặng hợp lý | To keep one's body weight within a range that is considered healthy for their height. | **Maintaining a healthy weight** can reduce the risk of many chronic diseases. |
| **To get an adequate amount of sleep** | Ngủ đủ giấc | To sleep for the recommended duration for one's age and health needs. | **Getting an adequate amount of sleep** is as important for your health as diet and exercise. |
| **To boost one's immune system** | Tăng cường hệ miễn dịch | To strengthen the body's natural defense system against illness. | A diet rich in vitamins and minerals can help **to boost one's immune system**. |
| **Mental well-being** | Sức khỏe tinh thần | A state of well-being in which an individual realizes their own abilities and can cope with the normal stresses of life. | A healthy lifestyle is crucial not only for physical health but also for **mental well-being**. |
| **Preventative healthcare** | Chăm sóc sức khỏe phòng ngừa | Measures taken to prevent diseases rather than curing them. | A healthy lifestyle is the most effective form of **preventative healthcare**. |

### 👎 **Thói quen & Lối sống Không lành mạnh (Unhealthy Habits & Lifestyle)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A sedentary lifestyle** | Lối sống thụ động, ít vận động | A lifestyle characterized by a lot of sitting and very little physical activity. | A **sedentary lifestyle**, often associated with office work, can lead to numerous health problems. |
| **Poor dietary habits** | Thói quen ăn uống kém lành mạnh | Unhealthy eating patterns, such as high consumption of processed foods, sugar, and fat. | **Poor dietary habits** are a primary contributor to the global obesity epidemic. |
| **A diet high in processed foods** | Chế độ ăn nhiều thực phẩm chế biến sẵn | An eating pattern that relies heavily on foods that have been chemically altered. | A **diet high in processed foods** is linked to an increased risk of chronic disease. |
| **To consume excessive amounts of...** | Tiêu thụ lượng quá mức... | To eat or drink far too much of something. | **Consuming excessive amounts of** sugary drinks can be detrimental to one's health. |
| **Chronic sleep deprivation** | Tình trạng thiếu ngủ kinh niên | A long-term condition of not getting enough sleep. | **Chronic sleep deprivation** can impair cognitive function and weaken the immune system. |
| **Physical inactivity** | Sự thiếu hoạt động thể chất | A lack of sufficient moderate-to-vigorous physical activity. | **Physical inactivity** is a significant risk factor for many lifestyle-related diseases. |
| **To engage in risky health behaviors** | Tham gia vào các hành vi sức khỏe rủi ro | To perform actions that can have a negative impact on one's health, such as smoking or excessive drinking. | Smoking and binge drinking are considered **risky health behaviors**. |
| **To be out of shape / in poor physical condition** | Mất dáng / có thể trạng kém | To not be physically fit or healthy. | After years of a sedentary lifestyle, he realized he was seriously **out of shape**. |
| **A contributing factor to...** | Một yếu tố góp phần vào... | Something that helps to cause a particular situation or result. | A poor diet is a major **contributing factor to** obesity. |
| **To lead a stressful life** | Sống một cuộc sống căng thẳng | To have a life with a high level of mental or emotional strain. | Many people in high-pressure jobs **lead a stressful life**, which can affect their health. |

### 🧑‍⚖️ **Trách nhiệm Cá nhân & Xã hội (Personal & Societal Responsibility)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **To take personal responsibility for one's health** | Tự chịu trách nhiệm cá nhân cho sức khỏe của mình | To accept that you are accountable for your own health choices and outcomes. | Proponents of this view argue that individuals must **take personal responsibility for their health**. |
| **To make informed lifestyle choices** | Đưa ra những lựa chọn lối sống có hiểu biết | To make decisions about how to live based on good information and knowledge. | The goal of health education is to empower people **to make informed lifestyle choices**. |
| **A matter of individual choice** | Một vấn đề về lựa chọn cá nhân | Something that is ultimately up to an individual to decide for themselves. | While there are societal influences, what a person eats is largely a **matter of individual choice**. |
| **To exercise self-discipline / self-control** | Thực hành kỷ luật / kiểm soát bản thân | To have the ability to control one's own behavior and desires. | **Exercising self-discipline** is necessary to stick to a diet or fitness regimen. |
| **To adopt healthy habits / routines** | Thực hiện các thói quen / lề thói lành mạnh | To start and maintain a regular pattern of healthy behaviors. | It can be challenging **to adopt healthy routines**, but the long-term benefits are significant. |
| **To be proactive about one's health** | Chủ động về sức khỏe của mình | To take action to manage and improve your health before problems arise. | **Being proactive about your health** involves regular check-ups and preventative measures. |
| **The role of government in public health** | Vai trò của chính phủ trong y tế công cộng | The responsibility of the government to protect and improve the health of its population. | There is a debate about **the role of government in public health**, such as whether to tax sugary drinks. |
| **Public health campaigns** | Các chiến dịch y tế công cộng | Organized efforts to communicate health messages to the population. | **Public health campaigns** can be effective in raising awareness about the dangers of smoking. |
| **To create a supportive environment** | Tạo ra một môi trường hỗ trợ | To establish a social and physical context that makes it easier to make healthy choices. | Urban planning can **create a supportive environment** for physical activity by building parks and bike lanes. |
| **Individual accountability vs. societal factors** | Trách nhiệm cá nhân và các yếu tố xã hội | The debate over whether health is determined by personal choices or broader social conditions. | The issue of obesity involves a complex interplay of **individual accountability and societal factors**. |

### 📈 **Hậu quả & Kết quả (Consequences & Outcomes)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **To be susceptible / prone to illness** | Dễ bị mắc bệnh | To be more likely to get sick. | People with weak immune systems are more **susceptible to illness**. |
| **Lifestyle-related diseases / Chronic diseases** | Các bệnh liên quan đến lối sống / Bệnh mãn tính | Diseases that are largely caused by unhealthy lifestyles and are long-lasting. | Obesity, type 2 diabetes, and heart disease are common **lifestyle-related diseases**. |
| **To have a profound impact on...** | Có tác động sâu sắc lên... | To have a very strong and significant effect on something. | A sedentary lifestyle can **have a profound impact on** a person's physical and mental health. |
| **To reduce / increase one's life expectancy** | Giảm / tăng tuổi thọ trung bình của một người | To make the average lifespan of an individual shorter or longer. | A healthy lifestyle is proven **to increase one's life expectancy**. |
| **To be at a higher risk of developing...** | Có nguy cơ cao mắc... | To have a greater probability of getting a particular disease. | People who are overweight are **at a higher risk of developing** heart problems. |
| **To improve one's overall well-being** | Cải thiện sức khỏe và hạnh phúc tổng thể | To make one's general physical, mental, and social condition better. | Regular exercise can **improve one's overall well-being**. |
| **A direct correlation between...** | Một mối tương quan trực tiếp giữa... | A clear and direct connection where one thing affects another. | There is a **direct correlation between** a lack of physical activity and an increased risk of obesity. |
| **The long-term health consequences** | Các hậu quả sức khỏe lâu dài | The effects on one's health that appear over a long period of time. | The **long-term health consequences** of an unhealthy lifestyle may not be immediately apparent. |
| **To enjoy a long and healthy life / longevity** | Tận hưởng một cuộc sống lâu dài và khỏe mạnh / sự trường thọ | To live for many years in good health. | The ultimate goal of a healthy lifestyle is **to enjoy a long and healthy life**. |
| **To place a burden on the healthcare system** | Đặt gánh nặng lên hệ thống y tế | To create a significant strain on a country's healthcare services and resources. | The rising prevalence of chronic diseases **places a huge burden on the healthcare system**. |
