### ⚕️ **Y học Hiện đại (Conventional Medicine)**

| Collocation                                         | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                                     | Short English Meaning                                                                              | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                      |
| :-------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Conventional / Orthodox / Mainstream medicine**   | Y học hiện đại / chính thống                         | The standard system of medicine in Western societies, based on scientific evidence and principles. | **Conventional medicine** relies on scientifically tested treatments and pharmaceutical drugs.        |
| **Evidence-based practice**                         | Thực hành dựa trên bằng chứng                        | The use of current best evidence from research in making decisions about patient care.             | Modern doctors are trained in **evidence-based practice**.                                            |
| **Scientifically proven treatments**                | Các phương pháp điều trị đã được khoa học chứng minh | Treatments whose effectiveness has been demonstrated through rigorous scientific testing.          | Patients should be wary of any claims not supported by **scientifically proven treatments**.          |
| **Pharmaceutical drugs / medication**               | Dược phẩm / thuốc                                    | Chemical substances used to treat, cure, prevent, or diagnose a disease.                           | A doctor will prescribe **pharmaceutical drugs** to treat the bacterial infection.                    |
| **To undergo surgery / a surgical procedure**       | Trải qua phẫu thuật / một thủ thuật phẫu thuật       | To have a medical operation in which a surgeon cuts into the body.                                 | In severe cases, the patient may need **to undergo a surgical procedure**.                            |
| **A qualified medical professional / practitioner** | Một chuyên gia / người hành nghề y tế có trình độ    | A person with the necessary formal qualifications and training to practice medicine.               | You should always seek advice from a **qualified medical professional**.                              |
| **To treat the symptoms of a disease**              | Điều trị các triệu chứng của một căn bệnh            | To address the observable signs of an illness, which may not be its underlying cause.              | This medicine will help **to treat the symptoms** of the cold, but it will not cure the virus itself. |
| **To make a clinical diagnosis**                    | Đưa ra chẩn đoán lâm sàng                            | To identify an illness based on a physical examination and medical tests.                          | A series of tests were required before the doctor could **make a clinical diagnosis**.                |
| **To focus on a specific ailment**                  | Tập trung vào một bệnh cụ thể                        | To concentrate on treating a particular illness or part of the body.                               | Conventional medicine often **focuses on a specific ailment** rather than the whole person.           |
| **To have potential side effects**                  | Có các tác dụng phụ tiềm tàng                        | To have the possibility of causing unwanted secondary effects.                                     | All pharmaceutical drugs **have potential side effects**.                                             |

### 🌿 **Y học Thay thế (Alternative Medicine)**

| Collocation                                                 | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                                                | Short English Meaning                                                                                       | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                       |
| :---------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Alternative / Complementary medicine**                    | Y học thay thế / bổ sung                                        | A broad range of healing philosophies and therapies that are not part of conventional medicine.             | **Alternative medicine** includes therapies such as acupuncture, homeopathy, and herbal remedies.      |
| **Natural remedies / Herbal medicine**                      | Các phương thuốc tự nhiên / y học thảo dược                     | The use of plants and plant extracts for medicinal purposes.                                                | Many people prefer **natural remedies** because they believe they have fewer side effects.             |
| **A holistic approach to health**                           | Một cách tiếp cận toàn diện đối với sức khỏe                    | An approach that treats the whole person—mind, body, and spirit—rather than just the symptoms of a disease. | **A holistic approach to health** is a central principle of many alternative therapies.                |
| **To treat the root cause of an illness**                   | Điều trị nguyên nhân gốc rễ của một căn bệnh                    | To address the fundamental reason for a health problem, not just its outward signs.                         | Practitioners of alternative medicine often claim **to treat the root cause of an illness**.           |
| **To restore the body's natural balance / healing ability** | Phục hồi sự cân bằng tự nhiên / khả năng chữa lành của cơ thể   | To bring the body back to a state of equilibrium and support its innate capacity to heal itself.            | The aim of the therapy is **to restore the body's natural healing ability**.                           |
| **Acupuncture / Homeopathy / Chiropractic**                 | Châm cứu / Vi lượng đồng căn / Trị liệu thần kinh cột sống      | Specific forms of alternative therapy.                                                                      | **Acupuncture** is a traditional Chinese therapy that is used to relieve pain.                         |
| **A non-invasive treatment**                                | Một phương pháp điều trị không xâm lấn                          | A treatment that does not involve making an incision into the body or removing tissue.                      | Massage therapy is a popular **non-invasive treatment** for muscle pain.                               |
| **To have minimal side effects**                            | Có tác dụng phụ tối thiểu                                       | To have very few unwanted secondary effects.                                                                | Proponents claim that herbal medicines **have minimal side effects** compared to pharmaceutical drugs. |
| **Time-honored / ancient healing practices**                | Các phương pháp chữa bệnh cổ xưa / đã được thời gian kiểm chứng | Traditional healing methods that have been used for a very long time.                                       | Many alternative therapies are based on **time-honored healing practices**.                            |
| **To promote overall well-being**                           | Thúc đẩy sức khỏe và hạnh phúc toàn diện                        | To contribute to a person's general health and happiness.                                                   | A holistic approach aims **to promote overall well-being**.                                            |

### ⚖️ **Tranh luận & Lựa chọn (The Debate & Patient Choice)**

| Collocation                                            | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                                        | Short English Meaning                                                                          | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                         |
| :----------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------ | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A growing trend towards...**                         | Một xu hướng ngày càng tăng đối với...                  | An increasing tendency for people to choose or do something.                                   | There is a **growing trend towards** using complementary therapies alongside conventional medicine.      |
| **To seek alternative treatments**                     | Tìm kiếm các phương pháp điều trị thay thế              | To look for therapies that are not part of mainstream medicine.                                | Some patients **seek alternative treatments** when conventional medicine fails to provide a cure.        |
| **A matter of patient choice and autonomy**            | Một vấn đề về sự lựa chọn và quyền tự chủ của bệnh nhân | The principle that a patient has the right to make their own decisions about their healthcare. | The decision to use alternative therapies is a **matter of patient choice and autonomy**.                |
| **To be deeply skeptical of...**                       | Hoài nghi sâu sắc về...                                 | To have strong doubts about the truth or effectiveness of something.                           | The scientific community is often **deeply skeptical of** the claims made by alternative practitioners.  |
| **To be used as a complementary therapy**              | Được sử dụng như một liệu pháp bổ sung                  | To be used alongside and in support of conventional medical treatments.                        | Massage can be **used as a complementary therapy** to help cancer patients manage pain.                  |
| **An integrative / integrated approach to healthcare** | Một cách tiếp cận tích hợp đối với chăm sóc sức khỏe    | An approach that combines conventional and alternative medicine in a coordinated way.          | An **integrative approach to healthcare** aims to combine the best of both worlds.                       |
| **To exhaust all conventional options**                | Thử hết tất cả các lựa chọn y học hiện đại              | To have tried all available standard medical treatments without success.                       | Patients often turn to alternative medicine only after they have **exhausted all conventional options**. |
| **The divide between...**                              | Sự chia rẽ giữa...                                      | A significant disagreement or separation between two groups or ideas.                          | There remains a significant **divide between** mainstream and alternative medicine.                      |
| **To make an informed decision**                       | Đưa ra một quyết định có hiểu biết                      | To make a choice based on a clear understanding of all the relevant facts.                     | Patients should be given enough information **to make an informed decision** about their treatment.      |
| **A last resort**                                      | Giải pháp cuối cùng                                     | The last option when all other options have failed.                                            | For some patients with chronic illness, alternative medicine is seen as a **last resort**.               |

### 🔬 **Hiệu quả & Bằng chứng (Effectiveness & Evidence)**

| Collocation                                      | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                                | Short English Meaning                                                                                                          | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                        |
| :----------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **A lack of robust scientific evidence**         | Sự thiếu bằng chứng khoa học vững chắc          | Not enough strong, reliable scientific proof.                                                                                  | A major criticism of many alternative therapies is the **lack of robust scientific evidence**.          |
| **The placebo effect**                           | Hiệu ứng giả dược                               | A phenomenon where a patient experiences real improvements from a fake treatment due to their belief in it.                    | Some studies suggest that the benefits of certain remedies can be attributed to **the placebo effect**. |
| **Anecdotal evidence versus empirical evidence** | Bằng chứng giai thoại và bằng chứng thực nghiệm | Evidence based on personal stories versus evidence based on scientific observation and experimentation.                        | The claims are mostly supported by **anecdotal evidence** rather than **empirical evidence**.           |
| **Rigorous, double-blind clinical trials**       | Các thử nghiệm lâm sàng mù đôi, nghiêm ngặt     | The gold standard of medical research where neither the patients nor the researchers know who is receiving the real treatment. | All new drugs must undergo **rigorous, double-blind clinical trials**.                                  |
| **To alleviate / relieve symptoms**              | Làm dịu / giảm bớt các triệu chứng              | To reduce the severity of the signs of an illness.                                                                             | The main purpose of the treatment is **to alleviate the patient's symptoms**.                           |
| **To have a therapeutic effect**                 | Có tác dụng trị liệu                            | To have a healing, calming, or beneficial effect on the body or mind.                                                          | Many people find that listening to music has a **therapeutic effect**.                                  |
| **A lack of regulation and oversight**           | Sự thiếu quy định và giám sát                   | Not enough official rules, control, or supervision.                                                                            | **A lack of regulation and oversight** in the alternative medicine industry is a cause for concern.     |
| **To question the efficacy and safety of...**    | Đặt câu hỏi về tính hiệu quả và an toàn của...  | To express doubts about how effective and safe something is.                                                                   | It is reasonable for patients **to question the efficacy and safety of** any unregulated treatment.     |
| **Unsubstantiated claims**                       | Những tuyên bố vô căn cứ                        | Assertions made without any evidence to support them.                                                                          | The website made several **unsubstantiated claims** about the product's ability to cure cancer.         |
| **Peer-reviewed scientific literature**          | Tài liệu khoa học có bình duyệt                 | Academic articles that have been evaluated by other experts in the same field.                                                 | The effectiveness of a treatment should be demonstrated in the **peer-reviewed scientific literature**. |
