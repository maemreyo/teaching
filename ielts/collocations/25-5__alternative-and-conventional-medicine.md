### ⚕️ **Y học Hiện đại (Conventional Medicine)**

Còn được gọi là y học chính thống, y học Tây phương, dựa trên bằng chứng khoa học.

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Conventional / Mainstream medicine** | Y học hiện đại / chính thống | The standard system of medicine based on scientific evidence. | **Conventional medicine** relies on scientifically tested treatments and pharmaceutical drugs. |
| **Evidence-based medicine** | Y học dựa trên bằng chứng | Medical care that is based on scientific proof. | Modern doctors are trained to practice **evidence-based medicine**. |
| **Scientifically proven treatments** | Các phương pháp điều trị đã được khoa học chứng minh | Treatments that have been shown to be effective by science. | Patients should be wary of any claims that are not supported by **scientifically proven treatments**. |
| **To prescribe medication / drugs** | Kê đơn thuốc | To officially tell someone to take a specific medicine. | A doctor will **prescribe medication** to treat the bacterial infection. |
| **To undergo surgery** | Trải qua phẫu thuật | To have a medical operation. | In severe cases, the patient may need **to undergo surgery**. |
| **A qualified medical professional** | Một chuyên gia y tế có trình độ | A person with the necessary qualifications to practice medicine. | You should always seek advice from a **qualified medical professional**. |
| **To treat the symptoms** | Điều trị các triệu chứng | To deal with the signs of an illness, but not the cause. | This medicine will help **to treat the symptoms** of the cold, but it will not cure it. |
| **To make a diagnosis** | Đưa ra chẩn đoán | To identify an illness. | A series of tests were required before the doctor could **make an accurate diagnosis**. |

### 🌿 **Y học Thay thế (Alternative Medicine)**

Bao gồm các phương pháp trị liệu truyền thống, bổ sung không thuộc y học chính thống.

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Alternative / Complementary medicine** | Y học thay thế / bổ sung | Medical treatments that are not part of standard Western medicine. | **Alternative medicine** includes therapies such as acupuncture, homeopathy, and herbal remedies. |
| **Natural remedies / Herbal medicine** | Các phương thuốc tự nhiên / y học thảo dược | Treatments using natural plants and herbs. | Many people prefer **natural remedies** because they believe they have fewer side effects. |
| **A holistic approach** | Một cách tiếp cận toàn diện | An approach that considers the whole person, not just the illness. | **A holistic approach** to health considers the mind, body, and spirit. |
| **To treat the root cause** | Điều trị nguyên nhân gốc rễ | To deal with the fundamental cause of a problem. | Practitioners of alternative medicine often claim **to treat the root cause** of an illness, not just the symptoms. |
| **To restore the body's natural balance** | Phục hồi sự cân bằng tự nhiên của cơ thể | To bring the body back to a state of natural health. | The aim of the therapy is **to restore the body's natural balance** and healing abilities. |
| **Acupuncture / Homeopathy** | Châm cứu / Vi lượng đồng căn | A traditional Chinese therapy using needles / a system of alternative medicine. | **Acupuncture** is a traditional Chinese therapy that is used to relieve pain. |
| **A non-invasive treatment** | Một phương pháp điều trị không xâm lấn | A treatment that does not involve cutting the body. | Massage therapy is a popular **non-invasive treatment** for muscle pain. |
| **To have few side effects** | Có ít tác dụng phụ | To have few unwanted effects. | Proponents claim that herbal medicines **have fewer side effects** than pharmaceutical drugs. |

### ⚖️ **Tranh luận & Lựa chọn (The Debate & Patient Choice)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A growing trend towards...** | Một xu hướng ngày càng tăng đối với... | An increasing tendency to do something. | There is a **growing trend towards** using complementary therapies alongside conventional medicine. |
| **To seek alternative treatments** | Tìm kiếm các phương pháp điều trị thay thế | To look for treatments that are not part of conventional medicine. | Some patients **seek alternative treatments** when conventional medicine fails to help them. |
| **A matter of personal choice** | Một vấn đề về lựa chọn cá nhân | Something that is up to you to decide. | Ultimately, the decision to use alternative therapies is a **matter of personal choice**. |
| **To be skeptical of...** | Hoài nghi về... | To have doubts about something. | Many scientists **are skeptical of** the claims made by homeopathic practitioners. |
| **To complement conventional treatments** | Bổ sung cho các phương pháp điều trị hiện đại | To be used alongside standard medical treatments. | Some alternative therapies can be used **to complement conventional treatments**, for example, to reduce stress. |
| **An integrated approach to healthcare** | Một cách tiếp cận tích hợp đối với chăm sóc sức khỏe | An approach that combines conventional and alternative medicine. | An **integrated approach to healthcare** combines the best of both conventional and alternative medicine. |
| **To exhaust all conventional options** | Thử hết tất cả các lựa chọn y học hiện đại | To try all the standard medical treatments without success. | Patients often turn to alternative medicine only after they have **exhausted all conventional options**. |

### 🔬 **Hiệu quả & Bằng chứng (Effectiveness & Evidence)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A lack of scientific evidence** | Sự thiếu bằng chứng khoa học | Not enough scientific proof. | A major criticism of many alternative therapies is the **lack of scientific evidence** to support their effectiveness. |
| **The placebo effect** | Hiệu ứng giả dược | A beneficial effect produced by a placebo drug or treatment, that cannot be attributed to the properties of the placebo itself, and must therefore be due to the patient's belief in that treatment. | Some studies suggest that the benefits of certain remedies can be attributed to **the placebo effect**. |
| **Anecdotal evidence** | Bằng chứng giai thoại (dựa trên kể lại, không có hệ thống) | Evidence based on personal stories, not scientific data. | The claims are mostly supported by **anecdotal evidence** rather than rigorous scientific trials. |
| **Rigorous clinical trials** | Các thử nghiệm lâm sàng nghiêm ngặt | Very careful and thorough tests of a new drug or treatment. | All new drugs must undergo **rigorous clinical trials** to prove their safety and efficacy. |
| **To relieve pain / symptoms** | Làm giảm đau / các triệu chứng | To reduce pain or the signs of an illness. | The main purpose of the treatment is **to relieve the patient's symptoms**. |
| **To have a therapeutic effect** | Có tác dụng trị liệu | To have a healing or calming effect. | Many people find that listening to music has a **therapeutic effect**. |
| **A lack of regulation** | Sự thiếu quy định quản lý | Not enough rules or control. | **A lack of regulation** in the alternative medicine industry is a cause for concern. |
| **To question the effectiveness of...** | Đặt câu hỏi về tính hiệu quả của... | To express doubts about how well something works. | It is reasonable for patients **to question the effectiveness of** any treatment they receive. |
