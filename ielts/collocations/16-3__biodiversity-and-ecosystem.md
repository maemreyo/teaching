### 🌿 **Các Khái niệm Cốt lõi (Core Concepts)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Biological diversity (Biodiversity)** | Đa dạng sinh học | The variety of life in the world or in a particular habitat. | The Amazon rainforest is known for its immense **biological diversity**. |
| **A delicate / fragile ecosystem** | Một hệ sinh thái mỏng manh / dễ bị tổn thương | A natural community that is easily damaged or disturbed. | Coral reefs are a **delicate ecosystem** that is highly sensitive to changes in water temperature. |
| **The food chain** | Chuỗi thức ăn | The series of living things in which each one feeds on the one below it. | The extinction of one species can have a ripple effect throughout **the food chain**. |
| **Natural habitat** | Môi trường sống tự nhiên | The natural home or environment of an animal, plant, or other organism. | The primary threat to wildlife is the destruction of their **natural habitat**. |
| **Ecological balance** | Cân bằng sinh thái | The stable state of an ecosystem where all parts are in harmony. | Human activities have upset the **ecological balance** in many parts of the world. |
| **A keystone species** | Loài chủ chốt (loài có vai trò quan trọng trong hệ sinh thái) | A species on which other species in an ecosystem largely depend. | Sea otters are a **keystone species** because they control the sea urchin population. |
| **Marine ecosystem** | Hệ sinh thái biển | The community of living things in the ocean. | Plastic pollution is causing significant harm to the **marine ecosystem**. |

### 멸종 **Mối đe dọa & Sự suy giảm (Threats & Decline)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Loss of biodiversity** | Mất đa dạng sinh học | The reduction in the variety of life on Earth. | **Loss of biodiversity** is considered one of the most critical environmental issues today. |
| **To be threatened with extinction** | Bị đe dọa tuyệt chủng | To be at risk of disappearing completely. | Many large mammals, such as tigers and rhinos, are **threatened with extinction**. |
| **To be on the brink / verge of extinction** | Trên bờ vực tuyệt chủng | To be very close to extinction. | The Sumatran orangutan is **on the brink of extinction** due to deforestation. |
| **Endangered / Threatened species** | Các loài có nguy cơ tuyệt chủng / bị đe dọa | Animals or plants that are at risk of extinction. | Laws have been passed to protect **endangered species** from illegal hunting. |
| **Invasive species** | Loài xâm lấn | A non-native species that harms the environment. | **Invasive species** can outcompete native animals for resources and disrupt the ecosystem. |
| **Habitat destruction / loss** | Sự phá hủy / mất môi trường sống | The process by which a natural habitat is rendered unable to support its native species. | **Habitat destruction** is the leading cause of species extinction. |
| **The illegal wildlife trade** | Nạn buôn bán động vật hoang dã trái phép | The unlawful buying and selling of wild animals. | **The illegal wildlife trade** is a multi-billion dollar industry that threatens countless species. |
| **A sharp decline in population** | Sự sụt giảm mạnh về số lượng quần thể | A rapid decrease in the number of individuals in a population. | Scientists have observed a **sharp decline in the population** of many insect species. |

### ❤️ **Bảo tồn & Bảo vệ (Conservation & Protection)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Wildlife conservation** | Bảo tồn động vật hoang dã | The protection of wild animals and their habitats. | **Wildlife conservation** requires international cooperation and local community involvement. |
| **Conservation efforts / programs** | Các nỗ lực / chương trình bảo tồn | Organized actions to protect the environment. | **Conservation efforts** are underway to restore the local wolf population. |
| **To preserve natural habitats** | Gìn giữ môi trường sống tự nhiên | To protect the natural homes of plants and animals. | One of the most effective conservation strategies is **to preserve natural habitats**. |
| **To restore a habitat** | Phục hồi một môi trường sống | To repair a damaged natural environment. | The project aims **to restore the wetland habitat** that was drained for farming. |
| **A nature reserve / wildlife sanctuary** | Khu bảo tồn thiên nhiên / động vật hoang dã | A protected area for wildlife. | The area was designated as a **wildlife sanctuary** to protect rare birds. |
| **A captive breeding program** | Một chương trình nhân giống trong điều kiện nuôi nhốt | A program to breed endangered animals in zoos or other facilities. | Pandas have been saved from extinction thanks to successful **captive breeding programs**. |
| **To reintroduce a species** | Tái thả một loài (về tự nhiên) | To put a species back into its native habitat. | There are plans **to reintroduce** the beaver **to** its native habitat in Scotland. |
| **Sustainable tourism / Ecotourism** | Du lịch bền vững / Du lịch sinh thái | Tourism that is environmentally friendly and benefits local communities. | **Ecotourism** can provide funds for conservation while educating the public. |

### 🌐 **Động lực & Chức năng Hệ sinh thái (Ecosystem Dynamics & Functions)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **To play a crucial role in** | Đóng một vai trò thiết yếu trong | To have a very important part in something. | Bees **play a crucial role in** the pollination of many crops. |
| **To adapt to an environment** | Thích nghi với một môi trường | To change in order to survive in a new environment. | Animals that cannot **adapt to a changing environment** are at risk of extinction. |
| **The survival of the fittest** | Sự sống còn của kẻ mạnh nhất (thích nghi tốt nhất) | The principle that only the strongest and most adaptable will survive. | Natural selection is often described as **the survival of the fittest**. |
| **The natural cycle** | Vòng tuần hoàn tự nhiên | The series of natural processes, such as the water cycle. | The decomposition of organic matter is a vital part of **the natural cycle**. |
| **To maintain the balance of nature** | Duy trì sự cân bằng của tự nhiên | To keep the natural world in a stable state. | Predators help **to maintain the balance of nature** by controlling prey populations. |
| **A thriving ecosystem** | Một hệ sinh thái phát triển mạnh | A healthy and successful ecosystem. | The restored river is now home to a **thriving ecosystem** of fish and birds. |
