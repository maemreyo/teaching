### 💨 **Các loại Ô nhiễm & Nguồn gốc (Types & Sources of Pollution)**

| Collocation                                  | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                              | Short English Meaning                                                                           | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                   |
| :------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Air, water, and soil pollution**           | Ô nhiễm không khí, nước và đất                | The contamination of air, water, or soil by harmful substances.                                 | **Air pollution** from traffic fumes is a major health concern in urban areas.                     |
| **Plastic pollution**                        | Ô nhiễm nhựa                                  | The accumulation of plastic objects and particles in the Earth's environment.                   | **Plastic pollution** poses a serious and persistent threat to marine ecosystems.                  |
| **Noise and light pollution**                | Ô nhiễm tiếng ồn và ánh sáng                  | Excessive, harmful levels of noise or artificial light in the environment.                      | **Light pollution** in cities can disrupt the natural cycles of wildlife and human sleep patterns. |
| **Industrial waste / effluent**              | Chất thải / nước thải công nghiệp             | Waste products and contaminated liquid produced by industrial processes.                        | The river was contaminated by untreated **industrial effluent** from the factory.                  |
| **Vehicle / exhaust emissions**              | Khí thải từ phương tiện giao thông            | Harmful gases and particulate matter released from the exhaust pipes of vehicles.               | **Vehicle emissions** are a primary source of urban air pollution.                                 |
| **The combustion of fossil fuels**           | Việc đốt nhiên liệu hóa thạch                 | The process of burning coal, oil, or gas, which releases CO2 and other pollutants.              | **The combustion of fossil fuels** for electricity is a major contributor to acid rain.            |
| **Agricultural runoff**                      | Dòng chảy nông nghiệp                         | The flow of water from farm fields, often containing fertilizers, pesticides, and animal waste. | **Agricultural runoff** is a leading cause of water pollution in many rural areas.                 |
| **Improper waste disposal**                  | Việc xử lý rác thải không đúng cách           | The act of getting rid of waste in an unsafe or environmentally harmful manner.                 | **Improper waste disposal** can lead to the contamination of soil and groundwater.                 |
| **Toxic / hazardous waste**                  | Chất thải độc hại / nguy hại                  | Waste material that can cause serious harm to human health or the environment.                  | Strict regulations are needed for the safe disposal of **hazardous waste**.                        |
| **The proliferation of single-use plastics** | Sự gia tăng nhanh chóng của nhựa dùng một lần | The rapid increase in the production and use of disposable plastic items.                       | **The proliferation of single-use plastics** has created a global environmental crisis.            |

### 📉 **Ảnh hưởng & Hậu quả (Effects & Consequences)**

| Collocation                                       | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                                     | Short English Meaning                                                                  | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                                |
| :------------------------------------------------ | :--------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **To contaminate groundwater and water supplies** | Làm ô nhiễm nước ngầm và nguồn cung cấp nước         | To make underground and public water sources impure and unsafe for consumption.        | Leaking chemicals from a landfill can **contaminate groundwater and water supplies**.                           |
| **To degrade ecosystems**                         | Làm suy thoái hệ sinh thái                           | To damage the quality and health of natural environments.                              | Acid rain can severely **degrade forest ecosystems** and harm aquatic life.                                     |
| **To pose a significant threat to public health** | Gây ra mối đe dọa đáng kể cho sức khỏe cộng đồng     | To be a serious danger to the health of the general population.                        | Air pollution **poses a significant threat to public health**, causing respiratory and cardiovascular diseases. |
| **Chronic respiratory diseases**                  | Các bệnh hô hấp mãn tính                             | Long-term illnesses affecting the lungs and airways, such as asthma and bronchitis.    | Long-term exposure to smog can lead to **chronic respiratory diseases**.                                        |
| **Adverse health effects / outcomes**             | Những ảnh hưởng / kết quả sức khỏe bất lợi           | Negative impacts on a person's health.                                                 | The report details the **adverse health effects** of exposure to industrial pollutants.                         |
| **To disrupt the delicate marine food chain**     | Phá vỡ chuỗi thức ăn mỏng manh của biển              | To upset the natural order of which sea creatures eat which other creatures.           | Microplastics can enter and **disrupt the delicate marine food chain**.                                         |
| **To inflict irreversible harm on marine life**   | Gây ra tác hại không thể đảo ngược cho sinh vật biển | To cause permanent and catastrophic damage to ocean animals and plants.                | Oil spills are known **to inflict irreversible harm on marine life**.                                           |
| **The accumulation of toxic substances**          | Sự tích tụ của các chất độc hại                      | The gradual build-up of poisonous materials in the environment or in living organisms. | **The accumulation of toxic substances** in the soil makes it unfit for agriculture.                            |
| **A severe deterioration in air quality**         | Sự suy giảm nghiêm trọng về chất lượng không khí     | A major decline in how clean and healthy the air is.                                   | Forest fires can cause a **severe deterioration in air quality** over a vast area.                              |
| **To have far-reaching consequences**             | Có những hậu quả sâu rộng                            | To have effects that are extensive and spread over a large area or a long time.        | Plastic pollution has **far-reaching consequences** for wildlife, human health, and the economy.                |

### ✅ **Giải pháp & Biện pháp Kiểm soát (Solutions & Control Measures)**

| Collocation                                                   | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                                              | Short English Meaning                                                                                 | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                               |
| :------------------------------------------------------------ | :------------------------------------------------------------ | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **To combat / tackle pollution**                              | Chống lại / giải quyết vấn đề ô nhiễm                         | To take decisive action to fight or deal with the problem of pollution.                               | A global effort is needed **to combat plastic pollution** in our oceans.                       |
| **To curb / curtail / reduce harmful emissions**              | Hạn chế / cắt giảm / giảm thiểu khí thải độc hại              | To control or lower the amount of harmful gases released into the atmosphere.                         | The new law aims **to curb harmful emissions** from factories and power plants.                |
| **To impose and enforce stricter regulations**                | Áp đặt và thực thi các quy định nghiêm ngặt hơn               | To introduce and ensure compliance with tougher rules.                                                | Governments must **impose and enforce stricter regulations** on industrial waste disposal.     |
| **Pollution control measures / technologies**                 | Các biện pháp / công nghệ kiểm soát ô nhiễm                   | Actions and technologies designed to reduce or prevent pollution.                                     | Catalytic converters in cars are an effective **pollution control technology**.                |
| **Sustainable waste management systems**                      | Hệ thống quản lý chất thải bền vững                           | Systems for collecting, treating, and disposing of waste in an environmentally friendly way.          | Cities need to invest in **sustainable waste management systems**.                             |
| **To transition to green technologies**                       | Chuyển đổi sang các công nghệ xanh                            | To shift towards using technologies that are environmentally friendly.                                | **Transitioning to green technologies** can help industries reduce their environmental impact. |
| **To promote the three R's (Reduce, Reuse, Recycle)**         | Thúc đẩy 3R (Giảm thiểu, Tái sử dụng, Tái chế)                | To encourage the principles of reducing consumption, reusing items, and recycling materials.          | Schools can **promote the three R's** to instill environmentally conscious habits in children. |
| **To transition to a circular economy**                       | Chuyển đổi sang nền kinh tế tuần hoàn                         | To shift to an economic model based on reusing, repairing, and recycling materials to minimize waste. | **Transitioning to a circular economy** is essential for long-term sustainability.             |
| **To switch to renewable energy sources**                     | Chuyển sang các nguồn năng lượng tái tạo                      | To change from using fossil fuels to using energy from sources like the sun and wind.                 | **Switching to renewable energy sources** is key to reducing air pollution.                    |
| **To raise public awareness and encourage behavioral change** | Nâng cao nhận thức cộng đồng và khuyến khích thay đổi hành vi | To make more people know about an issue and motivate them to alter their habits.                      | Media campaigns can **raise public awareness and encourage behavioral change**.                |
