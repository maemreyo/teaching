### 💨 **Các loại Ô nhiễm & Nguồn gốc (Types & Sources of Pollution)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Air / Water / Soil pollution** | Ô nhiễm không khí / nước / đất | Contamination of air, water, or soil. | **Air pollution** from traffic fumes is a major health concern in urban areas. |
| **Plastic pollution** | Ô nhiễm nhựa | Harmful plastic waste in the environment. | **Plastic pollution** poses a serious threat to marine ecosystems. |
| **Noise / Light pollution** | Ô nhiễm tiếng ồn / ánh sáng | Harmful or annoying levels of noise or artificial light. | **Light pollution** in cities can disrupt the natural cycles of wildlife. |
| **Industrial waste / discharge** | Chất thải / nước thải công nghiệp | Waste produced by factories. | The river was contaminated by untreated **industrial waste**. |
| **Vehicle exhaust fumes** | Khí thải từ phương tiện giao thông | The harmful gases released from vehicles. | **Vehicle exhaust fumes** are a primary source of urban air pollution. |
| **The burning of fossil fuels** | Việc đốt nhiên liệu hóa thạch | Using coal, oil, or gas for energy. | **The burning of fossil fuels** releases harmful gases into the atmosphere. |
| **Agricultural runoff** | Dòng chảy nông nghiệp (phân bón, thuốc trừ sâu) | Harmful chemicals from farms that flow into water. | **Agricultural runoff** containing pesticides can pollute nearby rivers. |
| **Improper waste disposal** | Việc xử lý rác thải không đúng cách | Getting rid of waste in a way that harms the environment. | **Improper waste disposal** can lead to the contamination of soil and groundwater. |
| **Toxic waste / Hazardous waste** | Chất thải độc hại / nguy hại | Waste material that can cause harm to humans or the environment. | Strict regulations are needed for the handling of **toxic waste**. |
| **Single-use plastics** | Nhựa dùng một lần | Plastic items designed to be used only once. | Governments around the world are taking action to ban **single-use plastics**. |

### 📉 **Ảnh hưởng & Hậu quả (Effects & Consequences)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **To contaminate the water supply** | Làm ô nhiễm nguồn cung cấp nước | To make the public water source dirty and unsafe. | Leaking chemicals from the factory could **contaminate the public water supply**. |
| **To degrade ecosystems** | Làm suy thoái hệ sinh thái | To damage or worsen natural environments. | Acid rain can severely **degrade forest ecosystems**. |
| **To pose a threat to public health** | Gây ra mối đe dọa cho sức khỏe cộng đồng | To be a danger to the health of the population. | Air pollution **poses a serious threat to public health**, causing respiratory illnesses. |
| **Respiratory problems / diseases** | Các vấn đề / bệnh về đường hô hấp | Illnesses affecting the lungs and breathing. | Long-term exposure to smog can lead to chronic **respiratory problems**. |
| **Adverse health effects** | Những ảnh hưởng xấu đến sức khỏe | Negative impacts on health. | The report details the **adverse health effects** of living near the industrial zone. |
| **To disrupt the food chain** | Phá vỡ chuỗi thức ăn | To upset the natural order of which animals eat which other animals. | Microplastics can enter and **disrupt the marine food chain**. |
| **To harm marine life** | Gây hại cho sinh vật biển | To cause injury or damage to ocean animals. | Oil spills are known **to harm marine life** in catastrophic ways. |
| **The build-up of toxic substances** | Sự tích tụ của các chất độc hại | The accumulation of poisonous materials. | **The build-up of toxic substances** in the soil makes it unfit for agriculture. |
| **To reduce air quality** | Làm giảm chất lượng không khí | To make the air less clean and healthy. | Forest fires can significantly **reduce air quality** over a large area. |

### ✅ **Giải pháp & Biện pháp Kiểm soát (Solutions & Control Measures)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **To combat / tackle pollution** | Chống lại / giải quyết vấn đề ô nhiễm | To fight or deal with pollution. | A global effort is needed **to combat plastic pollution** in our oceans. |
| **To curb / reduce / limit emissions** | Cắt giảm / giảm thiểu khí thải | To control or lower the amount of gases released. | The new law aims **to curb emissions** from factories and power plants. |
| **To impose stricter regulations** | Áp đặt các quy định nghiêm ngặt hơn | To enforce tougher rules. | Governments must **impose stricter regulations** on industrial waste disposal. |
| **Pollution control measures** | Các biện pháp kiểm soát ô nhiễm | Actions taken to reduce pollution. | Investing in public transport is an effective **pollution control measure**. |
| **Waste management systems** | Hệ thống quản lý chất thải | Systems for collecting, treating, and disposing of waste. | Cities need to develop more efficient **waste management systems**. |
| **To introduce green technologies** | Đưa vào các công nghệ xanh | To start using environmentally friendly technologies. | **Introducing green technologies** can help industries reduce their environmental impact. |
| **To promote recycling programs** | Thúc đẩy các chương trình tái chế | To encourage and support recycling initiatives. | Schools can **promote recycling programs** to educate children from a young age. |
| **To switch to renewable energy** | Chuyển sang năng lượng tái tạo | To change to using energy from sources like sun and wind. | **Switching to renewable energy** is key to reducing air pollution. |
| **To raise public awareness** | Nâng cao nhận thức cộng đồng | To make more people know about an issue. | Media campaigns can help **to raise public awareness** about the issue. |
