### 👀 **Các hình thức & Tín hiệu (Forms & Signals)**

| Collocation                   | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                     | Short English Meaning                                           | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                   |
| :---------------------------- | :----------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Non-verbal communication**  | Giao tiếp phi ngôn ngữ               | Communication without using words.                              | **Non-verbal communication** can often be more powerful than words.                                |
| **Non-verbal cues / signals** | Các tín hiệu / dấu hiệu phi ngôn ngữ | The signals you send without speaking.                          | It's important to be aware of the **non-verbal cues** you are sending.                             |
| **Body language**             | Ngôn ngữ cơ thể                      | The movements or positions of your body that show how you feel. | His defensive **body language**, such as crossing his arms, suggested he disagreed.                |
| **Facial expressions**        | Biểu cảm khuôn mặt                   | The looks on your face that show your emotions.                 | **Facial expressions** are a universal way of conveying basic emotions like happiness and sadness. |
| **Eye contact**               | Giao tiếp bằng mắt                   | The act of looking at someone's eyes.                           | In many Western cultures, making direct **eye contact** is a sign of honesty.                      |
| **Gestures and posture**      | Cử chỉ và tư thế                     | Movements of the hands and body, and the way you hold yourself. | His confident **posture** and open **gestures** made him a persuasive speaker.                     |
| **Tone of voice**             | Tông giọng                           | The way your voice sounds, which can show your feelings.        | The same words can have a different meaning depending on your **tone of voice**.                   |
| **Personal space**            | Không gian cá nhân                   | The physical space immediately surrounding someone.             | The amount of **personal space** people are comfortable with varies between cultures.              |

### 🤝 **Chức năng & Tầm quan trọng (Functions & Importance)**

| Collocation                              | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                        | Short English Meaning                                    | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                    |
| :--------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **To convey emotions / feelings**        | Truyền tải cảm xúc / tình cảm           | To communicate emotions or feelings.                     | A smile can **convey feelings** of warmth and friendliness without any words.                       |
| **To reveal one's true feelings**        | Tiết lộ cảm xúc thật                    | To show how you really feel.                             | Sometimes, a person's body language can **reveal their true feelings**, even if their words do not. |
| **To build rapport with someone**        | Xây dựng mối quan hệ tốt với ai đó      | To create a good and friendly relationship with someone. | Mirroring someone's body language is a subconscious way **to build rapport**.                       |
| **An essential part of communication**   | Một phần thiết yếu của giao tiếp        | A very important part of communication.                  | Non-verbal cues are an **essential part of face-to-face communication**.                            |
| **To complement the verbal message**     | Bổ sung cho thông điệp bằng lời         | To add to the spoken message to make it clearer.         | Nodding your head while saying "yes" helps **to complement the verbal message**.                    |
| **To regulate the flow of conversation** | Điều tiết dòng chảy của cuộc trò chuyện | To control the pace and turn-taking in a conversation.   | We use eye contact **to regulate the flow of conversation**, indicating whose turn it is to speak.  |
| **To send a powerful message**           | Gửi đi một thông điệp mạnh mẽ           | To communicate something very effectively.               | A firm handshake can **send a powerful message** of confidence and determination.                   |

### ❓ **Diễn giải & Hiểu lầm (Interpretation & Misunderstanding)**

| Collocation                          | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                 | Short English Meaning                                          | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                         |
| :----------------------------------- | :------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **To read someone's body language**  | Đọc vị ngôn ngữ cơ thể của ai đó | To understand what someone is feeling by their body movements. | Experienced negotiators are skilled at **reading their opponent's body language**.       |
| **To interpret a signal**            | Diễn giải một tín hiệu           | To understand the meaning of a signal.                         | It can be difficult **to interpret non-verbal signals** correctly in a foreign culture.  |
| **To send mixed signals / messages** | Gửi đi những tín hiệu mâu thuẫn  | To communicate contradictory things.                           | He said he was happy, but his sad expression **sent a mixed message**.                   |
| **To misinterpret a gesture**        | Hiểu sai một cử chỉ              | To misunderstand the meaning of a hand or body movement.       | It is easy **to misinterpret a gesture** if you are not familiar with the local culture. |
| **An unconscious signal**            | Một tín hiệu vô thức             | A signal that you send without being aware of it.              | Tapping your foot can be an **unconscious signal** of nervousness.                       |
| **To be unaware of...**              | Không nhận thức được...          | To not know about something.                                   | People are often **unaware of** the non-verbal messages they are sending.                |
| **A subtle hint**                    | Một gợi ý tinh tế                | An indirect suggestion.                                        | Her glance at the clock was a **subtle hint** that it was time to leave.                 |

### 🌍 **Sự khác biệt Văn hóa (Cultural Differences)**

| Collocation                          | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                         | Short English Meaning                                    | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                  |
| :----------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Cultural norms**                   | Các chuẩn mực văn hóa                    | The accepted standards of behavior in a culture.         | **Cultural norms** dictate what is considered appropriate body language.                          |
| **To vary from culture to culture**  | Khác nhau giữa các nền văn hóa           | To be different in different cultures.                   | The meaning of a particular gesture can **vary widely from culture to culture**.                  |
| **A universal expression**           | Một biểu cảm mang tính toàn cầu          | An expression that has the same meaning in all cultures. | While many gestures are cultural, a smile is a largely **universal expression** of happiness.     |
| **A culture-specific gesture**       | Một cử chỉ đặc trưng của một nền văn hóa | A gesture that is unique to one culture.                 | The 'thumbs up' is a **culture-specific gesture** that can be offensive in some countries.        |
| **To cause offense unintentionally** | Vô tình gây khó chịu, xúc phạm           | To accidentally upset or insult someone.                 | Using the wrong gesture can **cause offense unintentionally** when you are abroad.                |
| **Cross-cultural communication**     | Giao tiếp đa văn hóa                     | Communication between people from different cultures.    | Understanding non-verbal cues is vital for effective **cross-cultural communication**.            |
| **To be considered rude / polite**   | Bị coi là thô lỗ / lịch sự               | To be thought of as impolite / polite.                   | In Japan, direct eye contact can sometimes **be considered rude**.                                |
| **To have different meanings**       | Có những ý nghĩa khác nhau               | To mean different things.                                | The same hand gesture can **have completely different meanings** in different parts of the world. |
