### 📈 **Hiện tượng Dân số Già hóa (The Phenomenon of an Aging Population)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **An aging / A graying population** | Một dân số đang già đi | A population with a rising average age. | Japan is a well-known example of a country with **an aging population**. |
| **A demographic shift / trend** | Một sự thay đổi / xu hướng nhân khẩu học | A change in the structure of a population. | The world is experiencing a significant **demographic shift** towards older populations. |
| **Increased life expectancy** | Tuổi thọ trung bình tăng | People living longer on average. | Advances in healthcare have led to a dramatically **increased life expectancy**. |
| **A decline in the birth rate** | Tỷ lệ sinh giảm | A decrease in the number of births. | A **decline in the birth rate** is one of the primary causes of a graying population. |
| **Senior citizens / The elderly** | Người cao tuổi / người già | Older people, especially those who have retired. | The government provides special subsidies for **senior citizens**. |
| **The proportion of older people** | Tỷ lệ người lớn tuổi | The percentage of the population that is old. | **The proportion of older people** in society is projected to increase sharply. |

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
