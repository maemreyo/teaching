### ✈️ **Các loại hình Du lịch & Thuật ngữ chung (Types of Tourism & General Terms)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mass tourism** | Du lịch đại chúng | Tourism involving large numbers of people. | **Mass tourism** can lead to overcrowding and put a strain on local infrastructure. |
| **Ecotourism / Sustainable tourism** | Du lịch sinh thái / Du lịch bền vững | Tourism that is environmentally friendly and benefits local people. | **Ecotourism** aims to minimize the environmental impact of travel. |
| **A holiday / tourist destination** | Một điểm đến du lịch/nghỉ dưỡng | A place where people go for a holiday. | The island has become a popular **tourist destination** for international travelers. |
| **A tourist attraction** | Một điểm thu hút khách du lịch | A place that tourists like to visit. | The ancient temple is the region's main **tourist attraction**. |
| **An influx of tourists** | Một dòng khách du lịch (ồ ạt) | A large number of tourists arriving. | The city struggles to cope with the massive **influx of tourists** during the summer. |
| **To go off the beaten track** | Đi đến những nơi ít người lui tới | To go to places that are not common for tourists. | I prefer **to go off the beaten track** to discover the authentic local culture. |
| **A seasoned traveler** | Một người du lịch dày dạn kinh nghiệm | An experienced traveler. | As a **seasoned traveler**, she is comfortable navigating unfamiliar cities. |

### ✅ **Tác động Tích cực (Positive Impacts)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **To boost the local economy** | Thúc đẩy kinh tế địa phương | To help the local economy grow. | The tourism industry can **boost the local economy** by creating jobs and bringing in revenue. |
| **To create job opportunities** | Tạo ra cơ hội việc làm | To make new jobs available. | New hotels and restaurants **create job opportunities** for local residents. |
| **A major source of revenue / income** | Một nguồn doanh thu / thu nhập chính | A main source of money. | For many developing countries, tourism is a **major source of national income**. |
| **To promote cultural exchange** | Thúc đẩy giao lưu văn hóa | To encourage the sharing of ideas and customs between cultures. | Tourism can **promote cultural exchange** between visitors and the host community. |
| **To foster mutual understanding** | Nuôi dưỡng sự thấu hiểu lẫn nhau | To encourage people to understand each other. | Interacting with people from different backgrounds can **foster mutual understanding**. |
| **To invest in infrastructure** | Đầu tư vào cơ sở hạ tầng | To put money into basic facilities like roads and power supplies. | Revenue from tourism allows governments **to invest in infrastructure** such as roads and airports. |
| **To preserve cultural heritage** | Gìn giữ, bảo tồn di sản văn hóa | To protect the traditions and culture of a country. | Tourism can provide the financial incentive **to preserve historic buildings and cultural traditions**. |
| **To showcase a country's culture** | Phô bày, giới thiệu văn hóa của một quốc gia | To display a country's culture. | Festivals are a great way **to showcase a country's rich culture** to the world. |

### ❌ **Tác động Tiêu cực (Negative Impacts)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **To have a detrimental effect on...** | Có ảnh hưởng bất lợi/gây hại cho... | To have a harmful effect on something. | Mass tourism can **have a detrimental effect on** the natural environment. |
| **Environmental degradation** | Sự suy thoái môi trường | The process of the environment becoming worse. | The construction of large resorts often leads to **environmental degradation**. |
| **The erosion of local culture** | Sự xói mòn văn hóa địa phương | The gradual destruction of local culture. | Some people fear that tourism leads to **the erosion of local culture** and traditions. |
| **To damage the natural environment** | Phá hủy môi trường tự nhiên | To harm the natural world. | Careless tourists can **damage the natural environment** by leaving litter or disturbing wildlife. |
| **Overcrowding** | Tình trạng quá đông đúc | A situation where there are too many people in a place. | **Overcrowding** during peak season can diminish the quality of the visitor experience. |
| **A rise in the cost of living** | Sự gia tăng chi phí sinh hoạt | An increase in the cost of basic necessities. | A boom in tourism can cause a **rise in the cost of living** for local residents. |
| **To put a strain on local resources** | Gây áp lực lên tài nguyên địa phương | To be a burden on local resources. | A large number of tourists can **put a strain on local resources** like water and electricity. |
| **The commercialization of culture** | Sự thương mại hóa văn hóa | The process of turning culture into a product to be sold. | Critics argue that tourism leads to **the commercialization of culture**, turning authentic traditions into performances. |
| **A tourist trap** | Nơi bẫy khách du lịch (giá đắt, chất lượng kém) | A place that attracts many tourists and is often expensive and low quality. | The area is full of **tourist traps** selling overpriced souvenirs. |
| **To spoil the natural beauty** | Phá hỏng vẻ đẹp tự nhiên | To ruin the beauty of a natural place. | The construction of large hotels has **spoiled the natural beauty** of the coastline. |

### 🌱 **Du lịch Bền vững & Có trách nhiệm (Sustainable & Responsible Tourism)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Responsible tourism** | Du lịch có trách nhiệm | Tourism that is ethical and sustainable. | **Responsible tourism** involves making choices that have a positive impact on the destination. |
| **To minimize the environmental impact** | Giảm thiểu tác động môi trường | To reduce the harm done to the environment. | The goal of ecotourism is **to minimize the environmental impact** of travel. |
| **To respect local customs and traditions** | Tôn trọng phong tục và truyền thống địa phương | To show respect for the customs and traditions of a place. | Tourists should always **respect local customs and traditions**. |
| **To support the local community** | Hỗ trợ cộng đồng địa phương | To help the people who live in a particular area. | One way to be a responsible tourist is **to support the local community** by buying local products. |
| **To raise environmental awareness** | Nâng cao nhận thức về môi trường | To make people more knowledgeable about environmental issues. | Guided nature tours can **raise environmental awareness** among visitors. |
| **To implement regulations** | Thực thi các quy định | To put rules into effect. | The government needs **to implement strict regulations** to control the development of tourist resorts. |
| **To limit the number of visitors** | Giới hạn số lượng du khách | To control the number of people who can visit a place. | Some fragile sites have had **to limit the number of visitors** to prevent damage. |
| **To promote off-season travel** | Thúc đẩy du lịch trái mùa | To encourage people to travel during the less popular times of the year. | **Promoting off-season travel** can help to reduce the problem of overcrowding. |
| **Low-impact tourism** | Du lịch ít tác động | Tourism that has a small effect on the environment. | Activities like hiking and kayaking are considered forms of **low-impact tourism**. |
