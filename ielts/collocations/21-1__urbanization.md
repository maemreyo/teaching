| Collocation                                   | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                        | Short English Meaning                                                                                | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                                |
| :-------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

| **Inadequate infrastructure**                 | Cơ sở hạ tầng không đầy đủ              | A lack of sufficient basic facilities like transport, sanitation, and power systems.                 | Many fast-growing cities suffer from **inadequate infrastructure** that cannot keep up with population growth.  |
| **A chronic shortage of affordable housing**  | Sự thiếu hụt nhà ở giá rẻ kinh niên     | A long-term and persistent lack of housing that is affordable for the average person.                | There is a **chronic shortage of affordable housing** in many global cities.                                    |
| **The rising cost of living**                 | Chi phí sinh hoạt ngày càng tăng        | The increasing amount of money needed to cover basic expenses such as housing, food, and healthcare. | **The rising cost of living** makes it difficult for low-income families to reside in city centers.             |
| **Substandard living conditions**             | Điều kiện sống dưới tiêu chuẩn          | Living in a place that is unsafe, unsanitary, or structurally unsound.                               | People in urban slums often endure **substandard living conditions** with no access to clean water.             |
| **Urban poverty and inequality**              | Đói nghèo và bất bình đẳng ở đô thị     | The state of being extremely poor and the unequal distribution of resources within a city.           | **Urban poverty and inequality** are often concentrated in specific neighborhoods.                              |
| **A lack of green spaces**                    | Sự thiếu hụt không gian xanh            | An insufficient amount of parks, gardens, and other natural areas within a city.                     | The **lack of green spaces** in cities can have a negative impact on residents' mental and physical well-being. |
| **Environmental degradation**                 | Sự suy thoái môi trường                 | The deterioration of the environment through depletion of resources such as air, water, and soil.    | Air and water pollution are common forms of **environmental degradation** in large cities.                      |
| **To put a severe strain on public services** | Gây áp lực nặng nề lên các dịch vụ công | To be a major burden on essential services like hospitals, schools, and waste management.            | A sudden influx of migrants can **put a severe strain on public services**.                                     |
| **Social alienation**                         | Sự xa lánh, lạc lõng trong xã hội       | The feeling of being isolated or disconnected from others in a large, anonymous urban environment.   | Despite being surrounded by people, city dwellers can sometimes experience **social alienation**.               |

| **To improve urban governance**                       | Cải thiện quản trị đô thị                                | To make the management and administration of a city more effective, transparent, and responsive.                     | **Improving urban governance** is crucial for tackling complex urban challenges.                                   |
