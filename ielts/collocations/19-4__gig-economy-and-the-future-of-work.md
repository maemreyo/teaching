### 🌐 **Khái niệm & Thuật ngữ Cốt lõi (Core Concepts & Terminology)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The gig economy** | Nền kinh tế tự do (gig) | A labor market characterized by short-term contracts or freelance work. | **The gig economy** is characterized by short-term contracts and freelance work as opposed to permanent jobs. |
| **A freelance worker / A freelancer** | Người làm nghề tự do | A self-employed person who works for different companies on particular assignments. | As a **freelancer**, she has the freedom to choose which projects she works on. |
| **A short-term contract** | Hợp đồng ngắn hạn | A contract for a job that lasts for a limited time. | Many companies are hiring staff on **short-term contracts** to increase flexibility. |
| **On-demand work** | Công việc theo yêu cầu | Work that is available when it is needed. | Ride-sharing apps are a prime example of **on-demand work**. |
| **Self-employment** | Tự kinh doanh, tự làm chủ | Working for yourself instead of for an employer. | **Self-employment** offers autonomy but often comes with less financial stability. |
| **A portfolio career** | Sự nghiệp đa dạng (làm nhiều việc, dự án khác nhau) | A career made up of a series of different jobs or projects. | A **portfolio career** allows individuals to utilize a wide range of their skills. |
| **A non-traditional work arrangement** | Một sự sắp xếp công việc phi truyền thống | A work schedule or setup that is different from the standard 9-to-5 job. | Remote working and flexible hours are types of **non-traditional work arrangements**. |
| **The platform economy** | Nền kinh tế nền tảng | An economy where online platforms connect workers directly with customers. | The **platform economy** connects service providers directly with customers via apps like Uber and Airbnb. |

### 🧑‍💻 **Góc nhìn của Người lao động (The Worker's Perspective)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **To have flexible working hours** | Có giờ làm việc linh hoạt | To be able to choose the hours that you work. | The main advantage of freelance work is the ability **to have flexible working hours**. |
| **To be one's own boss** | Tự làm chủ | To work for yourself. | Many people are attracted to the idea of **being their own boss**. |
| **A greater degree of autonomy** | Mức độ tự chủ cao hơn | A higher level of freedom to make your own decisions. | Freelancers enjoy a **greater degree of autonomy** in how they complete their work. |
| **A lack of job security** | Sự thiếu đảm bảo công việc | Not having a secure and stable job. | A major drawback of the gig economy is the **lack of job security**. |
| **An unstable / unpredictable income** | Thu nhập không ổn định / khó đoán | An income that is not regular or reliable. | Working from project to project can lead to an **unstable and unpredictable income**. |
| **To have no access to employee benefits** | Không được hưởng các phúc lợi của nhân viên | To not receive benefits like sick pay or a pension. | Gig workers often have **no access to employee benefits** such as sick pay or a pension. |
| **To blur the lines between...** | Xóa nhòa ranh giới giữa... | To make the difference between two things less clear. | Working from home can **blur the lines between** one's professional and personal life. |
| **A sense of precariousness** | Cảm giác bấp bênh | A feeling of being insecure and uncertain. | The constant search for the next project can create a **sense of precariousness**. |

### 📈 **Góc nhìn của Doanh nghiệp & Nền Kinh tế (The Employer's & Economic Perspective)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **To hire on a project-by-project basis** | Thuê nhân sự theo từng dự án | To employ someone for one project at a time. | Companies can **hire specialists on a project-by-project basis** without long-term commitment. |
| **To reduce labor costs** | Giảm chi phí nhân công | To lower the amount of money spent on workers. | Outsourcing tasks to freelancers is one way for a business **to reduce labor costs**. |
| **A flexible workforce** | Một lực lượng lao động linh hoạt | A workforce that can adapt to changing needs. | The gig economy provides companies with a **flexible workforce** that can adapt to changing demands. |
| **To outsource tasks** | Thuê ngoài các công việc | To pay another company to do some of your work. | Many corporations **outsource tasks** like customer service to specialized agencies. |
| **A shift away from traditional employment** | Một sự chuyển dịch khỏi việc làm truyền thống | A change from the old model of having a long-term job. | We are witnessing a clear **shift away from traditional, lifelong employment**. |
| **To disrupt traditional industries** | Phá vỡ các ngành công nghiệp truyền thống | To completely change the way traditional industries work. | Online streaming services have completely **disrupted the traditional television industry**. |
| **A dynamic labor market** | Một thị trường lao động năng động | A labor market that is active and changing. | Proponents argue that the gig economy creates a more **dynamic and efficient labor market**. |

### 🔮 **Tương lai của Việc làm (The Future of Work)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The future of work** | Tương lai của việc làm | The way that work is expected to change in the future. | Debates about AI and the gig economy are central to discussions about **the future of work**. |
| **Lifelong learning and continuous upskilling** | Học tập suốt đời và nâng cao kỹ năng liên tục | The process of continuing to learn and develop new skills throughout your life. | In a fast-changing job market, **lifelong learning and continuous upskilling** are essential. |
| **The changing nature of employment** | Bản chất đang thay đổi của việc làm | The way that jobs and work are changing. | Individuals must adapt to **the changing nature of employment**. |
| **A demand for specialized skills** | Nhu cầu về các kỹ năng chuyên môn hóa | A need for skills in a particular area. | There is a growing **demand for specialized skills** in areas like data science and AI. |
| **To adapt to a changing job market** | Thích nghi với một thị trường việc làm đang thay đổi | To change in order to be successful in a changing job market. | The ability **to adapt to a changing job market** is the key to long-term career success. |
| **The decline of the nine-to-five job** | Sự suy giảm của công việc 9 giờ sáng - 5 giờ chiều | The trend of fewer people working standard office hours. | We are witnessing **the decline of the traditional nine-to-five job**. |
| **Remote collaboration** | Hợp tác từ xa | Working together with people in different locations. | Tools like Slack and Zoom have made **remote collaboration** more effective than ever. |
| **An entrepreneurial workforce** | Một lực lượng lao động có tinh thần khởi nghiệp | A workforce with the skills and attitude to start new businesses. | The gig economy may foster a more **entrepreneurial workforce**. |
