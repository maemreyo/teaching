### 🏢 **Môi trường làm việc Hiện đại (The Modern Workplace)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **An open-plan office** | Văn phòng không gian mở | An office with few or no dividing walls. | **Open-plan offices** are designed to encourage collaboration, but they can also be noisy. |
| **A collaborative workspace** | Không gian làm việc hợp tác | A workspace designed for people to work together. | Co-working spaces provide a **collaborative workspace** for freelancers and startups. |
| **Remote working / Telecommuting** | Làm việc từ xa | Working from home instead of in an office. | **Remote working** has become a mainstream practice since the pandemic. |
| **Flexible working hours/arrangements** | Giờ làm việc / sắp xếp công việc linh hoạt | A system where employees can choose when they work. | Offering **flexible working hours** can help employees achieve a better work-life balance. |
| **A corporate culture** | Văn hóa doanh nghiệp | The shared values and practices of a company. | A positive **corporate culture** is essential for retaining talented employees. |
| **A dynamic work environment** | Một môi trường làm việc năng động | An exciting and fast-paced work environment. | Young graduates are often attracted to the **dynamic work environment** of tech companies. |
| **A hierarchical structure** | Cấu trúc phân cấp | A system where people are ranked according to their level of importance. | A traditional company often has a rigid **hierarchical structure**. |

### 😊 **Sự hài lòng & Động lực trong Công việc (Job Satisfaction & Motivation)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Job satisfaction** | Sự hài lòng trong công việc | How happy and content you are with your job. | **Job satisfaction** is often cited as a more important factor than a high salary. |
| **A sense of fulfillment / achievement** | Cảm giác mãn nguyện / thành tựu | A feeling of happiness and satisfaction from your work. | Helping others gives nurses a deep **sense of fulfillment**. |
| **To feel valued and appreciated** | Cảm thấy được trân trọng và công nhận | To feel that your work and contribution are respected. | Employees are more motivated when they **feel valued and appreciated** by their managers. |
| **Opportunities for career advancement** | Các cơ hội thăng tiến sự nghiệp | Chances to get a better job in a company. | The company offers excellent **opportunities for career advancement**. |
| **To receive recognition for** | Nhận được sự công nhận cho | To be praised or rewarded for your work. | It is important for staff **to receive recognition for** their hard work. |
| **A competitive salary** | Một mức lương cạnh tranh | A salary that is as good as, or better than, other companies offer. | To attract top talent, businesses need to offer a **competitive salary**. |
| **Financial rewards** | Phần thưởng tài chính | Money given in recognition of good work. | **Financial rewards** such as bonuses can be a powerful motivator. |
| **Fringe benefits / Perks** | Các phúc lợi phụ | Extra benefits in addition to a salary (e.g., a company car). | **Fringe benefits** like health insurance and a company car can be very attractive to job seekers. |
| **Non-monetary rewards** | Các phần thưởng phi tiền tệ | Rewards that are not money, such as extra time off. | Flexible working hours and extra holidays are examples of **non-monetary rewards**. |
| **Employee morale** | Tinh thần của nhân viên | The general level of confidence and happiness of a person or group of people. | A positive work environment is crucial for maintaining high **employee morale**. |

### ⚖️ **Cân bằng & Sức khỏe tại nơi làm việc (Work-Life Balance & Well-being)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Work-life balance** | Cân bằng giữa công việc và cuộc sống | The balance between your work and your personal life. | Many people are leaving high-paying jobs in search of a better **work-life balance**. |
| **Employee well-being** | Sức khỏe và hạnh phúc của nhân viên | The health, happiness, and welfare of employees. | Companies are increasingly focusing on **employee well-being** to reduce stress and improve productivity. |
| **To avoid burnout** | Tránh tình trạng kiệt sức vì công việc | To prevent a state of emotional, physical, and mental exhaustion caused by excessive and prolonged stress. | Taking regular breaks is essential **to avoid burnout**. |
| **A heavy workload** | Khối lượng công việc lớn | A large amount of work to do. | A **heavy workload** is a common cause of work-related stress. |
| **To work long hours** | Làm việc nhiều giờ | To work for more hours than is usual. | **Working long hours** over a prolonged period can be detrimental to one's health. |
| **A supportive work environment** | Một môi trường làm việc có tính hỗ trợ | A workplace where employees feel supported by their colleagues and managers. | A **supportive work environment** is one where colleagues help each other. |

### 📉 **Thách thức & Sự không hài lòng (Challenges & Dissatisfaction)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A dead-end job** | Một công việc không có tương lai | A job with no prospects of promotion. | He felt he was stuck in a **dead-end job** with no opportunities for promotion. |
| **A mundane / repetitive job** | Một công việc nhàm chán / lặp đi lặp lại | A boring job that involves doing the same things over and over again. | Automation is replacing many **mundane and repetitive jobs**. |
| **Job insecurity** | Sự bất an, không đảm bảo trong công việc | The feeling that your job is not secure. | The gig economy has led to a rise in **job insecurity** for many workers. |
| **A toxic work environment** | Một môi trường làm việc độc hại | A workplace that is unpleasant and harmful to employees. | A **toxic work environment** can be characterized by bullying and a lack of trust. |
| **To be stuck in a rut** | Bị mắc kẹt trong một lối mòn | To be in a boring situation that is hard to change. | After ten years in the same role, she felt she was **stuck in a rut**. |
| **A lack of autonomy** | Sự thiếu tự chủ | Not having the freedom to make your own decisions. | Micromanagement can lead to a **lack of autonomy** and lower job satisfaction. |
| **High staff turnover** | Tỷ lệ nhân viên nghỉ việc cao | A high rate of employees leaving a company. | A **high staff turnover** rate can be a sign of poor management. |
| **Work-related stress** | Căng thẳng liên quan đến công việc | Stress caused by your job. | **Work-related stress** is a significant public health issue. |
| **To hand in one's resignation** | Nộp đơn xin nghỉ việc | To officially say that you are leaving your job. | After the disagreement with his boss, he decided **to hand in his resignation**. |
