### 🌪️ **Hiện tượng & Thảm họa (Phenomena & Disasters)**

| Collocation                                 | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                           | Short English Meaning                                                              | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                        |
| :------------------------------------------ | :----------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Extreme weather events**                  | Các hiện tượng thời tiết cực đoan          | Severe or unusual weather conditions, such as heatwaves, floods, or hurricanes.    | Climate change is increasing the frequency and intensity of **extreme weather events**. |
| **A natural catastrophe / disaster**        | Một thảm họa / thảm kịch tự nhiên          | A major adverse event resulting from natural processes of the Earth.               | The earthquake was a **natural catastrophe** of unprecedented scale.                    |
| **Torrential rain and widespread flooding** | Mưa xối xả và lũ lụt trên diện rộng        | Very heavy rain that leads to flooding across a large area.                        | The region experienced **torrential rain and widespread flooding**.                     |
| **Prolonged periods of drought**            | Các đợt hạn hán kéo dài                    | Long periods of abnormally low rainfall, leading to a shortage of water.           | **Prolonged periods of drought** have led to critical water shortages and crop failure. |
| **Record-breaking heatwaves**               | Các đợt nắng nóng kỷ lục                   | Periods of abnormally hot weather that are the most severe ever recorded.          | The summer was marked by **record-breaking heatwaves** across the continent.            |
| **The melting of polar ice caps**           | Sự tan chảy của các chỏm băng ở hai cực    | The process of ice at the North and South Poles turning into water due to warming. | **The melting of polar ice caps** is a major contributor to rising sea levels.          |
| **Rising sea levels**                       | Mực nước biển dâng                         | The increase in the average level of the Earth's oceans.                           | **Rising sea levels** threaten coastal communities and ecosystems around the world.     |
| **Devastating wildfires and hurricanes**    | Cháy rừng và bão tàn khốc                  | Extremely destructive fires in wild areas and powerful tropical storms.            | The news was filled with images of **devastating wildfires and hurricanes**.            |
| **A devastating impact on...**              | Một tác động tàn khốc lên...               | A highly destructive and damaging effect on something.                             | The oil spill had a **devastating impact on** marine wildlife.                          |
| **Unprecedented in scale and intensity**    | Chưa từng có tiền lệ về quy mô và cường độ | Larger and more powerful than ever before in recorded history.                     | The recent floods were **unprecedented in scale and intensity**.                        |

### 🔥 **Nguyên nhân & Vấn đề (Causes & Problems)**

| Collocation                                       | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                            | Short English Meaning                                                                                          | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                                 |
| :------------------------------------------------ | :------------------------------------------ | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **The burning of fossil fuels**                   | Việc đốt nhiên liệu hóa thạch               | The combustion of coal, oil, and natural gas to produce energy.                                                | **The burning of fossil fuels** is the primary source of man-made greenhouse gas emissions.                      |
| **Greenhouse gas emissions**                      | Khí thải nhà kính                           | Gases in the atmosphere that trap heat, such as carbon dioxide and methane.                                    | We must drastically reduce **greenhouse gas emissions** to combat global warming.                                |
| **Carbon dioxide (CO2) levels in the atmosphere** | Mức độ carbon dioxide (CO2) trong khí quyển | The concentration of CO2 in the air.                                                                           | There is a direct link between rising **CO2 levels in the atmosphere** and global temperatures.                  |
| **Deforestation and land clearing**               | Nạn phá rừng và giải phóng mặt bằng         | The cutting down of forests and clearing of land for agriculture or development.                               | **Deforestation and land clearing** contribute to climate change by reducing the Earth's capacity to absorb CO2. |
| **Industrial pollution / emissions**              | Ô nhiễm / khí thải công nghiệp              | Waste and pollutants released by factories and industrial processes.                                           | **Industrial pollution** has contaminated rivers and damaged air quality in the region.                          |
| **Unsustainable agricultural practices**          | Các phương pháp nông nghiệp không bền vững  | Farming methods that deplete natural resources and harm the environment.                                       | **Unsustainable agricultural practices** can lead to soil erosion and water pollution.                           |
| **The overconsumption of natural resources**      | Sự tiêu thụ quá mức tài nguyên thiên nhiên  | Using natural resources at a rate faster than they can be replenished.                                         | Our consumer lifestyle leads to **the overconsumption of the world's natural resources**.                        |
| **Man-made / Anthropogenic climate change**       | Biến đổi khí hậu do con người gây ra        | Climate change that is caused by human activities.                                                             | The scientific consensus is that **anthropogenic climate change** is a reality.                                  |
| **The depletion of the ozone layer**              | Sự suy giảm của tầng ozone                  | The thinning of the layer of gas in the upper atmosphere that protects the Earth from harmful solar radiation. | The use of CFCs led to **the depletion of the ozone layer**.                                                     |
| **Loss of biodiversity**                          | Sự mất mát đa dạng sinh học                 | The reduction in the variety of life in a particular habitat or in the world as a whole.                       | The destruction of rainforests is causing a catastrophic **loss of biodiversity**.                               |

### 📉 **Tác động & Hậu quả (Impacts & Consequences)**

| Collocation                                           | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                                             | Short English Meaning                                                                                                     | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                            |
| :---------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Irreversible climate change**                       | Biến đổi khí hậu không thể đảo ngược                         | Climate change that has passed a tipping point and cannot be stopped or reversed.                                         | Scientists warn that we are approaching the point of **irreversible climate change**.                       |
| **Far-reaching consequences**                         | Những hậu quả sâu rộng                                       | Effects that are extensive and have a wide-ranging impact.                                                                | The **far-reaching consequences** of global warming will affect every country on Earth.                     |
| **Endangered species are on the brink of extinction** | Các loài có nguy cơ tuyệt chủng đang trên bờ vực tuyệt chủng | Animals or plants at risk of disappearing forever.                                                                        | Many **endangered species are on the brink of extinction** due to habitat loss.                             |
| **The disruption of ecosystems**                      | Sự gián đoạn các hệ sinh thái                                | The disturbance of the delicate balance of interactions between organisms and their environment.                          | Rising ocean temperatures cause a severe **disruption of marine ecosystems** like coral reefs.              |
| **Food and water scarcity**                           | Sự khan hiếm thực phẩm và nước                               | A lack of sufficient food and water to meet the demands of a population.                                                  | Prolonged droughts can lead to severe **food and water scarcity**.                                          |
| **Vulnerable coastal populations**                    | Các cộng đồng dân cư ven biển dễ bị tổn thương               | Communities living in low-lying coastal areas that are at high risk from rising sea levels.                               | **Vulnerable coastal populations** are the most at risk from storm surges and flooding.                     |
| **Climate refugees / Climate-induced migration**      | Người tị nạn khí hậu / Di cư do khí hậu                      | People who are forced to leave their homes due to the impacts of climate change.                                          | The number of **climate refugees** is expected to increase dramatically in the coming decades.              |
| **A global public health crisis**                     | Một cuộc khủng hoảng sức khỏe cộng đồng toàn cầu             | A serious problem affecting the health of people all over the world.                                                      | The spread of tropical diseases due to warming temperatures could create a **global public health crisis**. |
| **Threats to global food security**                   | Các mối đe dọa đối với an ninh lương thực toàn cầu           | Dangers to the availability of and access to sufficient, safe, and nutritious food for all people.                        | Extreme weather events pose a significant **threat to global food security**.                               |
| **To reach a tipping point**                          | Đạt đến điểm bùng phát (không thể quay đầu)                  | To reach the critical point in a situation beyond which a significant and often unstoppable effect or change takes place. | Scientists fear we may **reach a tipping point** where climate change becomes irreversible.                 |

### ✅ **Giải pháp & Hành động (Solutions & Actions)**

| Collocation                                                 | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                                         | Short English Meaning                                                                            | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                           |
| :---------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **To transition to renewable energy sources**               | Chuyển đổi sang các nguồn năng lượng tái tạo             | To shift from using fossil fuels to using energy sources like solar, wind, and hydro power.      | We must accelerate the **transition to renewable energy sources**.                                         |
| **To reduce one's carbon footprint**                        | Giảm dấu chân carbon của một người                       | To lower the total amount of greenhouse gases produced by one's activities.                      | We can all **reduce our carbon footprint** by consuming less and recycling more.                           |
| **To implement robust green policies**                      | Thực hiện các chính sách xanh mạnh mẽ                    | To put effective and comprehensive environmentally friendly rules into action.                   | The government needs **to implement robust green policies** to meet its climate targets.                   |
| **To adopt sustainable practices**                          | Áp dụng các phương pháp bền vững                         | To start using methods in business, agriculture, or daily life that do not harm the environment. | Farmers are encouraged **to adopt sustainable agricultural practices**.                                    |
| **To protect and restore natural habitats**                 | Bảo vệ và phục hồi môi trường sống tự nhiên              | To keep the homes of plants and animals safe and to repair damaged ones.                         | It is vital **to protect and restore natural habitats** like forests and wetlands.                         |
| **To offset one's carbon emissions**                        | Bù đắp lượng khí thải carbon của một người               | To compensate for the CO2 one produces by funding an equivalent CO2 saving elsewhere.            | Many airlines offer passengers the option **to offset their carbon emissions**.                            |
| **A binding international climate agreement**               | Một thỏa thuận khí hậu quốc tế có tính ràng buộc pháp lý | A formal agreement between countries with legal force to address climate change.                 | The world needs a **binding international climate agreement** with ambitious targets.                      |
| **To improve energy efficiency**                            | Cải thiện hiệu quả năng lượng                            | To use less energy to provide the same service.                                                  | **Improving energy efficiency** in homes and industries can significantly reduce emissions.                |
| **Reforestation and afforestation projects**                | Các dự án tái trồng rừng và trồng rừng                   | The process of replanting trees in a forest that has been depleted, and planting new forests.    | **Reforestation and afforestation projects** can help to absorb CO2 from the atmosphere.                   |
| **To raise public awareness and foster a sense of urgency** | Nâng cao nhận thức cộng đồng và thúc đẩy ý thức cấp bách | To make more people know about an issue and feel that it requires immediate action.              | Campaigns are needed **to raise public awareness and foster a sense of urgency** about the climate crisis. |
