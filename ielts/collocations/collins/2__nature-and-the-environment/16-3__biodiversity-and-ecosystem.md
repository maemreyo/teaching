### 🌿 **Các Khái niệm Cốt lõi (Core Concepts)**

| Collocation                                       | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                               | Short English Meaning                                                                                                                 | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                                                  |
| :------------------------------------------------ | :--------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Biological diversity (Biodiversity)**           | Đa dạng sinh học                               | The variety of plant and animal life in the world or in a particular habitat.                                                         | The Amazon rainforest is a region of immense **biological diversity**.                                                            |
| **A delicate and complex ecosystem**              | Một hệ sinh thái mỏng manh và phức tạp         | A natural community of organisms and their environment that is easily damaged and has intricate interconnections.                     | Coral reefs are a **delicate and complex ecosystem** highly sensitive to changes in water temperature.                            |
| **The intricate web of life / The food web**      | Mạng lưới sự sống / mạng lưới thức ăn phức tạp | The complex network of feeding relationships between organisms in an ecosystem.                                                       | The extinction of one species can have a ripple effect throughout **the intricate web of life**.                                  |
| **A species' natural habitat**                    | Môi trường sống tự nhiên của một loài          | The natural home or environment of a particular animal, plant, or other organism.                                                     | The primary threat to wildlife is the destruction of **a species' natural habitat**.                                              |
| **Ecological balance**                            | Cân bằng sinh thái                             | The stable state of an ecosystem where the relationships between organisms remain relatively unchanged.                               | Human activities have profoundly upset the **ecological balance** in many parts of the world.                                     |
| **A keystone species**                            | Loài chủ chốt                                  | A species on which other species in an ecosystem largely depend, such that if it were removed the ecosystem would change drastically. | Sea otters are a **keystone species** because they control the sea urchin population, which would otherwise destroy kelp forests. |
| **A marine / terrestrial / freshwater ecosystem** | Hệ sinh thái biển / trên cạn / nước ngọt       | The community of living things in the ocean / on land / in rivers and lakes.                                                          | Plastic pollution is causing significant harm to **marine ecosystems**.                                                           |
| **Genetic diversity**                             | Đa dạng di truyền                              | The total number of genetic characteristics in the genetic makeup of a species.                                                       | **Genetic diversity** is crucial for a species' ability to adapt to changing environments.                                        |
| **A biodiversity hotspot**                        | Một điểm nóng đa dạng sinh học                 | A biogeographic region that is both a significant reservoir of biodiversity and is under threat from humans.                          | The island of Madagascar is considered a global **biodiversity hotspot**.                                                         |
| **Ecosystem services**                            | Các dịch vụ hệ sinh thái                       | The many and varied benefits that humans freely gain from the natural environment and from properly-functioning ecosystems.           | Pollination of crops by bees is one of the most important **ecosystem services**.                                                 |

### 멸종 **Mối đe dọa & Sự suy giảm (Threats & Decline)**

| Collocation                                      | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                                    | Short English Meaning                                                                                    | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                    |
| :----------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **An unprecedented loss of biodiversity**        | Sự mất mát đa dạng sinh học chưa từng có            | A reduction in the variety of life on Earth at a rate never seen before.                                 | Scientists warn that we are facing an **unprecedented loss of biodiversity**.                       |
| **To be threatened with extinction**             | Bị đe dọa tuyệt chủng                               | To be at a high risk of disappearing completely from the planet.                                         | Many large mammals, such as tigers and rhinos, are **threatened with extinction**.                  |
| **To be on the brink / verge of extinction**     | Trên bờ vực tuyệt chủng                             | To be extremely close to becoming extinct.                                                               | The Sumatran orangutan is **on the brink of extinction** due to rapid deforestation.                |
| **Endangered and critically endangered species** | Các loài có nguy cơ tuyệt chủng và cực kỳ nguy cấp  | Species that are at a very high or extremely high risk of extinction in the wild.                        | Laws have been passed to protect **endangered species** from illegal hunting.                       |
| **Invasive alien species**                       | Các loài ngoại lai xâm lấn                          | A non-native species that is introduced to an ecosystem and causes harm.                                 | **Invasive alien species** can outcompete native animals for resources and disrupt the ecosystem.   |
| **Habitat destruction and fragmentation**        | Sự phá hủy và phân mảnh môi trường sống             | The process by which a natural habitat is destroyed or broken up into smaller, isolated pieces.          | **Habitat destruction and fragmentation** is the leading cause of species extinction worldwide.     |
| **The illegal wildlife trade / poaching**        | Nạn buôn bán / săn trộm động vật hoang dã trái phép | The unlawful harvesting of and trade in wild animals and plants.                                         | **The illegal wildlife trade** is a multi-billion dollar industry that threatens countless species. |
| **A sharp decline in population numbers**        | Sự sụt giảm mạnh về số lượng quần thể               | A rapid and significant decrease in the number of individuals of a particular species.                   | Scientists have observed a **sharp decline in the population numbers** of many insect species.      |
| **To push a species towards extinction**         | Đẩy một loài đến bờ vực tuyệt chủng                 | To cause a species to become endangered or extinct.                                                      | Climate change is **pushing many species towards extinction**.                                      |
| **The sixth mass extinction**                    | Cuộc đại tuyệt chủng lần thứ sáu                    | The ongoing extinction event of species during the present Holocene epoch, mainly due to human activity. | Some scientists believe we are currently in the midst of **the sixth mass extinction**.             |

### ❤️ **Bảo tồn & Bảo vệ (Conservation & Protection)**

| Collocation                                     | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                                      | Short English Meaning                                                                                  | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                     |
| :---------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Wildlife conservation**                       | Bảo tồn động vật hoang dã                             | The practice of protecting wild plant and animal species and their habitats.                           | **Wildlife conservation** requires international cooperation and local community involvement.        |
| **Conservation efforts and initiatives**        | Các nỗ lực và sáng kiến bảo tồn                       | Organized actions and new plans to protect the natural environment.                                    | **Conservation efforts** are underway to protect the region's remaining old-growth forests.          |
| **To preserve and protect natural habitats**    | Gìn giữ và bảo vệ môi trường sống tự nhiên            | To keep the natural homes of plants and animals safe from harm or destruction.                         | One of the most effective conservation strategies is **to preserve and protect natural habitats**.   |
| **Habitat restoration**                         | Phục hồi môi trường sống                              | The process of repairing a damaged natural environment and bringing it back to its original state.     | The project aims to achieve the **habitat restoration** of the wetland that was drained for farming. |
| **A protected area / a nature reserve**         | Khu bảo tồn / khu dự trữ thiên nhiên                  | A location which receives protection because of its recognized natural, ecological or cultural values. | The area was designated as a **nature reserve** to protect rare birds and plants.                    |
| **Captive breeding programs**                   | Các chương trình nhân giống trong điều kiện nuôi nhốt | The process of breeding animals in controlled environments within well-defined settings, such as zoos. | The California condor was saved from extinction thanks to successful **captive breeding programs**.  |
| **The reintroduction of species into the wild** | Việc tái thả các loài về tự nhiên                     | The process of releasing a species back into its native habitat from which it has disappeared.         | **The reintroduction of wolves into Yellowstone** has had a profound impact on the ecosystem.        |
| **Sustainable tourism / Ecotourism**            | Du lịch bền vững / Du lịch sinh thái                  | Tourism that is environmentally friendly and provides financial benefits for conservation.             | **Ecotourism** can provide a sustainable source of funding for conservation projects.                |
| **To enforce anti-poaching laws**               | Thực thi luật chống săn trộm                          | To ensure that laws against illegal hunting are obeyed.                                                | Governments must **enforce anti-poaching laws** more strictly to protect endangered animals.         |
| **To raise awareness about biodiversity loss**  | Nâng cao nhận thức về mất mát đa dạng sinh học        | To make the public more knowledgeable and concerned about the decline in the variety of life.          | Documentaries can be a powerful tool **to raise awareness about biodiversity loss**.                 |

### 🌐 **Động lực & Chức năng Hệ sinh thái (Ecosystem Dynamics & Functions)**

| Collocation                                       | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                                            | Short English Meaning                                                                                         | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                   |
| :------------------------------------------------ | :---------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **To play a vital / crucial role in...**          | Đóng một vai trò sống còn / thiết yếu trong...              | To have a very important and necessary part in something.                                                     | Bees **play a vital role in** the pollination of many of the world's crops.                        |
| **To adapt to a changing environment**            | Thích nghi với một môi trường đang thay đổi                 | To evolve or change one's behavior in order to survive in a new or altered environment.                       | Animals that cannot **adapt to a changing environment** are at a high risk of extinction.          |
| **Natural selection and survival of the fittest** | Chọn lọc tự nhiên và sự sống còn của kẻ thích nghi tốt nhất | The process whereby organisms better adapted to their environment tend to survive and produce more offspring. | **Natural selection** is the driving force of evolution.                                           |
| **The natural life cycle**                        | Vòng đời tự nhiên                                           | The series of changes in the life of an organism including reproduction.                                      | The decomposition of organic matter is a crucial part of **the natural life cycle**.               |
| **To maintain the delicate balance of nature**    | Duy trì sự cân bằng mỏng manh của tự nhiên                  | To keep the natural world in a stable and harmonious state.                                                   | Predators help **to maintain the delicate balance of nature** by controlling prey populations.     |
| **A thriving and resilient ecosystem**            | Một hệ sinh thái phát triển mạnh và có sức bật              | A healthy, successful, and robust ecosystem that can withstand or recover from disturbances.                  | The restored river is now home to a **thriving and resilient ecosystem**.                          |
| **Interdependence between species**               | Sự phụ thuộc lẫn nhau giữa các loài                         | The state where different species in an ecosystem rely on each other for survival.                            | The concept of the food web highlights the **interdependence between species**.                    |
| **The ripple effect**                             | Hiệu ứng gợn sóng (lan tỏa)                                 | A situation in which one event causes a series of other events to happen.                                     | The extinction of a keystone species can have a **ripple effect** throughout the entire ecosystem. |
| **To be an indicator of ecosystem health**        | Là một chỉ báo về sức khỏe của hệ sinh thái                 | To be a species whose presence, absence, or abundance reflects a specific environmental condition.            | The population of frogs is a good **indicator of ecosystem health**.                               |
| **To go extinct**                                 | Bị tuyệt chủng                                              | To cease to exist as a species.                                                                               | Dinosaurs **went extinct** about 65 million years ago.                                             |
