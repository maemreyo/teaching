| **Mechanized farming** | Canh tác cơ giới hóa | Farming that uses machines like tractors and combine harvesters. | **Mechanized farming** has dramatically increased agricultural productivity. |
| **Organic farming** | Nông nghiệp hữu cơ | Farming without the use of synthetic chemicals like pesticides and fertilizers. | **Organic farming** is often better for the environment but can have lower yields. |

### 🌱 **Cây trồng & Đất đai (Crops & Soil)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Genetically modified (GM) crops** | Cây trồng biến đổi gen | Crops whose genes have been scientifically changed to give them new traits. | **Genetically modified crops** can be engineered to be resistant to drought or pests. |
| **A high-yield variety** | Một giống cây trồng năng suất cao | A type of crop that has been bred to produce a large amount. | The Green Revolution introduced **high-yield varieties** of wheat and rice. |
| **Climate-resilient / Drought-resistant crops** | Cây trồng chống chịu khí hậu / chịu hạn | Crops that can survive and produce in changing or dry climate conditions. | Scientists are developing **drought-resistant crops** to ensure food security. |
| **Soil fertility management** | Quản lý độ phì nhiêu của đất | The practice of keeping soil healthy and full of nutrients for growing crops. | Good **soil fertility management** involves using compost and practicing crop rotation. |
| **Soil erosion** | Xói mòn đất | The washing or blowing away of the top layer of soil by water or wind. | Deforestation can lead to severe **soil erosion**. |
| **Land degradation** | Suy thoái đất | The process by which the quality of land becomes worse and less productive. | Overgrazing is a major cause of **land degradation** in arid regions. |
| **Pest management** | Quản lý dịch hại | The control of pests like insects, weeds, and plant diseases. | Integrated **pest management** combines various techniques to control pests sustainably. |
| **Biological control** | Kiểm soát sinh học | Using natural predators or parasites to control pests instead of chemicals. | Introducing ladybugs to eat aphids is a form of **biological control**. |
| **Excessive use of chemical fertilizers** | Sử dụng phân bón hóa học quá mức | Using too much chemical fertilizer, which can harm the environment. | The **excessive use of chemical fertilizers** can pollute rivers and groundwater. |
| **Pesticide residues** | Dư lượng thuốc trừ sâu | Small amounts of pesticide chemicals left on or in crops after they are harvested. | Health regulations set strict limits on acceptable **pesticide residues** in food. |

### 🚚 **An ninh Lương thực & Chuỗi Cung ứng (Food Security & The Supply Chain)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **To ensure food security** | Đảm bảo an ninh lương thực | To make sure that a population has enough safe and nutritious food to eat. | The government's primary goal is **to ensure food security** for its citizens. |
| **To feed the growing population** | Nuôi sống dân số ngày càng tăng | To produce enough food for an increasing number of people in the world. | A major challenge for the 21st century is how **to feed the growing global population**. |
| **Post-harvest losses** | Tổn thất sau thu hoạch | The loss of crops that occurs after they have been harvested, during storage or transport. | Improving storage facilities can significantly reduce **post-harvest losses**. |
| **Cold chain logistics** | Chuỗi cung ứng lạnh | The system for keeping perishable food cold during transport from farm to consumer. | Effective **cold chain logistics** are essential for the safe distribution of fresh produce. |
| **To promote local produce** | Khuyến khích dùng nông sản địa phương | To encourage people to buy and consume food that is grown in their local area. | Farmers' markets are a great way **to promote local produce**. |
| **To support rural livelihoods** | Hỗ trợ sinh kế nông thôn | To help people in the countryside earn a living, often through agriculture. | Fair trade initiatives aim **to support rural livelihoods** in developing countries. |
| **Value-added products** | Các sản phẩm giá trị gia tăng | Products that have been processed to increase their market value, like turning fruit into jam. | Farmers can increase their income by creating **value-added products**. |
| **Water scarcity** | Sự khan hiếm nước | A lack of sufficient water resources to meet the demands of a region. | **Water scarcity** is a major constraint on agricultural production in many countries. |
| **Precision agriculture / irrigation** | Nông nghiệp / Tưới tiêu chính xác | Using technology like GPS and sensors to make farming and water use more efficient. | **Precision agriculture** can help farmers reduce their environmental impact. |
| **To invest in agricultural research** | Đầu tư vào nghiên cứu nông nghiệp | To put money into the scientific study of farming to develop new technologies and methods. | Governments must **invest in agricultural research** to address future food challenges. |
