### 🎯 **Mục tiêu & Mục đích (Aims & Goals)**

| Collocation                                             | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                           | Short English Meaning                                                                                | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                    |
| :------------------------------------------------------ | :----------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **To preserve and protect biodiversity**                | Gìn giữ và bảo vệ sự đa dạng sinh học      | To safeguard the variety of plant and animal life on Earth.                                          | The primary goal of national parks is **to preserve and protect biodiversity**.     |
| **To conserve finite natural resources**                | Bảo tồn các tài nguyên thiên nhiên hữu hạn | To protect and use limited natural resources wisely to ensure their future availability.             | We must **conserve finite natural resources** like fresh water and fossil fuels.    |
| **To maintain ecological balance**                      | Duy trì cân bằng sinh thái                 | To keep the natural world in a stable and harmonious state.                                          | Protecting keystone species is essential **to maintain ecological balance**.        |
| **To prevent further environmental degradation**        | Ngăn chặn sự suy thoái môi trường thêm nữa | To stop the natural environment from becoming worse.                                                 | Immediate action is needed **to prevent further environmental degradation**.        |
| **To restore degraded ecosystems**                      | Phục hồi các hệ sinh thái bị suy thoái     | To repair damaged natural environments and help them recover.                                        | The project focuses on **restoring degraded mangrove ecosystems** along the coast.  |
| **To achieve sustainable development**                  | Đạt được sự phát triển bền vững            | To achieve economic and social development that meets present needs without compromising the future. | The challenge is **to achieve sustainable development** on a global scale.          |
| **To safeguard the environment for future generations** | Bảo vệ môi trường cho các thế hệ tương lai | To protect the natural world so that it will be healthy for the people who live after us.            | We have a moral obligation **to safeguard the environment for future generations**. |
| **To mitigate the effects of climate change**           | Giảm nhẹ các tác động của biến đổi khí hậu | To make the harmful effects of climate change less severe.                                           | A key goal is **to mitigate the effects of climate change** by reducing emissions.  |
| **To foster a sense of environmental stewardship**      | Nuôi dưỡng tinh thần quản lý môi trường    | To encourage a sense of responsibility for taking care of the natural world.                         | Education should **foster a sense of environmental stewardship** in young people.   |
| **To live in harmony with nature**                      | Sống hòa hợp với thiên nhiên               | To live in a way that does not damage the natural world.                                             | The ultimate goal is for humanity **to live in harmony with nature**.               |

### 📜 **Chính sách & Quy định (Policies & Regulations)**

| Collocation                                     | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                                            | Short English Meaning                                                                                    | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                        |
| :---------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Robust environmental protection laws**        | Các luật bảo vệ môi trường mạnh mẽ                          | Strong and effective laws designed to protect the natural world.                                         | **Robust environmental protection laws** are crucial for holding polluters accountable.                 |
| **To impose stricter regulations on polluters** | Áp đặt các quy định nghiêm ngặt hơn lên các bên gây ô nhiễm | To enforce tougher rules on companies and industries that pollute.                                       | The government should **impose stricter regulations on polluters**.                                     |
| **To strictly enforce environmental laws**      | Thực thi nghiêm ngặt luật môi trường                        | To ensure that environmental laws are obeyed without exception.                                          | The challenge lies not in creating new laws, but in **strictly enforcing existing environmental laws**. |
| **A binding international agreement**           | Một thỏa thuận quốc tế có tính ràng buộc pháp lý            | A formal treaty between countries that has legal force and must be obeyed.                               | The Paris Agreement is a landmark **international agreement** on climate change.                        |
| **To phase out / ban the use of...**            | Loại bỏ dần / cấm việc sử dụng...                           | To gradually stop using or to officially forbid the use of something.                                    | Several countries have **banned the use of** single-use plastic bags.                                   |
| **To introduce a carbon tax**                   | Đưa ra thuế carbon                                          | To implement a tax on the emission of carbon dioxide.                                                    | **Introducing a carbon tax** can create a financial incentive for companies to reduce their emissions.  |
| **To provide subsidies for green technology**   | Cung cấp trợ cấp cho công nghệ xanh                         | To give government money to support the development and adoption of environmentally friendly technology. | The government **provides subsidies for** households that install solar panels.                         |
| **Government intervention**                     | Sự can thiệp của chính phủ                                  | The involvement of the government to influence a situation, especially the economy.                      | In many cases, **government intervention** is necessary to solve large-scale environmental problems.    |
| **Environmental impact assessments**            | Đánh giá tác động môi trường                                | A formal process used to predict the environmental consequences of a proposed project.                   | All new construction projects must undergo a thorough **environmental impact assessment**.              |
| **The polluter pays principle**                 | Nguyên tắc "bên gây ô nhiễm trả tiền"                       | The principle that those who produce pollution should bear the costs of managing it.                     | The new law is based on **the polluter pays principle**.                                                |
