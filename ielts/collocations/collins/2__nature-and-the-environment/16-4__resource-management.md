### 💎 **Các loại Tài nguyên (Types of Resources)**

| Collocation                          | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                      | Short English Meaning                                                                                                              | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                             |
| :----------------------------------- | :------------------------------------ | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Natural resources**                | Tài nguyên thiên nhiên                | Materials or substances such as minerals, forests, water, and fertile land that occur in nature and can be used for economic gain. | A country's economic wealth often depends on its abundance of **natural resources**.                         |
| **Finite versus infinite resources** | Tài nguyên hữu hạn và vô hạn          | The contrast between resources that are limited in quantity and those that are not.                                                | We must remember that fossil fuels are **finite resources**, while solar energy is effectively **infinite**. |
| **Renewable resources**              | Tài nguyên tái tạo                    | Resources that can be replenished naturally over time, such as wind, solar, and timber.                                            | **Renewable resources** are at the heart of a sustainable energy strategy.                                   |
| **Non-renewable resources**          | Tài nguyên không tái tạo              | Resources that cannot be readily replaced by natural means at a pace quick enough to keep up with consumption.                     | Our society's dependence on **non-renewable resources** like oil and coal is unsustainable.                  |
| **Raw materials**                    | Nguyên liệu thô                       | The basic, unprocessed material from which a product is made.                                                                      | The factory imports **raw materials** like iron ore and rubber.                                              |
| **Mineral resources**                | Tài nguyên khoáng sản                 | Naturally occurring minerals in the Earth's crust that can be extracted, such as copper and diamonds.                              | The country is rich in **mineral resources**.                                                                |
| **Water resources**                  | Nguồn tài nguyên nước                 | The natural sources of water that are potentially useful, such as rivers, lakes, and groundwater.                                  | The effective management of **water resources** is crucial in arid regions.                                  |
| **Fossil fuels**                     | Nhiên liệu hóa thạch                  | Natural fuels such as coal or gas, formed in the geological past from the remains of living organisms.                             | The burning of **fossil fuels** is the primary driver of climate change.                                     |
| **Strategic natural resources**      | Các tài nguyên thiên nhiên chiến lược | Resources that are vital for a country's economy and national security.                                                            | Oil and rare earth metals are considered **strategic natural resources**.                                    |
| **The Earth's natural capital**      | Vốn tự nhiên của Trái Đất             | The world's stock of natural assets, including geology, soil, air, water, and all living things.                                   | We are depleting **the Earth's natural capital** at an unsustainable rate.                                   |

### 📉 **Sử dụng & Khai thác (Use & Exploitation)**

| Collocation                                  | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                   | Short English Meaning                                                                                | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                              |
| :------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **The depletion of natural resources**       | Sự cạn kiệt tài nguyên thiên nhiên | The severe reduction in the quantity of natural resources due to consumption.                        | **The depletion of natural resources** is a direct consequence of overconsumption and population growth.      |
| **To exploit natural resources**             | Khai thác tài nguyên thiên nhiên   | To make use of natural resources, often for commercial gain.                                         | It is unethical **to exploit natural resources** without considering the long-term environmental impact.      |
| **The unsustainable exploitation of...**     | Sự khai thác không bền vững...     | The use of resources in a way that cannot be maintained and will cause long-term damage.             | **The unsustainable exploitation of** forests has led to widespread deforestation.                            |
| **Resource management**                      | Quản lý tài nguyên                 | The process of planning and controlling the use of resources to ensure their long-term availability. | Proper **resource management** is key to achieving sustainable development.                                   |
| **To consume resources at an alarming rate** | Tiêu thụ tài nguyên ở mức báo động | To use up resources very quickly in a way that causes great concern.                                 | The modern world is **consuming resources at an alarming rate**.                                              |
| **The over-exploitation of...**              | Sự khai thác quá mức...            | The act of using a resource to an excessive degree, leading to its depletion.                        | **The over-exploitation of** fish stocks has led to the collapse of some marine ecosystems.                   |
| **Resource scarcity**                        | Sự khan hiếm tài nguyên            | A situation where the demand for a resource is greater than the available supply.                    | **Resource scarcity** can lead to economic instability and geopolitical conflict.                             |
| **To put an immense strain on resources**    | Gây áp lực khổng lồ lên tài nguyên | To place a very heavy burden on the available supply of resources.                                   | A rapidly growing global population will **put an immense strain on** the world's resources.                  |
| **The extraction of raw materials**          | Việc khai thác nguyên liệu thô     | The process of removing natural materials like minerals, oil, and timber from the Earth.             | **The extraction of raw materials** often involves destructive practices like mining and drilling.            |
| **A resource curse**                         | Lời nguyền tài nguyên              | A paradox where countries with an abundance of natural resources tend to have less economic growth.  | Some developing nations suffer from the **"resource curse"** due to corruption and a lack of diversification. |

### 🌱 **Sự Bền vững & Các Hoạt động Bền vững (Sustainability & Sustainable Practices)**

| Collocation                                          | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                            | Short English Meaning                                                                                                           | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                              |
| :--------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------ | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Sustainable development**                          | Phát triển bền vững                         | Development that meets the needs of the present without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs. | The 17 UN Goals are a global framework for **sustainable development**.                                       |
| **Sustainable agriculture and forestry**             | Nông nghiệp và lâm nghiệp bền vững          | Farming and forestry practices that are environmentally sound, socially just, and economically viable.                          | **Sustainable agriculture** aims to produce food while protecting soil health and biodiversity.               |
| **To live a sustainable lifestyle**                  | Sống một lối sống bền vững                  | To live in a way that minimizes one's negative impact on the environment.                                                       | **Living a sustainable lifestyle** involves reducing consumption, conserving resources, and minimizing waste. |
| **To adopt sustainable business practices**          | Áp dụng các phương pháp kinh doanh bền vững | For companies to start using methods in their operations that are environmentally and socially responsible.                     | More corporations are beginning **to adopt sustainable business practices**.                                  |
| **A circular economy**                               | Nền kinh tế tuần hoàn                       | An economic model that aims to eliminate waste and the continual use of resources by reusing, repairing, and recycling.         | In a **circular economy**, products are designed to be reused, repaired, or recycled.                         |
| **Corporate social responsibility (CSR)**            | Trách nhiệm xã hội của doanh nghiệp         | A business model that helps a company be socially accountable to itself, its stakeholders, and the public.                      | **Corporate social responsibility** includes minimizing a company's environmental footprint.                  |
| **Environmentally friendly / eco-friendly products** | Các sản phẩm thân thiện với môi trường      | Products that are designed to have a minimal harmful impact on the environment.                                                 | There is a growing consumer demand for **environmentally friendly products**.                                 |
| **Resource conservation**                            | Bảo tồn tài nguyên                          | The protection and careful management of natural resources.                                                                     | **Resource conservation** is essential for ensuring that future generations have the resources they need.     |
| **The principles of sustainability**                 | Các nguyên tắc của sự bền vững              | The core ideas of sustainability, often summarized as meeting environmental, social, and economic needs.                        | Our company's strategy is guided by **the principles of sustainability**.                                     |
| **To achieve long-term sustainability**              | Đạt được sự bền vững lâu dài                | To create a state that can be maintained for a long time without depleting resources.                                           | The ultimate goal is **to achieve long-term sustainability**.                                                 |

### ⚡ **Năng lượng & Tiêu dùng (Energy & Consumption)**

| Collocation                               | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                           | Short English Meaning                                                                                       | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                |
| :---------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Global energy consumption**             | Mức tiêu thụ năng lượng toàn cầu           | The total amount of energy used by the entire world.                                                        | **Global energy consumption** has been rising steadily for the past century.                    |
| **To conserve energy**                    | Tiết kiệm, bảo tồn năng lượng              | To reduce the consumption of energy by using it efficiently or not at all.                                  | Simple actions like turning off lights can help **to conserve energy**.                         |
| **Energy efficiency**                     | Hiệu quả năng lượng                        | The use of less energy to perform the same task or produce the same result.                                 | Improving the **energy efficiency** of buildings is a key climate goal.                         |
| **To be heavily reliant on fossil fuels** | Phụ thuộc nặng nề vào nhiên liệu hóa thạch | To depend almost entirely on coal, oil, and gas for energy.                                                 | Many national economies are still **heavily reliant on fossil fuels**.                          |
| **A consumerist society**                 | Một xã hội tiêu dùng                       | A society where a high value is placed on buying and owning material goods.                                 | We live in a **consumerist society** that encourages constant acquisition.                      |
| **A throw-away culture**                  | Văn hóa "dùng một lần rồi vứt"             | A society that is characterized by the excessive consumption and disposal of single-use items.              | Our **throw-away culture** is responsible for the massive amount of landfill waste.             |
| **Sustainable consumption**               | Tiêu dùng bền vững                         | The use of products and services in a way that minimizes the impact on the environment.                     | **Sustainable consumption** encourages people to buy less but choose products that last longer. |
| **The transition to renewable energy**    | Sự chuyển đổi sang năng lượng tái tạo      | The shift from using fossil fuels to using renewable energy sources like solar and wind.                    | **The transition to renewable energy** is essential for combating climate change.               |
| **Energy security**                       | An ninh năng lượng                         | The association between national security and the availability of natural resources for energy consumption. | **Energy security** has become a major geopolitical issue.                                      |
| **Unsustainable consumption patterns**    | Các mô hình tiêu dùng không bền vững       | Ways of using resources that cannot be continued in the long term without causing environmental harm.       | We must change our **unsustainable consumption patterns** to protect the planet.                |
