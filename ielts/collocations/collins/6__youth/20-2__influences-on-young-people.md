### 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 **Ảnh hưởng từ Gia đình (Family Influence)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Parental guidance and influence** | Sự hướng dẫn và ảnh hưởng của cha mẹ | The advice, support, and effect that parents have on their children's development. | **Parental guidance and influence** are paramount during a child's formative years. |
| **A positive / negative role model** | Một hình mẫu tích cực / tiêu cực | A person whose behavior is a good or bad example for others to emulate. | Parents should strive to be **positive role models** for their children. |
| **A stable and nurturing home environment** | Một môi trường gia đình ổn định và nuôi dưỡng | A secure, supportive, and caring home life that fosters healthy development. | A **stable and nurturing home environment** is crucial for a child's emotional well-being. |
| **To instill moral values** | Truyền thụ, thấm nhuần các giá trị đạo đức | To teach and embed fundamental principles of right and wrong. | It is a primary role of parents **to instill moral values** in their children. |
| **To shape a child's worldview** | Định hình thế giới quan của trẻ | To have a strong influence on how a child perceives and interprets the world. | The beliefs and attitudes of parents can significantly **shape a child's worldview**. |
| **Upbringing and formative years** | Sự nuôi dưỡng và những năm tháng định hình | The way a child is cared for and the critical early period of their development. | A person's character is heavily influenced by their **upbringing and formative years**. |
| **A strong family bond** | Một sự gắn kết gia đình bền chặt | A close, supportive, and loving relationship between family members. | Children who have a **strong family bond** may be more resilient to negative peer pressure. |
| **Intergenerational transmission of values** | Sự chuyển giao giá trị giữa các thế hệ | The process by which values and beliefs are passed down from one generation to the next. | The **intergenerational transmission of values** is a key function of the family unit. |
| **To follow in one's parents' footsteps** | Nối nghiệp, theo gương cha mẹ | To choose the same career or lifestyle as one's parents. | Many young people feel an implicit pressure **to follow in their parents' footsteps**. |
| **Parenting styles** | Các phong cách nuôi dạy con cái | The different strategies and approaches that parents use in their child-rearing. | Research shows that different **parenting styles** can have a profound impact on a child's development. |

### 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 **Ảnh hưởng từ Bạn bè (Peer Influence)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Peer pressure** | Áp lực từ bạn bè đồng trang lứa | The social influence a peer group exerts on its individual members to conform. | **Peer pressure** can be a powerful force, influencing both positive and negative behaviors. |
| **The desire to conform to group norms** | Mong muốn tuân theo các chuẩn mực của nhóm | The strong wish to behave in a way that is considered normal and acceptable by one's peer group. | **The desire to conform to group norms** is a natural part of adolescent development. |
| **To fit in with one's peers** | Hòa nhập với bạn bè | To be accepted and seen as a member of a social group. | Some young people may engage in risky behavior simply **to fit in with their peers**. |
| **To be susceptible to peer influence** | Dễ bị ảnh hưởng bởi bạn bè | To be easily affected or influenced by the actions and attitudes of one's friends. | Teenagers are often highly **susceptible to peer influence**. |
| **To seek approval from one's peer group** | Tìm kiếm sự chấp thuận từ nhóm bạn bè | To want acceptance and validation from one's friends. | Young people often **seek approval from their peer group** as they form their identity. |
| **Social acceptance** | Sự chấp nhận của xã hội | The fact of being accepted by a group of people. | The need for **social acceptance** is a powerful motivator during adolescence. |
| **To fall in with the wrong crowd** | Giao du với nhóm bạn xấu | To begin to associate with people who are a negative influence. | Without positive guidance, some teenagers may **fall in with the wrong crowd**. |
| **The behavior of their peers** | Hành vi của bạn bè đồng trang lứa | The way that friends of the same age act. | Adolescents are often more influenced by **the behavior of their peers** than by parental advice. |
| **Peer dynamics** | Động lực nhóm bạn bè | The patterns of interaction and social relationships within a peer group. | Understanding **peer dynamics** is key to addressing issues like bullying. |
| **A sense of belonging** | Cảm giác thân thuộc, thuộc về một nơi nào đó | The feeling of being happy and comfortable as part of a group. | Joining a sports team can provide young people with a valuable **sense of belonging**. |

### 🤳 **Ảnh hưởng từ Truyền thông & Công nghệ (Media & Technology Influence)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The pervasive influence of social media** | Ảnh hưởng sâu rộng, lan tỏa của mạng xã hội | The widespread and often inescapable effect that social media has on society. | **The pervasive influence of social media** on youth culture is a topic of much debate. |
| **Exposure to media content** | Sự tiếp xúc với nội dung truyền thông | The act of seeing, hearing, or experiencing content from various media sources. | Children today have unprecedented **exposure to media content** from a very young age. |
| **Celebrity culture and idolization** | Văn hóa người nổi tiếng và sự thần tượng hóa | The societal focus on famous people and the act of admiring them excessively. | **Celebrity culture and idolization** can promote unrealistic lifestyle and beauty standards. |
| **The portrayal of... in the media** | Sự khắc họa... trên truyền thông | The way that a particular subject or group is represented in films, TV, and news. | **The portrayal of** violence **in the media** can have a desensitizing effect on young viewers. |
| **A consumer culture** | Văn hóa tiêu dùng | A culture that encourages the acquisition of goods and services in ever-increasing amounts. | Advertising plays a major role in promoting a **consumer culture** among the youth. |
| **To be bombarded with advertisements** | Bị "dội bom" bởi quảng cáo | To be subjected to a very large number of advertisements constantly. | Young people **are constantly bombarded with advertisements** that shape their desires. |
| **To shape perceptions and attitudes** | Định hình nhận thức và thái độ | To have a strong influence on how people see the world and what they believe. | The media can **shape young people's perceptions and attitudes** towards success and happiness. |
| **The cultivation of artificial needs** | Sự vun trồng những nhu cầu nhân tạo | The process by which advertising makes people feel they need things that they don't. | Marketing often involves **the cultivation of artificial needs** to drive sales. |
| **The echo chamber effect** | Hiệu ứng buồng vang | An environment where a person only encounters information that reinforces their existing beliefs. | **The echo chamber effect** on social media can limit young people's exposure to diverse viewpoints. |
| **The normalization of certain behaviors** | Sự bình thường hóa các hành vi nhất định | The process through which a previously unacceptable behavior becomes seen as normal. | The media can contribute to **the normalization of certain behaviors**, both positive and negative. |

### 🏫 **Ảnh hưởng từ Trường học & Xã hội (School & Societal Influence)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The school environment and culture** | Môi trường và văn hóa học đường | The overall atmosphere, values, and practices within a school. | A positive **school environment and culture** can have a significant impact on a student's development. |
| **A formative influence** | Một sự ảnh hưởng có tính định hình | An influence that plays a major role in shaping someone's character and development. | A great teacher can be a powerful **formative influence** in a young person's life. |
| **Societal norms and values** | Các chuẩn mực và giá trị xã hội | The unwritten rules of behavior and the shared beliefs of a society. | Through education and socialization, children learn the **societal norms and values** of their community. |
| **Cultural assimilation** | Sự đồng hóa văn hóa | The process by which a person or group's language and/or culture come to resemble those of another group. | Immigrant children often face the challenge of **cultural assimilation** in a new country. |
| **To absorb the values of society** | Hấp thụ các giá trị của xã hội | To learn and internalize the beliefs and principles of the surrounding community. | Children naturally **absorb the values of the society** in which they are raised. |
| **A competitive academic culture** | Một văn hóa học tập cạnh tranh | A school environment where there is a strong emphasis on outperforming other students. | A highly **competitive academic culture** can put immense pressure on students. |
| **To broaden one's horizons** | Mở rộng tầm hiểu biết | To expand one's range of knowledge, experience, and interests. | Education and travel can help young people **to broaden their horizons**. |
| **The hidden curriculum** | Chương trình giảng dạy ẩn | The unstated lessons, values, and perspectives that students learn in school. | The **hidden curriculum** teaches students about social hierarchies and norms. |
| **Civic education** | Giáo dục công dân | The study of the rights and responsibilities of citizens. | **Civic education** aims to prepare young people to be active and responsible members of society. |
| **To be shaped by one's cultural background** | Được định hình bởi nền tảng văn hóa | To be influenced by the traditions, values, and customs of the culture one grew up in. | Our beliefs and behaviors **are largely shaped by our cultural background**. |
