### 😟 **Áp lực Học tập & Xã hội (Academic & Social Pressures)**

| Collocation                                    | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                             | Short English Meaning                                                                                   | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                  |
| :--------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Intense academic pressure**                  | Áp lực học tập nặng nề                       | Strong and constant pressure to achieve high grades and succeed in school.                              | Many students suffer from **intense academic pressure** from a very young age.                    |
| **The pressure to excel academically**         | Áp lực phải học tập xuất sắc                 | The strong expectation from oneself or others to perform exceptionally well in academics.               | **The pressure to excel academically** can lead to anxiety and a fear of failure.                 |
| **The burden of parental expectations**        | Gánh nặng của sự kỳ vọng từ cha mẹ           | The heavy emotional weight of trying to meet the goals and ambitions parents have for their children.   | **The burden of parental expectations** can be a significant source of stress for teenagers.      |
| **Peer pressure**                              | Áp lực từ bạn bè đồng trang lứa              | The influence from members of one's peer group to conform to their attitudes or behaviors.              | **Peer pressure** can influence everything from fashion choices to engaging in risky behaviors.   |
| **To conform to unrealistic beauty standards** | Tuân theo các tiêu chuẩn sắc đẹp phi thực tế | To try to match an ideal of physical attractiveness that is often unattainable and promoted by media.   | Social media often pressures young people **to conform to unrealistic beauty standards**.         |
| **A culture of hyper-competition**             | Một nền văn hóa cạnh tranh khốc liệt         | A social environment where there is an extreme and excessive level of competition in all areas of life. | A **culture of hyper-competition** in schools can be detrimental to students' mental health.      |
| **Social comparison**                          | So sánh xã hội                               | The tendency to compare oneself with others, often leading to feelings of inadequacy.                   | Social media platforms can amplify feelings of **social comparison** and envy among young people. |
| **Fear of failure**                            | Nỗi sợ thất bại                              | An intense fear of not succeeding, which can lead to avoidance of challenges.                           | The **fear of failure** can prevent young people from taking risks and exploring their potential. |
| **The pursuit of perfectionism**               | Sự theo đuổi chủ nghĩa hoàn hảo              | The constant striving for flawlessness and setting excessively high performance standards.              | **The pursuit of perfectionism** is often a source of chronic stress and dissatisfaction.         |
| **To live up to expectations**                 | Đáp ứng được kỳ vọng                         | To be as good as people hope or expect.                                                                 | Many children feel a constant pressure **to live up to their parents' expectations**.             |

### ❤️ **Sức khỏe & An sinh (Health & Well-being)**

| **Emotional well-being**                       | Sức khỏe tinh thần và cảm xúc                                   | The state of being emotionally healthy, resilient, and content.                                    | A supportive family environment is crucial for a child's **emotional well-being**.                      |
| **To cope with stress**                        | Đối phó với căng thẳng                                          | To deal effectively with difficult or stressful situations.                                        | Schools should teach students healthy strategies **to cope with stress**.                               |
| **A sedentary lifestyle**                      | Lối sống thụ động, ít vận động                                  | A lifestyle characterized by a lot of sitting and very little physical activity.                   | The rise of digital entertainment has contributed to a more **sedentary lifestyle** among children.     |
| **Sleep deprivation**                          | Tình trạng thiếu ngủ                                            | A chronic lack of sufficient sleep.                                                                | **Sleep deprivation** is a common problem for students, affecting their concentration and mood.         |
| **Poor dietary habits / rising obesity rates** | Thói quen ăn uống kém lành mạnh / tỷ lệ béo phì gia tăng        | Unhealthy eating patterns and the resulting increase in obesity.                                   | The prevalence of processed foods has led to **poor dietary habits** and **rising obesity rates**.      |

| **Cyberbullying**                              | Bắt nạt trên mạng                                               | The use of electronic communication to bully a person, typically by sending intimidating messages. | **Cyberbullying** can have a devastating impact on a young person's self-esteem.                        |
| **Digital addiction / excessive screen time**  | Nghiện kỹ thuật số / thời gian sử dụng thiết bị điện tử quá mức | The compulsive use of digital devices, which interferes with daily life.                           | **Excessive screen time** is linked to a range of problems, from sleep deprivation to social isolation. |
| **A lack of self-esteem**                      | Sự thiếu lòng tự trọng                                          | A lack of confidence in one's own worth or abilities.                                              | Negative feedback and social comparison can lead to a severe **lack of self-esteem**.                   |

| **Substance abuse**                    | Lạm dụng chất gây nghiện                   | The harmful or hazardous use of psychoactive substances, including alcohol and illicit drugs.  | **Substance abuse** among teenagers is a serious public health concern.                            |
| **Antisocial behavior**                | Hành vi chống đối xã hội                   | Behavior that lacks consideration for others and may cause damage to the community.            | Vandalism and public nuisance are examples of **antisocial behavior**.                             |
| **Juvenile delinquency**               | Tội phạm vị thành niên                     | The participation of minors in illegal behavior or activities.                                 | A lack of opportunities is often cited as a root cause of **juvenile delinquency**.                |
| **Negative peer influence**            | Ảnh hưởng tiêu cực từ bạn bè               | The effect of one's peers encouraging them to act in a way that is harmful or not responsible. | It is important for parents to be aware of **negative peer influence**.                            |
| **The generation gap**                 | Khoảng cách thế hệ                         | A difference in attitudes and beliefs between one generation and another.                      | **The generation gap** can sometimes lead to misunderstandings between parents and their children. |

| **A lack of parental guidance**        | Sự thiếu hướng dẫn của cha mẹ              | Insufficient direction, support, and supervision from parents.                                 | Some behavioral problems in children can be linked to a **lack of parental guidance**.             |
| **The erosion of traditional values**  | Sự xói mòn các giá trị truyền thống        | The gradual destruction or weakening of long-held cultural or moral beliefs.                   | Some commentators worry about **the erosion of traditional values** in modern society.             |

| **Lack of affordable housing**                          | Sự thiếu hụt nhà ở giá cả phải chăng                              | A situation where there is not enough housing that is affordable for young people or low-income earners.            | The **lack of affordable housing** makes it difficult for young adults to achieve independence.    |
