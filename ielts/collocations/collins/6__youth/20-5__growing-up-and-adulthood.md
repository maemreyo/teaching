| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **To come of age** | Đến tuổi trưởng thành | To reach the age when you are legally considered an adult. | The novel is a classic coming-of-age story about a young girl in the 1960s. |
| **The formative years** | Những năm tháng định hình nhân cách | The early years of a person's life when their character is still forming. | The experiences we have during our **formative years** can have a lifelong impact. |

| **To juggle multiple responsibilities** | Tung hứng nhiều trách nhiệm cùng lúc | To try to manage and balance several different important roles or tasks at once. | Many working parents have **to juggle** the **responsibilities** of their job and their family. |
