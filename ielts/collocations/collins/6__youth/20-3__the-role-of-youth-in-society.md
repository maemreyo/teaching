| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **To shape the future** | Định hình tương lai | To have a decisive influence on how the future develops. | The decisions and actions of today's youth will **shape the future** of our planet. |

| **To challenge the status quo** | Thách thức hiện trạng | To question and actively oppose the existing state of affairs. | It is often the role of the younger generation **to challenge the status quo** and push for reform. |
| **To have a powerful voice** | Có một tiếng nói mạnh mẽ | To have a significant ability to influence public opinion and policy. | Through social media, young activists **have a powerful voice** to raise global awareness. |
| **To drive social progress** | Thúc đẩy tiến bộ xã hội | To be a major force in causing society to improve and advance. | Youth movements have historically been crucial in **driving social progress**. |

| **To question established norms** | Chất vấn các chuẩn mực đã được thiết lập | To express doubts about and challenge long-accepted social rules and traditions. | A healthy society allows its youth **to question established norms**. |
| **Inherent creativity and innovation** | Sự sáng tạo và đổi mới vốn có | The natural ability of young people to think of new ideas and solutions. | The **inherent creativity and innovation** of the youth are vital for solving future challenges. |

### 🤝 **Trách nhiệm & Sự Tham gia (Responsibilities & Engagement)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A sense of social responsibility** | Ý thức trách nhiệm xã hội | An ethical framework suggesting that an individual has an obligation to act for the benefit of society at large. | Education should foster a **sense of social responsibility** in young citizens. |
| **Community involvement / engagement** | Sự tham gia vào cộng đồng | The act of participating in and contributing to one's local community. | **Community involvement** is a great way for teenagers to develop empathy and practical skills. |
| **To participate in the political process** | Tham gia vào quá trình chính trị | To take part in political activities, such as voting, campaigning, or running for office. | Lowering the voting age would allow more young people **to participate in the political process**. |
| **Social and political activism** | Hoạt động xã hội và chính trị | The practice of taking direct, often vigorous, action to achieve social or political goals. | Youth **social and political activism** has been a major force in the climate change debate. |
| **To volunteer for a cause** | Tình nguyện vì một mục đích | To offer one's time and labor for a cause or organization one believes in, without payment. | Many students are willing **to volunteer for a cause** they are passionate about. |
| **To make a positive contribution to society** | Đóng góp tích cực cho xã hội | To do something that improves the community or the lives of others. | The program empowers young people **to make a positive contribution to their society**. |
| **Civic engagement / participation** | Sự tham gia của công dân | The involvement of citizens in the life of their community and government. | **Civic engagement** among the youth is a key indicator of a healthy democracy. |
| **To have a say in decision-making** | Có tiếng nói trong việc ra quyết định | To have the right and opportunity to be involved in making a decision that affects you. | Young people feel more valued when they **have a say in decision-making** processes. |
| **To hold leaders accountable** | Yêu cầu các nhà lãnh đạo phải chịu trách nhiệm | To make sure that people in power are responsible for their actions. | The younger generation is increasingly using social media **to hold leaders accountable**. |
| **Grassroots movements** | Các phong trào quần chúng (từ cơ sở) | Movements that use the people in a given district, region, or community as the basis for political or economic action. | Many environmental campaigns are youth-led **grassroots movements**. |

### 🚧 **Thách thức & Rào cản (Challenges & Barriers)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **To be marginalized or excluded** | Bị gạt ra ngoài lề hoặc loại trừ | To be treated as insignificant or to be prevented from participating fully in society. | Without a voice in policy-making, young people can feel **marginalized or excluded**. |
| **A lack of political representation** | Sự thiếu đại diện chính trị | Not having enough people in positions of power to represent the interests of a particular group. | There is a significant **lack of youth political representation** in most parliaments. |
| **To be dismissed as naive or idealistic** | Bị gạt bỏ vì cho là ngây thơ hoặc duy tâm | To have one's ideas rejected because they are seen as overly simple or unrealistic. | Young activists are often **dismissed as naive or idealistic** by established politicians. |
| **To face ageism / age discrimination** | Đối mặt với chủ nghĩa tuổi tác / sự phân biệt đối xử về tuổi tác | To be treated unfairly because of being young. | Young professionals sometimes **face ageism** in the workplace, where their opinions are not valued. |
| **To feel disempowered** | Cảm thấy bất lực, không có quyền hành | To feel that you have no power or control over your own life or circumstances. | A lack of opportunities can leave young people feeling **disempowered** and hopeless. |
| **Barriers to participation** | Các rào cản đối với sự tham gia | Obstacles that prevent people from getting involved in political or community life. | Complicated voting procedures can be **barriers to participation** for young, first-time voters. |
| **Political apathy** | Sự thờ ơ với chính trị | A lack of interest or concern in political affairs. | A feeling of disempowerment can lead to **political apathy** among the youth. |
| **Tokenism** | Chủ nghĩa hình thức (chỉ mời tham gia cho có) | The practice of making only a symbolic effort to be inclusive to members of minority groups. | Inviting one young person to a conference without giving them a real voice is an act of **tokenism**. |
| **A credibility gap** | Một khoảng trống về sự tín nhiệm | A difference between what is promised or said and what is actually true. | There is often a **credibility gap** between what politicians promise young people and what they deliver. |
| **To be disenfranchised** | Bị tước quyền công dân | To be deprived of a right or privilege, especially the right to vote. | Young people can feel **disenfranchised** when they believe the political system does not represent them. |

### ✨ **Kỳ vọng & Hỗ trợ từ Xã hội (Societal Expectations & Support)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **To empower young people** | Trao quyền cho người trẻ | To give young people the authority, resources, and confidence to take control of their own lives. | The goal of the organization is **to empower young people** to become community leaders. |
| **To foster youth engagement** | Thúc đẩy sự tham gia của giới trẻ | To encourage and promote the active involvement of young people in civic life. | We need to create more platforms **to foster youth engagement** in politics. |
| **To invest in the next generation** | Đầu tư vào thế hệ tiếp theo | To allocate money and resources to support the development and well-being of young people. | **Investing in the next generation** through education is the most important investment a country can make. |
| **To listen to the voices of the youth** | Lắng nghe tiếng nói của giới trẻ | To pay serious attention to the opinions, concerns, and ideas of young people. | It is crucial for leaders **to listen to the voices of the youth** when making decisions about the future. |
| **To nurture young talent** | Nuôi dưỡng tài năng trẻ | To help and encourage the development of young people's skills and abilities. | Competitions and mentorship programs are effective ways **to nurture young talent**. |
| **Mentorship programs** | Các chương trình cố vấn | Programs where experienced individuals guide and support less experienced people. | **Mentorship programs** can be invaluable for helping young professionals navigate their careers. |
| **Intergenerational dialogue** | Đối thoại liên thế hệ | Meaningful conversation and exchange of ideas between people of different generations. | **Intergenerational dialogue** can help bridge the understanding gap between young and old. |

| **To value the contribution of...** | Coi trọng sự đóng góp của... | To recognize and appreciate the positive impact that someone has. | A healthy society is one that **values the contribution of** its younger generation. |
