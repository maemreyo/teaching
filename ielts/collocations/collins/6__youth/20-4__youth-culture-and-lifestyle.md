### 🎧 **Xu hướng, Bản sắc & Sự thể hiện (Trends, Identity & Expression)**

| Collocation                        | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                  | Short English Meaning                                                                                            | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                          |
| :--------------------------------- | :-------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Youth culture**                  | Văn hóa giới trẻ                  | The distinct styles, behaviors, and interests that characterize young people as a group.                         | **Youth culture** is often a driving force behind new trends in music, fashion, and language.             |
| **A subculture**                   | Một tiểu văn hóa                  | A cultural group within a larger culture, often with beliefs or interests that differ from the mainstream.       | Punk was a prominent youth **subculture** in the 1980s, with its own distinct anti-establishment ethos.   |
| **To follow the latest trends**    | Chạy theo những xu hướng mới nhất | To adopt the styles, behaviors, or interests that are currently popular.                                         | Many teenagers feel pressure **to follow the latest trends** to gain social acceptance.                   |
| **A form of self-expression**      | Một hình thức thể hiện bản thân   | A way of communicating one's personality, identity, and feelings.                                                | For many young people, their fashion sense is a powerful **form of self-expression**.                     |
| **To express one's individuality** | Thể hiện cá tính cá nhân          | To show one's unique character and be distinct from others.                                                      | Youth culture often encourages people **to express their individuality** rather than conforming to norms. |
| **The formation of identity**      | Sự hình thành bản sắc             | The complex process through which individuals develop a clear and unique view of themselves.                     | Adolescence is a critical period for **the formation of identity**.                                       |
| **Pop culture (Popular culture)**  | Văn hóa đại chúng                 | The set of practices, beliefs, and objects that are dominant or prevalent in a society at a given point in time. | **Pop culture** has a significant influence on the aspirations and lifestyles of the youth.               |
| **Mainstream culture**             | Văn hóa đại trà                   | The cultural patterns that are broadly in line with a society's cultural ideals and values.                      | Some subcultures define themselves by their opposition to **mainstream culture**.                         |
| **A cultural phenomenon**          | Một hiện tượng văn hóa            | An event, trend, or person that becomes very popular and influential in a culture.                               | The global success of K-pop has become a major **cultural phenomenon**.                                   |
| **A rite of passage**              | Một nghi thức trưởng thành        | An important act or event that marks a passage from one stage of life to another.                                | Attending a music festival for the first time is seen as a **rite of passage** for many young people.     |
