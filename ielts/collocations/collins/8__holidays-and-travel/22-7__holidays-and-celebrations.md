### 🎉 **Các loại Kỳ nghỉ & Lễ (Types of Holidays & Celebrations)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A public / national holiday** | Một ngày nghỉ lễ chung / quốc gia | A day of celebration when most people in a country do not have to work. | New Year's Day is a **public holiday** in many countries around the world. |
| **A religious holiday** | Một ngày lễ tôn giáo | A day that has special religious significance. | Christmas and Easter are important **religious holidays** for Christians. |
| **A cultural festival** | Một lễ hội văn hóa | An organized series of events to celebrate the arts and culture of a place. | The city hosts an annual **cultural festival** with music, dance, and food from around the world. |
| **A festive season** | Mùa lễ hội | A period of the year when there are many celebrations, such as around Christmas or New Year. | The city is beautifully decorated during the **festive season**. |
| **A long weekend** | Một kỳ nghỉ cuối tuần dài | A weekend that is extended by one or more days, often due to a public holiday. | We're planning a short trip to the coast for the upcoming **long weekend**. |
| **A family vacation** | Một kỳ nghỉ của gia đình | A holiday that you take with your family. | We have fond memories of our annual **family vacation** to the beach. |
| **A solo trip** | Một chuyến đi một mình | A journey that you take by yourself. | Taking a **solo trip** can be a great way to build confidence and self-reliance. |
| **A staycation** | Một kỳ nghỉ tại nhà | A holiday that you spend in your own country or at home, rather than traveling abroad. | With rising flight costs, many people are opting for a **staycation** this year. |
| **A festive mood / atmosphere** | Một không khí / tâm trạng lễ hội | A cheerful and celebratory feeling or environment. | The whole town was in a **festive mood** during the carnival. |
| **To mark an occasion** | Đánh dấu một dịp | To do something to celebrate an important event. | The family held a large party **to mark the occasion** of her graduation. |

### 🎊 **Truyền thống & Hoạt động (Traditions & Activities)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Time-honored traditions** | Các truyền thống lâu đời | Traditions that have been observed for a very long time and are respected. | The festival is rich in **time-honored traditions** that have been passed down for centuries. |
| **To exchange gifts** | Trao đổi quà tặng | To give presents to one another. | It is customary for family members **to exchange gifts** on Christmas morning. |
| **A festive meal** | Một bữa ăn ngày lễ | A special, celebratory meal eaten during a holiday. | The whole family gathers for a **festive meal** on Thanksgiving Day. |
| **To set off fireworks** | Đốt pháo hoa | To light fireworks so that they explode in the sky. | People around the world **set off fireworks** to celebrate the New Year. |
| **To hold a parade / procession** | Tổ chức một cuộc diễu hành | To have an organized event where people move through the streets in celebration. | The city **holds a massive parade** every year for St. Patrick's Day. |
| **To wear traditional costumes** | Mặc trang phục truyền thống | To wear the special clothes associated with a particular culture or festival. | Dancers **wear colorful traditional costumes** during the carnival. |
| **To pay respect to one's ancestors** | Tỏ lòng thành kính với tổ tiên | To show honor and respect to the members of your family who have died. | During the festival, many people visit graves **to pay respect to their ancestors**. |
| **A sense of community and togetherness** | Một cảm giác cộng đồng và sự sum vầy | A feeling of being connected to others in a group and sharing a common bond. | National holidays can foster a **sense of community and togetherness**. |
| **To take part in the festivities** | Tham gia vào các hoạt động lễ hội | To join in with the organized events and celebrations. | Tourists are welcome **to take part in the local festivities**. |
| **To be steeped in history and tradition** | Đậm đà lịch sử và truyền thống | To be deeply filled with the history and traditions of the past. | The annual ceremony is **steeped in history and tradition**. |
