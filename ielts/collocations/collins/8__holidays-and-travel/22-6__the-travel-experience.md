### ✈️ **Phương tiện & Hành trình (Transport & The Journey)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **To travel by air / rail / sea / road** | Đi bằng đường hàng không / đường sắt / đường biển / đường bộ | To use a particular mode of transport for a journey. | For long distances, it's fastest **to travel by air**. |
| **To take a long-haul / short-haul flight** | Đi một chuyến bay đường dài / ngắn | To take a flight that covers a long or short distance. | I have **to take a long-haul flight** from London to Tokyo next week. |
| **To go on a road trip** | Thực hiện một chuyến đi bằng ô tô | To go on a long journey in a car, usually with friends or family. | This summer, we're planning **to go on a road trip** across the United States. |
| **To use public transportation** | Sử dụng phương tiện giao thông công cộng | To travel using buses, trains, subways, etc., that are available to the public. | In many European cities, it's easy and efficient **to use public transportation**. |
| **To get stuck in a traffic jam** | Bị kẹt xe | To be in a long line of vehicles that are moving very slowly or not at all. | We **got stuck in a massive traffic jam** on the way to the airport. |
| **To suffer from jet lag** | Bị lệch múi giờ | To feel tired and unwell after a long flight across different time zones. | After flying from New York to Sydney, I **suffered from terrible jet lag** for days. |
| **A travel delay / cancellation** | Sự chậm trễ / hủy bỏ chuyến đi | When a flight, train, or other transport is later than planned or is cancelled. | Our flight was cancelled, and the **travel delay** meant we missed our connecting flight. |
| **To live out of a suitcase** | Sống trong va li (liên tục di chuyển) | To live temporarily in different places with only the contents of your suitcase. | As a consultant who travels constantly, I often feel like I'm **living out of a suitcase**. |
| **To travel light** | Du lịch gọn nhẹ | To travel with only a small amount of luggage. | I always try **to travel light** to avoid checking in a bag at the airport. |
| **To hit the road** | Lên đường | To begin a journey. | We plan **to hit the road** early in the morning to avoid the traffic. |

### 🏨 **Chỗ ở & Hoạt động (Accommodation & Activities)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **To check into / out of a hotel** | Nhận / trả phòng khách sạn | To arrive and register at a hotel / to leave and pay for your stay. | We need **to check out of the hotel** by 11 AM at the latest. |
| **A budget / luxury accommodation** | Chỗ ở giá rẻ / sang trọng | A place to stay that is either inexpensive or very expensive and comfortable. | Backpackers often look for **budget accommodation** like hostels. |
| **To go sightseeing / to see the sights** | Đi tham quan, ngắm cảnh | To visit the interesting places, especially as a tourist. | We spent the whole day **going sightseeing** around the historic city center. |
| **A tourist attraction / trap** | Một điểm thu hút / bẫy du lịch | A place that many tourists visit / a place that is expensive and created only for tourists. | The Eiffel Tower is a world-famous **tourist attraction**, but some souvenir shops nearby are just **tourist traps**. |
| **To soak up the local atmosphere / culture** | Đắm mình vào không khí / văn hóa địa phương | To experience and enjoy the character and culture of a place. | I love to sit in a local cafe and just **soak up the local atmosphere**. |
| **To try the local cuisine** | Thử ẩm thực địa phương | To eat the traditional food of the place you are visiting. | One of the best parts of traveling is getting **to try the local cuisine**. |
| **To get off the beaten track** | Đi đến những nơi ít người lui tới | To go to a place where few people go, far from main roads and towns. | We decided **to get off the beaten track** and explore some of the smaller villages. |
| **To buy souvenirs** | Mua quà lưu niệm | To buy things to remind you of a place you visited. | I always **buy souvenirs** for my family when I go on holiday. |
| **To send a postcard** | Gửi một tấm bưu thiếp | To mail a card with a picture on it, often from a holiday destination. | It feels a bit old-fashioned, but I still love **to send postcards** to my grandparents. |
| **To have a thirst for adventure** | Có một niềm khao khát phiêu lưu | To have a strong desire for exciting and unusual experiences. | He **has a thirst for adventure** and loves activities like rock climbing and scuba diving. |

### ⚠️ **Vấn đề & Trải nghiệm (Problems & Experiences)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **To have a travel mishap** | Gặp một sự cố khi đi du lịch | To have a minor accident or piece of bad luck while traveling. | We **had a travel mishap** when our rental car broke down in the middle of nowhere. |
| **To lose one's passport / luggage** | Mất hộ chiếu / hành lý | To have your passport or bags lost or stolen during a trip. | **Losing your passport** while abroad is one of the most stressful travel experiences. |
| **To get food poisoning** | Bị ngộ độc thực phẩm | To become ill after eating contaminated food. | Be careful with street food in some areas, as it's easy **to get food poisoning**. |
| **To fall victim to a scam** | Trở thành nạn nhân của một vụ lừa đảo | To be tricked by a dishonest scheme, especially as a tourist. | Unwary tourists can easily **fall victim to scams** in crowded areas. |
| **The language barrier** | Rào cản ngôn ngữ | The difficulty of communication between people who do not speak the same language. | **The language barrier** made it difficult to ask for directions. |
| **Culture shock** | Sốc văn hóa | The feeling of confusion and disorientation when experiencing an unfamiliar culture. | When I first moved to Japan, I experienced significant **culture shock**. |
| **To feel homesick** | Cảm thấy nhớ nhà | To feel sad because you are away from your home and family. | During his first long trip away, he started **to feel homesick**. |
| **To have a trip of a lifetime** | Có một chuyến đi để đời | To have a very special and memorable trip that you will never forget. | Our safari in Tanzania was truly the **trip of a lifetime**. |
| **To broaden one's horizons** | Mở rộng tầm hiểu biết | To expand your range of knowledge, experience, and interests. | Traveling is one of the best ways **to broaden one's horizons**. |
| **To create lasting memories** | Tạo ra những kỷ niệm khó phai | To form memories that will stay with you for a very long time. | The goal of a family holiday is **to create lasting memories** together. |
