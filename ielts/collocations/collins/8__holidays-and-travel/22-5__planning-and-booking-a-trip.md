### 🎟️ **Đặt chỗ & Sắp xếp (Booking & Arrangements)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **To book a flight / accommodation** | Đặt vé máy bay / chỗ ở | To reserve and pay for a flight or a place to stay. | I need **to book my flight** to New York as soon as possible. |
| **To find a good deal on...** | Tìm được một món hời về... | To find a product or service at a low price. | I managed **to find a good deal on** our hotel by booking in advance. |
| **To compare prices online** | So sánh giá trực tuyến | To check the prices of a service from different providers on the internet. | It's always wise **to compare prices online** before booking a flight. |
| **To make a reservation / booking** | Đặt chỗ trước | To arrange to have a hotel room, a table at a restaurant, etc. saved for you. | I'd like **to make a reservation** for a double room for two nights. |
| **To purchase travel insurance** | Mua bảo hiểm du lịch | To buy a policy that covers you for problems that might occur while traveling. | It is essential **to purchase travel insurance** to cover medical emergencies. |
| **To arrange transportation** | Sắp xếp phương tiện đi lại | To organize how you will travel around during your trip. | We need **to arrange transportation** from the airport to our hotel. |
| **A package holiday / tour** | Một kỳ nghỉ / chuyến đi trọn gói | A holiday organized by a company at a fixed price that includes travel, accommodation, etc. | For their first trip abroad, they chose a **package holiday** for convenience. |
| **All-inclusive resort** | Khu nghỉ dưỡng trọn gói | A resort where the price includes accommodation, food, drinks, and activities. | An **all-inclusive resort** can be a good option for families who want a stress-free vacation. |
| **To get everything sorted in advance** | Sắp xếp mọi thứ trước | To organize and plan all the details of a trip before you leave. | I like **to get everything sorted in advance** so I can relax on holiday. |
| **Last-minute booking** | Việc đặt chỗ vào phút chót | A booking made very close to the travel date. | You can sometimes find great deals with a **last-minute booking**, but it's risky. |
