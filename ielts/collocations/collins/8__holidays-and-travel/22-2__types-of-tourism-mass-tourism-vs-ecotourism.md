### ✈️ **Du lịch Đại chúng (Mass Tourism)**

| Collocation                                     | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                                        | Short English Meaning                                                                             | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                                |
| :---------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------ | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Mass tourism / Conventional tourism**         | Du lịch đại chúng / Du lịch thông thường                | Large-scale tourism focused on popular destinations and standardized experiences.                 | **Mass tourism** often involves thousands of people visiting the same destinations during peak season.          |
| **Large-scale infrastructure development**      | Sự phát triển hạ tầng quy mô lớn                        | The construction of large hotels, resorts, airports, and roads to accommodate many tourists.      | The coastline has been transformed by the **large-scale infrastructure development** required for mass tourism. |
| **A huge influx of tourists**                   | Một dòng khách du lịch khổng lồ                         | The arrival of a very large number of tourists in a short period.                                 | **A huge influx of tourists** can overwhelm a small town's public services.                                     |
| **All-inclusive package holidays**              | Các kỳ nghỉ trọn gói                                    | Holidays where flights, accommodation, and meals are all included in one price.                   | **All-inclusive package holidays** are convenient but can limit tourists' interaction with the local economy.   |
| **Standardized and commercialized experiences** | Những trải nghiệm được tiêu chuẩn hóa và thương mại hóa | Tourist experiences that are uniform, lack authenticity, and are treated as a product to be sold. | Mass tourism can lead to **standardized and commercialized experiences**.                                       |
| **To cater to mass-market tastes**              | Phục vụ cho thị hiếu của thị trường đại chúng           | To provide products and services that appeal to a large number of ordinary people.                | The entertainment and food options in large resorts are designed **to cater to mass-market tastes**.            |
| **High-volume, low-cost travel**                | Du lịch số lượng lớn, chi phí thấp                      | A travel model based on attracting a large number of people with cheap prices.                    | Budget airlines have fueled the growth of **high-volume, low-cost travel**.                                     |
| **Profit maximization**                         | Tối đa hóa lợi nhuận                                    | The primary goal of making as much profit as possible.                                            | The business model of many large tour operators is focused on **profit maximization**.                          |
| **Leakage of tourism revenue**                  | Sự rò rỉ doanh thu du lịch                              | The process where money spent by tourists in a destination is paid to foreign-owned companies.    | The dominance of international hotel chains can lead to a high **leakage of tourism revenue**.                  |
| **To have a significant carbon footprint**      | Có dấu chân carbon đáng kể                              | To produce a large amount of carbon dioxide emissions through activities like air travel.         | The long-haul flights associated with mass tourism **have a significant carbon footprint**.                     |

### 🌱 **Du lịch Sinh thái & Bền vững (Ecotourism & Sustainable Tourism)**

| Collocation                                        | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                                        | Short English Meaning                                                                                           | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                 |
| :------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------ | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Ecotourism**                                     | Du lịch sinh thái                                       | Responsible travel to natural areas that conserves the environment and improves the well-being of local people. | The main principle of **ecotourism** is to foster environmental and cultural respect.            |
| **Responsible and ethical travel**                 | Du lịch có trách nhiệm và đạo đức                       | Traveling in a way that is mindful of the environmental, social, and economic impacts.                          | **Responsible and ethical travel** involves making choices that benefit the host community.      |
| **Low-impact travel**                              | Du lịch ít tác động                                     | A form of tourism that has a minimal effect on the environment and local culture.                               | Activities like hiking and bird-watching are considered forms of **low-impact travel**.          |
| **An eco-friendly lodge / resort**                 | Một nhà nghỉ / khu nghỉ dưỡng thân thiện với môi trường | Accommodation that is designed and operated to be environmentally sustainable.                                  | We stayed in an **eco-friendly lodge** that used solar power and locally sourced food.           |
| **To venture off the beaten track**                | Mạo hiểm đến những nơi ít người lui tới                 | To visit remote or less-traveled places.                                                                        | Ecotourists often prefer **to venture off the beaten track** to experience unspoiled nature.     |
| **An authentic and immersive cultural experience** | Một trải nghiệm văn hóa đích thực và đắm chìm           | A genuine and deeply engaging cultural experience that goes beyond superficial sightseeing.                     | Staying with a local family can provide a more **authentic and immersive cultural experience**.  |
| **To support locally-owned businesses**            | Hỗ trợ các doanh nghiệp do người địa phương sở hữu      | To spend money at businesses that are owned and operated by local people.                                       | **Supporting locally-owned businesses** ensures that tourism revenue stays within the community. |
| **Small-scale, community-based operations**        | Các hoạt động quy mô nhỏ, dựa vào cộng đồng             | Tourism businesses that are small in size and are managed or owned by the local community.                      | Ecotourism often involves **small-scale, community-based operations**.                           |
| **To have a strong educational component**         | Có yếu tố giáo dục mạnh mẽ                              | To include a significant element of learning about the local environment and culture.                           | A key feature of ecotourism is that it should **have a strong educational component**.           |
| **To contribute to conservation efforts**          | Đóng góp cho các nỗ lực bảo tồn                         | To provide money or support for the protection of the natural environment.                                      | Entrance fees to the national park directly **contribute to conservation efforts**.              |

### ⚖️ **Nguyên tắc & Tác động So sánh (Principles & Comparative Impacts)**

| Collocation                                               | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                                  | Short English Meaning                                                                                | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                                   |
| :-------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------ | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A fundamental difference in philosophy**                | Một sự khác biệt cơ bản về triết lý               | A core disagreement in the guiding principles and beliefs.                                           | There is a **fundamental difference in philosophy** between mass tourism and ecotourism.                           |
| **To minimize the negative impacts**                      | Giảm thiểu các tác động tiêu cực                  | To reduce the harmful effects on the environment and society as much as possible.                    | The primary goal of sustainable tourism is **to minimize the negative impacts** of travel.                         |
| **To maximize the positive benefits**                     | Tối đa hóa các lợi ích tích cực                   | To ensure that the positive effects, especially for local people, are as large as possible.          | Community-based tourism aims **to maximize the positive benefits** for the host population.                        |
| **To promote environmental conservation**                 | Thúc đẩy bảo tồn môi trường                       | To actively encourage and support the protection of the natural world.                               | Revenue from ecotourism can be used **to promote environmental conservation**.                                     |
| **To empower local communities**                          | Trao quyền cho cộng đồng địa phương               | To give local people more control, resources, and opportunities to improve their own lives.          | Ecotourism can **empower local communities** by providing alternative and sustainable livelihoods.                 |
| **To preserve fragile ecosystems**                        | Gìn giữ các hệ sinh thái mỏng manh                | To protect natural environments that are delicate and easily damaged.                                | Strict regulations are needed **to preserve fragile ecosystems** from the impacts of mass tourism.                 |
| **To provide a more meaningful and enriching experience** | Cung cấp một trải nghiệm ý nghĩa và phong phú hơn | To offer an experience that is more valuable, educational, and personally rewarding.                 | Many travelers find that ecotourism **provides a more meaningful and enriching experience**.                       |
| **To generate significant waste and pollution**           | Tạo ra lượng rác thải và ô nhiễm đáng kể          | To produce a large amount of rubbish and harmful substances.                                         | Large cruise ships and all-inclusive resorts can **generate significant waste and pollution**.                     |
| **Economic leakage vs. local economic benefit**           | Rò rỉ kinh tế vs. lợi ích kinh tế địa phương      | The contrast between money leaving the local economy versus money staying and circulating within it. | Mass tourism often suffers from high **economic leakage**, whereas ecotourism aims for **local economic benefit**. |
| **Authenticity vs. commercialization**                    | Tính đích thực vs. sự thương mại hóa              | The conflict between genuine cultural experiences and those that are simplified and sold for profit. | There is a constant tension between preserving **authenticity** and the pressures of **commercialization**.        |
