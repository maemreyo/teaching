### ❌ **Tác động Tiêu cực lên Văn hóa & Xã hội (Negative Cultural & Social Impacts)**

| Collocation                                              | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                              | Short English Meaning                                                                                      | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                                      |
| :------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **The erosion of cultural identity**                     | Sự xói mòn bản sắc văn hóa                    | The gradual weakening and loss of a culture's unique character and traditions.                             | Some fear that mass tourism leads to **the erosion of cultural identity** as local customs are abandoned.             |
| **The commercialization and commodification of culture** | Sự thương mại hóa và hàng hóa hóa văn hóa     | The process of turning cultural practices and traditions into products to be bought and sold.              | **The commercialization of culture** occurs when sacred rituals are turned into shows for tourists.                   |
| **The loss of authenticity**                             | Sự mất đi tính chân thực                      | The process of something losing its genuine and traditional character.                                     | As a destination becomes more popular, it can suffer from a **loss of authenticity**.                                 |
| **Staged authenticity**                                  | Tính chân thực được dàn dựng                  | The presentation of a culture in a simplified, inauthentic way that is tailored for tourists.              | Many tourists unknowingly consume **staged authenticity** in the form of contrived cultural performances.             |
| **Cultural homogenization**                              | Sự đồng nhất văn hóa                          | The process by which cultures become more similar to one another.                                          | The global tourism industry can contribute to **cultural homogenization**, making destinations feel alike.            |
| **The demonstration effect**                             | Hiệu ứng phô bày (bắt chước)                  | The effect on the behavior of individuals caused by observation of the actions of others (e.g., tourists). | Through **the demonstration effect**, local youth may adopt the consumption patterns of wealthy tourists.             |
| **To disrupt the social fabric**                         | Phá vỡ kết cấu xã hội                         | To disturb the social structure, relationships, and norms of a community.                                  | A large influx of tourists with different values can **disrupt the social fabric** of a small, traditional community. |
| **A clash of cultures**                                  | Một sự xung đột văn hóa                       | A conflict arising from the interaction of people with different cultural values and beliefs.              | Differences in behavior and social norms can sometimes lead to a **clash of cultures**.                               |
| **To create social tension and resentment**              | Tạo ra căng thẳng và sự phẫn uất trong xã hội | To cause feelings of anger, disagreement, and bitterness between groups (e.g., locals and tourists).       | Competition for resources and rising prices can **create social tension and resentment**.                             |
| **The creation of tourist enclaves**                     | Sự hình thành các khu biệt lập cho du khách   | The development of areas that are exclusively for tourists, separating them from the local community.      | All-inclusive resorts can become **tourist enclaves**, limiting meaningful interaction.                               |

| **A surge in the cost of living**                | Sự tăng vọt chi phí sinh hoạt                               | A sudden and large increase in the cost of basic necessities like housing and food.                                               | Tourism often causes a **surge in the cost of living**, displacing local residents.                                  |
| **Economic dependency on tourism**               | Sự phụ thuộc kinh tế vào du lịch                            | A situation where a local or national economy is overly reliant on the tourism sector.                                            | **Economic dependency on tourism** makes a region vulnerable to global crises like pandemics.                        |
| **The displacement of local residents**          | Sự di dời của cư dân địa phương                             | The process of forcing local people to move out of their homes, often due to rising prices or development.                        | The development of luxury hotels has led to **the displacement of local residents** in some coastal areas.           |
| **To alter the social structure**                | Thay đổi cấu trúc xã hội                                    | To fundamentally change the way society is organized, including family structures and traditional jobs.                           | The shift from an agricultural to a service-based economy can **alter the social structure** of a village.           |
| **The nature of tourist-local interactions**     | Bản chất của sự tương tác giữa du khách và người địa phương | The quality and type of contact between tourists and local people.                                                                | **The nature of tourist-local interactions** can range from superficial commercial exchanges to genuine friendships. |
| **Gentrification**                               | Quá trình "thượng lưu hóa"                                  | The process whereby a poor urban area is changed by wealthier people moving in, improving housing, and attracting new businesses. | Tourist-driven **gentrification** can raise property values but also push out long-time residents.                   |
| **To exacerbate existing inequalities**          | Làm trầm trọng thêm những bất bình đẳng hiện có             | To make existing social or economic inequalities even worse.                                                                      | If not managed well, tourism can **exacerbate existing inequalities** within a community.                            |
| **A dual economy**                               | Nền kinh tế kép                                             | An economy where a modern, prosperous tourism sector exists alongside a poor, traditional local economy.                          | The creation of tourist enclaves can lead to a **dual economy** with limited benefits for the poor.                  |
| **To strike a balance between...**               | Đạt được sự cân bằng giữa...                                | To find a successful compromise between two competing interests.                                                                  | The challenge is **to strike a balance between** economic development and cultural preservation.                     |
