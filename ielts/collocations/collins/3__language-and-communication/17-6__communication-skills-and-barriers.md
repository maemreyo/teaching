### 🤝 **Kỹ năng & Phong cách Giao tiếp (Communication Skills & Styles)**

| Collocation                                  | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                               | Short English Meaning                                                                | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                           |
| :------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Effective communication**                  | Giao tiếp hiệu quả                             | Communicating in a way that is clearly understood and achieves its purpose.          | **Effective communication** is a key skill in any leadership role.                         |
| **Interpersonal skills**                     | Kỹ năng giao tiếp giữa các cá nhân             | The skills we use to interact and communicate with other people.                     | Teamwork and collaboration require strong **interpersonal skills**.                        |
| **To express oneself clearly and concisely** | Diễn đạt bản thân một cách rõ ràng và ngắn gọn | To say what you mean in a clear and brief way.                                       | In a business presentation, it is important **to express oneself clearly and concisely**.  |
| **Active listening**                         | Lắng nghe chủ động                             | Listening with full concentration, understanding, and response.                      | **Active listening** involves paying full attention to the speaker and providing feedback. |
| **Public speaking skills**                   | Kỹ năng nói trước công chúng                   | The ability to speak effectively in front of an audience.                            | Many people join clubs like Toastmasters to improve their **public speaking skills**.      |
| **To get a message across**                  | Truyền tải một thông điệp                      | To make someone understand your message or idea.                                     | He used simple diagrams **to get his message across** to the audience.                     |
| **To articulate one's thoughts**             | Diễn đạt suy nghĩ của mình                     | To express your ideas and thoughts clearly in words.                                 | She finds it difficult **to articulate her thoughts** when she is nervous.                 |
| **A confident communicator**                 | Một người giao tiếp tự tin                     | Someone who is self-assured when they communicate.                                   | Through practice, he has become a very **confident communicator**.                         |
| **To engage the audience**                   | Thu hút khán giả                               | To capture and hold the attention of the audience.                                   | A good speaker knows how **to engage the audience** with stories and questions.            |
| **Assertiveness skills**                     | Kỹ năng quyết đoán                             | The ability to express your opinions and needs confidently without being aggressive. | Developing **assertiveness skills** can help you in negotiations.                          |

### 🚧 **Rào cản & Vấn đề Giao tiếp (Communication Barriers & Problems)**

| Collocation                              | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                   | Short English Meaning                                                         | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                            |
| :--------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A language barrier**                   | Một rào cản ngôn ngữ               | A difficulty in communication because of different languages.                 | The **language barrier** made it difficult for tourists to communicate with locals.                         |
| **A communication breakdown**            | Sự đổ vỡ/gián đoạn trong giao tiếp | A failure to exchange information, resulting in a lack of communication.      | The argument was caused by a complete **communication breakdown**.                                          |
| **To have a misunderstanding**           | Có một sự hiểu lầm                 | To fail to understand someone or something correctly.                         | I think we **had a misunderstanding**; that's not what I meant at all.                                      |
| **To misinterpret someone's intentions** | Hiểu sai ý định của ai đó          | To understand someone's aims or purposes incorrectly.                         | It's easy **to misinterpret someone's intentions** in a short email.                                        |
| **To be lost in translation**            | Bị mất ý nghĩa khi dịch            | When the original meaning or nuance is lost after being translated.           | Jokes and cultural references are often **lost in translation**.                                            |
| **To speak at cross-purposes**           | "Ông nói gà, bà nói vịt"           | When two people think they are talking about the same thing but they are not. | I realized we were **speaking at cross-purposes** when he started talking about a different project.        |
| **A breakdown in communication**         | Sự đổ vỡ trong giao tiếp           | A failure to communicate effectively.                                         | A simple **breakdown in communication** led to the major shipping error.                                    |
| **To create ambiguity**                  | Tạo ra sự mơ hồ                    | To say something that is unclear and can be understood in more than one way.  | The vague wording of the contract **creates ambiguity** and could lead to disputes.                         |
| **A failure to communicate**             | Thất bại trong việc giao tiếp      | A situation where people do not or cannot share information effectively.      | The conflict was a result of a complete **failure to communicate** between the two departments.             |
| **To talk past each other**              | Nói chuyện không hiểu nhau         | To talk without understanding what the other person is saying.                | The two politicians were just **talking past each other** in the debate, not responding to the points made. |
