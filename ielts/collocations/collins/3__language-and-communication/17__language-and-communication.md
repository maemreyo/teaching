### 🗣️ **Học & Sử dụng Ngôn ngữ (Learning & Using Language)**

| Collocation                         | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                     | Short English Meaning                                 | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                       |
| :---------------------------------- | :----------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Mother tongue / Native language** | Tiếng mẹ đẻ                          | The first language you learn as a child.              | My **mother tongue** is Vietnamese, but I am also fluent in English.                   |
| **Foreign language**                | Ngoại ngữ                            | A language that is not your native language.          | Learning a **foreign language** can open up new cultural experiences.                  |
| **To be bilingual / multilingual**  | Thông thạo hai/nhiều ngôn ngữ        | To be able to speak two / more than two languages.    | Children who grow up in multicultural families are often **bilingual**.                |
| **To have a good command of**       | Sử dụng thành thạo (một ngôn ngữ)    | To know a language well.                              | To work as an interpreter, you must **have a good command of** at least two languages. |
| **Language proficiency**            | Sự thành thạo ngôn ngữ               | Your skill level in a language.                       | The job requires a high level of **language proficiency** in Japanese.                 |
| **To pick up a language**           | Học một ngôn ngữ (một cách tự nhiên) | To learn a language naturally without formal lessons. | He managed **to pick up** basic Spanish while travelling in Mexico.                    |
| **To master a language**            | Làm chủ một ngôn ngữ                 | To learn a language completely and perfectly.         | It takes years of practice **to master a language** completely.                        |
| **Vocabulary acquisition**          | Sự tiếp thu từ vựng                  | The process of learning new words.                    | Reading widely is the best method for **vocabulary acquisition**.                      |
| **Linguistic diversity**            | Sự đa dạng ngôn ngữ                  | The existence of many different languages.            | Globalization poses a threat to the world's **linguistic diversity**.                  |
| **Endangered language**             | Ngôn ngữ bị đe dọa                   | A language that is at risk of disappearing.           | Efforts are being made to preserve **endangered languages** from extinction.           |

### 🤝 **Kỹ năng & Phong cách Giao tiếp (Communication Skills & Styles)**

| Collocation                    | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                   | Short English Meaning                                 | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                  |
| :----------------------------- | :--------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Effective communication**    | Giao tiếp hiệu quả                 | Communicating in a way that is clearly understood.    | **Effective communication** is a key skill in any leadership role.                                |
| **To get a message across**    | Truyền tải một thông điệp          | To make someone understand your message.              | He used simple diagrams **to get his message across** to the audience.                            |
| **To express oneself clearly** | Diễn đạt bản thân một cách rõ ràng | To say what you mean in a clear way.                  | Some people find it difficult **to express themselves clearly** in public.                        |
| **To maintain eye contact**    | Duy trì giao tiếp bằng mắt         | To look at someone's eyes while talking to them.      | **Maintaining eye contact** shows that you are confident and engaged.                             |
| **Active listening**           | Lắng nghe chủ động                 | Listening with full concentration and engagement.     | **Active listening** involves paying full attention to the speaker and providing feedback.        |
| **Face-to-face communication** | Giao tiếp trực tiếp                | Communication in person.                              | Despite technology, **face-to-face communication** is still important for building relationships. |
| **Public speaking**            | Nói trước công chúng               | Speaking in front of an audience.                     | Many people have a fear of **public speaking**.                                                   |
| **Interpersonal skills**       | Kỹ năng giao tiếp giữa các cá nhân | Skills for communicating and interacting with others. | Teamwork requires strong **interpersonal skills**.                                                |

### 🤫 **Giao tiếp Phi ngôn ngữ & Ẩn ý (Non-verbal & Implicit Communication)**

| Collocation                   | Nghĩa tiếng Việt          | Short English Meaning                                           | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                              |
| :---------------------------- | :------------------------ | :-------------------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Non-verbal cues**           | Các tín hiệu phi ngôn ngữ | Communication without words (e.g., body language).              | We can learn a lot about people's feelings from their **non-verbal cues**.    |
| **Body language**             | Ngôn ngữ cơ thể           | The movements or positions of your body that show how you feel. | His defensive **body language** suggested he was not open to discussion.      |
| **Facial expressions**        | Biểu cảm khuôn mặt        | The looks on your face that show your emotions.                 | **Facial expressions** can convey a wide range of emotions.                   |
| **Tone of voice**             | Tông giọng                | The way your voice sounds, which can show your feelings.        | Her sharp **tone of voice** indicated that she was angry.                     |
| **To convey a meaning**       | Truyền tải một ý nghĩa    | To communicate a message or idea.                               | The colour red is often used **to convey a meaning** of danger or importance. |
| **To read between the lines** | Đọc và hiểu được ẩn ý     | To understand the hidden meaning in something.                  | To understand his true intentions, you have **to read between the lines**.    |

### 🚧 **Rào cản & Vấn đề Giao tiếp (Communication Barriers & Problems)**

| Collocation                    | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                   | Short English Meaning                                                         | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                                |
| :----------------------------- | :--------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Language barrier**           | Rào cản ngôn ngữ                   | A difficulty in communication because of different languages.                 | The **language barrier** made it difficult for tourists to communicate with locals.                             |
| **Communication breakdown**    | Sự đổ vỡ/gián đoạn trong giao tiếp | A failure to communicate effectively.                                         | The argument was caused by a complete **communication breakdown**.                                              |
| **To have a misunderstanding** | Có một sự hiểu lầm                 | To fail to understand someone correctly.                                      | I think we **had a misunderstanding**; that's not what I meant.                                                 |
| **To misinterpret someone**    | Hiểu sai ý ai đó                   | To understand someone incorrectly.                                            | It's easy **to misinterpret someone's** tone in a text message.                                                 |
| **Lost in translation**        | Bị mất ý nghĩa khi dịch            | When the original meaning is lost after being translated.                     | Humour is often **lost in translation** from one language to another.                                           |
| **To speak at cross-purposes** | "Ông nói gà, bà nói vịt"           | When two people think they are talking about the same thing but they are not. | I realized we were **speaking at cross-purposes** when he started talking about a completely different project. |
