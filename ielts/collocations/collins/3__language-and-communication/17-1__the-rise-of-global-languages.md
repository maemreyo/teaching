### 🌐 **Mô tả Hiện tượng & Lợi ích (Describing the Phenomenon & Advantages)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A global language** | Một ngôn ngữ toàn cầu | A language spoken and understood internationally. | English has become the **global language** of business, science, and tourism. |
| **A lingua franca** | Ngôn ngữ chung | A common language used between people who speak different native languages. | In multinational meetings, English often serves as the **lingua franca**. |
| **The dominant language** | Ngôn ngữ thống trị | The most powerful and widely used language. | The influence of American culture has contributed to English becoming **the dominant language** worldwide. |
| **A common means of communication** | Một phương tiện giao tiếp chung | A shared way for people to communicate. | Having a **common means of communication** can facilitate international cooperation. |
| **To facilitate international communication** | Tạo điều kiện thuận lợi cho giao tiếp quốc tế | To make communication between countries easier. | A global language helps **to facilitate international communication** and understanding. |
| **To break down communication barriers** | Phá vỡ các rào cản giao tiếp | To remove difficulties in communication. | The internet, combined with a common language, has helped **to break down communication barriers**. |
| **To foster global understanding** | Thúc đẩy sự thấu hiểu toàn cầu | To encourage understanding between people worldwide. | Sharing a language can help **to foster greater global understanding** and cultural exchange. |
| **To promote international trade** | Thúc đẩy thương mại quốc tế | To help trade between countries grow. | A single, dominant language can **promote international trade** by simplifying negotiations. |
| **To enhance cross-cultural communication** | Nâng cao khả năng giao tiếp đa văn hóa | To improve communication between different cultures. | Learning English can **enhance cross-cultural communication** skills. |
| **Access to a wealth of information** | Tiếp cận một kho thông tin khổng lồ | The ability to get a huge amount of information. | English proficiency provides **access to a wealth of information** available online. |

| **To face the threat of extinction** | Đối mặt với nguy cơ tuyệt chủng | To be in danger of disappearing completely. | Thousands of languages around the world currently **face the threat of extinction**. |
| **An endangered language** | Một ngôn ngữ bị đe dọa | A language that is at risk of dying out. | An **endangered language** is one that is at risk of falling out of use. |
| **The loss of linguistic diversity** | Sự mất mát đa dạng ngôn ngữ | The reduction in the number of languages spoken. | **The loss of linguistic diversity** means the loss of unique ways of seeing the world. |
| **To lead to language death** | Dẫn đến cái chết của ngôn ngữ | To cause a language to no longer be spoken. | If a language is no longer spoken by children, it will eventually **lead to language death**. |
| **To be at risk of disappearing** | Có nguy cơ biến mất | To be in danger of vanishing. | Many indigenous languages are **at risk of disappearing** within the next generation. |
| **To marginalize speakers of...** | Gạt ra bên lề những người nói (tiếng...) | To make speakers of a language feel unimportant. | The dominance of one language can **marginalize speakers of** other, less common languages. |

| **To feel a sense of linguistic inferiority** | Cảm thấy tự ti về mặt ngôn ngữ | To feel that your language is not as good as others. | Speakers of minority languages may **feel a sense of linguistic inferiority**. |
| **To erode cultural identity** | Xói mòn bản sắc văn hóa | To gradually destroy the unique identity of a culture. | The dominance of a global language can **erode the cultural identity** of smaller communities. |

### 🌍 **Ngôn ngữ & Văn hóa (Language & Culture)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Language and cultural identity** | Ngôn ngữ và bản sắc văn hóa | The connection between the language you speak and your sense of who you are. | **Language and cultural identity** are inextricably linked. |
| **To lose one's cultural heritage** | Đánh mất di sản văn hóa | To lose the traditions and culture of your ancestors. | When a language dies, a community can **lose its cultural heritage**. |
| **Cultural homogenization** | Sự đồng nhất văn hóa | The process of cultures becoming more similar. | Critics argue that a global language contributes to **cultural homogenization**. |
| **A rich linguistic and cultural heritage** | Một di sản văn hóa và ngôn ngữ phong phú | A valuable inheritance of language and culture. | Each language represents a **rich linguistic and cultural heritage**. |
| **To preserve a language** | Gìn giữ, bảo tồn một ngôn ngữ | To keep a language from disappearing. | Efforts are being made **to preserve** the Welsh language in the UK. |
| **Language revitalization efforts** | Các nỗ lực phục hồi ngôn ngữ | Attempts to bring a language back into common use. | **Language revitalization efforts** often involve teaching the language in schools. |
| **To be inextricably linked to** | Có mối liên hệ mật thiết, không thể tách rời với | To be connected in a very essential way. | A person's native language is **inextricably linked to** their sense of self. |

| **Cultural imperialism** | Chủ nghĩa đế quốc văn hóa | The practice of one culture imposing its values on another. | The spread of a global language is sometimes seen as a form of **cultural imperialism**. |
| **To transmit cultural values** | Truyền tải các giá trị văn hóa | To pass cultural beliefs from one generation to the next. | Language is the main vehicle used **to transmit cultural values** across generations. |
