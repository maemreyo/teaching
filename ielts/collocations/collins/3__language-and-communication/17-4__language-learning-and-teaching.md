### 🧠 **Người học & Quá trình học (The Learner & The Process)**

| Collocation                              | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                      | Short English Meaning                                     | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                    |
| :--------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------ | :-------------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A native speaker**                     | Người bản xứ                          | Someone who has spoken a language since they were a baby. | It's always beneficial to practice conversation with a **native speaker**.          |
| **To be bilingual / multilingual**       | Thông thạo hai / nhiều ngôn ngữ       | To speak two / more than two languages fluently.          | Being **bilingual** has been shown to have many cognitive advantages.               |
| **Second-language acquisition**          | Sự thụ đắc ngôn ngữ thứ hai           | The process of learning a second language.                | Theories of **second-language acquisition** explore how people learn new languages. |
| **To pick up a language**                | Học một ngôn ngữ (một cách tự nhiên)  | To learn a language informally by being exposed to it.    | Children can often **pick up a second language** effortlessly when living abroad.   |
| **To be immersed in a language/culture** | Đắm mình trong một ngôn ngữ / văn hóa | To be completely surrounded by a language or culture.     | The best way to learn a language is **to be completely immersed in it**.            |
| **To have a natural aptitude for**       | Có năng khiếu bẩm sinh về             | To have a natural talent for something.                   | She seems **to have a natural aptitude for** learning languages.                    |
| **To make steady progress**              | Có sự tiến bộ đều đặn                 | To improve at a regular and consistent rate.              | With daily practice, you will **make steady progress**.                             |
| **To set learning goals**                | Đặt ra các mục tiêu học tập           | To decide what you want to achieve in your learning.      | It's important **to set clear learning goals** to stay motivated.                   |

### 🛠️ **Phương pháp & Kỹ thuật (Methods & Techniques)**

| Collocation                       | Nghĩa tiếng Việt               | Short English Meaning                                                    | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                          |
| :-------------------------------- | :----------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Language immersion**            | Sự đắm mình trong ngôn ngữ     | A method of teaching where the target language is used for all subjects. | **Language immersion** programs, where all subjects are taught in a foreign language, are very effective. |
| **A communicative approach**      | Phương pháp giao tiếp          | A teaching method that focuses on real-life communication.               | **A communicative approach** to teaching focuses on using the language in real-life situations.           |
| **To build vocabulary**           | Xây dựng, mở rộng vốn từ vựng  | To increase the number of words you know.                                | Reading books and articles is an excellent way **to build your vocabulary**.                              |
| **To improve grammar and syntax** | Cải thiện ngữ pháp và cú pháp  | To get better at the rules of a language.                                | Learners need to focus on both fluency and accuracy **to improve their grammar**.                         |
| **To take a language course**     | Tham gia một khóa học ngôn ngữ | To enroll in a class to learn a language.                                | I decided **to take an intensive language course** before moving to Japan.                                |
| **Self-study methods**            | Các phương pháp tự học         | Ways of learning by yourself.                                            | Many successful language learners rely on **self-study methods** like using apps and watching movies.     |
| **To practice pronunciation**     | Luyện tập phát âm              | To work on speaking a language with the correct sounds.                  | Shadowing native speakers is a useful technique **to practice pronunciation**.                            |

### 🎯 **Kỹ năng & Mức độ Thành thạo (Skills & Proficiency)**

| Collocation                                | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                            | Short English Meaning                                 | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                               |
| :----------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------ | :---------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **The four language skills**               | Bốn kỹ năng ngôn ngữ (nghe, nói, đọc, viết) | Listening, speaking, reading, and writing.            | A balanced study plan should cover all **four language skills**.                               |
| **To have a good command of**              | Sử dụng thành thạo                          | To know a language well.                              | To work as a translator, you must **have an excellent command of** at least two languages.     |
| **To achieve a high level of proficiency** | Đạt được trình độ thành thạo cao            | To become very skilled in a language.                 | It takes years of dedication **to achieve a high level of proficiency**.                       |
| **To be fluent in...**                     | Thông thạo (ngôn ngữ nào đó)                | To be able to speak a language easily and well.       | After living in France for five years, she **is fluent in** French.                            |
| **A native-like accent**                   | Giọng nói giống người bản xứ                | An accent that sounds like a native speaker's.        | His goal is to develop a **native-like accent**.                                               |
| **To expand one's vocabulary**             | Mở rộng vốn từ vựng                         | To learn more words.                                  | I use flashcards **to expand my vocabulary** on a daily basis.                                 |
| **To make grammatical mistakes**           | Mắc lỗi ngữ pháp                            | To make errors in the rules of a language.            | Don't be afraid **to make grammatical mistakes** when you are speaking; it's part of learning. |
| **To improve accuracy and fluency**        | Cải thiện độ chính xác và lưu loát          | To get better at being correct and speaking smoothly. | The course aims **to improve both the accuracy and fluency** of your spoken English.           |

### ✨ **Lợi ích & Thách thức (Benefits & Challenges)**

| Collocation                          | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                        | Short English Meaning                                                    | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                         |
| :----------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Cognitive benefits**               | Các lợi ích về mặt nhận thức            | The benefits for your brain and thinking skills.                         | Research shows that bilingualism has significant **cognitive benefits**, such as improved memory.        |
| **To enhance career opportunities**  | Nâng cao cơ hội nghề nghiệp             | To improve your job prospects.                                           | Being multilingual can greatly **enhance your career opportunities** in a globalized world.              |
| **To broaden one's horizons**        | Mở rộng tầm hiểu biết                   | To expand your range of knowledge and experience.                        | Learning a new language allows you **to broaden your horizons** and understand different cultures.       |
| **To overcome the language barrier** | Vượt qua rào cản ngôn ngữ               | To solve the problem of not speaking the same language.                  | A tour guide can help you **overcome the language barrier** when travelling.                             |
| **To face challenges**               | Đối mặt với những thách thức            | To deal with difficult situations.                                       | Every language learner will **face challenges**, such as complex grammar rules or unfamiliar sounds.     |
| **A sense of achievement**           | Cảm giác thành tựu, đạt được điều gì đó | A feeling of pride after accomplishing something.                        | Having your first successful conversation in a new language brings a great **sense of achievement**.     |
| **Mother tongue interference**       | Sự can thiệp của tiếng mẹ đẻ (gây lỗi)  | When your first language causes errors in the language you are learning. | **Mother tongue interference** is when your native language's grammar affects your foreign language use. |
| **To be a confident communicator**   | Là một người giao tiếp tự tin           | To be able to communicate with self-assurance.                           | The goal is not just to be fluent, but **to be a confident communicator**.                               |
