### 🏛️ **Cấu trúc & Hoạt động của Chính phủ (Government Structure & Actions)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Effective governance** | Quản trị hiệu quả | A well-run government that is effective and fair. | **Effective governance** is crucial for a country's long-term stability and development. |
| **To exercise authority** | Thực thi quyền lực | To use official power to make decisions and enforce laws. | The government must **exercise its authority** to maintain public order. |
| **Centralized vs. decentralized power** | Quyền lực tập trung và phân quyền | Power held by a central government vs. power given to local regions. | There is an ongoing debate between the benefits of **centralized and decentralized power**. |
| **To devolve authority to local governments** | Trao quyền cho chính quyền địa phương | To give responsibilities and power from a central government to local ones. | The plan aims **to devolve more authority to local governments** to manage their own affairs. |
| **A system of checks and balances** | Một hệ thống kiểm soát và đối trọng | A system that prevents one part of government from having too much power. | A democratic constitution provides a **system of checks and balances** between the executive, legislative, and judicial branches. |
| **A cabinet reshuffle** | Một cuộc cải tổ nội các | A change in the group of ministers who lead government departments. | The Prime Minister announced a major **cabinet reshuffle** following the election. |
| **The civil service** | Nền công vụ | The administrative departments of a government. | A professional and impartial **civil service** is the backbone of an effective state. |
| **Government transparency** | Sự minh bạch của chính phủ | When the government's actions are open to public scrutiny. | **Government transparency** is essential to prevent corruption and build public trust. |
| **To hold leaders accountable** | Yêu cầu các nhà lãnh đạo phải chịu trách nhiệm | To make people in power responsible for their actions and decisions. | A free press plays a vital role in **holding leaders accountable**. |
| **Abuse of power** | Lạm dụng quyền lực | The illegal or unethical use of an official position for personal gain. | The minister was forced to resign over allegations of **abuse of power**. |

### 📜 **Chính sách & Pháp luật (Policy & Legislation)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **To introduce / implement a policy** | Ban hành / Triển khai một chính sách | To start or put into action a new government plan or rule. | The government plans **to introduce a new policy** on renewable energy. |
| **Policy implementation and evaluation** | Triển khai và đánh giá chính sách | The process of putting a policy into action and then checking its effectiveness. | Effective **policy implementation** is just as important as the policy itself. |
| **To draft a bill** | Soạn thảo một dự luật | To write a formal proposal for a new law. | A special committee was formed **to draft a bill** on data protection. |
| **To pass legislation** | Thông qua luật pháp | To formally vote on and approve a new law. | Parliament is expected **to pass the new legislation** before the summer recess. |
| **A regulatory framework** | Một khung pháp lý quản lý | A set of rules, laws, and guidelines for a particular industry or activity. | The financial industry operates within a strict **regulatory framework**. |
| **To enforce regulations** | Thực thi các quy định | To make sure that laws and rules are obeyed. | The agency's job is **to enforce environmental regulations**. |
| **To strengthen the rule of law** | Tăng cường pháp quyền | To make the legal system stronger, more respected, and applied equally to all. | International aid is being provided **to help strengthen the rule of law**. |
| **To uphold justice** | Duy trì, bảo vệ công lý | To maintain fairness and ensure that the law is applied correctly. | The court system has a fundamental duty **to uphold justice**. |
| **Electoral reform** | Cải cách bầu cử | Changes made to the voting system to improve fairness or representation. | Activists are campaigning for **electoral reform** to create a more proportional system. |
| **Anti-corruption measures** | Các biện pháp chống tham nhũng | Actions, laws, and policies designed to fight dishonest and illegal behavior by officials. | The new government promised to introduce tough **anti-corruption measures**. |

### 💰 **Kinh tế & Dịch vụ Công (Economy & Public Services)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Government intervention in the economy** | Sự can thiệp của chính phủ vào nền kinh tế | The involvement of the government in the country's economy to influence outcomes. | There is much debate about the appropriate level of **government intervention in the economy**. |
| **To control public spending** | Kiểm soát chi tiêu công | To limit the amount of money the government spends. | The finance minister announced plans **to control public spending** to reduce the budget deficit. |
| **A budget deficit / A balanced budget** | Thâm hụt ngân sách / Ngân sách cân bằng | When spending is more than income / When spending equals income. | The country is facing a large **budget deficit** due to the recession. |
| **To provide public services** | Cung cấp các dịch vụ công | To offer essential services like healthcare, education, and transport to the public. | A key role of the government is **to provide public services** for its citizens. |
| **A government subsidy** | Một khoản trợ cấp của chính phủ | Money given by the government to help an industry or to keep the price of a service low. | The agricultural sector receives significant **government subsidies**. |
| **Social welfare** | Phúc lợi xã hội | Government support and services for people in need, such as the unemployed or disabled. | The **social welfare** system provides a safety net for the most vulnerable in society. |
| **To address social issues** | Giải quyết các vấn đề xã hội | To take action to deal with problems in society, such as poverty or inequality. | The government has a responsibility **to address social issues**. |
| **To ensure national security** | Đảm bảo an ninh quốc gia | To protect the country from internal and external threats. | The primary duty of any government is **to ensure national security**. |
| **A public-private partnership (PPP)** | Một hình thức hợp tác công-tư | A project or service involving a cooperative arrangement between the government and a private company. | The new highway was financed through a **public-private partnership**. |
| **E-government services** | Các dịch vụ chính phủ điện tử | Government services that are provided to citizens online. | Renewing your driver's license is now a simple process thanks to **e-government services**. |
