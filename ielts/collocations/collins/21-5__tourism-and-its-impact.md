### ✈️ **Các loại hình Du lịch & Thuật ngữ chung (Types of Tourism & General Terms)**

| Collocation                                 | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                           | Short English Meaning                                                                                             | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                               |
| :------------------------------------------ | :----------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **The tourism industry / sector**           | Ngành / lĩnh vực du lịch                   | The businesses and services that provide for tourists.                                                            | **The tourism sector** is a significant contributor to the country's GDP.                      |
| **Mass tourism**                            | Du lịch đại chúng                          | Tourism involving large numbers of people going to the same place at the same time.                               | **Mass tourism** can lead to overcrowding and put a strain on local infrastructure.            |
| **Ecotourism**                              | Du lịch sinh thái                          | Responsible travel to natural areas that conserves the environment and improves the well-being of local people.   | **Ecotourism** aims to educate travelers and provide funds for ecological conservation.        |
| **Cultural tourism**                        | Du lịch văn hóa                            | Tourism concerned with a country or region's culture, specifically its lifestyle, history, art, and architecture. | **Cultural tourism** is popular among those who wish to explore the heritage of a destination. |
| **A popular tourist destination / hotspot** | Một điểm đến / điểm nóng du lịch nổi tiếng | A place that is very popular with tourists.                                                                       | The island has become a popular **tourist hotspot** for international travelers.               |
| **An influx of tourists**                   | Một dòng khách du lịch (ồ ạt)              | The arrival of a large number of tourists.                                                                        | The city struggles to cope with the massive **influx of tourists** during the peak season.     |
| **Peak season / Off-season**                | Mùa cao điểm / Mùa thấp điểm               | The busiest / quietest time of year for tourism.                                                                  | Traveling during the **off-season** is often cheaper and less crowded.                         |
| **To go off the beaten track**              | Đi đến những nơi ít người lui tới          | To visit places that are not common tourist destinations.                                                         | I prefer **to go off the beaten track** to discover the authentic local culture.               |
| **A seasoned traveler**                     | Một người du lịch dày dạn kinh nghiệm      | A person who has a lot of experience with traveling.                                                              | As a **seasoned traveler**, she is comfortable navigating unfamiliar cities on her own.        |
| **The travel itinerary**                    | Lịch trình chuyến đi                       | A detailed plan or route of a journey.                                                                            | We planned our **travel itinerary** carefully to include all the major landmarks.              |

### ✅ **Tác động Tích cực (Positive Impacts)**

| Collocation                                      | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                               | Short English Meaning                                                                               | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                               |
| :----------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **To boost the local economy**                   | Thúc đẩy kinh tế địa phương                    | To help the local economy to grow and improve.                                                      | The tourism industry can **boost the local economy** by creating jobs and bringing in revenue. |
| **To generate employment opportunities**         | Tạo ra cơ hội việc làm                         | To create new jobs.                                                                                 | New hotels and restaurants **generate employment opportunities** for local residents.          |
| **A major source of foreign exchange earnings**  | Một nguồn thu ngoại tệ chính                   | A main way a country earns money from other countries.                                              | For many developing countries, tourism is a **major source of foreign exchange earnings**.     |
| **To promote cross-cultural exchange**           | Thúc đẩy giao lưu chéo văn hóa                 | To encourage the sharing of ideas, customs, and beliefs between different cultures.                 | Tourism can **promote cross-cultural exchange** between visitors and the host community.       |
| **To foster mutual understanding and tolerance** | Nuôi dưỡng sự thấu hiểu và khoan dung lẫn nhau | To help people from different backgrounds understand and accept each other.                         | Interacting with local people can **foster mutual understanding and tolerance**.               |
| **To fund infrastructure development**           | Cấp vốn cho phát triển cơ sở hạ tầng           | To provide the money to build basic facilities like roads, airports, and power supplies.            | Revenue from tourism allows governments **to fund infrastructure development**.                |
| **To preserve cultural and historical heritage** | Gìn giữ, bảo tồn di sản văn hóa và lịch sử     | To protect the traditions, buildings, and artifacts of a country that have historical significance. | Tourism can provide the financial incentive **to preserve cultural and historical heritage**.  |
| **To revitalize local traditions**               | Hồi sinh các truyền thống địa phương           | To bring new life and energy to old customs and practices.                                          | The tourist demand for authentic crafts can help **to revitalize local traditions**.           |
| **To improve the standard of living**            | Cải thiện mức sống                             | To increase the level of wealth, comfort, and material goods available to a community.              | A thriving tourism industry can help **to improve the standard of living** for local people.   |
| **To showcase a country's natural beauty**       | Phô bày vẻ đẹp tự nhiên của một quốc gia       | To display a country's beautiful natural landscapes to the world.                                   | National parks are a great way **to showcase a country's natural beauty**.                     |

### ❌ **Tác động Tiêu cực (Negative Impacts)**

| Collocation                             | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                     | Short English Meaning                                                                                      | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                                          |
| :-------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **To have a detrimental effect on...**  | Có ảnh hưởng bất lợi/gây hại cho...  | To have a harmful or damaging effect on something.                                                         | Mass tourism can **have a detrimental effect on** fragile ecosystems.                                                     |
| **Environmental degradation**           | Sự suy thoái môi trường              | The deterioration of the environment through depletion of resources and pollution.                         | The construction of large resorts often leads to **environmental degradation**.                                           |
| **The erosion of local culture**        | Sự xói mòn văn hóa địa phương        | The gradual destruction or weakening of local customs, traditions, and identity.                           | Some people fear that global tourism leads to **the erosion of local culture**.                                           |
| **To destroy natural habitats**         | Phá hủy môi trường sống tự nhiên     | To damage or eliminate the natural environment where an animal or plant normally lives.                    | The development of golf courses can **destroy natural habitats** and waste water resources.                               |
| **Overcrowding and congestion**         | Tình trạng quá đông đúc và tắc nghẽn | A situation where there are too many people and vehicles in a place.                                       | **Overcrowding and congestion** during peak season can diminish the quality of the visitor experience.                    |
| **A surge in the cost of living**       | Sự tăng vọt chi phí sinh hoạt        | A sudden and large increase in the cost of basic necessities.                                              | A tourism boom can cause a **surge in the cost of living** for local residents.                                           |
| **To put a strain on local resources**  | Gây áp lực lên tài nguyên địa phương | To create a burden on local supplies like water, electricity, and food.                                    | A large influx of tourists can **put a strain on local resources**.                                                       |
| **The commercialization of culture**    | Sự thương mại hóa văn hóa            | The process of treating culture as a commodity to be bought and sold.                                      | Critics argue that tourism leads to **the commercialization of culture**, turning authentic traditions into performances. |
| **Seasonal employment**                 | Việc làm thời vụ                     | Work that is only available during certain times of the year.                                              | The reliance on tourism often leads to **seasonal employment**, which is unstable for workers.                            |
| **The displacement of local residents** | Sự di dời của cư dân địa phương      | The process of forcing local people to move out of their homes, often due to rising prices or development. | The construction of luxury hotels has led to **the displacement of local residents** in some areas.                       |

### 🌱 **Du lịch Bền vững & Có trách nhiệm (Sustainable & Responsible Tourism)**

| Collocation                                   | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                               | Short English Meaning                                                                                            | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                            |
| :-------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Responsible tourism**                       | Du lịch có trách nhiệm                         | The practice of traveling in a way that is respectful of the local culture and environment.                      | **Responsible tourism** involves making choices that have a positive impact on the destination.             |
| **To minimize one's environmental footprint** | Giảm thiểu dấu chân môi trường của một người   | To reduce the total amount of greenhouse gases produced by one's travel activities.                              | The goal of ecotourism is **to minimize one's environmental footprint**.                                    |
| **To respect local customs and traditions**   | Tôn trọng phong tục và truyền thống địa phương | To show consideration for and behave appropriately according to the cultural norms of a place.                   | Tourists should always research and **respect local customs and traditions**.                               |
| **To support the local community**            | Hỗ trợ cộng đồng địa phương                    | To help the local people, often by buying local products and using local services.                               | One way to be a responsible tourist is **to support the local community** directly.                         |
| **Community-based tourism**                   | Du lịch dựa vào cộng đồng                      | Tourism in which local residents invite tourists to visit their communities and provide overnight accommodation. | **Community-based tourism** ensures that economic benefits flow directly to the host community.             |
| **To raise environmental awareness**          | Nâng cao nhận thức về môi trường               | To make people more knowledgeable and concerned about environmental issues.                                      | Guided nature tours can **raise environmental awareness** among visitors.                                   |
| **To implement stricter regulations**         | Thực thi các quy định chặt chẽ hơn             | To put more rigorous rules and controls into effect.                                                             | The government needs **to implement stricter regulations** to control hotel development in protected areas. |
| **To limit the number of visitors**           | Giới hạn số lượng du khách                     | To impose a cap on the number of people who can visit a place at any one time.                                   | Some fragile sites have had **to limit the number of visitors** to prevent damage.                          |
| **To impose a tourist tax**                   | Áp đặt thuế du lịch                            | To charge tourists an extra fee that is used to fund local services and conservation.                            | Many cities have decided **to impose a tourist tax** to manage the impact of overcrowding.                  |
| **To promote off-season travel**              | Thúc đẩy du lịch trái mùa                      | To encourage people to travel during the less popular times of the year.                                         | **Promoting off-season travel** can help to reduce pressure on popular destinations.                        |
