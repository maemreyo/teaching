| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A balanced diet** | Một chế độ ăn uống cân bằng | A diet that contains an adequate quantity of the nutrients we require. | Maintaining a **balanced diet** is essential for good health. |
| **Nutritional value** | Giá trị dinh dưỡng | The quality of food in terms of the nutrients it provides. | Processed foods often have very low **nutritional value**. |
| **To follow a diet** | Tuân theo một chế độ ăn kiêng | To eat a specific type or amount of food for a period, often to lose weight. | She is **following a strict diet** to prepare for the competition. |

| **To have a food intolerance / allergy** | Không dung nạp / dị ứng với thực phẩm | To have a medical condition where you cannot eat a certain type of food. | He has a dairy **intolerance** and must avoid all milk products. |

### 🏃 **Tập thể dục & Hoạt động Thể chất (Exercise & Physical Activity)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **To take regular exercise** | Tập thể dục đều đặn | To exercise consistently and frequently. | The doctor advised him **to take regular exercise** to improve his heart health. |
| **A sedentary lifestyle** | Một lối sống thụ động, ít vận động | A lifestyle characterized by a lot of sitting and very little physical activity. | A **sedentary lifestyle** is a major risk factor for many chronic diseases. |
| **To keep fit / stay in shape** | Giữ dáng, giữ sức khỏe | To maintain a good level of physical fitness. | He goes to the gym three times a week **to keep fit**. |
| **A fitness regime / program** | Một chế độ / chương trình tập luyện | A structured plan of physical exercise. | I started a new **fitness regime** that includes both cardio and weight training. |
| **To build muscle and improve stamina** | Xây dựng cơ bắp và cải thiện sức bền | To increase muscle mass and the ability to sustain prolonged physical effort. | His goal is **to build muscle and improve his stamina**. |
| **Cardiovascular exercise (Cardio)** | Bài tập tim mạch | Exercise that raises your heart rate, such as running or cycling. | **Cardiovascular exercise** is crucial for a healthy heart. |
| **Strength training / Weightlifting** | Tập luyện sức mạnh / Cử tạ | Exercise focused on using resistance to build muscle strength. | **Strength training** can help increase metabolism and bone density. |
| **To lead an active lifestyle** | Sống một lối sống năng động | To have a lifestyle that includes a lot of physical activity. | **Leading an active lifestyle** can prevent many health problems in old age. |
| **To warm up / cool down** | Khởi động / Thả lỏng | To do gentle exercises to prepare for / recover from strenuous exercise. | It's essential **to warm up** properly before a run to prevent injuries. |
| **To be out of shape** | Mất dáng, không khỏe | To be in poor physical condition. | After months without exercise, I feel really **out of shape**. |

### 🍔 **Thói quen Ăn uống & Sức khỏe (Eating Habits & Health)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **To consume excessive calories** | Tiêu thụ quá nhiều calo | To eat more calories than your body needs. | **Consuming excessive calories** on a regular basis will lead to weight gain. |
| **To skip breakfast** | Bỏ bữa sáng | To not eat the first meal of the day. | Nutritionists advise not **to skip breakfast** as it's the most important meal. |
| **To crave junk food** | Thèm đồ ăn vặt không lành mạnh | To have a strong desire for unhealthy food. | When I'm tired, I often **crave junk food** like crisps and chocolate. |
| **To limit fat / sodium intake** | Hạn chế lượng chất béo / natri nạp vào | To reduce the amount of fat or salt you eat. | For a healthy heart, you should **limit your fat and sodium intake**. |
| **To trigger health problems** | Gây ra các vấn đề sức khỏe | To cause health issues to start or become active. | A diet high in processed food can **trigger health problems** like diabetes. |
| **To eat in moderation** | Ăn uống điều độ | To not eat too much of something; to be balanced. | It's fine to have dessert, as long as you **eat in moderation**. |
| **To practice portion control** | Thực hành kiểm soát khẩu phần ăn | To be careful about how much food you eat at one time. | **Practicing portion control** is an effective strategy for weight management. |
| **To avoid sugary drinks** | Tránh đồ uống có đường | To not drink beverages with a high sugar content. | One of the easiest ways to be healthier is **to avoid sugary drinks**. |
| **To stay hydrated** | Giữ cơ thể đủ nước | To drink enough water and other fluids. | It is important **to stay hydrated**, especially when you are exercising. |
| **To boost metabolism** | Tăng cường trao đổi chất | To increase the speed at which your body burns calories. | Regular exercise and eating protein can help **to boost your metabolism**. |
