### 🏛️ **Hệ thống & Chính sách Y tế (Healthcare Systems & Policy)**

| Collocation                                   | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                        | Short English Meaning                                                                                                                             | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                                |
| :-------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Universal healthcare**                      | Chăm sóc sức khỏe toàn dân              | A system that provides healthcare and financial protection to all citizens of a particular country.                                               | Many believe that **universal healthcare** should be a fundamental human right.                                 |
| **Public versus private healthcare**          | Y tế công và y tế tư                    | The debate between healthcare funded by the government versus healthcare funded by private entities.                                              | There is an ongoing debate about the merits of **public versus private healthcare**.                            |
| **A state-funded / government-funded system** | Một hệ thống được nhà nước tài trợ      | A system that is paid for by the government using taxpayers' money.                                                                               | The UK's National Health Service (NHS) is a prime example of a **state-funded system**.                         |
| **To reform the healthcare system**           | Cải cách hệ thống y tế                  | To make significant changes to a country's healthcare system to improve it.                                                                       | The government announced a plan **to reform the healthcare system** to improve efficiency and patient outcomes. |
| **The allocation of healthcare resources**    | Sự phân bổ các nguồn lực y tế           | The distribution of money, staff, and equipment within the healthcare sector.                                                                     | **The allocation of healthcare resources** is a complex ethical and economic challenge.                         |
| **To fund medical research**                  | Tài trợ cho nghiên cứu y học            | To provide money for scientific studies aimed at improving medicine.                                                                              | More public money should be used **to fund medical research** into chronic diseases.                            |
| **The standard of care**                      | Tiêu chuẩn chăm sóc (y tế)              | The level of quality and competence expected of a medical professional or system.                                                                 | The hospital was praised for its exceptionally high **standard of care**.                                       |
| **Healthcare policy**                         | Chính sách y tế                         | The plans, decisions, and actions undertaken to achieve specific healthcare goals within a society.                                               | **Healthcare policy** should be driven by evidence-based medicine.                                              |
| **A two-tier healthcare system**              | Một hệ thống y tế hai tầng              | A system where a basic, government-provided system exists alongside a superior, private system for those who can pay.                             | Critics argue that this will create a **two-tier healthcare system**, exacerbating inequality.                  |
| **Primary, secondary, and tertiary care**     | Chăm sóc ban đầu, thứ cấp và chuyên sâu | The different levels of healthcare, from a general practitioner (primary) to specialists (secondary) and highly specialized treatment (tertiary). | A well-functioning system requires seamless integration between **primary, secondary, and tertiary care**.      |

### 👩‍⚕️ **Chuyên gia Y tế & Bệnh nhân (Medical Professionals & Patients)**

| Collocation                            | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                              | Short English Meaning                                                                                                | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                           |
| :------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **The doctor-patient relationship**    | Mối quan hệ giữa bác sĩ và bệnh nhân          | The professional relationship between a physician and their patient, ideally based on trust and communication.       | Trust is a crucial element of a healthy **doctor-patient relationship**.                   |
| **To seek medical attention / advice** | Tìm kiếm sự chăm sóc / lời khuyên y tế        | To consult a doctor or other medical professional for help.                                                          | You should **seek medical attention** immediately if you experience chest pain.            |
| **A routine check-up / screening**     | Một buổi kiểm tra / sàng lọc sức khỏe định kỳ | A regular medical examination to check for health problems before they become serious.                               | A **routine screening** can help detect diseases in their early, most treatable stages.    |
| **To make an accurate diagnosis**      | Đưa ra một chẩn đoán chính xác                | To correctly identify an illness or medical condition.                                                               | After several tests, the specialist was able **to make an accurate diagnosis**.            |
| **Informed consent**                   | Sự chấp thuận sau khi được thông tin đầy đủ   | A patient's permission for a medical procedure, given after understanding all the risks and benefits.                | Before any surgery, patients are required to give their **informed consent**.              |
| **Patient confidentiality**            | Bảo mật thông tin bệnh nhân                   | The ethical principle that a doctor must keep a patient's personal health information private.                       | All medical professionals are legally and ethically bound by **patient confidentiality**.  |
| **To get a second opinion**            | Lấy ý kiến thứ hai (từ một bác sĩ khác)       | To seek advice from another medical expert to confirm a diagnosis or treatment plan.                                 | It is often wise **to get a second opinion** before undergoing a major surgical procedure. |
| **A general practitioner (GP)**        | Bác sĩ đa khoa                                | A doctor who provides general medical treatment and is the first point of contact in a healthcare system.            | Your **general practitioner** can refer you to a specialist if necessary.                  |
| **Patient-centered care**              | Chăm sóc lấy bệnh nhân làm trung tâm          | An approach to healthcare that is respectful of and responsive to individual patient preferences, needs, and values. | Modern medicine is increasingly moving towards a model of **patient-centered care**.       |
| **Medical malpractice**                | Sơ suất y khoa                                | Negligent or incompetent treatment by a medical professional that results in harm to the patient.                    | The patient sued the hospital for **medical malpractice**.                                 |

### 💉 **Điều trị & Y học (Treatment & Medicine)**

| Collocation                                  | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                     | Short English Meaning                                                                                                            | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                 |
| :------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A course of treatment**                    | Một liệu trình điều trị              | A planned series of medical treatments for a particular illness.                                                                 | The doctor prescribed a six-week **course of treatment** with antibiotics.                       |
| **Preventive medicine**                      | Y học phòng ngừa                     | The branch of medicine focused on preventing diseases rather than curing them.                                                   | **Preventive medicine**, such as vaccination and health education, is crucial for public health. |
| **Alternative versus conventional medicine** | Y học thay thế và y học thông thường | The debate between traditional Western medicine and other treatments like acupuncture or herbalism.                              | There is ongoing discussion about the efficacy of **alternative versus conventional medicine**.  |
| **Palliative care**                          | Chăm sóc giảm nhẹ                    | Specialized medical care for people with serious illnesses, focused on providing relief from symptoms and stress.                | **Palliative care** aims to improve the quality of life for patients and their families.         |
| **Rigorous clinical trials**                 | Các thử nghiệm lâm sàng nghiêm ngặt  | Thorough and strict research studies conducted with people to test a new medical treatment.                                      | New drugs must go through **rigorous clinical trials** before they are approved for public use.  |
| **A medical breakthrough**                   | Một đột phá y học                    | A major new discovery or development in medicine.                                                                                | The development of antibiotics was a **medical breakthrough** that saved millions of lives.      |
| **Surgical intervention / procedure**        | Can thiệp / thủ thuật phẫu thuật     | An operation in which a doctor cuts into the body to repair or remove a damaged part.                                            | In some cases, early **surgical intervention** is necessary to save a patient's life.            |
| **Pharmacological treatment**                | Điều trị bằng thuốc                  | The use of drugs and medications to treat a disease.                                                                             | **Pharmacological treatment** is often combined with lifestyle changes.                          |
| **Evidence-based medicine**                  | Y học dựa trên bằng chứng            | The use of current best evidence from research in making decisions about the care of individual patients.                        | Modern healthcare policies should be guided by the principles of **evidence-based medicine**.    |
| **The placebo effect**                       | Hiệu ứng giả dược                    | A beneficial effect produced by a placebo drug or treatment, which cannot be attributed to the properties of the placebo itself. | **The placebo effect** highlights the powerful connection between the mind and the body.         |

### 💰 **Tiếp cận & Chi phí Y tế (Access & Cost of Healthcare)**

| Collocation                                    | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                                 | Short English Meaning                                                                                    | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                  |
| :--------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Equitable access to healthcare**             | Tiếp cận công bằng với dịch vụ chăm sóc sức khỏe | The principle that all individuals should have a fair opportunity to attain their full health potential. | Improving **equitable access to healthcare** in rural areas is a government priority.             |
| **Affordable healthcare**                      | Chăm sóc sức khỏe giá cả phải chăng              | Medical care that people can afford to pay for without suffering financial hardship.                     | The new policy aims to make **healthcare more affordable** for low-income families.               |
| **The escalating cost of healthcare**          | Chi phí y tế ngày càng leo thang                 | The rapidly increasing price of medical care, drugs, and insurance.                                      | **The escalating cost of healthcare** is a major concern for both individuals and governments.    |
| **Comprehensive health insurance**             | Bảo hiểm y tế toàn diện                          | An insurance plan that covers a wide range of healthcare services.                                       | Having **comprehensive health insurance** is essential in countries with high medical costs.      |
| **Out-of-pocket expenses**                     | Các chi phí tự trả                               | Medical costs that an individual must pay themselves because they are not covered by insurance.          | Even with insurance, patients may still face significant **out-of-pocket expenses**.              |
| **An immense strain on the healthcare system** | Một gánh nặng khổng lồ cho hệ thống y tế         | A very heavy burden on a country's healthcare services and resources.                                    | An aging population places an **immense strain on the healthcare system**.                        |
| **Catastrophic health expenditure**            | Chi tiêu y tế thảm họa                           | Healthcare costs that are so high they can push a household into poverty.                                | A serious illness can lead to **catastrophic health expenditure** for families without insurance. |
| **A barrier to accessing care**                | Một rào cản trong việc tiếp cận chăm sóc y tế    | An obstacle that prevents people from getting the medical care they need.                                | High cost is a significant **barrier to accessing care** for the uninsured.                       |
| **Medical debt**                               | Nợ y tế                                          | Debt incurred by an individual due to health care costs.                                                 | **Medical debt** is a leading cause of bankruptcy in some countries.                              |
| **To ration healthcare**                       | Phân bổ hạn chế dịch vụ y tế                     | To control the supply of healthcare services because they are limited, often by delaying treatment.      | In underfunded systems, hospitals may be forced **to ration healthcare**.                         |
