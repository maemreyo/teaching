### 👕 **Phong cách & Lựa chọn Cá nhân (Personal Style & Choices)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **To express your identity through fashion** | Thể hiện bản sắc qua thời trang | To show who you are through your clothes. | Many teenagers **express their identity through fashion**. |
| **To embrace a style** | Chấp nhận/theo một phong cách | To accept and adopt a particular fashion style. | He decided **to embrace a more minimalist style** with neutral colors. |
| **A minimalist style** | Phong cách tối giản | A simple style with few colors and clean lines. | Her home and wardrobe both reflect her **minimalist style**. |
| **Vintage clothing** | Quần áo cổ điển (cũ) | Old clothes from a previous era, often valued for their quality and style. | She loves shopping for **vintage clothing** in second-hand stores. |
| **To mix and match styles** | Phối hợp các phong cách | To combine different styles of clothing to create a unique look. | She has a talent for **mixing and matching** high-end and vintage pieces. |
| **To dress for the occasion** | Ăn mặc phù hợp với dịp (sự kiện) | To wear clothes suitable for a particular event. | It is important **to dress for the occasion** when attending a formal wedding. |
| **To dress to impress** | Ăn mặc để gây ấn tượng | To wear clothes that will make others admire you. | He always **dresses to impress** for important business meetings. |
| **A fashion faux pas** | Một lỗi thời trang (sai lầm khi ăn mặc) | An embarrassing mistake in dressing or style. | Wearing socks with sandals is considered a classic **fashion faux pas**. |
| **Outfit coordination** | Phối đồ (sự sắp xếp hài hòa) | The skill of choosing clothes and accessories that look good together. | Her **outfit coordination** is always impeccable. |
| **To revamp one's look** | Thay đổi diện mạo, cải tổ phong cách | To completely change your personal style. | After the breakup, she decided **to revamp her look** with a new haircut and wardrobe. |

### 📈 **Xu hướng & Ngành Công nghiệp (Trends & The Industry)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **To follow the latest trends** | Chạy theo những xu hướng mới nhất | To wear the newest styles that are currently popular. | Many young people feel pressure **to follow the latest trends** on social media. |
| **To be out of fashion / to go out of fashion** | Lỗi thời, không còn hợp mốt | To no longer be popular or fashionable. | That style of jeans **went out of fashion** a decade ago. |
| **To be the height of fashion** | Là đỉnh cao của thời trang, rất hợp mốt | To be extremely fashionable and popular at a particular time. | In the 1920s, flapper dresses were **the height of fashion**. |
| **To set a trend** | Tạo ra một xu hướng | To start a new popular style that others begin to copy. | The influential designer is known for her ability **to set a trend**. |
| **A fashion-forward design** | Một thiết kế thời thượng, đi đầu xu hướng | A design that is modern and ahead of current trends. | The architect is known for his **fashion-forward designs**. |
| **A seasonal collection** | Một bộ sưu tập theo mùa | A set of new clothes designed by a fashion house for a specific season. | The brand just launched its autumn/winter **seasonal collection**. |
| **High-end fashion / Haute couture** | Thời trang cao cấp | Expensive, high-quality, custom-fitted clothing made by top designers. | She works as a model in the world of **high-end fashion**. |
| **A designer label** | Một thương hiệu thiết kế (hàng cao cấp) | A brand that sells expensive, high-quality clothes. | He prefers to invest in timeless pieces from well-known **designer labels**. |
| **Fast fashion** | Thời trang nhanh | Inexpensive clothing produced rapidly by mass-market retailers in response to the latest trends. | **Fast fashion** has been criticized for its environmental impact and poor labor practices. |
| **Sustainable fashion** | Thời trang bền vững | Clothes made in an environmentally and socially responsible way. | There is a growing movement towards **sustainable fashion** among conscious consumers. |

### ✨ **Văn hóa & Tác động Xã hội (Culture & Social Impact)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A fashion icon** | Một biểu tượng thời trang | A person who is famous for their sense of style and influences others. | Princess Diana remains a global **fashion icon**. |
| **A fashion statement** | Một tuyên ngôn thời trang | An item of clothing worn to attract attention and express one's views or personality. | Her brightly colored suit was a bold **fashion statement** at the formal event. |
| **Street style** | Phong cách đường phố | Fashion worn by ordinary people in public, as opposed to on the runway. | Fashion photographers often find their best inspiration from **street style**. |
| **Celebrity endorsement** | Quảng cáo có người nổi tiếng bảo trợ | When a famous person is paid to promote a product or brand. | The brand's sales soared after a major **celebrity endorsement**. |
| **To reflect cultural identity** | Phản ánh bản sắc văn hóa | To show the values, beliefs, and customs of a culture through clothing. | Traditional attire often **reflects the cultural identity** of a nation. |
| **To conform to a dress code** | Tuân thủ quy định về trang phục | To follow the rules about what you are allowed to wear in a certain place. | Employees are required **to conform to a strict dress code**. |
| **Wearable technology** | Công nghệ đeo được | Electronic devices that can be worn as accessories, such as smartwatches. | **Wearable technology** is blurring the line between fashion and electronics. |
| **Fashion waste problems** | Vấn đề rác thải thời trang | The environmental issue of unwanted clothing being thrown away in large quantities. | The fast fashion industry is a major contributor to global **fashion waste problems**. |
| **Pop culture fashion** | Thời trang văn hóa đại chúng | Styles influenced by popular movies, music, and media. | T-shirts with band logos are a classic example of **pop culture fashion**. |
| **Youth fashion trends** | Xu hướng thời trang của giới trẻ | The styles that are popular among young people at a particular time. | Social media platforms like TikTok are now the biggest drivers of **youth fashion trends**. |
