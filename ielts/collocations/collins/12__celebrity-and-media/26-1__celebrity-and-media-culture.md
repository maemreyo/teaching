### ⭐ **Bản chất của Sự nổi tiếng (The Nature of Fame)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Celebrity status** | Vị thế người nổi tiếng | The state of being famous. | She achieved **celebrity status** after her debut album went viral. |
| **An A-list actor** | Một diễn viên hạng A | A top, very famous, and successful actor. | The film features two **A-list actors** in the leading roles. |
| **A high-profile celebrity** | Một người nổi tiếng có tiếng tăm | A very famous and well-known person who receives a lot of media attention. | The charity event was attended by many **high-profile celebrities**. |
| **An overnight sensation** | Một hiện tượng nổi tiếng sau một đêm | Someone who becomes very famous in a very short time. | The young singer became an **overnight sensation** after winning the competition. |
| **To monetize one's fame** | Kiếm tiền từ sự nổi tiếng | To make money from being famous, often through endorsements or merchandise. | Many YouTubers **monetize their fame** through brand sponsorships. |
| **Star power** | Sức hút / ảnh hưởng của ngôi sao | The influence and appeal that a famous performer has. | The movie's box office success was largely due to the **star power** of its lead actress. |
| **A cult following** | Một lượng fan hâm mộ cuồng nhiệt | A small but very dedicated and passionate group of fans. | The independent film didn't make much money but developed a **cult following**. |
| **A fan base** | Lượng người hâm mộ | The community of people who admire a particular celebrity or group. | The band has a loyal **fan base** that has supported them for decades. |
| **Fan loyalty** | Sự trung thành của người hâm mộ | The dedication of fans to a celebrity, brand, or team. | The artist's success is a testament to her incredible **fan loyalty**. |
| **To go viral** | Lan truyền chóng mặt | To become extremely popular on the internet in a very short time. | The dance challenge was the first video **to go viral** on the new platform. |

### 📸 **Truyền thông & Sự chú ý của Công chúng (Media & Public Attention)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The media spotlight** | Sự chú ý của truyền thông | Intense attention from newspapers, TV, and the internet. | Young stars often struggle with the pressure of being in **the media spotlight**. |
| **Media coverage** | Sự đưa tin của truyền thông | The amount of attention that the media pays to a story. | The royal wedding received extensive **media coverage** around the world. |
| **A media frenzy** | Sự cuồng nhiệt của truyền thông | A state of great excitement and activity in the media over a particular story. | The actor's controversial statement caused a **media frenzy**. |
| **Tabloid coverage** | Tin tức lá cải | Reports in sensationalist newspapers that often focus on gossip. | He tries to avoid the intrusive **tabloid coverage** of his private life. |
| **Paparazzi culture** | Văn hóa săn ảnh | The culture of photographers who follow famous people to take pictures of them. | **Paparazzi culture** raises serious questions about privacy and harassment. |
| **To escape the spotlight** | Tránh sự chú ý của công chúng | To get away from public and media attention. | The famous singer moved to a remote island **to escape the spotlight**. |
| **A public appearance** | Xuất hiện trước công chúng | An event where a celebrity is seen in public, often for promotional purposes. | This was her first **public appearance** since the birth of her child. |
| **To exposé one's personal life** | Phơi bày đời sống cá nhân | To reveal private details about someone, often in a sensational way. | The tabloid newspaper is known for its attempts **to exposé the personal lives** of celebrities. |
| **A publicity stunt** | Một chiêu trò PR | An action done solely to get public attention. | Their supposed argument was just a **publicity stunt** to promote their new movie. |
| **Overexposure** | Sự xuất hiện quá nhiều trước công chúng | Too much public attention, which can lead to people becoming tired of a celebrity. | The actor's constant presence in the media led to **overexposure** and a decline in his popularity. |

### ✨ **Tầm ảnh hưởng & Trách nhiệm (Influence & Responsibility)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **To influence the public** | Ảnh hưởng đến công chúng | To have an effect on what people think or do. | Celebrities have a significant platform **to influence the public** on important issues. |
| **A role model for young people** | Một hình mẫu cho giới trẻ | A person whose behavior is a good example for young people to follow. | Athletes are often seen as **role models for young people**. |
| **To set a good example** | Làm một tấm gương tốt | To behave in a way that others should admire and copy. | Celebrities have a responsibility **to set a good example** for their fans. |
| **A moral compass** | La bàn đạo đức (chuẩn mực đạo đức) | A person's internal sense of what is right and wrong. | It is hoped that famous people have a strong **moral compass**. |
| **Celebrity endorsement** | Quảng cáo có người nổi tiếng bảo trợ | When a famous person publicly promotes a product or brand. | A **celebrity endorsement** can significantly boost a product's sales. |
| **Brand collaboration** | Hợp tác thương hiệu | When a celebrity works with a brand to create a product or campaign. | The sportswear company announced a new **brand collaboration** with the famous tennis player. |
| **To raise awareness about an issue** | Nâng cao nhận thức về một vấn đề | To make the public more knowledgeable and concerned about a problem. | The actress used her platform **to raise awareness about** mental health. |
| **Charitable activities / Celebrity charity work** | Các hoạt động từ thiện / Công việc từ thiện của người nổi tiếng | Actions or work done to help people in need, often by a famous person. | His **celebrity charity work** has raised millions for cancer research. |
| **To promote unrealistic standards** | Cổ xúy cho các tiêu chuẩn phi thực tế | To encourage ideals, especially of beauty or wealth, that are impossible to achieve. | Critics argue that social media influencers often **promote unrealistic standards** of living. |
| **Public image** | Hình ảnh trước công chúng | The way a person is perceived by the general public. | The politician hired a PR firm to help improve his **public image**. |
