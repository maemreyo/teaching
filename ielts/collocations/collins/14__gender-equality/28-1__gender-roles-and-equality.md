### ♀️♂️ **Vai trò & Định kiến Giới (Gender Roles & Stereotypes)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Traditional gender roles** | Các vai trò giới tính truyền thống | The conventional roles that men and women are expected to play in society. | **Traditional gender roles** often confine women to domestic duties. |
| **To challenge gender stereotypes** | Thách thức những định kiến giới tính | To question and act against oversimplified and traditional ideas about gender. | The advertising campaign was praised for **challenging gender stereotypes**. |

### ⚖️ **Bình đẳng & Phân biệt đối xử (Equality & Discrimination)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **To promote gender equality** | Thúc đẩy bình đẳng giới | To encourage and support equal rights, responsibilities, and opportunities for all genders. | The United Nations works **to promote gender equality** worldwide. |
| **To fight gender discrimination** | Chống lại sự phân biệt đối xử về giới | To work against the unfair treatment of people based on their gender. | We must all **fight gender discrimination** in our communities and workplaces. |
| **To experience gender bias** | Trải qua thành kiến giới | To be treated unfairly because of your gender, often in a subtle or unconscious way. | Many women in STEM fields **experience gender bias** in their careers. |
| **The gender pay gap** | Khoảng cách thu nhập theo giới | The difference in average earnings between men and women. | The government has introduced new laws to help close **the gender pay gap**. |
| **To shatter the glass ceiling** | Phá vỡ rào cản vô hình ("trần kính") | To break through the invisible barrier that stops women from reaching top leadership positions. | She was the first woman in her family **to shatter the glass ceiling** and become a CEO. |
| **To empower women** | Trao quyền cho phụ nữ | To give women more power, control, and confidence over their own lives. | Education is one of the most powerful tools **to empower women**. |
| **To seek parity** | Tìm kiếm sự ngang bằng/bình đẳng | To try to achieve a state of equality, especially in pay or status. | The union is **seeking parity** in pay for its male and female members. |
| **To address the imbalance** | Giải quyết sự mất cân bằng | To take action to correct an unfair situation where one group has an advantage. | We need new policies **to address the gender imbalance** in leadership roles. |
| **To expose discrimination** | Vạch trần sự phân biệt đối xử | To reveal unfair treatment so that it can be seen by the public. | The investigation **exposed widespread discrimination** within the police force. |
| **To perpetuate inequality** | Duy trì/Làm kéo dài sự bất bình đẳng | To cause a situation of inequality to continue for a long time. | Biased hiring practices can **perpetuate inequality** in the workplace. |

| **To hold a senior position** | Giữ một vị trí cấp cao | To have a high-level job in an organization. | The number of women who **hold a senior position** in the company has doubled. |
