### 📢 **Các loại hình & Chiến lược Quảng cáo (Types & Strategies of Advertising)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **To launch an advertising campaign** | Phát động một chiến dịch quảng cáo | To start a new, organized series of advertisements. | The company is preparing **to launch a major advertising campaign** for its new product. |
| **Word-of-mouth advertising** | Quảng cáo truyền miệng | When satisfied customers tell other people how much they like a product. | **Word-of-mouth advertising** is often the most effective form of promotion. |
| **Social media advertising** | Quảng cáo trên mạng xã hội | Placing advertisements on platforms like Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok. | **Social media advertising** allows companies to target very specific demographics. |
| **A misleading advertisement** | Một quảng cáo gây hiểu lầm | An advertisement that gives false or deceptive information to consumers. | The company was fined for running a **misleading advertisement**. |
| **Product placement** | Quảng cáo cài cắm (trong phim, v.v.) | The practice of placing branded products in movies or TV shows. | The film was criticized for its blatant **product placement**. |
| **A call to action (CTA)** | Lời kêu gọi hành động | The part of an advertisement that tells the consumer what to do next. | A strong advertisement always has a clear **call to action**, such as "Buy now!" or "Learn more." |
| **A target audience / demographic** | Đối tượng khán giả / nhân khẩu học mục tiêu | The specific group of people that an advertisement is aimed at. | The **target audience** for the new video game is teenagers and young adults. |
| **To raise brand awareness** | Nâng cao nhận diện thương hiệu | To make more people know about and recognize a particular brand. | The main goal of the campaign was **to raise brand awareness** in a new market. |
| **A catchy jingle / tagline** | Một bài hát / khẩu hiệu quảng cáo hấp dẫn | A short, memorable tune or phrase used in advertising. | The product's **catchy tagline** was so effective that everyone remembered it. |
| **Pop-up ads** | Quảng cáo bật lên (trên web) | Advertisements that appear in a new window on a computer screen. | Most internet users find **pop-up ads** to be extremely annoying. |

### 🤔 **Tác động & Ảnh hưởng lên Người tiêu dùng (Impact & Influence on Consumers)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **To shape consumer preferences** | Định hình thị hiếu người tiêu dùng | To have a strong influence on what consumers like and want to buy. | Advertising can **shape consumer preferences** over a long period of time. |
| **To manipulate consumer behavior** | Thao túng hành vi người tiêu dùng | To control or influence how people buy things in a clever and often unfair way. | Critics argue that some advertising techniques **manipulate consumer behavior**. |
| **To drive purchasing decisions** | Thúc đẩy các quyết định mua hàng | To be the main factor that causes someone to buy something. | A celebrity endorsement can be a powerful factor **to drive purchasing decisions**. |
| **To create unrealistic expectations** | Tạo ra những kỳ vọng phi thực tế | To make people believe something is better or more effective than it really is. | Beauty ads are often accused of **creating unrealistic expectations** about appearance. |
| **An emotional appeal** | Sự kêu gọi về mặt cảm xúc | An advertising strategy that uses emotions like fear, happiness, or nostalgia to persuade. | The charity's campaign used a powerful **emotional appeal** to encourage donations. |
| **To exploit psychological triggers** | Khai thác các tác nhân tâm lý | To use an understanding of human psychology to influence people to buy things. | Ads often **exploit psychological triggers** like the fear of missing out. |
| **To target vulnerable audiences** | Nhắm vào các đối tượng dễ bị tổn thương | To aim advertising at people who are easily influenced, such as children or the elderly. | It is considered unethical **to target vulnerable audiences** with gambling advertisements. |
| **Brand loyalty** | Lòng trung thành với thương hiệu | The tendency of a consumer to continue buying the same brand of goods. | Apple has successfully cultivated incredible **brand loyalty** among its customers. |
| **To build brand equity** | Xây dựng giá trị thương hiệu | To increase the value and recognition of a brand in the minds of consumers. | Consistent, high-quality advertising is essential **to build brand equity**. |
| **Ad fatigue** | Sự nhàm chán, mệt mỏi vì quảng cáo | The feeling of being tired or bored of seeing the same advertisement multiple times. | To avoid **ad fatigue**, the company rotates several different versions of its online ad. |
