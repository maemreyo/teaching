### 🛍️ **Khái niệm & Văn hóa Tiêu dùng (Core Concepts & Consumer Culture)**

| Collocation                                 | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                        | Short English Meaning                                                                                               | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                              |
| :------------------------------------------ | :-------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **A consumer society / culture**            | Một xã hội / văn hóa tiêu dùng          | A society in which the buying and selling of goods and services is the most important social and economic activity. | We live in a **consumer culture** that constantly encourages the acquisition of new products.                 |
| **Materialism / To be materialistic**       | Chủ nghĩa vật chất / Coi trọng vật chất | A tendency to consider material possessions and physical comfort as more important than spiritual values.           | **Materialism** is the belief that happiness can be found through the accumulation of wealth and possessions. |
| **The acquisition of material possessions** | Sự tích lũy của cải vật chất            | The act of obtaining and collecting physical items.                                                                 | Some people find fulfillment in **the acquisition of material possessions**.                                  |
| **A throw-away society**                    | Một xã hội "vứt bỏ"                     | A society strongly influenced by consumerism, leading people to discard items after a short use.                    | The problem of plastic waste is a direct result of our **throw-away society**.                                |
| **Planned obsolescence**                    | Lỗi thời có kế hoạch                    | A policy of designing a product with a limited useful life, so it will become obsolete after a certain period.      | **Planned obsolescence** in electronics forces consumers to frequently upgrade their devices.                 |
| **To keep up with the latest trends**       | Chạy theo những xu hướng mới nhất       | To stay informed about and adopt the newest styles in fashion, technology, etc.                                     | The fashion industry thrives on people's desire **to keep up with the latest trends**.                        |
| **Brand consciousness**                     | Ý thức về thương hiệu                   | An awareness of and interest in popular and prestigious brands.                                                     | **Brand consciousness** among young consumers is often driven by social media.                                |
| **Designer labels / Luxury goods**          | Hàng hiệu / Hàng xa xỉ                  | Products made by well-known, prestigious, and expensive brands.                                                     | For some, owning **designer labels** is a way to display their social status.                                 |
| **A status symbol**                         | Một biểu tượng của địa vị               | A possession that is intended to indicate its owner's high social or economic status.                               | A luxury car is often seen as the ultimate **status symbol**.                                                 |
| **Conspicuous consumption**                 | Tiêu dùng phô trương                    | Spending money on luxury goods and services to publicly display economic power.                                     | The purchase of superyachts is a form of **conspicuous consumption** among the ultra-wealthy.                 |

### 💸 **Nguyên nhân & Động lực (Causes & Drivers)**

| Collocation                              | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                          | Short English Meaning                                                                                        | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                         |
| :--------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Mass production and affordability**    | Sản xuất hàng loạt và khả năng chi trả    | The manufacturing of large quantities of standardized products, making them cheaper and more accessible.     | **Mass production and affordability** have made a wide variety of goods available to the average person. |
| **Persuasive advertising and marketing** | Quảng cáo và tiếp thị có tính thuyết phục | Campaigns designed to skillfully influence people's feelings and persuade them to buy products.              | **Persuasive advertising and marketing** can create a sense of need for non-essential items.             |
| **To be bombarded with advertisements**  | Bị "dội bom" bởi quảng cáo                | To be constantly exposed to a very large number of advertisements.                                           | Every day, we **are bombarded with advertisements** on TV, social media, and billboards.                 |
| **The cultivation of artificial needs**  | Sự vun trồng những nhu cầu nhân tạo       | The process by which advertising makes people feel they need things that they don't actually require.        | The role of marketing is often **the cultivation of artificial needs** for new products.                 |
| **Peer pressure and social comparison**  | Áp lực từ bạn bè và sự so sánh xã hội     | The influence from one's peers and the tendency to compare oneself to others.                                | **Peer pressure and social comparison** can drive young people to demand the latest gadgets.             |
| **To keep up with the Joneses**          | Đua đòi, cố gắng bằng bạn bằng bè         | The social pressure to maintain a similar or better material standard of living as one's neighbors or peers. | The desire **to "keep up with the Joneses"** fuels a cycle of competitive consumption.                   |
| **The rise of disposable income**        | Sự gia tăng thu nhập khả dụng             | An increase in the amount of money a household has available for spending and saving after taxes.            | **The rise of disposable income** in developed countries has fueled consumer spending.                   |
| **Easy access to credit**                | Việc dễ dàng tiếp cận tín dụng            | The widespread availability of credit cards and loans, making it easy to borrow money.                       | **Easy access to credit** encourages people to spend money they don't have.                              |
| **The influence of celebrity culture**   | Ảnh hưởng của văn hóa người nổi tiếng     | The impact that famous people have on public tastes and consumer behavior.                                   | **The influence of celebrity culture** is evident in fashion and beauty trends.                          |
| **The desire for social status**         | Mong muốn có địa vị xã hội                | The wish to have a high rank or position in society, often displayed through possessions.                    | **The desire for social status** is a powerful psychological driver of consumerism.                      |

### 💔 **Tác động lên Cá nhân & Xã hội (Individual & Social Impacts)**

| Collocation                                         | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                                        | Short English Meaning                                                                                      | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                    |
| :-------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------ | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **To equate happiness with material wealth**        | Đánh đồng hạnh phúc với sự giàu có vật chất             | To believe that being happy is the same as having a lot of money and possessions.                          | Our society often makes the mistake of **equating happiness with material wealth**.                 |
| **A perpetual cycle of desire and dissatisfaction** | Một vòng luẩn quẩn của ham muốn và bất mãn              | A continuous loop of wanting new things, feeling temporary pleasure, and then becoming dissatisfied again. | Consumerism can trap people in a **perpetual cycle of desire and dissatisfaction**.                 |
| **To live beyond one's means**                      | Sống vượt quá khả năng tài chính                        | To spend more money than you earn.                                                                         | Credit cards make it easy for people **to live beyond their means** and fall into debt.             |
| **The accumulation of debt**                        | Sự tích lũy nợ nần                                      | The process of building up an amount of money that you owe over time.                                      | Many people face **the accumulation of debt** from trying to maintain a materialistic lifestyle.    |
| **A superficial sense of fulfillment**              | Một cảm giác mãn nguyện hời hợt                         | A shallow and temporary feeling of satisfaction.                                                           | Buying new things may provide a **superficial sense of fulfillment**, but it rarely lasts.          |
| **The erosion of traditional values**               | Sự xói mòn các giá trị truyền thống                     | The gradual weakening of long-established principles like community, thrift, and family.                   | Critics argue that materialism has led to **the erosion of traditional values**.                    |
| **To place a high value on possessions**            | Đặt nặng giá trị vào của cải                            | To believe that owning things is extremely important.                                                      | A materialistic person tends **to place a high value on possessions** rather than on relationships. |
| **The neglect of interpersonal relationships**      | Sự sao nhãng các mối quan hệ giữa các cá nhân           | The failure to give enough attention to one's relationships with other people.                             | An obsession with acquiring wealth can lead to **the neglect of interpersonal relationships**.      |
| **A source of social competition and envy**         | Một nguồn gốc của sự cạnh tranh và ghen tị trong xã hội | Something that causes people to compete with and feel jealous of each other.                               | The display of wealth on social media has become a major **source of social competition and envy**. |
| **A decline in personal well-being**                | Sự suy giảm sức khỏe và hạnh phúc cá nhân               | A reduction in the overall health and happiness of an individual.                                          | Studies have linked high levels of materialism to a **decline in personal well-being**.             |

### 🌍 **Tác động lên Môi trường (Environmental Impacts)**

| Collocation                                             | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                                     | Short English Meaning                                                                                   | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                              |
| :------------------------------------------------------ | :--------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **The overconsumption of natural resources**            | Sự tiêu thụ quá mức tài nguyên thiên nhiên           | The use of natural resources at a rate faster than they can be replenished.                             | Our consumer lifestyle leads to **the overconsumption of the world's natural resources**.                     |
| **To generate vast amounts of waste**                   | Tạo ra lượng rác thải khổng lồ                       | To produce extremely large quantities of rubbish.                                                       | A throw-away society **generates vast amounts of** household **waste**, much of which ends up in landfills.   |
| **The depletion of finite/non-renewable resources**     | Sự cạn kiệt các tài nguyên hữu hạn/không thể tái tạo | The reduction and exhaustion of resources that are limited in quantity, like fossil fuels and minerals. | Mass production contributes to **the depletion of non-renewable resources**.                                  |
| **To exacerbate the landfill crisis**                   | Làm trầm trọng thêm cuộc khủng hoảng bãi rác         | To make the problem of overflowing rubbish disposal sites even worse.                                   | Discarded electronics and single-use plastics **exacerbate the landfill crisis**.                             |
| **To have a large ecological/carbon footprint**         | Có dấu chân sinh thái/carbon lớn                     | To have a significant negative impact on the environment, measured in resource use and CO2 emissions.   | A lifestyle based on high consumption inevitably **has a large ecological footprint**.                        |
| **Unsustainable consumption patterns**                  | Các mô hình tiêu dùng không bền vững                 | Ways of using resources that cannot be continued in the long term without causing environmental harm.   | We must shift towards more sustainable consumption patterns to protect the planet.                            |
| **A major driver of environmental degradation**         | Một động lực chính của sự suy thoái môi trường       | A primary cause of the deterioration of the natural environment.                                        | Consumerism is a **major driver of environmental degradation** worldwide.                                     |
| **The extraction of raw materials**                     | Việc khai thác nguyên liệu thô                       | The process of removing natural materials like minerals, oil, and timber from the Earth.                | **The extraction of raw materials** for consumer goods often involves destructive mining and deforestation.   |
| **The pollution from manufacturing and transportation** | Ô nhiễm từ sản xuất và vận chuyển                    | The release of harmful substances into the environment during the production and shipping of goods.     | We must consider **the pollution from manufacturing and transportation** associated with the products we buy. |
| **E-waste (electronic waste)**                          | Rác thải điện tử                                     | Discarded electrical or electronic devices.                                                             | The rapid pace of technological upgrades has created a massive problem of **e-waste**.                        |
