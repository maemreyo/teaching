### 🌍 **Toàn cầu hóa & Sự Kết nối (Globalization & Connectivity)**

| Collocation                                    | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                             | Short English Meaning                                                                                  | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                     |
| :--------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **The process of globalization**               | Quá trình toàn cầu hóa                       | The process by which businesses, ideas, and cultures start to operate on a global scale.               | **The process of globalization** has been accelerated by advances in technology and transportation.  |
| **An increasingly interconnected world**       | Một thế giới ngày càng kết nối               | A world where countries and people are more and more linked through trade, communication, and culture. | We live in an **increasingly interconnected world**, where global events have local impacts.         |
| **The global village**                         | Ngôi làng toàn cầu                           | The concept that the world is becoming like one large community due to electronic media.               | Mass media and the internet have turned our planet into a **global village**.                        |
| **The free flow of goods, capital, and ideas** | Dòng chảy tự do của hàng hóa, vốn và ý tưởng | The ability for products, money, and concepts to move easily across international borders.             | Globalization facilitates the **free flow of goods, capital, and ideas**.                            |
| **To break down geographical barriers**        | Phá vỡ các rào cản địa lý                    | To reduce or eliminate the difficulties and limitations imposed by distance.                           | Air travel and the internet have helped **to break down geographical barriers**.                     |
| **A globalized economy**                       | Một nền kinh tế toàn cầu hóa                 | An international economy where national economies are increasingly interdependent.                     | In a **globalized economy**, multinational corporations operate across many different countries.     |
| **Multinational corporations (MNCs)**          | Các tập đoàn đa quốc gia                     | Large companies that own or control production of goods or services in two or more countries.          | **Multin-ational corporations** like McDonald's and Coca-Cola are powerful symbols of globalization. |
| **Global supply chains**                       | Các chuỗi cung ứng toàn cầu                  | The worldwide network of businesses and processes involved in producing and distributing a product.    | The pandemic revealed the vulnerability of our complex **global supply chains**.                     |
| **Cultural diffusion**                         | Sự khuếch tán văn hóa                        | The spread of cultural beliefs and social activities from one group of people to another.              | The spread of sushi restaurants worldwide is a clear example of **cultural diffusion**.              |
| **The global spread of...**                    | Sự lan rộng toàn cầu của...                  | The dissemination of something all over the world.                                                     | We are witnessing the **global spread of** Western consumer culture.                                 |

### 획일화 **Đồng nhất & Thống trị Văn hóa (Cultural Homogenization & Dominance)**

| Collocation                                 | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                              | Short English Meaning                                                                                                          | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                               |
| :------------------------------------------ | :-------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Cultural homogenization**                 | Sự đồng nhất văn hóa                          | The process by which local cultures are transformed or absorbed by a dominant outside culture.                                 | A major concern is that globalization will lead to **cultural homogenization**.                                |
| **The dominance of Western culture**        | Sự thống trị của văn hóa phương Tây           | The powerful and widespread influence of Western cultural norms, values, and products.                                         | The global popularity of Hollywood films is often cited as an example of the **dominance of Western culture**. |
| **The erosion of cultural identity**        | Sự xói mòn bản sắc văn hóa                    | The gradual weakening and loss of a culture's unique character, traditions, and sense of self.                                 | Many people fear the **erosion of their national cultural identity** due to pervasive foreign influences.      |
| **To lose one's unique cultural character** | Đánh mất đi nét đặc trưng văn hóa độc đáo     | For a place or group to no longer be special or distinct in its cultural practices.                                            | If every city has the same global brands, they will **lose their unique cultural character**.                  |
| **The marginalization of local traditions** | Sự bên lề hóa các truyền thống địa phương     | The process of making local traditions seem unimportant or pushing them to the edge of society.                                | The influence of global media can lead to the **marginalization of local traditions**.                         |
| **A bland, uniform global culture**         | Một nền văn hóa toàn cầu nhạt nhẽo, đồng nhất | A single, boring, and similar culture that lacks diversity and richness.                                                       | Critics argue that consumerism will lead to a **bland, uniform global culture**.                               |
| **Cultural imperialism**                    | Chủ nghĩa đế quốc văn hóa                     | The practice of promoting and imposing a culture, usually that of a politically powerful nation, over a less powerful society. | Some countries actively resist what they perceive as **cultural imperialism** from the West.                   |
| **To threaten cultural diversity**          | Đe dọa sự đa dạng văn hóa                     | To pose a danger to the existence of a variety of cultures and cultural expressions.                                           | The dominance of a single language like English can **threaten linguistic and cultural diversity**.            |
| **The standardization of tastes**           | Sự tiêu chuẩn hóa thị hiếu                    | The process by which people's preferences in things like food, music, and fashion become more similar.                         | Fast-food chains contribute to the **standardization of tastes** around the world.                             |
| **The decline of indigenous languages**     | Sự suy giảm của các ngôn ngữ bản địa          | The process of native languages being spoken by fewer and fewer people, often leading to extinction.                           | Globalization is a contributing factor in **the decline of indigenous languages**.                             |

### ✨ **Giao thoa & Đa dạng Văn hóa (Cultural Exchange & Diversity)**

| Collocation                                     | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                                   | Short English Meaning                                                                             | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                           |
| :---------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Cross-cultural exchange**                     | Giao lưu chéo văn hóa                              | The sharing of ideas, customs, and beliefs between people from different cultural backgrounds.    | Globalization can foster greater **cross-cultural exchange** and understanding.            |
| **A vibrant multicultural society**             | Một xã hội đa văn hóa sôi động                     | A society that is full of energy and is composed of many different ethnic and cultural groups.    | Cities like London and Toronto are proud of being **vibrant multicultural societies**.     |
| **To promote and celebrate cultural diversity** | Thúc đẩy và tôn vinh sự đa dạng văn hóa            | To actively encourage and show appreciation for a variety of cultures.                            | International festivals are a great way **to promote and celebrate cultural diversity**.   |
| **Increased exposure to different cultures**    | Sự tiếp xúc gia tăng với các nền văn hóa khác nhau | The opportunity to experience a wider range of different cultures.                                | Through travel and the internet, we have **increased exposure to different cultures**.     |
| **A rich tapestry of cultures**                 | Một "tấm thảm" văn hóa phong phú                   | A complex and varied mixture of different cultures coexisting.                                    | Proponents argue that globalization creates a **rich tapestry of cultures**.               |
| **To embrace foreign cultures and influences**  | Đón nhận văn hóa và các ảnh hưởng từ nước ngoài    | To welcome and accept ideas, customs, and products from other countries.                          | Young people are often more willing **to embrace foreign cultures and influences**.        |
| **Cultural fusion / hybridity**                 | Sự kết hợp / lai ghép văn hóa                      | The process of mixing elements from different cultures to create something new.                   | The country's cuisine is a delicious **cultural fusion** of Asian and European influences. |
| **To foster a sense of global citizenship**     | Nuôi dưỡng ý thức công dân toàn cầu                | To encourage an identity that transcends geographical or political borders.                       | Exposure to global issues can **foster a sense of global citizenship** among young people. |
| **To challenge stereotypes and prejudices**     | Thách thức các định kiến và thành kiến             | To question and dismantle oversimplified and often negative beliefs about other groups.           | Meaningful cultural exchange can help **to challenge stereotypes and prejudices**.         |
| **The enrichment of local cultures**            | Sự làm giàu cho các nền văn hóa địa phương         | The process of local cultures becoming more interesting and diverse by adopting foreign elements. | The introduction of new musical genres can lead to **the enrichment of local cultures**.   |

### identidade **Bản sắc & Truyền thống (Identity & Tradition)**

| Collocation                                      | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                                 | Short English Meaning                                                                       | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                             |
| :----------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **To preserve one's cultural heritage**          | Gìn giữ di sản văn hóa                           | To protect the traditions, arts, and historical legacy of one's culture.                    | It is important for every country **to preserve its unique cultural heritage** in the face of globalization. |
| **A strong sense of national identity**          | Một ý thức mạnh mẽ về bản sắc dân tộc            | A powerful feeling of belonging to a particular nation and sharing its values.              | In an era of globalization, maintaining a **strong sense of national identity** can be a challenge.          |
| **To pass on traditions to the next generation** | Truyền lại các truyền thống cho thế hệ tiếp theo | To teach and ensure that traditions are continued by younger people.                        | Elders play a vital role in **passing on traditions to the next generation**.                                |
| **To resist cultural assimilation**              | Chống lại sự đồng hóa văn hóa                    | To actively oppose the process of being absorbed into a different, often dominant, culture. | Indigenous communities are fighting **to resist cultural assimilation**.                                     |
| **To maintain traditional values and customs**   | Duy trì các giá trị và phong tục truyền thống    | To continue practicing long-established beliefs and social behaviors.                       | It is possible to embrace modernity while still **maintaining traditional values and customs**.              |
| **To be a threat to local customs**              | Là một mối đe dọa đối với phong tục địa phương   | To pose a danger to the survival of local traditions.                                       | The influx of mass tourism can **be a threat to local customs** and ways of life.                            |
| **To adapt to external influences**              | Thích nghi với những ảnh hưởng từ bên ngoài      | To change and evolve in response to new ideas and practices from other cultures.            | Cultures are not static; they constantly **adapt to external influences**.                                   |
| **Cultural resilience**                          | Sức bật / khả năng phục hồi văn hóa              | The ability of a culture to maintain its core identity and practices in the face of change. | Despite centuries of colonization, the local culture has shown remarkable **cultural resilience**.           |
| **The tension between tradition and modernity**  | Sự căng thẳng giữa truyền thống và hiện đại      | The conflict between old, established ways of life and new, contemporary ideas.             | There is often a **tension between tradition and modernity** in rapidly developing countries.                |
| **To negotiate one's cultural identity**         | Đàm phán / xác định bản sắc văn hóa của mình     | The process of defining one's identity in a world with multiple cultural influences.        | Young people in multicultural societies often have **to negotiate their cultural identity**.                 |
