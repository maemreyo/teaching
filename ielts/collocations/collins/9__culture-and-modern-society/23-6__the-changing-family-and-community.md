### 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 **Cấu trúc & Vai trò Gia đình (Family Structures & Roles)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The nuclear family** | Gia đình hạt nhân | A family unit consisting of two parents and their children. | In many Western countries, **the nuclear family** has become the dominant family structure. |
| **The extended family** | Gia đình mở rộng (đa thế hệ) | A family that extends beyond the nuclear family, including grandparents, aunts, uncles, etc. | In many Asian cultures, **the extended family** plays a central role in social life. |
| **A single-parent household** | Hộ gia đình đơn thân | A household in which one parent has the primary responsibility for raising a child or children. | There has been a significant increase in the number of **single-parent households**. |
| **Changing family roles** | Sự thay đổi vai trò trong gia đình | The shifts in the traditional responsibilities and functions of family members. | **Changing family roles** mean that fathers are now more involved in childcare than ever before. |
| **A dual-income family** | Một gia đình có hai nguồn thu nhập | A family in which both parents work and earn money. | In a **dual-income family**, household chores are often shared more equally. |
| **The breadwinner** | Trụ cột gia đình (người kiếm tiền chính) | The person in a family who earns the money to support the others. | The traditional model of the man as the sole **breadwinner** is becoming less common. |
| **To provide for one's family** | Chu cấp cho gia đình | To earn enough money to support your family's needs. | His main motivation for working long hours is **to provide for his family**. |
| **Child-rearing responsibilities** | Trách nhiệm nuôi dạy con cái | The duties involved in bringing up and caring for children. | Modern couples often share **child-rearing responsibilities** more equally. |
| **The decline of the traditional family unit** | Sự suy giảm của đơn vị gia đình truyền thống | The trend of fewer people living in a traditional nuclear family structure. | Some commentators worry about **the decline of the traditional family unit**. |
| **To start a family later in life** | Lập gia đình muộn hơn | To have children at an older age than was common in the past. | Many people are choosing **to start a family later in life** in order to focus on their careers first. |

### 🏘️ **Cộng đồng & Sự gắn kết (Community & Cohesion)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A sense of community** | Ý thức cộng đồng | A feeling of fellowship and belonging with others in a particular area. | Living in a small town often provides a stronger **sense of community**. |
| **Social cohesion / social fabric** | Sự gắn kết xã hội / kết cấu xã hội | The willingness of members of a society to cooperate with each other. | Community events are important for strengthening **social cohesion**. |
| **Neighborly relations** | Mối quan hệ hàng xóm | The relationship and interactions between people who live near each other. | Good **neighborly relations** can improve the quality of life in a residential area. |
| **A close-knit community** | Một cộng đồng gắn bó, khăng khít | A community where people have close relationships and support each other. | The village is a **close-knit community** where everyone knows each other. |
| **Community spirit** | Tinh thần cộng đồng | A positive attitude and willingness to participate in community activities. | The festival was a great success, thanks to the amazing **community spirit**. |
| **Social isolation / alienation** | Sự cô lập / xa lánh xã hội | The state of feeling separated and alone from the rest of society. | The decline of local clubs can lead to **social isolation**, especially among the elderly. |
| **The breakdown of community life** | Sự tan rã của đời sống cộng đồng | The decline and failure of the social structures and relationships within a community. | Some people argue that urbanization has led to **the breakdown of community life**. |
| **To foster a sense of belonging** | Nuôi dưỡng cảm giác thân thuộc | To create an environment where people feel accepted and part of a group. | Schools should aim **to foster a sense of belonging** for all students. |
| **Local community groups** | Các nhóm cộng đồng địa phương | Organizations formed by local people to pursue a shared interest or goal. | Joining **local community groups** is a great way to meet new people. |
| **Civic participation / engagement** | Sự tham gia của công dân | The involvement of citizens in the life of their community and government. | Volunteering is a powerful form of **civic participation**. |
