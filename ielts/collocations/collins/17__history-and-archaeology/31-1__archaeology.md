### 🔬 **Phân tích & Kỹ thuật (Analysis & Techniques)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **To apply scientific techniques** | Ứng dụng các kỹ thuật khoa học | To use scientific methods to study something. | Archaeologists **apply scientific techniques** to date artifacts and analyze remains. |
| **Carbon dating / Radiocarbon dating** | Định tuổi bằng carbon / carbon phóng xạ | A scientific method to determine the age of old organic objects. | **Carbon dating** of the bones revealed they were over 5,000 years old. |
| **Geophysical survey** | Khảo sát địa vật lý | Using methods like radar to study what is under the ground before digging. | A **geophysical survey** indicated the presence of buried walls. |
| **Ground-penetrating radar (GPR)** | Radar xuyên đất | A tool used to see what is under the ground without excavating. | **Ground-penetrating radar** helped the team map the layout of the hidden city. |
| **Remote sensing** | Viễn thám | Getting information about a site from a distance, e.g., from satellites. | **Remote sensing** is used to identify potential archaeological sites in dense jungles. |
| **Stratigraphic excavation** | Khai quật theo địa tầng | A method of excavating layer by layer to understand the sequence of events. | **Stratigraphic excavation** allows for the precise dating of different cultural layers. |
| **Osteological analysis** | Phân tích di cốt học | The scientific study of bones to learn about past people. | **Osteological analysis** can reveal a person's age, sex, diet, and diseases. |
| **Stable isotope analysis** | Phân tích đồng vị bền | A method to study diet and migration patterns from bones and teeth. | **Stable isotope analysis** showed that the individuals had a diet rich in seafood. |
| **Dendrochronology** | Niên đại học vòng cây | A method of dating based on analyzing the pattern of tree rings. | They used **dendrochronology** to determine the exact year the wooden structure was built. |
| **To document excavation results** | Ghi chép kết quả khai quật | To record all the findings of a dig in a detailed and systematic way. | It is crucial for archaeologists **to document their excavation results** meticulously. |
