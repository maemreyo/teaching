### 🌉 **Mô tả & Khái niệm Cốt lõi (Describing the Gap & Core Concepts)**

| Collocation                               | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                           | Short English Meaning                                                                    | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                   |
| :---------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **The generation gap**                    | Khoảng cách thế hệ                         | A difference in attitudes, values, and beliefs between one generation and another.       | **The generation gap** is often most pronounced between teenagers and their parents.               |
| **A widening / growing chasm**            | Một hố sâu ngày càng lớn (ẩn dụ)           | A very large and increasing difference between two groups or ideas.                      | A **widening chasm** between generations can be attributed to rapid technological change.          |
| **A clash of values**                     | Sự xung đột về giá trị                     | A conflict arising from fundamentally different beliefs about what is important in life. | The debate over social norms often represents a **clash of values** between young and old.         |
| **Intergenerational conflict / friction** | Xung đột / va chạm giữa các thế hệ         | Disagreement and lack of harmony between people of different generations.                | **Intergenerational conflict** is a common theme in literature and sociology.                      |
| **To belong to different generations**    | Thuộc về các thế hệ khác nhau              | To be part of distinct age groups in society with their own unique experiences.          | My parents and I **belong to different generations**, so we naturally see the world differently.   |
| **The older / younger generation**        | Thế hệ lớn tuổi / trẻ tuổi hơn             | The cohort of people who are older / younger in a society.                               | **The older generation** often values tradition, while **the younger generation** embraces change. |
| **A lack of mutual understanding**        | Sự thiếu thấu hiểu lẫn nhau                | A failure between two or more people or groups to understand each other's perspectives.  | **A lack of mutual understanding** is the root cause of many family arguments.                     |
| **A communication breakdown**             | Sự đổ vỡ trong giao tiếp                   | A failure to exchange information or feelings effectively.                               | The generation gap is often exacerbated by a **communication breakdown**.                          |
| **Divergent worldviews**                  | Các thế giới quan khác biệt                | Fundamentally different ways of seeing and interpreting the world.                       | Having grown up in different eras, they hold **divergent worldviews**.                             |
| **Generational cohorts**                  | Các nhóm thế hệ (Gen Z, Millennials, etc.) | Groups of people born and living during the same time period.                            | Different **generational cohorts**, like Millennials and Baby Boomers, often have distinct values. |

### 🔥 **Nguyên nhân & Lĩnh vực Bất đồng (Causes & Areas of Disagreement)**

| Collocation                                  | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                                  | Short English Meaning                                                                                   | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                                |
| :------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------ | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Rapid technological advancement**          | Sự tiến bộ công nghệ nhanh chóng                  | The very fast pace of progress in technology, creating new ways of living and communicating.            | **Rapid technological advancement** is a major factor contributing to the generation gap.                       |
| **Shifting social norms and values**         | Các chuẩn mực và giá trị xã hội đang thay đổi     | The evolution of a society's unwritten rules of behavior and its core beliefs.                          | **Shifting social norms** regarding marriage and careers often cause friction between generations.              |
| **To hold traditional / conservative views** | Giữ vững quan điểm truyền thống / bảo thủ         | To have beliefs and opinions that are resistant to change and favor established traditions.             | My grandparents still **hold traditional views** about family roles.                                            |
| **To have a liberal / progressive outlook**  | Có một cái nhìn tự do / tiến bộ                   | To have up-to-date ideas and attitudes that are open to change and social reform.                       | Young people tend **to have a more liberal outlook** on social issues.                                          |
| **Vastly different life experiences**        | Những trải nghiệm sống khác biệt một trời một vực | Having lived through completely different historical events, economic conditions, and cultural moments. | Having lived through war and poverty, they have **vastly different life experiences** from their grandchildren. |
| **Evolving attitudes towards...**            | Thái độ đang thay đổi đối với...                  | The gradual change in public or personal opinions about a particular subject.                           | **Evolving attitudes towards** work-life balance can be a point of contention.                                  |
| **The digital divide**                       | Khoảng cách số                                    | The gap between those who have ready access to computers and the internet, and those who do not.        | **The digital divide** can create a communication barrier between grandparents and their grandchildren.         |
| **Different cultural reference points**      | Các điểm tham chiếu văn hóa khác nhau             | The different music, films, books, and historical events that shape a generation's identity.            | Their **different cultural reference points** mean they often don't understand each other's jokes.              |
| **Political ideologies**                     | Các hệ tư tưởng chính trị                         | The sets of beliefs and ideas that shape a person's political opinions.                                 | Disagreements over **political ideologies** are a common source of intergenerational conflict.                  |
| **Work ethic**                               | Đạo đức làm việc                                  | A belief in the moral benefit and importance of work and its inherent ability to strengthen character.  | There is often a perceived difference in **work ethic** between older and younger employees.                    |

### 📉 **Hậu quả & Tác động (Consequences & Effects)**

| Collocation                                          | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                                          | Short English Meaning                                                                                  | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                       |
| :--------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A sense of alienation and isolation**              | Cảm giác bị xa lánh và cô lập                             | A feeling of being disconnected from others and not belonging to any group.                            | Teenagers may feel a **sense of alienation** if their parents do not understand their world.           |
| **Mutual misunderstanding and mistrust**             | Sự hiểu lầm và thiếu tin tưởng lẫn nhau                   | A situation where different groups fail to understand each other and become suspicious of one another. | The conflict arose from **mutual misunder-standing and mistrust**.                                     |
| **To feel disconnected from society**                | Cảm thấy mất kết nối với xã hội                           | To feel that you are not a part of or in tune with the wider community.                                | Many elderly people **feel disconnected from** a world dominated by social media.                      |
| **The erosion of family bonds**                      | Sự xói mòn tình cảm gia đình                              | The gradual weakening of the emotional connections and relationships within a family.                  | Constant conflict between generations can lead to **the erosion of family bonds**.                     |
| **A lack of respect for elders and their wisdom**    | Sự thiếu tôn trọng người lớn tuổi và sự thông thái của họ | Not showing due respect to older people and the knowledge they have gained from experience.            | Some argue that modern society suffers from a **lack of respect for elders and their wisdom**.         |
| **Social fragmentation**                             | Sự phân mảnh xã hội                                       | The process by which a society divides into smaller, separate, and often uncooperative groups.         | A wide generation gap can contribute to **social fragmentation**.                                      |
| **The loss of intergenerational knowledge transfer** | Sự mất mát trong việc chuyển giao kiến thức liên thế hệ   | The failure to pass down skills, stories, and wisdom from older to younger generations.                | **The loss of intergenerational knowledge transfer** is a significant cultural cost.                   |
| **To create a polarized society**                    | Tạo ra một xã hội phân cực                                | To cause a society to divide into two sharply opposing groups.                                         | Political disagreements between generations can help **to create a polarized society**.                |
| **Strained family relationships**                    | Các mối quan hệ gia đình căng thẳng                       | Family relationships that are tense, difficult, and lacking in warmth.                                 | The generation gap often leads to **strained family relationships**.                                   |
| **A failure to learn from the past**                 | Sự thất bại trong việc học hỏi từ quá khứ                 | Not using the lessons and experiences of previous generations to inform present decisions.             | A complete rejection of the older generation's views can lead to a **failure to learn from the past**. |

### ✅ **Thu hẹp Khoảng cách & Giải pháp (Bridging the Gap & Solutions)**

| Collocation                                    | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                            | Short English Meaning                                                                                      | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                              |
| :--------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------ | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **To bridge / narrow the generation gap**      | Thu hẹp khoảng cách thế hệ                  | To reduce the differences and improve understanding between older and younger people.                      | Open communication is the best way **to bridge the generation gap**.                          |
| **To foster mutual understanding and respect** | Thúc đẩy sự thấu hiểu và tôn trọng lẫn nhau | To encourage people of different generations to understand and value each other.                           | Intergenerational activities can help **to foster mutual understanding and respect**.         |
| **Open and honest dialogue**                   | Đối thoại cởi mở và trung thực              | A frank and sincere conversation where all parties can express their views without fear.                   | Families should encourage **open and honest dialogue** to prevent misunderstandings.          |
| **To find common ground**                      | Tìm ra điểm chung                           | To discover shared interests, beliefs, or opinions despite other differences.                              | Despite their age difference, they managed **to find common ground** in their love for music. |
| **To share life experiences**                  | Chia sẻ kinh nghiệm sống                    | To tell each other about the significant events and lessons from one's life.                               | When different generations **share life experiences**, they learn from one another.           |
| **To respect differing opinions**              | Tôn trọng các ý kiến khác biệt              | To accept and value that others have different viewpoints, even if you do not agree.                       | The key is **to respect differing opinions** on topics like politics and lifestyle.           |
| **To see things from a different perspective** | Nhìn nhận sự việc từ một góc độ khác        | To try to understand a situation from another person's point of view.                                      | Trying **to see things from your parents' perspective** can be very enlightening.             |
| **Intergenerational programs and activities**  | Các chương trình và hoạt động liên thế hệ   | Activities that are designed to bring people from different generations together.                          | Schools can organize **intergenerational programs** involving students and senior citizens.   |
| **Reverse mentoring**                          | Cố vấn ngược                                | A system where a younger employee mentors an older, more senior employee, often on topics like technology. | **Reverse mentoring** can be a powerful tool for bridging the skills gap in the workplace.    |
| **Valuing the wisdom of elders**               | Coi trọng sự thông thái của người lớn tuổi  | To appreciate and respect the knowledge and good judgment that older people have gained from experience.   | Modern society should not forget the importance of **valuing the wisdom of elders**.          |
