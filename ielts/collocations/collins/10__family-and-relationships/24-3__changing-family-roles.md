### 🏛️ **Vai trò Truyền thống (Traditional Roles)**

| Collocation                                                 | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                         | Short English Meaning                                                                                                 | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                            |
| :---------------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **The traditional family model / The nuclear family model** | Mô hình gia đình truyền thống / hạt nhân | The historical model of a family unit consisting of a working father, a homemaker mother, and their children.         | In **the traditional family model**, roles were often rigidly defined by gender.            |
| **The male breadwinner model**                              | Mô hình người đàn ông trụ cột kinh tế    | A societal model where the man is the sole or primary earner for the family.                                          | **The male breadwinner model** has become less prevalent in recent decades.                 |
| **The female homemaker role**                               | Vai trò người phụ nữ nội trợ             | The traditional role of a woman as the manager of the household and primary caregiver for children.                   | Historically, **the female homemaker role** was seen as a woman's primary duty.             |
| **Clearly delineated / defined gender roles**               | Các vai trò giới được phân định rõ ràng  | Social roles for men and women that are distinct, separate, and well-understood.                                      | Traditional societies often had **clearly delineated gender roles**.                        |
| **Gender stereotypes**                                      | Định kiến giới                           | Oversimplified and often inaccurate beliefs about the characteristics and roles of men and women.                     | Education is key to challenging outdated **gender stereotypes**.                            |
| **A patriarchal structure**                                 | Một cấu trúc phụ quyền                   | A social system in which men hold primary power and predominate in roles of political leadership and moral authority. | In a **patriarchal structure**, family decisions are typically made by the husband.         |
| **To be confined to the domestic sphere**                   | Bị giới hạn trong phạm vi gia đình       | To be restricted to the home and family life, with limited participation in public or professional life.              | For centuries, women were largely **confined to the domestic sphere**.                      |
| **To fulfill one's traditional role**                       | Hoàn thành vai trò truyền thống của mình | To perform the duties and behaviors expected of one's gender in a traditional society.                                | She felt pressure from her family **to fulfill her traditional role** as a wife and mother. |
| **The head of the household**                               | Chủ hộ                                   | The person who is considered the leader and main decision-maker in a family.                                          | The father was traditionally seen as **the head of the household**.                         |
| **Domestic responsibilities**                               | Các trách nhiệm trong gia đình           | The duties and tasks related to managing a home, such as cooking, cleaning, and childcare.                            | Women were traditionally expected to handle all **domestic responsibilities**.              |

| **A dual-income / dual-earner family**              | Gia đình có hai nguồn thu nhập                           | A family in which both partners have paid jobs.                                                      | **Dual-income families** are now the norm in many parts of the world.                           |
| **A fundamental shift in traditional roles**        | Một sự chuyển dịch cơ bản trong các vai trò truyền thống | A profound and significant change away from historical family roles.                                 | We are witnessing a **fundamental shift in traditional roles** within the family.               |
| **To share parental and domestic responsibilities** | Chia sẻ trách nhiệm làm cha mẹ và gia đình               | To divide the duties of raising children and managing the household between partners.                | Modern couples are more likely **to share parental and domestic responsibilities**.             |
| **An equitable division of labor**                  | Một sự phân chia lao động công bằng                      | A fair and just distribution of tasks and chores within the household.                               | An egalitarian marriage is one where partners strive for an **equitable division of labor**.    |

| **A career-oriented woman / a working mother**      | Người phụ nữ hướng về sự nghiệp / người mẹ đi làm        | A woman who prioritizes her professional career / a mother who has a paid job.                       | **Working mothers** often have to juggle their careers with family commitments.                 |
| **An egalitarian partnership / marriage**           | Một mối quan hệ đối tác / hôn nhân bình đẳng             | A relationship where partners are considered equal and share power and responsibilities.             | They strive to build an **egalitarian partnership** based on mutual respect.                    |
| **The blurring of traditional gender roles**        | Sự xóa nhòa các vai trò giới truyền thống                | The process of gender roles becoming less distinct and more flexible.                                | **The blurring of traditional gender roles** is a key feature of the modern family.             |
| **Co-parenting**                                    | Cùng nuôi dạy con                                        | The practice of divorced or separated parents sharing the responsibility for raising their children. | **Co-parenting** requires a high level of communication and cooperation.                        |
