### 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 **Tình bạn & Các mối quan hệ Xã hội (Friendships & Social Connections)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **To form a strong / lasting friendship** | Xây dựng một tình bạn bền chặt / lâu dài | To develop a friendship that is powerful and continues for a long time. | We **formed a lasting friendship** during our time at university together. |
| **A circle of friends** | Một nhóm bạn | A group of friends that you regularly spend time with. | She has a wide **circle of friends** with very diverse interests. |
| **A casual acquaintance vs. a close friend** | Một người quen sơ và một người bạn thân | Someone you know but not very well vs. a very good friend that you know well. | I have many **casual acquaintances** at work, but only a few **close friends**. |
| **To strike up a friendship with someone** | Bắt đầu một tình bạn với ai đó | To start a friendship with someone. | I **struck up a friendship with** a fellow traveler on the train to Lisbon. |
| **To enjoy someone's company** | Thích ở bên cạnh ai đó | To like spending time with someone. | He's very easygoing and I really **enjoy his company**. |
| **To have a lot in common** | Có nhiều điểm chung | To share similar interests, beliefs, attitudes, or opinions. | We became friends instantly because we **have a lot in common**. |
| **To lose touch with someone** | Mất liên lạc với ai đó | To stop communicating with someone, usually because they live far away. | It's sad how you can **lose touch with** old school friends over the years. |
| **To drift apart** | Trở nên xa cách | To gradually become less friendly or close to someone. | We were best friends in college, but we started **to drift apart** after graduation. |
| **A shoulder to cry on** | Một bờ vai để nương tựa (người để tâm sự) | Someone who is willing to listen to your problems and give you sympathy. | When I'm having a tough time, my best friend is always there as **a shoulder to cry on**. |
| **To see eye to eye on most things** | Đồng quan điểm về hầu hết mọi thứ | To agree on or have the same opinion about most things. | We have a strong friendship because we **see eye to eye on most things**. |

### 💬 **Giao tiếp & Tương tác Xã hội (Communication & Social Interaction)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **To socialize with someone** | Giao lưu với ai đó | To spend time with people for enjoyment. | I enjoy **socializing with** my colleagues outside of work. |
| **To have a deep and meaningful conversation** | Có một cuộc trò chuyện sâu sắc và ý nghĩa | To have a conversation that is very personal and significant. | We stayed up all night **having a deep and meaningful conversation**. |
| **To build rapport with someone** | Xây dựng mối quan hệ tốt với ai đó | To create a good and friendly relationship with someone. | A good manager knows how **to build rapport with** their team members. |
| **To break the ice** | Phá vỡ bầu không khí ngại ngùng ban đầu | To say or do something to make people feel more relaxed in a social situation. | He told a joke **to break the ice** at the beginning of the meeting. |
| **To make small talk** | Trò chuyện xã giao | To have a polite, informal conversation about unimportant things. | I'm not very good at **making small talk** at parties. |
| **A sense of camaraderie** | Tinh thần đồng đội, tình bạn thân thiết | A feeling of friendship and trust among people who work or spend time together. | There was a great **sense of camaraderie** among the members of the sports team. |
| **To keep the conversation flowing** | Duy trì cuộc trò chuyện | To ensure that a conversation does not stop or become awkward. | Asking open-ended questions is a good way **to keep the conversation flowing**. |
| **To be a good conversationalist** | Là một người nói chuyện thú vị | To be someone who is skilled and interesting in conversation. | He is a **good conversationalist** who can talk about a wide range of topics. |
| **To dominate the conversation** | Thống trị cuộc trò chuyện | To talk too much and prevent others from speaking. | She has a tendency **to dominate the conversation** and doesn't let anyone else speak. |
| **An awkward silence** | Một sự im lặng ngượng ngùng | An uncomfortable pause in a conversation. | His inappropriate comment was met with an **awkward silence**. |

### 🌱 **Xây dựng & Duy trì Mối quan hệ (Building & Maintaining Relationships)** (NOT-DONE)

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **To build a relationship** | Xây dựng một mối quan hệ | To develop a connection with someone over time. | It takes time and effort **to build a strong relationship**. |
| **To nurture a relationship** | Nuôi dưỡng một mối quan hệ | To care for and encourage the growth of a relationship. | You have **to nurture a relationship** by spending quality time together. |
| **To establish rapport** | Thiết lập mối quan hệ hòa hợp | To create a friendly, understanding connection with someone. | The teacher quickly **established a good rapport** with her new students. |
| **To build mutual trust** | Xây dựng sự tin tưởng lẫn nhau | To create a feeling of trust between two or more people. | Honesty and reliability are essential **to build mutual trust**. |
| **To earn someone's trust** | Giành được sự tin tưởng của ai đó | To do things that make someone trust you. | You can **earn someone's trust** by being dependable and honest. |
| **To keep in touch / maintain close contact** | Giữ liên lạc / duy trì liên lạc mật thiết | To continue to communicate with someone regularly. | Let's promise **to keep in touch** after you move to the new city. |
| **To be there for someone** | Ở bên cạnh khi ai đó cần | To be available to provide support when someone needs it. | A true friend will always **be there for you**, no matter what. |
| **To provide emotional support** | Cung cấp sự hỗ trợ về mặt cảm xúc | To give comfort, encouragement, and reassurance to someone. | He relied on his friends **to provide emotional support** during his illness. |
| **To set healthy boundaries** | Đặt ra những ranh giới lành mạnh | To establish clear limits in a relationship to protect your well-being. | It is important **to set healthy boundaries** in all of your relationships. |
| **To resolve a conflict** | Giải quyết một mâu thuẫn | To find a solution to a disagreement. | They sat down to talk and were able **to resolve the conflict** peacefully. |
