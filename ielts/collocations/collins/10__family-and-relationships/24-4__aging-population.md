### 📈 **Hiện tượng Dân số Già hóa (The Phenomenon of an Aging Population)**

| Collocation                                  | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                           | Short English Meaning                                                                                   | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                     |
| :------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **An aging / A graying population**          | Một dân số đang già đi                     | A population with a rising proportion of older people and a rising median age.                          | Japan is a well-known example of a country grappling with the challenges of **an aging population**. |
| **A profound demographic shift**             | Một sự thay đổi nhân khẩu học sâu sắc      | A fundamental and significant change in the structure of a population.                                  | The world is experiencing a **profound demographic shift** towards older populations.                |
| **Increased life expectancy / Longevity**    | Tuổi thọ trung bình tăng / Sự trường thọ   | The average period that a person may expect to live.                                                    | Advances in healthcare and nutrition have led to a dramatically **increased life expectancy**.       |
| **A declining birth rate / fertility rate**  | Tỷ lệ sinh / tỷ lệ sinh sản giảm           | A decrease in the number of live births per 1,000 of the population per year.                           | A **declining birth rate** is one of the primary causes of a graying population.                     |
| **Senior citizens / The elderly**            | Người cao tuổi / người già                 | Older people, especially those who have retired from work.                                              | The government provides special subsidies and healthcare benefits for **senior citizens**.           |
| **The proportion of the elderly population** | Tỷ lệ dân số người cao tuổi                | The percentage of the total population that is aged 65 and over.                                        | **The proportion of the elderly population** in Europe is projected to increase sharply.             |
| **A top-heavy age structure**                | Cấu trúc tuổi "nặng đầu" (nhiều người già) | A population structure with a high percentage of older people and a smaller proportion of young people. | A **top-heavy age structure** creates significant economic and social challenges.                    |
| **The silver tsunami**                       | Cơn "sóng thần bạc" (ẩn dụ)                | A metaphor used to describe the expected increase in the elderly population.                            | Policymakers are preparing for the economic impact of the coming **"silver tsunami"**.               |
| **Population aging**                         | Sự già hóa dân số                          | The process by which the share of older individuals in a population becomes larger.                     | **Population aging** is a long-term trend that is affecting nearly every country in the world.       |
| **To live longer, healthier lives**          | Sống lâu hơn, khỏe mạnh hơn                | To have an increased lifespan with a better quality of health.                                          | People today can expect **to live longer, healthier lives** than any previous generation.            |

### socioeconomic **Tác động lên Xã hội & Kinh tế (Socio-economic Impacts)**

| Collocation                                           | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                                | Short English Meaning                                                                                         | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                               |
| :---------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **An increased strain on the healthcare system**      | Gánh nặng gia tăng cho hệ thống y tế            | A greater burden and pressure on healthcare services, staff, and budgets.                                     | An aging population places an **increased strain on the healthcare system**.                   |
| **A growing demand for healthcare services**          | Nhu cầu ngày càng tăng đối với các dịch vụ y tế | A rising need for medical care, long-term care facilities, and geriatric specialists.                         | There will be a **growing demand for healthcare services** tailored to the elderly.            |
| **A shrinking workforce / labor force**               | Lực lượng lao động bị thu hẹp                   | A decrease in the number of people of working age who are available for work.                                 | A **shrinking workforce** has to support a growing number of retirees.                         |
| **The impending pension crisis**                      | Cuộc khủng hoảng lương hưu sắp xảy ra           | A serious and imminent problem with funding and providing pensions for retired people.                        | Many countries are facing an **impending pension crisis** as their populations age.            |
| **An increased tax burden on the younger generation** | Gánh nặng thuế gia tăng lên thế hệ trẻ          | The heavy financial responsibility placed on younger, working people to fund social services for the elderly. | The cost of elderly care places an **increased tax burden on the younger generation**.         |
| **The social welfare system**                         | Hệ thống phúc lợi xã hội                        | The system of government agencies and programs that provide social and economic assistance to those in need.  | **The social welfare system** needs to be reformed to cope with these demographic changes.     |
| **Profound economic implications**                    | Các hệ quả kinh tế sâu sắc                      | The very significant and far-reaching effects on the economy.                                                 | The **profound economic implications** of a graying population include slower economic growth. |
| **A decline in economic productivity**                | Sự suy giảm năng suất kinh tế                   | A reduction in the rate at which goods and services are produced.                                             | A smaller workforce could lead to a **decline in economic productivity**.                      |
| **The dependency ratio**                              | Tỷ lệ phụ thuộc                                 | The ratio of dependents (people younger than 15 or older than 64) to the working-age population.              | An aging population leads to a higher **dependency ratio**.                                    |
| **To raise the retirement age**                       | Nâng tuổi nghỉ hưu                              | To increase the official age at which people stop working and can receive a pension.                          | Some governments are considering **raising the retirement age** to address the pension crisis. |

### ❤️ **Chăm sóc & Hỗ trợ Người cao tuổi (Elderly Care & Support)**

| Collocation                                               | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                                     | Short English Meaning                                                                                             | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                                  |
| :-------------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **To care for one's aging / elderly parents**             | Chăm sóc cha mẹ già                                  | To look after and provide for one's parents as they get older.                                                    | In many cultures, adult children have a strong sense of duty **to care for their aging parents**.                 |
| **To be in need of long-term care**                       | Cần được chăm sóc dài hạn                            | To require ongoing medical and personal assistance over an extended period.                                       | After the illness, he was **in need of long-term care**.                                                          |
| **A nursing home / residential care facility**            | Viện dưỡng lão / Cơ sở chăm sóc nội trú              | A place where elderly people who are infirm live and are cared for by professional staff.                         | The decision to move a parent into a **nursing home** can be very difficult.                                      |
| **In-home care / Domiciliary care**                       | Chăm sóc tại nhà                                     | Care and support provided to someone in their own home.                                                           | Many seniors prefer **in-home care** as they can stay in a familiar environment.                                  |
| **A professional caregiver**                              | Người chăm sóc chuyên nghiệp                         | A person who is trained and paid to provide care for someone who is ill or elderly.                               | Hiring a **professional caregiver** can relieve the pressure on family members.                                   |
| **A family responsibility versus a state responsibility** | Trách nhiệm của gia đình và trách nhiệm của nhà nước | The debate over whether the family or the government should be primarily responsible for elderly care.            | There is much discussion about whether elderly care is a **family responsibility versus a state responsibility**. |
| **Community-based support services**                      | Các dịch vụ hỗ trợ dựa vào cộng đồng                 | Care services, such as day centers and meal delivery, that are provided within the local community.               | **Community-based support services** can help elderly people to remain independent.                               |
| **Geriatric care**                                        | Chăm sóc lão khoa                                    | The branch of medicine that focuses on the health and care of elderly people.                                     | There is a growing need for doctors and nurses who specialize in **geriatric care**.                              |
| **Palliative care**                                       | Chăm sóc giảm nhẹ                                    | Specialized medical care for people with serious illnesses, focused on providing relief from symptoms and stress. | **Palliative care** aims to improve the quality of life for both the patient and their family.                    |
| **The sandwich generation**                               | Thế hệ "bánh mì kẹp"                                 | The generation of people who are caring for their aging parents while also supporting their own children.         | **The sandwich generation** faces immense pressure from both sides.                                               |

### ✨ **Sức khỏe & An sinh của Người cao tuổi (Health & Well-being of the Elderly)**

| Collocation                                   | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                                      | Short English Meaning                                                                                 | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                                     |
| :-------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **To lead an active and fulfilling life**     | Sống một cuộc sống năng động và trọn vẹn              | To live a life that is busy with activities and provides personal satisfaction.                       | It's important for retired people **to lead an active and fulfilling life**.                                         |
| **To maintain one's independence**            | Duy trì sự độc lập của mình                           | To continue to live by oneself and make one's own decisions without help.                             | The goal of many elderly people is **to maintain their independence** for as long as possible.                       |
| **Age-related health problems / illnesses**   | Các vấn đề sức khỏe / bệnh tật liên quan đến tuổi tác | Diseases and conditions that become more common as a person gets older.                               | Arthritis and dementia are common **age-related health problems**.                                                   |
| **Chronic health conditions**                 | Các bệnh lý mãn tính                                  | Health problems that are persistent or otherwise long-lasting in their effects.                       | Many seniors have to manage multiple **chronic health conditions**.                                                  |
| **To combat loneliness and social isolation** | Chống lại sự cô đơn và cô lập xã hội                  | To take action to fight against the state of being alone and disconnected from others.                | Community programs are crucial **to combat loneliness and social isolation** among the elderly.                      |
| **To ensure a high quality of life**          | Đảm bảo chất lượng cuộc sống cao                      | To make certain that life is good, comfortable, and satisfying.                                       | The primary aim of elderly care is **to ensure a high quality of life**.                                             |
| **To remain mentally and physically active**  | Duy trì sự năng động về tinh thần và thể chất         | To keep one's mind and body busy and engaged.                                                         | Learning a new skill can help older people **to remain mentally and physically active**.                             |
| **A sense of purpose and belonging**          | Ý nghĩa/mục đích sống và cảm giác thân thuộc          | A feeling that one's life has meaning and that one is part of a community.                            | Volunteering can give retirees a renewed **sense of purpose and belonging**.                                         |
| **Lifelong learning**                         | Học tập suốt đời                                      | The ongoing, voluntary, and self-motivated pursuit of knowledge for personal or professional reasons. | **Lifelong learning** is a great way for seniors to keep their minds sharp.                                          |
| **The valuable contributions of the elderly** | Những đóng góp quý báu của người cao tuổi             | The important and useful things that older people do for society.                                     | We should not overlook **the valuable contributions of the elderly**, such as volunteering and sharing their wisdom. |
