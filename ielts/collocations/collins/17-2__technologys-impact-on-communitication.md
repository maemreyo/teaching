### 📱 **Các hình thức & Lợi ích của Giao tiếp Kỹ thuật số (Forms & Benefits of Digital Communication)**

| Collocation                                 | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                  | Short English Meaning                                                | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                             |
| :------------------------------------------ | :-------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Digital communication**                   | Giao tiếp kỹ thuật số             | Communication using electronic devices.                              | **Digital communication** tools, such as email and instant messaging, are essential in the modern workplace. |
| **Social media platforms**                  | Các nền tảng mạng xã hội          | Websites and apps like Facebook and Instagram for social networking. | **Social media platforms** like Facebook and Instagram have changed how we share information.                |
| **Instant messaging apps**                  | Các ứng dụng nhắn tin tức thời    | Apps for sending and receiving messages immediately.                 | **Instant messaging apps** allow for real-time conversation with people anywhere in the world.               |
| **Video conferencing**                      | Hội nghị truyền hình              | A meeting held over the internet using video and audio.              | **Video conferencing** has become a vital tool for remote working and online learning.                       |
| **To stay in touch with**                   | Giữ liên lạc với                  | To continue to communicate with someone.                             | Technology makes it easier **to stay in touch with** friends and family who live far away.                   |
| **To connect with people**                  | Kết nối với mọi người             | To form a relationship with people.                                  | The internet allows us **to connect with people** who share similar interests.                               |
| **To bridge geographical distances**        | Thu hẹp khoảng cách địa lý        | To reduce the effect of being far apart.                             | Video calls can help **to bridge geographical distances** between loved ones.                                |
| **Instantaneous communication**             | Giao tiếp tức thời                | Communication that happens immediately.                              | The main advantage of text messaging is its **instantaneous communication**.                                 |
| **To foster global connections**            | Thúc đẩy các kết nối toàn cầu     | To encourage relationships between people around the world.          | Social media can **foster global connections** and promote cultural understanding.                           |
| **To build online communities**             | Xây dựng các cộng đồng trực tuyến | To create groups of people with shared interests online.             | People with niche hobbies can **build online communities** to share their passion.                           |
| **To maintain long-distance relationships** | Duy trì các mối quan hệ xa        | To keep a relationship strong when people are far apart.             | For many couples, technology is crucial **to maintain their long-distance relationship**.                    |

### 📉 **Tác động Tiêu cực & Bất lợi (Negative Impacts & Drawbacks)**

| Collocation                                   | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                     | Short English Meaning                                          | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                                |
| :-------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A lack of face-to-face interaction**        | Sự thiếu tương tác trực tiếp         | Not enough communication in person.                            | A heavy reliance on digital communication can lead to a **lack of face-to-face interaction**.                   |
| **To lead to social isolation**               | Dẫn đến sự cô lập xã hội             | To cause someone to be alone and without friends.              | Spending too much time online can **lead to social isolation** and loneliness.                                  |
| **The spread of misinformation**              | Sự lan truyền thông tin sai lệch     | The sharing of false or inaccurate information.                | Social media has been criticized for facilitating **the spread of misinformation**.                             |
| **Cyberbullying**                             | Bắt nạt trên mạng                    | Bullying that happens online.                                  | **Cyberbullying** is a serious problem that can have severe psychological effects on young people.              |
| **An invasion of privacy**                    | Sự xâm phạm quyền riêng tư           | An unwelcome intrusion into someone's private life.            | Sharing too much personal information online can result in an **invasion of privacy**.                          |
| **To be addicted to social media**            | Nghiện mạng xã hội                   | To be unable to stop using social media.                       | Many teenagers show signs of **being addicted to social media**.                                                |
| **Information overload**                      | Quá tải thông tin                    | Having too much information to deal with.                      | Constant notifications and updates can cause **information overload** and mental fatigue.                       |
| **A decline in the quality of communication** | Sự suy giảm chất lượng giao tiếp     | A reduction in how well people communicate.                    | The use of slang and abbreviations in texting may lead to a **decline in the quality of formal communication**. |
| **To misinterpret the tone of a message**     | Hiểu sai giọng điệu của một tin nhắn | To misunderstand the feeling or attitude of a written message. | Without non-verbal cues, it is easy **to misinterpret the tone of** a written **message**.                      |

### 🗣️ **Sự thay đổi Kỹ năng & Chuẩn mực Xã hội (Changing Social Skills & Norms)**

| Collocation                                     | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                                   | Short English Meaning                                             | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                           |
| :---------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A decline in interpersonal skills**           | Sự suy giảm các kỹ năng giao tiếp giữa các cá nhân | A reduction in the ability to communicate well with other people. | Some experts believe there has been a **decline in interpersonal skills** among the younger generation.    |
| **To lose the ability to**                      | Mất đi khả năng (làm gì đó)                        | To no longer be able to do something.                             | If we only communicate online, we may **lose the ability to** read body language effectively.              |
| **To have a shorter attention span**            | Có khoảng chú ý ngắn hơn                           | To be able to concentrate for a shorter time.                     | Constant exposure to short-form content may cause people **to have a shorter attention span**.             |
| **A virtual social life**                       | Một cuộc sống xã hội ảo                            | A social life that exists online.                                 | Some individuals prefer their **virtual social life** to real-world interactions.                          |
| **To prefer online interaction to...**          | Thích tương tác trực tuyến hơn...                  | To like communicating online more than in person.                 | A potential danger is when people start **to prefer online interaction to** face-to-face meetings.         |
| **To erode traditional forms of communication** | Xói mòn các hình thức giao tiếp truyền thống       | To gradually destroy old ways of communicating.                   | The popularity of instant messaging is **eroding traditional forms of communication** like letter writing. |
| **The nuances of communication**                | Các sắc thái tinh tế của giao tiếp                 | The small, subtle differences in meaning in communication.        | **The nuances of communication**, such as tone of voice and facial expressions, are lost in text.          |
