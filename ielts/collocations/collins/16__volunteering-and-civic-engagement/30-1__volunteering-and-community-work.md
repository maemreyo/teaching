### ❤️ **Bản chất & Động lực của Tình nguyện (The Nature & Motivation of Volunteering)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **To do volunteer work** | Làm công việc tình nguyện | To work for an organization without being paid, for the benefit of others. | Many students **do volunteer work** at local charities to gain experience. |
| **Unpaid work** | Công việc không lương | Work that you do without receiving any payment. | Volunteering is, by definition, a form of **unpaid work**. |
| **A good cause** | Một mục đích tốt đẹp/cao cả | An aim or organization that helps people or society. | The concert raised over a million dollars for a **good cause**. |
| **To give back to the community** | Đóng góp lại cho cộng đồng | To do something helpful for the community where you live. | After her success, the actress wanted **to give back to the community** she grew up in. |
| **A sense of civic duty** | Ý thức về nghĩa vụ công dân | A belief that you have a responsibility to be an active and helpful member of society. | For many, volunteering is driven by a strong **sense of civic duty**. |
| **To make a difference** | Tạo ra sự khác biệt | To have a positive and significant effect on a situation. | She became a teacher because she wanted **to make a difference** in children's lives. |
| **To nurture empathy and understanding** | Nuôi dưỡng sự đồng cảm và thấu hiểu | To help develop the ability to understand and share the feelings of others. | Volunteering in a different culture can **nurture empathy and understanding**. |
| **To build a sense of compassion** | Nuôi dưỡng lòng trắc ẩn | To develop a feeling of sympathy for the suffering of others. | Working with the homeless helps **to build a sense of compassion**. |
| **To improve one's employability** | Cải thiện khả năng tìm việc | To increase your chances of getting a job through new skills and experience. | Volunteering can **improve your employability** by demonstrating commitment and skills. |
| **To gain personal fulfillment** | Đạt được sự mãn nguyện cá nhân | To get a deep sense of satisfaction and happiness from your actions. | Helping others is a great way **to gain personal fulfillment**. |

### 🤝 **Các loại hình & Hoạt động Tình nguyện (Types & Acts of Volunteering)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **To take part in community service** | Tham gia phục vụ cộng đồng | To do unpaid work that helps the people in a local community. | Students are often encouraged **to take part in community service**. |
| **An environmental cleanup drive** | Một chiến dịch dọn dẹp môi trường | An organized event to remove litter and waste from a natural area. | We joined an **environmental cleanup drive** at the local beach. |
| **To run a soup kitchen** | Vận hành một bếp ăn từ thiện | To manage a place that provides free food for homeless or poor people. | The church **runs a soup kitchen** for the homeless every winter. |
| **To mentor underprivileged youth** | Kèm cặp thanh thiếu niên có hoàn cảnh khó khăn | To guide and support young people from poor or disadvantaged backgrounds. | He volunteers his time **to mentor underprivileged youth** in his city. |
| **Skills-based volunteering** | Tình nguyện dựa trên kỹ năng | Volunteering by using your professional skills to help a non-profit organization. | A lawyer might do **skills-based volunteering** by providing free legal advice to a charity. |
| **To provide pro bono services** | Cung cấp dịch vụ miễn phí vì cộng đồng | To offer professional services for free to those who cannot afford them. | The law firm **provides pro bono services** to low-income clients. |
| **A blood donation drive** | Một chiến dịch hiến máu tình nguyện | An event organized to collect blood from volunteer donors. | The Red Cross is holding a **blood donation drive** at the university. |
| **Disaster relief effort** | Nỗ lực cứu trợ thảm họa | The work done to help people affected by a disaster like an earthquake or flood. | Volunteers are a crucial part of any **disaster relief effort**. |
| **Animal shelter volunteering** | Tình nguyện tại trại cứu hộ động vật | Helping to care for abandoned or rescued animals in a shelter. | **Animal shelter volunteering** is a rewarding experience for any animal lover. |
| **Remote / Virtual volunteering** | Tình nguyện từ xa / ảo | Doing volunteer tasks online from your own home. | **Remote volunteering** allows people to contribute to causes regardless of their location. |

### 🏛️ **Tổ chức & Sự tham gia (Organizations & Engagement)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A charitable organization / A non-profit** | Một tổ chức từ thiện / phi lợi nhuận | An organization that works to help people or causes, not to make a profit. | She has worked for a **charitable organization** for over twenty years. |
| **To support marginalized communities** | Hỗ trợ các cộng đồng yếu thế | To help groups of people who are treated as unimportant or are at a disadvantage. | The program aims **to support marginalized communities** through education and job training. |
| **To partner with non-profits** | Hợp tác với các tổ chức phi lợi nhuận | For a company or individual to work together with non-profit organizations. | The corporation **partners with local non-profits** as part of its social responsibility program. |
| **To raise funds for charity** | Gây quỹ từ thiện | To collect money for a charitable organization. | They are running a marathon **to raise funds for charity**. |
| **To organize a donation drive** | Tổ chức một chiến dịch quyên góp | To plan an event to collect donations of money, food, or goods. | The students **organized a donation drive** for the local food bank. |
| **Civic participation / engagement** | Sự tham gia của công dân | The involvement of citizens in the life of their community and government. | Volunteering is a key form of **civic participation**. |
| **A youth volunteer network** | Một mạng lưới tình nguyện viên trẻ | A group of young people who organize and participate in volunteer activities. | He joined a **youth volunteer network** to meet like-minded people and make a difference. |
| **Volunteer recruitment and training** | Tuyển dụng và đào tạo tình nguyện viên | The process of finding new volunteers and preparing them for their roles. | Effective **volunteer recruitment and training** is essential for any non-profit. |
| **A volunteer retention strategy** | Một chiến lược giữ chân tình nguyện viên | A plan to keep volunteers motivated and engaged so they continue to help. | A good **volunteer retention strategy** involves showing appreciation and providing meaningful work. |
| **A volunteer recognition ceremony** | Một lễ vinh danh tình nguyện viên | An event held to thank and honor volunteers for their contributions. | The mayor will speak at the annual **volunteer recognition ceremony**. |
