### 🤖 **Khái niệm & Công nghệ Cốt lõi (Core Concepts & Technology)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Artificial intelligence (AI)** | Trí tuệ nhân tạo | The ability of computers to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence. | **Artificial intelligence** is transforming industries from healthcare to finance. |
| **Machine learning (ML)** | Học máy | A type of AI that allows computers to learn from data without explicit programming. | **Machine learning** algorithms improve their performance as they are exposed to more data. |
| **Deep learning** | Học sâu | A subset of ML using multi-layered neural networks to analyze complex patterns. | **Deep learning** is the technology behind facial recognition and natural language processing. |
| **Neural networks** | Mạng nơ-ron | Computer systems modeled on the human brain and nervous system. | **Neural networks** are powerful tools for finding patterns in large, complex datasets. |
| **Natural Language Processing (NLP)** | Xử lý ngôn ngữ tự nhiên | The ability of a computer to understand human language as it is spoken and written. | Chatbots and translation apps are common applications of **Natural Language Processing**. |
| **Computer vision** | Thị giác máy tính | A field of AI that trains computers to interpret and understand the visual world. | **Computer vision** enables self-driving cars to identify pedestrians and traffic signs. |
| **To be trained on a dataset** | Được huấn luyện trên một bộ dữ liệu | To be supplied with data in order to develop an AI model's abilities. | The AI model **was trained on a massive dataset** of images to learn to identify cats. |
| **To process vast amounts of data** | Xử lý lượng dữ liệu khổng lồ | To handle and analyze very large quantities of data. | AI is capable of **processing vast amounts of data** in a fraction of the time a human could. |
| **To recognize patterns** | Nhận dạng các quy luật, khuôn mẫu | To identify repeated or regular ways in which something happens. | Advanced algorithms can **recognize complex patterns** in consumer behavior. |
| **To mimic human intelligence** | Bắt chước trí tuệ của con người | To imitate the way humans think, learn, and problem-solve. | The goal of some AI research is **to create machines that can mimic human intelligence**. |

### 💼 **Tác động lên Việc làm & Xã hội (Impact on Employment & Society)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Job displacement** | Sự thay thế việc làm (bởi máy móc) | The loss of jobs caused by new technology. | The widespread adoption of AI could lead to significant **job displacement** in certain sectors. |
| **The automation of labor** | Sự tự động hóa lao động | The use of machines to do work, especially in factories or offices. | **The automation of labor** raises important questions about the future of work. |
| **To augment human capabilities** | Tăng cường năng lực của con người | To use technology to help people perform tasks better. | AI should be seen as a tool **to augment human capabilities**, not just replace them. |
| **A shrinking job market** | Một thị trường việc làm bị thu hẹp | A job market with fewer available jobs. | Some predict a **shrinking job market** for low-skilled manual labor. |
| **The workforce of the future** | Lực lượng lao động của tương lai | The people who will be working in the future. | **The workforce of the future** will need to be adaptable and skilled in working alongside AI. |
| **To create new job opportunities** | Tạo ra các cơ hội việc làm mới | To make new jobs available. | While some jobs will be lost, technology will also **create new job opportunities**. |
| **A skills gap** | Một khoảng trống về kỹ năng | A situation where workers do not have the skills needed for the available jobs. | There is a growing **skills gap** between the jobs available and the skills of the workforce. |
| **To require reskilling and upskilling** | Yêu cầu đào tạo lại và nâng cao kỹ năng | To need to learn new skills or improve existing ones. | The changing job market will **require** workers **to constantly reskill and upskill**. |
| **Cognitive labor vs. manual labor** | Lao động trí óc vs. lao động chân tay | Work requiring thinking and reasoning vs. work requiring physical effort. | AI is now capable of automating not just **manual labor**, but also some forms of **cognitive labor**. |
| **A profound impact on society** | Một tác động sâu sắc lên xã hội | A very strong and deep effect on society. | The rise of AI will have a **profound impact on society** as a whole. |

### ⚖️ **Quan ngại Đạo đức & Bất lợi (Ethical Concerns & Drawbacks)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ethical implications** | Các hàm ý về đạo đức | Moral questions or problems raised by a situation. | The use of AI in autonomous weapons raises serious **ethical implications**. |
| **Algorithmic bias** | Thiên vị thuật toán | Unfairness in an algorithm's results, often reflecting human prejudices. | If AI is trained on biased data, it can lead to **algorithmic bias** and unfair outcomes. |
| **The 'black box' problem** | Vấn đề 'hộp đen' | The difficulty in understanding how a complex AI system reached a specific decision. | **The 'black box' problem** in AI makes it hard to check for errors or bias in its reasoning. |
| **A lack of human oversight** | Sự thiếu giám sát của con người | Not enough human supervision over an automated system. | A key concern is the danger of autonomous systems operating with a **lack of human oversight**. |
| **To make life-or-death decisions** | Đưa ra các quyết định sinh tử | To make decisions that could result in someone's death. | We must decide who is responsible when an AI has **to make a life-or-death decision**. |
| **An invasion of privacy** | Sự xâm phạm quyền riêng tư | An unwelcome intrusion into someone's private life or personal information. | Facial recognition technology could lead to a massive **invasion of privacy**. |
| **The weaponization of AI** | Việc vũ khí hóa AI | The use of AI to create autonomous weapons. | There is a global debate on how to prevent **the weaponization of AI**. |
| **To surpass human intelligence** | Vượt qua trí tuệ con người | To become more intelligent than humans. | Some scientists are concerned about AI one day **surpassing human intelligence**. |
| **To pose an existential threat** | Gây ra một mối đe dọa υπαρξιακή (đe dọa sự tồn vong) | To be a threat to the continued existence of humanity. | A superintelligent AI could potentially **pose an existential threat** to humanity. |
| **To be held accountable for** | Bị quy trách nhiệm cho | To be responsible for something bad that has happened. | If an autonomous car has an accident, it is unclear who should **be held accountable**. |
