### 🔬 **Quy trình & Phương pháp (The Process & Method)**

| Collocation                              | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                   | Short English Meaning                                                 | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                |
| :--------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **The scientific method**                | Phương pháp khoa học               | A systematic procedure for collecting and analyzing evidence.         | **The scientific method** involves observation, hypothesis, experimentation, and conclusion.    |
| **To formulate a hypothesis**            | Xây dựng một giả thuyết            | To create a testable proposed explanation for a phenomenon.           | A scientist must first **formulate a hypothesis** before conducting an experiment.              |
| **To conduct / carry out an experiment** | Tiến hành một thí nghiệm           | To perform a scientific test to prove a hypothesis.                   | They will **conduct an experiment** to test the drug's effectiveness.                           |
| **To gather empirical evidence**         | Thu thập bằng chứng thực nghiệm    | To collect data through observation and experimentation.              | The study's goal is **to gather empirical evidence** on the effects of the new teaching method. |
| **To analyze the data / results**        | Phân tích dữ liệu / kết quả        | To examine data or results in detail to find meaning.                 | The next step is **to analyze the data** to see if it supports the hypothesis.                  |
| **To draw a conclusion**                 | Rút ra một kết luận                | To form a judgment or opinion after considering all the evidence.     | Based on the results, the researchers were able **to draw a firm conclusion**.                  |
| **To publish one's findings**            | Công bố các phát hiện của mình     | To make the results of one's research public in a scientific journal. | The team is ready **to publish their findings** in a peer-reviewed journal.                     |
| **A controlled experiment**              | Một thí nghiệm có đối chứng        | An experiment in which only one variable is changed at a time.        | A **controlled experiment** is essential for determining cause-and-effect relationships.        |
| **To be subjected to peer review**       | Được bình duyệt bởi các chuyên gia | To have one's work evaluated by other experts in the same field.      | All credible scientific research must **be subjected to peer review**.                          |
| **To replicate an experiment**           | Tái tạo một thí nghiệm             | To repeat an experiment to verify the original results.               | Other labs were unable **to replicate the experiment**, casting doubt on the findings.          |

### 💡 **Khám phá & Đột phá (Discovery & Breakthroughs)**

| Collocation                             | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                          | Short English Meaning                                                                  | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                        |
| :-------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **A scientific breakthrough**           | Một đột phá khoa học                      | A major and important new discovery.                                                   | The development of vaccines was a major **scientific breakthrough** in medicine.                        |
| **A groundbreaking discovery**          | Một khám phá đột phá                      | A discovery that is new and changes the way people think about something.              | The team made a **groundbreaking discovery** in the field of neuroscience.                              |
| **To make a discovery**                 | Thực hiện một khám phá                    | To find or learn something that was not known before.                                  | Marie Curie **made a discovery** that changed the course of physics.                                    |
| **Cutting-edge research**               | Nghiên cứu tiên tiến, hàng đầu            | The most advanced and innovative research in a field.                                  | The university is known for its **cutting-edge research** in artificial intelligence.                   |
| **To push the frontiers of knowledge**  | Mở rộng các giới hạn của tri thức         | To expand the limits of what is known about something.                                 | This research aims **to push the frontiers of knowledge** in particle physics.                          |
| **To pave the way for...**              | Mở đường cho...                           | To create a situation that makes it possible for something else to happen.             | The discovery of penicillin **paved the way for** the development of antibiotics.                       |
| **A paradigm shift**                    | Một sự thay đổi mô hình tư duy            | A fundamental change in the basic concepts and practices of a scientific discipline.   | The theory of relativity caused a **paradigm shift** in physics.                                        |
| **To challenge an existing theory**     | Thách thức một học thuyết hiện có         | To question a theory that is currently accepted.                                       | The new evidence **challenges the existing theory** of planet formation.                                |
| **To stand on the shoulders of giants** | Đứng trên vai những người khổng lồ        | To use the work and discoveries of previous great scientists to make further progress. | Isaac Newton famously said, "If I have seen further, it is by **standing on the shoulders of giants**." |
| **A chance discovery / Serendipity**    | Một khám phá tình cờ / Sự tình cờ may mắn | Finding something valuable or agreeable by chance.                                     | Penicillin was a **chance discovery** resulting from a contaminated experiment.                         |

| **To make science accessible**                          | Làm cho khoa học dễ tiếp cận                            | To present scientific information in a way that is easy for everyone to understand.         | The goal of the museum is **to make science accessible** and fun for children.                   |
| **Scientific literacy**                                 | Trình độ hiểu biết khoa học                             | The knowledge and understanding of scientific concepts needed for personal decision making. | A high level of **scientific literacy** is essential for citizens in a modern democracy.         |
| **To combat scientific misinformation**                 | Chống lại thông tin sai lệch về khoa học                | To fight against the spread of false or inaccurate scientific claims.                       | It is the responsibility of the media **to combat scientific misinformation**.                   |

| **The commercialization of science**                    | Sự thương mại hóa khoa học                              | The process of treating scientific research as a product to be bought and sold for profit.  | **The commercialization of science** can create conflicts of interest in academic research.      |
| **To foster a culture of scientific inquiry**           | Nuôi dưỡng một văn hóa tìm tòi khoa học                 | To encourage a general curiosity and a desire to ask questions and seek evidence.           | A good education system should **foster a culture of scientific inquiry**.                       |
