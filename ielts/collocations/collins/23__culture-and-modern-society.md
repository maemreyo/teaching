### 🏛️ **Bản sắc & Di sản Văn hóa (Cultural Identity & Heritage)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cultural heritage** | Di sản văn hóa | The traditions, art, and buildings that a society has from the past. | It's our duty to preserve the country's rich **cultural heritage** for future generations. |
| **Cultural identity** | Bản sắc văn hóa | The feeling of belonging to a group based on shared culture. | Globalization can sometimes be a threat to a nation's **cultural identity**. |
| **Traditional values / beliefs** | Những giá trị / tín ngưỡng truyền thống | The established beliefs and principles of a society. | The younger generation sometimes has different views on **traditional values**. |
| **A sense of belonging** | Cảm giác thân thuộc, thuộc về | A feeling that you are part of a group. | Participating in community festivals gives people **a sense of belonging**. |
| **To pass down through generations** | Truyền từ thế hệ này sang thế hệ khác | To give something from older family members to younger ones. | These folk tales have been **passed down through generations**. |
| **National identity** | Bản sắc dân tộc | The identity of a whole country and its people. | A country's language and history are core components of its **national identity**. |

### 🌍 **Giao thoa Văn hóa & Toàn cầu hóa (Cultural Interaction & Globalization)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cultural diversity** | Sự đa dạng văn hóa | The existence of many different cultures in one place. | Living in a multicultural city exposes you to great **cultural diversity**. |
| **Cultural exchange** | Trao đổi, giao lưu văn hóa | The sharing of ideas and practices between different cultures. | Student exchange programs are a great way to promote **cultural exchange**. |
| **A melting pot** | Nơi hội tụ, giao thoa (văn hóa) | A place where different peoples, styles, theories, etc., are mixed together. | New York City is often described as a **melting pot** of different cultures. |
| **Cultural assimilation** | Sự đồng hóa văn hóa | The process of a minority group adopting the culture of a larger one. | Immigrants often face the challenge of **cultural assimilation** in a new country. |
| **The global village** | Ngôi làng toàn cầu | The idea that the world is becoming like one large village due to technology. | The internet and mass media have turned the world into a **global village**. |
| **To embrace foreign cultures** | Đón nhận văn hóa nước ngoài | To welcome and accept cultures from other countries. | It's important to **embrace foreign cultures** with an open mind. |
| **Cultural homogenization** | Sự đồng nhất văn hóa | The process of cultures becoming more alike. | Some people worry that globalization leads to **cultural homogenization**. |

### 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 **Chuẩn mực & Lối sống Xã hội (Social Norms & Lifestyles)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Social norms** | Các chuẩn mực xã hội | The unwritten rules of behavior in a society. | **Social norms** dictate what is considered acceptable behavior in a society. |
| **To conform to the norm** | Tuân theo chuẩn mực | To behave in the way that is expected by society. | There is often pressure on individuals **to conform to social norms**. |
| **Consumer culture / society** | Văn hóa / Xã hội tiêu dùng | A society where buying and selling goods is very important. | We live in a **consumer society** that encourages people to buy the latest products. |
| **A fast-paced lifestyle** | Lối sống nhịp độ nhanh, hối hả | A life that is very busy and active. | People living in big cities often have a very **fast-paced lifestyle**. |
| **A sedentary lifestyle** | Lối sống thụ động, ít vận động | A lifestyle with little physical activity. | A **sedentary lifestyle** can lead to various health problems. |
| **The generation gap** | Khoảng cách thế hệ | The difference in attitudes between different generations. | Differences in opinions on technology often highlight the **generation gap**. |
| **The nuclear / extended family** | Gia đình hạt nhân / gia đình đa thế hệ | A family of parents and children / a family including relatives like grandparents. | In many Western countries, the **nuclear family** is the most common family unit. |
