### ⚖️ **Tiếp cận & Cơ hội Bình đẳng (Equal Access & Opportunity)**

| Collocation                                 | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                              | Short English Meaning                                                                                                                    | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                        |
| :------------------------------------------ | :-------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Equal access to quality education**       | Tiếp cận bình đẳng với giáo dục chất lượng    | The principle that everyone should have the same opportunity to receive a high-standard education.                                       | Ensuring **equal access to quality education** is a cornerstone of a just society.                      |
| **Equality of educational opportunity**     | Sự bình đẳng về cơ hội giáo dục               | The principle that all children should have the same chance to succeed in the education system, regardless of their background.          | A fair society must be based on the principle of **equality of educational opportunity**.               |
| **To overcome barriers to learning**        | Vượt qua các rào cản đối với việc học         | To defeat obstacles that prevent students from learning effectively, such as poverty or disability.                                      | Technology can help students with disabilities **to overcome barriers to learning**.                    |
| **To be denied access to education**        | Bị từ chối quyền tiếp cận giáo dục            | To be prevented from having the opportunity to go to school or university.                                                               | In some parts of the world, girls are still **denied access to education**.                             |
| **To bridge the educational divide/gap**    | Thu hẹp sự chênh lệch/khoảng cách về giáo dục | To reduce the difference in educational outcomes between different groups in society.                                                    | Digital learning platforms can help **to bridge the educational divide** between rural and urban areas. |
| **Students from disadvantaged backgrounds** | Học sinh/sinh viên từ các hoàn cảnh khó khăn  | Students who come from poor families or have other social disadvantages.                                                                 | The government has programs to support **students from disadvantaged backgrounds**.                     |
| **To create a level playing field**         | Tạo ra một sân chơi bình đẳng                 | To create a situation that is fair for everyone, where no one has an unfair advantage.                                                   | The goal of public education is **to create a level playing field** for all children.                   |
| **Socio-economic background**               | Nền tảng kinh tế-xã hội                       | A person's position in society as determined by their income, occupation, and social class.                                              | A child's educational success should not be determined by their **socio-economic background**.          |
| **The urban-rural educational gap**         | Khoảng cách giáo dục thành thị-nông thôn      | The disparity in educational resources and outcomes between cities and the countryside.                                                  | We need targeted policies to address **the urban-rural educational gap**.                               |
| **To ensure inclusivity in education**      | Đảm bảo sự hòa nhập trong giáo dục            | To make sure that the education system is open and welcoming to all students, including those with disabilities or from minority groups. | It is vital **to ensure inclusivity in education** for children with special needs.                     |

### 💰 **Tài trợ & Nguồn lực (Funding & Resources)**

| Collocation                                    | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                              | Short English Meaning                                                                                     | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                                    |
| :--------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **To be well-resourced / under-resourced**     | Được cung cấp đầy đủ / không đủ nguồn lực     | To have sufficient / insufficient resources like books, technology, and qualified teachers.               | Schools in wealthy neighborhoods are often **well-resourced**, while those in poorer areas are **under-resourced**. |
| **A disparity in funding**                     | Sự chênh lệch trong việc tài trợ              | An unfair difference in the amount of money provided to different schools or regions.                     | There is a significant **disparity in funding** between public and private schools.                                 |
| **To invest heavily in public education**      | Đầu tư mạnh mẽ vào giáo dục công lập          | To put a large amount of money into the state-funded education system.                                    | It is crucial for governments **to invest heavily in public education**.                                            |
| **Government funding / subsidies**             | Tài trợ / trợ cấp của chính phủ               | Money provided by the government to support the education system.                                         | **Government funding** is essential for maintaining the quality of state schools.                                   |
| **To allocate the education budget equitably** | Phân bổ ngân sách giáo dục một cách công bằng | To distribute the government's money for education in a fair and just way.                                | The budget should be **allocated equitably** to ensure all schools have adequate resources.                         |
| **Parental income and wealth**                 | Thu nhập và tài sản của cha mẹ                | The amount of money and assets a person's parents have.                                                   | A child's educational attainment should not be dependent on their **parental income and wealth**.                   |
| **Resource allocation**                        | Phân bổ nguồn lực                             | The process of assigning and managing assets in a manner that supports an organization's strategic goals. | Fair **resource allocation** is key to reducing educational inequality.                                             |
| **Inadequate facilities**                      | Cơ sở vật chất không đầy đủ                   | Buildings, equipment, and services that are not sufficient in quantity or quality.                        | Many rural schools suffer from **inadequate facilities**, such as a lack of science labs.                           |
| **The student-teacher ratio**                  | Tỷ lệ học sinh trên giáo viên                 | The number of students for every teacher in a school.                                                     | A low **student-teacher ratio** is often associated with better educational outcomes.                               |
| **Private tutoring**                           | Dạy kèm tư                                    | Private lessons outside of school, paid for by a student's family.                                        | The prevalence of **private tutoring** can exacerbate the gap between rich and poor students.                       |

### 🏫 **Các loại hình Trường học & Sự Phân hóa (Types of Schooling & Segregation)**

| Collocation                                    | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                              | Short English Meaning                                                                                                                 | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                               |
| :--------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **The public versus private school debate**    | Cuộc tranh luận giữa trường công và trường tư | The ongoing discussion about the merits and fairness of having both state-funded and fee-paying schools.                              | **The public versus private school debate** is central to discussions about educational equality.              |
| **Elite private schools**                      | Các trường tư thục ưu tú                      | Exclusive and expensive private schools that are considered to be of a very high quality.                                             | The graduates of **elite private schools** often have a significant advantage in university admissions.        |
| **Single-sex versus co-educational schooling** | Giáo dục một giới và giáo dục cả hai giới     | The debate between educating boys and girls in separate schools versus in the same schools.                                           | There is much debate about the benefits of **single-sex versus co-educational schooling**.                     |
| **Academic streaming / tracking**              | Phân luồng học sinh (theo năng lực)           | The practice of grouping students into different classes based on their perceived academic ability.                                   | Critics argue that **academic streaming** can lead to segregation and lower self-esteem.                       |
| **A two-tier education system**                | Một hệ thống giáo dục hai cấp                 | A system with two distinct levels of quality, typically a high-quality one for the wealthy and a lower-quality one for everyone else. | The existence of elite private schools and underfunded public schools creates a **two-tier education system**. |
| **Educational segregation**                    | Sự phân hóa trong giáo dục                    | The separation of students into different schools or classes based on factors like socio-economic status or race.                     | **Educational segregation** based on wealth can deepen social inequality.                                      |
| **To widen the attainment gap**                | Nới rộng khoảng cách về thành tích học tập    | To increase the difference in academic performance between different groups of students.                                              | The reliance on private tutoring can **widen the attainment gap** between rich and poor students.              |
| **Selective schooling**                        | Tuyển sinh chọn lọc                           | A system where schools select their students based on some measure of academic ability.                                               | **Selective schooling** is controversial because it can lead to greater inequality between schools.            |
| **To cream-skim the best students**            | "Hớt váng" những học sinh giỏi nhất           | For a school to select only the most able students, leaving other schools with the rest.                                              | Selective schools are often accused of **cream-skimming the best students**.                                   |
| **Comprehensive schooling**                    | Giáo dục toàn diện (không chọn lọc)           | A system where schools admit all students from a local area, regardless of their academic ability.                                    | The principle of **comprehensive schooling** is to provide the same opportunities for all children.            |

### 📈 **Kết quả & Sự Dịch chuyển Xã hội (Social Outcomes & Mobility)**

| Collocation                                       | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                                           | Short English Meaning                                                                                       | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                      |
| :------------------------------------------------ | :--------------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Social mobility**                               | Sự dịch chuyển xã hội                                      | The ability of individuals or families to move up or down the social and economic ladder.                   | Education is often seen as the most powerful engine for **social mobility**.                          |
| **To break the cycle of poverty**                 | Phá vỡ vòng luẩn quẩn của đói nghèo                        | To enable a poor family to escape from a situation of poverty that has lasted for generations.              | For many, getting a quality education is the only way **to break the cycle of poverty**.              |
| **To improve one's life chances**                 | Cải thiện cơ hội trong cuộc sống                           | To increase the opportunities an individual has for success and well-being in life.                         | A university degree can significantly **improve a young person's life chances**.                      |
| **To perpetuate and reproduce social inequality** | Duy trì và tái sản xuất sự bất bình đẳng xã hội            | To make existing social and economic inequalities continue across generations.                              | An unfair education system will only **perpetuate and reproduce social inequality**.                  |
| **A key determinant of future success**           | Một yếu tố quyết định chính đến thành công trong tương lai | A major factor that decides whether someone will be successful in the future.                               | A person's educational attainment is a **key determinant of their future success**.                   |
| **To reduce social disparities**                  | Giảm thiểu sự chênh lệch xã hội                            | To make the differences between different groups in society smaller.                                        | Investing in education for all is one of the most effective ways **to reduce social disparities**.    |
| **A meritocratic society**                        | Một xã hội nhân tài                                        | A society where power and success are based on ability and talent, not on inherited wealth or social class. | In a true **meritocratic society**, education should ensure that talent can rise from any background. |
| **To have a profound impact on...**               | Có tác động sâu sắc lên...                                 | To have a very strong and significant effect on something.                                                  | A child's early education can **have a profound impact on** their entire life.                        |
| **To be a pathway out of poverty**                | Là một con đường thoát nghèo                               | To be a way for people to escape from being poor.                                                           | For millions around the world, education is seen as **a pathway out of poverty**.                     |
| **To reinforce existing privileges**              | Củng cố các đặc quyền hiện có                              | To strengthen the advantages that are already held by a particular group in society.                        | A system of elite private schools can **reinforce the existing privileges** of the wealthy.           |
