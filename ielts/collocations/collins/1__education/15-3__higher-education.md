| :-------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------ | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |

| **To meet the stringent entry requirements**  | Đáp ứng các yêu-cầu đầu vào nghiêm ngặt     | To have the high-level qualifications and grades needed to be accepted into a course.                                   | Applicants must **meet the stringent entry requirements** to be considered for the program. |

| **To enroll in a degree program**             | Đăng ký một chương trình cấp bằng           | To officially register as a student in a course of study that leads to a qualification.                                 | She decided **to enroll in a degree program** in computer science.                          |

| **An undergraduate / a postgraduate student** | Sinh viên đại học / sau đại học             | A student studying for a first degree / a student who has completed a first degree and is studying for a higher degree. | The library is open to both **undergraduate and postgraduate students**.                    |
| **To graduate from university**               | Tốt nghiệp đại học                          | To successfully complete a degree course at a university.                                                               | After **graduating from university**, she plans to enter the workforce immediately.         |

### 💰 **Tài chính & Chi phí (Funding & Cost)**

| Collocation                               | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                                 | Short English Meaning                                                    | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                     |
| :---------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

| **To be state-funded / publicly funded**  | Được nhà nước / công quỹ tài trợ                 | To be paid for by the government using taxpayers' money.                 | In some European countries, higher education is largely **state-funded**.                            |
| **The rising cost of higher education**   | Chi phí giáo dục đại học ngày càng tăng          | The continuous increase in the price of university education.            | **The rising cost of higher education** is a significant barrier for students from poor backgrounds. |
| **A heavy financial burden on families**  | Một gánh nặng tài chính nặng nề lên các gia đình | A significant and difficult financial responsibility for families.       | High tuition fees can place a **heavy financial burden on families**.                                |
| **To be awarded a scholarship / a grant** | Được trao học bổng / một khoản trợ cấp           | To be given money by an organization to help pay for your education.     | She **was awarded a scholarship** to study at a prestigious university.                              |
| **Public investment in education**        | Đầu tư công vào giáo dục                         | The money that a government spends on education.                         | Many argue that increased **public investment in education** is crucial for a country's future.      |
| **Tuition-free university education**     | Giáo dục đại học miễn học phí                    | University education that does not charge students for tuition.          | The debate continues over whether **tuition-free university education** is a sustainable policy.     |
| **To incur debt**                         | Gánh nợ                                          | To get into a situation where you owe money.                             | Students are often forced **to incur significant debt** to complete their studies.                   |
| **To subsidize higher education**         | Trợ cấp cho giáo dục đại học                     | For the government to pay part of the cost of university education.      | The government has decided **to subsidize higher education** to make it more accessible.             |

### 🎯 **Mục đích & Kết quả (Purpose & Outcomes)**

| Collocation                                              | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                                  | Short English Meaning                                                                               | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                          |
| :------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------ | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **To acquire in-depth knowledge**                        | Tiếp thu kiến thức chuyên sâu                     | To learn a subject in great detail and with a high level of understanding.                          | The purpose of a university education is **to acquire in-depth knowledge** of a specific subject.         |
| **To specialize in a particular field**                  | Chuyên sâu về một lĩnh vực cụ thể                 | To concentrate one's studies and efforts on a specific area of expertise.                           | A Master's degree allows students **to specialize in a particular field**.                                |
| **Theoretical versus practical knowledge**               | Kiến thức lý thuyết và kiến thức thực tiễn        | The contrast between knowledge based on ideas and principles, and knowledge gained from doing.      | Critics argue that universities focus too much on **theoretical knowledge** over **practical knowledge**. |
| **To improve one's career prospects**                    | Cải thiện triển vọng nghề nghiệp của một người    | To increase one's chances of success and advancement in their career.                               | Many people get a postgraduate degree **to improve their career prospects**.                              |
| **A university graduate**                                | Một người tốt nghiệp đại học                      | A person who has successfully completed a university degree.                                        | **University graduates**, on average, have higher earning potential than non-graduates.                   |
| **To be well-prepared for the modern workforce**         | Được chuẩn bị tốt cho lực lượng lao động hiện đại | To be equipped with the necessary skills and knowledge for the contemporary world of work.          | Internships can help ensure that students **are well-prepared for the modern workforce**.                 |
| **The intrinsic value of a degree**                      | Giá trị nội tại của một tấm bằng                  | The inherent worth of a university education, beyond its monetary or career benefits.               | Some people question the economic benefits but still believe in **the intrinsic value of a degree**.      |
| **To foster intellectual curiosity and development**     | Thúc đẩy sự tò mò và phát triển trí tuệ           | To encourage a strong desire to learn and the growth of one's mental abilities.                     | Universities aim **to foster the intellectual curiosity and development** of their students.              |
| **The knowledge-based economy**                          | Nền kinh tế dựa trên tri thức                     | An economy in which growth is dependent on the quantity, quality, and accessibility of information. | A highly educated population is the backbone of **the knowledge-based economy**.                          |
| **To cultivate critical thinking and analytical skills** | Trau dồi kỹ năng tư duy phản biện và phân tích    | To develop the ability to think logically, analyze information, and form reasoned judgments.        | A key aim of higher education is **to cultivate critical thinking and analytical skills**.                |

### ⚖️ **Tranh luận & Các vấn đề (Debates & Issues)**

| Colocation                                          | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                                  | Short English Meaning                                                                                                  | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                |
| :-------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------ | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Equal access to higher education**                | Tiếp cận bình đẳng với giáo dục đại học           | The principle that everyone should have the same opportunity to attend university, regardless of their background.     | Ensuring **equal access to higher education** is a key goal for a fair society.                 |
| **Academic freedom**                                | Tự do học thuật                                   | The freedom of teachers and students to discuss and research ideas without fear of censorship or retaliation.          | **Academic freedom** is a cornerstone of a healthy and functioning university.                  |
| **To bridge the gap between academia and industry** | Thu hẹp khoảng cách giữa học thuật và công nghiệp | To reduce the differences and improve the connection between the academic world and the world of business.             | Universities should do more **to bridge the gap between academia and industry**.                |
| **A skills mismatch**                               | Sự không khớp về kỹ năng                          | A situation where the skills of the workforce do not match the skills needed for the available jobs.                   | There is a growing **skills mismatch** between what graduates learn and what employers require. |
| **An overemphasis on academic qualifications**      | Sự quá chú trọng vào bằng cấp học thuật           | Placing too much importance on formal degrees and certificates.                                                        | An **overemphasis on academic qualifications** can devalue practical experience and skills.     |
| **The commercialization of higher education**       | Sự thương mại hóa giáo dục đại học                | The process of treating higher education as a business focused on profit rather than a public good.                    | Critics are concerned about **the commercialization of higher education**.                      |
| **Grade inflation**                                 | Lạm phát điểm số                                  | The trend of awarding progressively higher academic grades for work that would have received lower grades in the past. | **Grade inflation** can devalue the meaning of a high grade.                                    |
| **The pressure to publish**                         | Áp lực phải xuất bản (bài báo khoa học)           | The pressure on academics to publish their research in academic journals to advance their careers.                     | **The pressure to publish** can sometimes lead to a focus on quantity over quality of research. |
| **Credentialism**                                   | Chủ nghĩa bằng cấp                                | The reliance on formal qualifications or certifications to determine if someone is suitable for a job.                 | **Credentialism** can create barriers for skilled individuals who lack a formal degree.         |
| **To be irrelevant to the needs of the economy**    | Không liên quan đến nhu cầu của nền kinh tế       | To not be useful or applicable to what the economy requires.                                                           | Some humanities degrees are criticized for being **irrelevant to the needs of the economy**.    |
