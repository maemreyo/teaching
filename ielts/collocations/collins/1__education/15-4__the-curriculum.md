### 📚 **Các môn học Cốt lõi & Học thuật (Core & Academic Subjects)**

| Collocation                                 | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                                | Short English Meaning                                                                        | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                          |
| :------------------------------------------ | :---------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Core subjects**                           | Các môn học cốt lõi                             | The most important subjects that all students are required to study.                         | **Core subjects** like mathematics, language, and science form the foundation of the curriculum.          |
| **Academic disciplines**                    | Các ngành học thuật                             | Different areas of study, especially at a university level.                                  | History, philosophy, and physics are all distinct **academic disciplines**.                               |
| **To gain a solid grounding in...**         | Có được nền tảng vững chắc về...                | To acquire a good basic knowledge and understanding of a subject.                            | A good primary education should help children **gain a solid grounding in** literacy and numeracy.        |
| **A broad and balanced curriculum**         | Một chương trình học rộng và cân bằng           | A curriculum that includes a wide and fair variety of subjects from different fields.        | Educators argue that a **broad and balanced curriculum** is essential for a child's holistic development. |
| **Rote memorization of facts and figures**  | Việc học vẹt các sự kiện và số liệu             | The process of learning information by repetition, often without deep understanding.         | An overemphasis on exams can lead to the **rote memorization of facts and figures**.                      |
| **Theoretical knowledge**                   | Kiến thức lý thuyết                             | Knowledge that is based on ideas, principles, and concepts rather than practical experience. | A university education often emphasizes **theoretical knowledge**.                                        |
| **To master the fundamentals of a subject** | Nắm vững những kiến thức cơ bản của một môn học | To learn and understand the most basic and important principles of a subject completely.     | Before moving to advanced topics, it is crucial **to master the fundamentals of a subject**.              |
| **Intellectual rigor**                      | Sự nghiêm ngặt về mặt trí tuệ                   | The quality of being intellectually demanding, thorough, and precise.                        | The curriculum should have sufficient **intellectual rigor** to challenge students.                       |
| **An academic-focused curriculum**          | Một chương trình học tập trung vào học thuật    | A curriculum that prioritizes the study of traditional academic subjects.                    | An **academic-focused curriculum** may not be suitable for all types of learners.                         |

### 🛠️ **Kỹ năng Thực tiễn & Hướng nghiệp (Practical & Vocational Skills)**

| Collocation                                      | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                                                     | Short English Meaning                                                                                            | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                         |
| :----------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Practical, real-world skills**                 | Các kỹ năng thực tế, đời thực                                        | Abilities that are useful and applicable in everyday life and the workplace.                                     | Many argue that schools should teach **practical, real-world skills** such as financial literacy.        |
| **Vocational training and courses**              | Đào tạo và các khóa học nghề                                         | Training and courses that prepare students for a specific trade or occupation.                                   | **Vocational courses** prepare students for trades like plumbing or information technology.              |
| **Hands-on, experiential learning**              | Học tập thực hành, qua trải nghiệm                                   | The process of learning by doing things oneself and reflecting on the experience.                                | **Hands-on, experiential learning** is often more memorable than passive listening.                      |
| **Financial literacy**                           | Kiến thức tài chính                                                  | The knowledge and skills to make informed and effective decisions with one's financial resources.                | A lack of **financial literacy** can lead to debt and poor financial decisions in adulthood.             |
| **To prepare students for the modern workforce** | Chuẩn bị cho học sinh/sinh viên bước vào lực lượng lao động hiện đại | To equip students with the skills and knowledge needed to succeed in today's job market.                         | The curriculum must be designed **to prepare students for the modern workforce**.                        |
| **To equip students with transferable skills**   | Trang bị cho học sinh/sinh viên các kỹ năng có thể chuyển đổi        | To provide students with abilities that are useful across many different jobs and industries.                    | The goal is **to equip students with transferable skills** like communication and teamwork.              |
| **Real-world applications**                      | Các ứng dụng trong thế giới thực                                     | The practical uses of a theory or skill in real-life situations.                                                 | Students are more motivated when they can see the **real-world applications** of what they are learning. |
| **Soft skills**                                  | Các kỹ năng mềm                                                      | Personal attributes that enable someone to interact effectively and harmoniously with other people.              | **Soft skills**, such as collaboration and emotional intelligence, are highly valued by employers.       |
| **Digital competence**                           | Năng lực số                                                          | The confident and critical use of information and communication technology for work, leisure, and communication. | **Digital competence** is a fundamental skill for life in the 21st century.                              |
| **Entrepreneurial skills**                       | Các kỹ năng khởi nghiệp                                              | The skills needed to start and run a new business, such as creativity, risk-taking, and leadership.              | Some schools are now offering courses to develop students' **entrepreneurial skills**.                   |

### 🎨 **Tranh luận: Nghệ thuật & Nhân văn vs. Khoa học (The Debate: Arts & Humanities vs. STEM)**

| Collocation                                                           | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                                                | Short English Meaning                                                                            | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                       |
| :-------------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **STEM subjects (Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics)** | Các môn học STEM                                                | The academic disciplines of science, technology, engineering, and mathematics.                   | Many governments are prioritizing investment in **STEM subjects** to drive economic innovation.        |
| **The arts and humanities**                                           | Các môn nghệ thuật và nhân văn                                  | Subjects such as art, music, literature, history, and philosophy that study human culture.       | **The arts and humanities** are crucial for fostering creativity, empathy, and cultural understanding. |
| **To foster creativity and self-expression**                          | Nuôi dưỡng sự sáng tạo và thể hiện bản thân                     | To encourage the development of imagination and the ability to express one's ideas and feelings. | Music and drama classes help **to foster creativity and self-expression**.                             |
| **To develop analytical and logical reasoning skills**                | Phát triển kỹ năng suy luận phân tích và logic                  | To cultivate the ability to think in a structured, analytical, and logical way.                  | Science and maths are excellent subjects **to develop analytical and logical reasoning skills**.       |
| **A well-rounded education**                                          | Một nền giáo dục toàn diện                                      | An education that is balanced, covering a wide range of subjects and developing various skills.  | A **well-rounded education** should include a balance of both STEM and arts subjects.                  |
| **To place greater emphasis on...**                                   | Chú trọng nhiều hơn vào...                                      | To give more importance, attention, or value to something.                                       | Some argue that schools should **place greater emphasis on** STEM education.                           |
| **To be marginalized or undervalued in the curriculum**               | Bị gạt ra ngoài lề hoặc bị đánh giá thấp trong chương trình học | To be treated as less important within the school curriculum.                                    | Subjects like art and music are often **marginalized or undervalued in the curriculum**.               |
| **Cultural appreciation**                                             | Sự cảm thụ, trân trọng văn hóa                                  | The understanding and enjoyment of the arts, customs, and beliefs of different cultures.         | Studying the humanities can lead to a deeper **cultural appreciation**.                                |
| **The perceived importance of...**                                    | Tầm quan trọng được nhận thức của...                            | How important something is considered to be by people in general.                                | **The perceived importance of** STEM subjects often leads to larger budgets for science departments.   |
| **To complement each other**                                          | Bổ sung cho nhau                                                | To combine well together, with each part enhancing the other.                                    | The skills learned in arts and sciences can **complement each other** perfectly.                       |

### 📜 **Thiết kế & Mục tiêu Chương trình học (Curriculum Design & Goals)**

| Collocation                                         | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                                                 | Short English Meaning                                                                                                       | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                         |
| :-------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **The national curriculum**                         | Chương trình giáo dục quốc gia                                   | The official, government-mandated curriculum for all state schools in a country.                                            | **The national curriculum** sets out the subjects that all state-funded schools are required to teach.   |
| **Curriculum design and development**               | Thiết kế và phát triển chương trình học                          | The process of creating, planning, and improving a curriculum.                                                              | **Curriculum design and development** should involve input from teachers, parents, and industry experts. |
| **To be a compulsory part of the syllabus**         | Là một phần bắt buộc của giáo trình                              | To be a required component of the plan of what is to be studied.                                                            | Physical education should **be a compulsory part of the school syllabus**.                               |
| **Extracurricular activities**                      | Các hoạt động ngoại khóa                                         | Activities, such as sports and clubs, that are pursued in addition to the normal course of study.                           | **Extracurricular activities** are vital for developing social skills and building character.            |
| **A holistic approach to education**                | Một cách tiếp cận toàn diện đối với giáo dục                     | An educational philosophy that focuses on developing the whole child—intellectually, emotionally, socially, and physically. | A **holistic approach to education** values personal well-being as much as academic achievement.         |
| **To meet the needs of a rapidly changing society** | Đáp ứng nhu cầu của một xã hội đang thay đổi nhanh chóng         | To be relevant and suitable for the demands of the modern, evolving world.                                                  | The curriculum must be updated regularly **to meet the needs of a rapidly changing society**.            |
| **To tailor the curriculum to individual needs**    | Điều chỉnh chương trình học cho phù hợp với nhu-cầu cá nhân      | To adapt the curriculum to suit the specific learning requirements and interests of each student.                           | It is a challenge in large classes **to tailor the curriculum to individual needs**.                     |
| **The aims and objectives of the curriculum**       | Các mục tiêu và mục đích của chương trình học                    | The intended goals and learning outcomes of the curriculum.                                                                 | **The aims and objectives of the curriculum** should be clearly defined.                                 |
| **To be relevant to students' lives**               | Phù hợp, liên quan đến cuộc sống của học sinh                    | To be connected to the real-world experiences and interests of students.                                                    | The curriculum is more engaging when it is **relevant to students' lives**.                              |
| **A rigid versus a flexible curriculum**            | Một chương trình học cứng nhắc và một chương trình học linh hoạt | The contrast between a fixed, unchangeable curriculum and one that can be adapted.                                          | Many educators advocate for a more **flexible curriculum** that can respond to student interests.        |
