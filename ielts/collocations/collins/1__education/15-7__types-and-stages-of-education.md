### 🏛️ **Các loại hình Giáo dục (Types of Education)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Formal education** | Giáo dục chính quy | Education in a structured system like school or university. | **Formal education** provides a systematic way of acquiring knowledge and qualifications. |
| **Informal education** | Giáo dục phi chính quy | Learning that occurs outside of a structured curriculum. | We gain a lot of **informal education** through reading, travel, and life experience. |
| **Mainstream education** | Giáo dục đại trà | The system of education that the majority of students attend. | Children with mild disabilities are often included in **mainstream education**. |
| **Special needs education** | Giáo dục đặc biệt | Education for students with disabilities or learning difficulties. | **Special needs education** requires specially trained teachers and resources. |
| **Vocational education** | Giáo dục nghề | Training for a specific job or trade. | **Vocational education** equips students with the practical skills needed for the workforce. |
| **Public / State education** | Giáo dục công lập | Education funded and controlled by the government. | Many people believe that **public education** should be free for all citizens. |
| **Private education** | Giáo dục tư thục | Education provided by a private, fee-paying institution. | The cost of **private education** can be a significant financial burden for families. |
| **Single-sex education** | Giáo dục một giới | Educating boys and girls in separate schools or classes. | Some studies suggest that **single-sex education** can benefit female students in STEM subjects. |
| **Co-educational education** | Giáo dục cả hai giới | Educating boys and girls in the same schools and classes. | **Co-educational education** prepares students for a mixed-gender world. |
| **Homeschooling** | Giáo dục tại gia | The practice of educating children at home instead of sending them to school. | The popularity of **homeschooling** has increased in recent years. |

### 🔄 **Các giai đoạn & Triết lý Giáo dục (Stages & Philosophies of Education)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Preschool education** | Giáo dục mầm non | Education for children before they start primary school. | **Preschool education** plays a vital role in a child's early social and cognitive development. |
| **Primary / Elementary education** | Giáo dục tiểu học | The first stage of formal education, typically for children aged 5-11. | The focus of **primary education** is on developing basic literacy and numeracy skills. |
| **Secondary education** | Giáo dục trung học | The stage of education following primary school, for adolescents. | In many countries, **secondary education** is divided into junior and senior high school. |
| **Compulsory education** | Giáo dục bắt buộc | The period of education that all children must receive by law. | In most countries, **compulsory education** lasts for at least nine years. |
| **Lifelong learning** | Học tập suốt đời | The voluntary pursuit of knowledge throughout life. | In a rapidly changing world, **lifelong learning** is essential for personal and professional growth. |
| **A well-rounded education** | Một nền giáo dục toàn diện | An education that is balanced and develops a person in multiple areas. | A **well-rounded education** should include arts and sports, not just academic subjects. |
| **A holistic approach to education** | Một cách tiếp cận toàn diện | An educational philosophy that focuses on developing the whole child. | A **holistic approach to education** values emotional and social well-being as much as academic success. |
| **Character education** | Giáo dục nhân cách | The process of teaching children about core ethical values. | **Character education** aims to develop traits like honesty, responsibility, and respect. |
| **Educational standards** | Tiêu chuẩn giáo dục | The level of quality that education systems are expected to meet. | The government has a responsibility to maintain high **educational standards** in all schools. |
| **Progressive education** | Giáo dục tiến bộ | A philosophy that emphasizes learning by doing and problem-solving. | **Progressive education** often involves hands-on projects and collaborative learning. |
