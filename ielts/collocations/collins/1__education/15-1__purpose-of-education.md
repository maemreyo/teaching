| :---------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

| **To acquire practical and job-specific skills**      | Tiếp thu các kỹ năng thực tiễn và chuyên môn cho công việc  | To learn skills that are directly applicable to a particular job or profession.                   | Many people attend vocational schools **to acquire practical and job-specific skills**.                     |
| **To enhance one's career prospects / employability** | Nâng cao triển vọng sự nghiệp / khả năng được tuyển dụng    | To improve one's future job opportunities and attractiveness to employers.                        | A university degree can significantly **enhance one's career prospects**.                                   |
| **To secure a stable and prosperous livelihood**      | Đảm bảo một cuộc sống ổn định và thịnh vượng                | To ensure one can earn enough money to live comfortably and securely.                             | For many, the main purpose of education is **to secure a stable and prosperous livelihood**.                |
| **To increase one's earning potential**               | Tăng khả năng kiếm tiền của một người                       | To raise the amount of money an individual can potentially earn over their lifetime.              | Higher education is often seen as a direct path **to increasing one's earning potential**.                  |
| **To gain a competitive edge in the job market**      | Có được lợi thế cạnh tranh trên thị trường việc làm         | To get an advantage over other candidates when applying for jobs.                                 | In a competitive job market, a Master's degree can help graduates **gain a competitive edge**.              |

| **To bridge the skills gap**                          | Thu hẹp khoảng trống về kỹ năng                             | To reduce the disparity between the skills employers need and the skills the workforce possesses. | The curriculum should be updated **to bridge the skills gap** between academia and industry.                |
| **To ensure upward social mobility**                  | Đảm bảo sự dịch chuyển xã hội đi lên                        | To make sure that people have the opportunity to move to a higher social and economic class.      | Education is one of the most powerful tools **to ensure upward social mobility**.                           |

### 🧠 **Kiến thức & Kỹ năng (Knowledge & Skills)**

| Collocation                                    | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                            | Short English Meaning                                                                                | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                       |
| :--------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------ | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **The acquisition of knowledge**               | Sự tiếp thu kiến thức                       | The process of learning and accumulating facts, information, and understanding.                      | While **the acquisition of knowledge** is important, the ability to apply it is crucial.               |
| **To develop critical thinking skills**        | Phát triển kỹ năng tư duy phản biện         | To cultivate the ability to analyze information objectively and make a reasoned judgment.            | A key goal of higher education is **to develop students' critical thinking skills**.                   |
| **To cultivate problem-solving abilities**     | Trau dồi khả năng giải quyết vấn đề         | To grow and improve the capacity to find effective solutions to complex problems.                    | Education should **cultivate problem-solving abilities** that are applicable in real-world situations. |
| **Rote memorization**                          | Học vẹt, ghi nhớ máy móc                    | The process of learning something by repeating it many times, without understanding it.              | An over-reliance on **rote memorization** can stifle creativity and critical thought.                  |
| **A broad and balanced curriculum**            | Một chương trình giảng dạy rộng và cân bằng | A curriculum that includes a wide range of subjects, covering both arts and sciences.                | A **broad and balanced curriculum** is essential for the holistic development of a child.              |
| **To broaden one's intellectual horizons**     | Mở rộng tầm hiểu biết tri thức              | To expand the range of things one knows about and is interested in.                                  | Studying philosophy and literature helps **to broaden one's intellectual horizons**.                   |
| **Core subjects versus peripheral subjects**   | Các môn học cốt lõi và các môn học phụ      | The debate between essential subjects like math and science versus subjects like art and music.      | There is a constant debate about funding for **core subjects versus peripheral subjects**.             |
| **Transferable skills**                        | Các kỹ năng có thể chuyển đổi               | Skills and abilities that are relevant and useful across different areas of life and work.           | Teamwork and communication are **transferable skills** that are valued in any profession.              |
| **Academic knowledge versus practical skills** | Kiến thức học thuật và kỹ năng thực hành    | The contrast between theoretical knowledge learned in a classroom and hands-on, practical abilities. | A good education system should balance **academic knowledge with practical skills**.                   |
| **Digital literacy**                           | Trình độ kỹ thuật số                        | The ability to use, understand, and evaluate information from digital sources.                       | In the 21st century, **digital literacy** is a fundamental skill.                                      |
