| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Academic performance** | Kết quả/Thành tích học tập | How well a student does in their studies. | A student's **academic performance** is often measured by their grades and exam results. |
| **To be a straight-A student** | Là một học sinh đạt điểm A | To get the best possible grades in all subjects. | Her goal was **to be a straight-A student** and get into a top university. |
| **To get good grades** | Đạt điểm cao | To receive high marks in academic work. | He studied hard **to get good grades** and make his parents proud. |
| **A diligent / conscientious student** | Một học sinh chăm chỉ / tận tâm | A student who works hard and is careful to do their work well. | She is a **diligent student** who always completes her homework on time. |

### 📝 **Đánh giá & Thi cử (Assessment & Examinations)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Formal examination** | Kỳ thi chính thức | An official test taken at the end of a course. | Students have to pass a **formal examination** to graduate. |
| **Standardized test** | Bài kiểm tra chuẩn hóa | A test that is administered and scored in a consistent manner. | **Standardized tests** are used to compare student performance across different schools. |
| **Continuous assessment** | Đánh giá liên tục | Evaluating students' work throughout a course. | Many educators prefer **continuous assessment** to a single final exam. |
| **Learning outcomes** | Kết quả học tập (đầu ra) | The specific knowledge or skills a student is expected to learn. | The syllabus clearly defines the **learning outcomes** for the course. |
| **To sit / take an exam** | Tham dự một kỳ thi | To participate in an exam. | All students are required **to sit an exam** in mathematics at the end of the year. |
| **To pass / fail an exam** | Đậu / Rớt một kỳ thi | To succeed / not succeed in an exam. | She was thrilled to **pass her final exams** with high marks. |
| **Exam results / grades** | Kết quả thi / Điểm số | The marks a student receives in an exam. | Your **exam results** will be sent to you by post. |
| **To cram for an exam** | Học nhồi nhét cho kỳ thi | To study intensively over a short period of time just before an exam. | **Cramming for an exam** is not an effective long-term learning strategy. |
| **To cheat in an exam** | Gian lận trong thi cử | To act dishonestly to gain an advantage in an exam. | Any student caught **cheating in an exam** will be immediately disqualified. |
| **A mock exam** | Kỳ thi thử | A practice exam taken before the real one. | We have **a mock exam** next week to prepare us for the final test. |
