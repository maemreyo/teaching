### 🏙️ **Cuộc sống Thành thị (Urban Life)**

| Collocation                                | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                               | Short English Meaning                                                                                            | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                           |
| :----------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **The hustle and bustle of city life**     | Sự hối hả và nhộn nhịp của cuộc sống thành phố | The busy, noisy, and energetic activity of a city.                                                               | Some people thrive on **the hustle and bustle of city life**, while others find it overwhelming.           |
| **A fast-paced lifestyle**                 | Lối sống nhịp độ nhanh                         | A life that is very busy, active, and often stressful.                                                           | A **fast-paced lifestyle** in a major city can be both exciting and exhausting.                            |
| **The anonymity of urban living**          | Sự ẩn danh của cuộc sống đô thị                | The state of being unknown and not recognized by others in a large city.                                         | While some find it lonely, others enjoy **the anonymity of urban living**.                                 |
| **A vibrant and multicultural metropolis** | Một đô thị đa văn hóa và sôi động              | A large, exciting city with a diverse mix of cultures, ethnicities, and ideas.                                   | London is a **vibrant and multicultural metropolis** with a rich tapestry of food, art, and music.         |
| **To be surrounded by modern amenities**   | Được bao quanh bởi các tiện nghi hiện đại      | To have easy access to modern facilities like shops, restaurants, public transport, and entertainment venues.    | Urban dwellers are **surrounded by modern amenities** that offer convenience and variety.                  |
| **Round-the-clock activity**               | Hoạt động 24/7                                 | A state where activities, services, and entertainment are available 24 hours a day.                              | In a megacity, there is **round-the-clock activity**, meaning the city never truly sleeps.                 |
| **A concrete jungle**                      | Một "rừng bê tông"                             | A disparaging term for a city with a high concentration of large, modern buildings, perceived as lacking nature. | Some people feel that large cities are just a **concrete jungle** with no connection to the natural world. |
| **A hub of innovation and opportunity**    | Trung tâm của sự đổi mới và cơ hội             | A central point for new ideas, economic activity, and career advancement.                                        | Silicon Valley is known as a global **hub of innovation and opportunity** in the tech industry.            |
| **Cultural hotspots**                      | Các điểm nóng văn hóa                          | Places that are famous for their cultural activities, such as theaters, galleries, and music venues.             | Cities are typically full of **cultural hotspots** that attract both tourists and residents.               |
| **A melting pot of cultures**              | Một "nồi lẩu" đa văn hóa                       | A place where many different people and cultures exist together and mix.                                         | New York City is a famous **melting pot of cultures**, with communities from all over the world.           |

### 🌳 **Cuộc sống Nông thôn (Rural Life)**

| Collocation                            | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                             | Short English Meaning                                                                      | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                     |
| :------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A slower pace of life**              | Nhịp sống chậm hơn                           | A less hurried, more relaxed, and less stressful way of living.                            | Many people move to the countryside in search of a **slower pace of life**.                          |
| **A tranquil and serene environment**  | Một môi trường yên tĩnh và thanh bình        | A calm, peaceful, and quiet atmosphere, free from noise and disruption.                    | The countryside offers a **tranquil and serene environment**, which is beneficial for mental health. |
| **A close-knit community**             | Một cộng đồng gắn bó, khăng khít             | A community where people have strong, supportive relationships with each other.            | In a **close-knit community**, neighbors often know and actively support one another.                |
| **To be immersed in nature**           | Đắm mình trong thiên nhiên                   | To be completely surrounded by and connected to the natural world.                         | Living in a rural area allows you **to be immersed in nature** and its changing seasons.             |
| **The idyllic countryside**            | Vùng nông thôn bình dị, đẹp như tranh        | A countryside that is extremely beautiful, peaceful, and picturesque.                      | The film was set in the **idyllic countryside** of Tuscany.                                          |
| **A strong sense of community spirit** | Tinh thần cộng đồng mạnh mẽ                  | A feeling of fellowship, belonging, and mutual support among the members of a community.   | Small villages often have a **strong sense of community spirit**.                                    |
| **Open green spaces**                  | Các không gian xanh mở                       | Large areas of land with grass and trees, such as parks or fields.                         | One of the biggest advantages of rural life is the abundance of **open green spaces**.               |
| **A sense of isolation**               | Cảm giác bị cô lập, tách biệt                | The feeling of being far away from other people and services.                              | A potential drawback of living in a remote rural area is **a sense of isolation**.                   |
| **Limited access to amenities**        | Sự hạn chế trong việc tiếp cận các tiện nghi | Having fewer modern facilities like specialized shops, hospitals, or entertainment venues. | Rural residents often face **limited access to specialized amenities** and services.                 |
| **Agricultural heritage**              | Di sản nông nghiệp                           | The traditions, practices, and culture associated with farming in a particular area.       | The region is proud of its rich **agricultural heritage**.                                           |

### ⚖️ **So sánh & Các khía cạnh chung (Comparisons & General Aspects)**

| Collocation                           | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                       | Short English Meaning                                                                            | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                        |
| :------------------------------------ | :------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **A stark contrast between...**       | Một sự tương phản rõ rệt giữa...       | A very clear and obvious difference between two things.                                          | There is a **stark contrast between** the pace of life in the city and the countryside.                 |
| **A matter of personal preference**   | Một vấn đề về sở thích cá nhân         | Something that depends entirely on an individual's personal likes and dislikes.                  | Whether to live in an urban or rural setting is ultimately a **matter of personal preference**.         |
| **The quality of life**               | Chất lượng cuộc sống                   | The general well-being of individuals and societies, including health, comfort, and happiness.   | The **quality of life** is often judged by factors like safety, pollution levels, and community spirit. |
| **The cost of living**                | Chi phí sinh hoạt                      | The amount of money needed to cover basic expenses such as housing, food, taxes, and healthcare. | **The cost of living** is significantly higher in major urban centers compared to rural areas.          |
| **A sense of belonging**              | Cảm giác thân thuộc, thuộc về          | The feeling of being an accepted and valued member of a group or community.                      | Some people find a greater **sense of belonging** in a small rural community.                           |
| **Career prospects / opportunities**  | Triển vọng / cơ hội nghề nghiệp        | The chances of finding a job and advancing in one's career.                                      | Major cities typically offer superior **career prospects** and a wider range of job opportunities.      |
| **Access to services and facilities** | Tiếp cận các dịch vụ và cơ sở vật chất | The ability to easily use services like healthcare, education, and public transport.             | **Access to specialized healthcare and higher education** is often better in urban areas.               |
| **The daily commute**                 | Việc đi lại hàng ngày                  | The regular journey between one's home and place of work.                                        | **The daily commute** can be a major source of stress for those living in the suburbs of large cities.  |
| **Peace and quiet**                   | Sự yên bình và tĩnh lặng               | A state of calm, tranquility, and freedom from noise.                                            | If you value **peace and quiet**, the countryside is the ideal place to live.                           |
| **The urban-rural divide**            | Sự phân hóa thành thị - nông thôn      | The social, economic, and cultural differences between urban and rural areas.                    | The government is implementing policies to bridge **the urban-rural divide**.                           |
