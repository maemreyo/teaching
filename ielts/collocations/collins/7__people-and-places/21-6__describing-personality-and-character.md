### 👍 **Phẩm chất Tích cực (Positive Qualities)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **To have a great sense of humor** | Có khiếu hài hước tuyệt vời | To be very good at making people laugh. | My best friend **has a great sense of humor**, which is why I love spending time with her. |
| **To be brutally honest** | Thẳng thắn một cách phũ phàng | To be honest in a way that can be hurtful, but is direct and true. | I can always rely on him **to be brutally honest** with me when I need a real opinion. |
| **To have a heart of gold** | Có một trái tim vàng (rất tốt bụng) | To be a very kind and generous person. | She volunteers every weekend; she truly **has a heart of gold**. |
| **To be down-to-earth** | Là người thực tế, không viển vông | To be practical, realistic, and friendly. | Despite his fame, the actor was very **down-to-earth** and easy to talk to. |
| **To be the life and soul of the party** | Là linh hồn của bữa tiệc | To be the most lively and entertaining person at a social gathering. | He's always the first on the dance floor; he's **the life and soul of the party**. |
| **To have a can-do attitude** | Có thái độ "làm được" (tích cực, tự tin) | To have a positive and confident approach to challenges. | Her **can-do attitude** makes her a great leader for the team. |
| **To be fiercely loyal** | Cực kỳ trung thành | To be extremely faithful and supportive of someone. | A good friend is someone who is **fiercely loyal** through thick and thin. |
| **To have a thirst for knowledge** | Có một niềm khao khát kiến thức | To have a strong desire to learn new things. | As a scientist, she has always **had a thirst for knowledge**. |
| **To be a good listener** | Là một người biết lắng nghe | To be good at paying attention to what others are saying. | A therapist needs **to be a good listener** to understand their clients. |
| **To be incredibly resilient** | Cực kỳ kiên cường | To be able to recover quickly from difficult situations. | Despite facing many setbacks, she has remained **incredibly resilient**. |

### 👎 **Phẩm chất Tiêu cực (Negative Qualities)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **To be painfully shy** | Nhút nhát một cách đáng thương | To be extremely shy and uncomfortable in social situations. | As a child, I was **painfully shy** and found it hard to make friends. |
| **To have a short temper / to be quick-tempered** | Nóng tính | To get angry very easily. | He **has a short temper**, so you have to be careful what you say to him. |
| **To be brutally blunt** | Thẳng thừng một cách tàn nhẫn | To say exactly what you think without trying to be polite. | His feedback was **brutally blunt**, leaving no room for misinterpretation. |
| **To be set in one's ways** | Cố định, khó thay đổi (bảo thủ) | To have fixed habits and opinions that you are unwilling to change. | My grandfather is very **set in his ways** and refuses to use a computer. |
| **To be a control freak** | Là một người thích kiểm soát | To be someone who wants to control every detail of a situation. | Working for a **control freak** can be very stressful and demotivating. |
| **To have a selfish streak** | Có một nét tính cách ích kỷ | To have a tendency to be selfish at times. | While he is generally kind, he can sometimes **have a selfish streak**. |
| **To be a bit of a gossip** | Là người hơi thích ngồi lê đôi mách | To be someone who enjoys talking about other people's private lives. | I try to avoid her at work because she's **a bit of a gossip**. |
| **To be arrogant and condescending** | Kiêu ngạo và trịch thượng | To behave as if you are more important or intelligent than other people. | His **arrogant and condescending** attitude makes him very unpopular. |
| **To be stubborn as a mule** | Cứng đầu như một con lừa | To be extremely stubborn and unwilling to change your mind. | I tried to convince him, but he's **as stubborn as a mule**. |
| **To be prone to exaggeration** | Dễ dàng, có xu hướng phóng đại | To have a tendency to make things seem larger or more important than they really are. | He's a great storyteller, but he's **prone to exaggeration**. |
