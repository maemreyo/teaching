### 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 **Mối quan hệ Gia đình (Family Relationships)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A close-knit family** | Một gia đình gắn bó, khăng khít | A family where the members have very close and supportive relationships. | We are a very **close-knit family** and we see each other almost every weekend. |
| **A strong family bond** | Sự gắn kết gia đình bền chặt | A powerful and loving connection between family members. | Despite the distance, they maintained a **strong family bond** through regular calls. |
| **Unconditional love and support** | Tình yêu và sự hỗ trợ vô điều kiện | Love and support that is given without any limitations or conditions. | The greatest gift a family can offer is **unconditional love and support**. |
| **To provide emotional security** | Cung cấp sự an toàn về mặt cảm xúc | To make someone feel emotionally safe, accepted, and cared for. | A stable home environment **provides emotional security** for a child. |
| **A dysfunctional family** | Một gia đình không êm ấm | A family in which conflict, misbehavior, and often abuse occur continually. | The film tells the story of a young boy growing up in a **dysfunctional family**. |
| **A family gathering / reunion** | Một buổi sum họp / đoàn tụ gia đình | An occasion when many members of a family get together. | We are having a big **family reunion** to celebrate my grandparents' 50th anniversary. |
| **Immediate family vs. extended family** | Gia đình ruột thịt và gia đình mở rộng | Your closest relatives (parents, children, siblings) vs. all your relatives (uncles, aunts, cousins). | The wedding was attended by both their **immediate and extended families**. |
| **To look up to a family member** | Ngưỡng mộ một thành viên trong gia đình | To admire and respect a relative, often as a role model. | I have always **looked up to my older sister** for her intelligence and determination. |
| **To drift apart from one's family** | Trở nên xa cách với gia đình | To gradually become less close to or involved with your family members. | After moving abroad, he began **to drift apart from his family**. |
| **Sibling rivalry** | Sự cạnh tranh giữa anh chị em | The natural jealousy and competition between brothers and sisters. | A certain amount of **sibling rivalry** is normal in most families. |

### 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 **Tình bạn (Friendships)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **To form a strong / lasting friendship** | Xây dựng một tình bạn bền chặt / lâu dài | To develop a friendship that is powerful and continues for a long time. | We **formed a lasting friendship** during our time at university together. |
| **A circle of friends** | Một nhóm bạn | A group of friends that you regularly spend time with. | She has a wide **circle of friends** with very diverse interests. |
| **A casual acquaintance vs. a close friend** | Một người quen sơ và một người bạn thân | Someone you know but not very well vs. a very good friend that you know well. | I have many **casual acquaintances** at work, but only a few **close friends**. |
| **To strike up a friendship with someone** | Bắt đầu một tình bạn với ai đó | To start a friendship with someone. | I **struck up a friendship with** a fellow traveler on the train to Lisbon. |
| **To enjoy someone's company** | Thích ở bên cạnh ai đó | To like spending time with someone. | He's very easygoing and I really **enjoy his company**. |
| **To have a lot in common** | Có nhiều điểm chung | To share similar interests, beliefs, attitudes, or opinions. | We became friends instantly because we **have a lot in common**. |
| **To lose touch with someone** | Mất liên lạc với ai đó | To stop communicating with someone, usually because they live far away. | It's sad how you can **lose touch with** old school friends over the years. |
| **To drift apart** | Trở nên xa cách | To gradually become less friendly or close to someone. | We were best friends in college, but we started **to drift apart** after graduation. |
| **A shoulder to cry on** | Một bờ vai để nương tựa (người để tâm sự) | Someone who is willing to listen to your problems and give you sympathy. | When I'm having a tough time, my best friend is always there as **a shoulder to cry on**. |
| **To see eye to eye on most things** | Đồng quan điểm về hầu hết mọi thứ | To agree on or have the same opinion about most things. | We have a strong friendship because we **see eye to eye on most things**. |
