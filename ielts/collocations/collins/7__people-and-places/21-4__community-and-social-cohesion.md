### 🤝 **Khái niệm & Tinh thần Cộng đồng (The Concept & Spirit of Community)**

| Collocation                         | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                        | Short English Meaning                                                                                       | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                          |
| :---------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A sense of community**            | Ý thức cộng đồng                        | A feeling of belonging and mutual support among people who share a common geographical area or interest.    | Living in a neighborhood with a strong **sense of community** can improve residents' overall well-being.  |
| **Community spirit**                | Tinh thần cộng đồng                     | The willingness of people to help each other and participate in activities within their community.          | Volunteering for local events is a great way to show **community spirit**.                                |
| **A close-knit community**          | Một cộng đồng gắn bó, khăng khít        | A community where people have strong, supportive relationships and are very involved in each other's lives. | In a **close-knit community**, people tend to know their neighbors and look out for each other.           |
| **A vibrant and diverse community** | Một cộng đồng sôi động và đa dạng       | A community that is full of life, energy, and a rich mix of different cultures and backgrounds.             | The area is known for its **vibrant and diverse community**, with frequent festivals and cultural events. |
| **To belong to a community**        | Thuộc về một cộng đồng                  | To be an accepted and integral member of a particular social group.                                         | Having a shared identity and values helps people feel that they **belong to a community**.                |
| **Community members / residents**   | Các thành viên / cư dân trong cộng đồng | The people who live in a particular area.                                                                   | **Community members** were consulted before the new park was designed.                                    |
| **Local identity**                  | Bản sắc địa phương                      | The unique character and sense of place of a particular area, shaped by its history, culture, and people.   | The old market is a cherished part of the town's **local identity**.                                      |
| **A sense of place**                | Cảm thức về nơi chốn                    | The feeling of connection and attachment that people have to a specific location.                           | Historic buildings and public parks can help to create a strong **sense of place**.                       |

### 📈 **Xây dựng & Củng cố Cộng đồng (Building & Strengthening Community)**

| Collocation                            | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                         | Short English Meaning                                                                                            | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                       |
| :------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **To foster a sense of community**     | Nuôi dưỡng tinh thần cộng đồng           | To consciously encourage and promote a feeling of connection and belonging among people.                         | Public spaces like parks and libraries can help **to foster a sense of community**.                    |
| **To build strong social ties**        | Xây dựng các mối quan hệ xã hội bền chặt | To develop deep and meaningful connections with other people.                                                    | Participating in local clubs is a good way **to build strong social ties** with neighbors.             |
| **Community-led initiatives**          | Các sáng kiến do cộng đồng dẫn dắt       | Projects or activities that are planned and run by the people within a community.                                | The community garden is a successful **community-led initiative** that has brought residents together. |
| **To participate in community events** | Tham gia vào các sự kiện cộng đồng       | To take part in local festivals, meetings, and other organized activities.                                       | **Participating in community events** is key to strengthening social bonds.                            |
| **Community centers**                  | Các trung tâm cộng đồng                  | Buildings that provide a space for social, educational, and recreational activities for the local community.     | **Community centers** provide a venue for a wide range of local activities and services.               |
| **To look out for one another**        | Trông chừng, chăm sóc lẫn nhau           | To take care of and be concerned about the welfare of each other.                                                | A strong community is one where people **look out for one another**, especially the vulnerable.        |
| **A strong support network**           | Một mạng lưới hỗ trợ vững chắc           | A group of people (friends, family, neighbors) who provide practical and emotional support.                      | Having a **strong support network** is crucial in times of crisis or hardship.                         |
| **To strengthen community bonds**      | Củng cố sự gắn kết cộng đồng             | To make the connections and relationships between people in a community stronger.                                | Local traditions and festivals can **strengthen community bonds**.                                     |
| **Grassroots organizations**           | Các tổ chức quần chúng (từ cơ sở)        | Organizations that are started and run by ordinary people in a local area to deal with a local issue.            | **Grassroots organizations** play a vital role in addressing the specific needs of a community.        |
| **Collective action**                  | Hành động tập thể                        | Action taken together by a group of people whose goal is to enhance their status and achieve a common objective. | The residents took **collective action** to protest the closure of their local library.                |

### 📉 **Sự Suy tàn & Thách thức (Decline & Challenges)**

| Collocation                                   | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                            | Short English Meaning                                                                                                | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                                           |
| :-------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------ | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **The erosion of community spirit**           | Sự xói mòn tinh thần cộng đồng              | The gradual weakening and disappearance of the feeling of community and mutual support.                              | The rise of individualism is often blamed for **the erosion of community spirit**.                                         |
| **Social isolation and alienation**           | Sự cô lập và xa lánh xã hội                 | The state of feeling separate, disconnected, and not part of any group.                                              | A lack of community interaction can lead to feelings of **social isolation and alienation**, especially among the elderly. |
| **The breakdown of community life**           | Sự tan rã của đời sống cộng đồng            | The failure or collapse of the social structures and relationships that define a community.                          | Critics argue that the modern, fast-paced lifestyle has caused a **breakdown of community life**.                          |
| **A lack of social interaction**              | Sự thiếu tương tác xã hội                   | Insufficient communication or contact between people.                                                                | The design of some modern residential areas leads to a **lack of social interaction** between neighbors.                   |
| **To feel disconnected from one's community** | Cảm thấy mất kết nối với cộng đồng của mình | To feel that you are not a part of or emotionally connected to your local area.                                      | Many people who move to big cities **feel disconnected from their local community**.                                       |
| **The anonymity of urban life**               | Sự ẩn danh của cuộc sống đô thị             | The state of being unknown and unrecognized in a large city.                                                         | **The anonymity of urban life** means that people can feel lonely even when surrounded by millions.                        |
| **Fragmented communities**                    | Các cộng đồng bị phân mảnh                  | Communities that are broken into smaller, disconnected parts, often with little interaction between them.            | Gated residential areas can sometimes create **fragmented communities**.                                                   |
| **Gentrification**                            | Quá trình "thượng lưu hóa"                  | The process whereby a poor urban area is changed by wealthier people moving in, often displacing existing residents. | **Gentrification** can improve an area's infrastructure but may also destroy its original community spirit.                |
| **To keep to oneself**                        | Sống khép kín, không giao du                | To avoid interacting with other people.                                                                              | In many urban neighborhoods, it is common for people **to keep to themselves**.                                            |
| **A transient population**                    | Một cộng đồng dân cư không ổn định          | A population that is constantly changing as people move in and out of an area.                                       | It is difficult to build a strong sense of community in an area with a highly **transient population**.                    |

### 🌍 **Gắn kết & Hòa nhập Xã hội (Social Cohesion & Inclusion)**

| Collocation                                | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                      | Short English Meaning                                                                                              | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                             |
| :----------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------ | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Social cohesion**                        | Sự gắn kết xã hội                     | The strength of relationships and the sense of solidarity among members of a community.                            | A multicultural society must work hard to maintain **social cohesion**.                      |
| **To live in harmony**                     | Sống hòa thuận                        | To live together peacefully and without disagreement.                                                              | The goal is to create a society where people from all backgrounds can **live in harmony**.   |
| **To promote social inclusion**            | Thúc đẩy sự hòa nhập xã hội           | To take action to ensure that all individuals and groups feel valued and are able to participate fully in society. | Public libraries play an important role in **promoting social inclusion**.                   |
| **To integrate into the local community**  | Hòa nhập vào cộng đồng địa phương     | To become an accepted and participating member of a local community.                                               | Language classes can help new immigrants **to integrate into the local community**.          |
| **To bridge the divide between groups**    | Thu hẹp khoảng cách giữa các nhóm     | To reduce the differences and improve understanding between two or more groups.                                    | The project aims **to bridge the divide between** different ethnic and religious groups.     |
| **A shared sense of identity and purpose** | Cảm thức chung về bản sắc và mục đích | A feeling among a group of having the same identity, goals, and values.                                            | A national celebration can create a **shared sense of identity and purpose** among citizens. |
| **The social fabric**                      | Kết cấu xã hội                        | The structure of relationships and customs that hold a society together.                                           | Trust and cooperation are essential threads in **the social fabric**.                        |
| **Civic participation**                    | Sự tham gia của công dân              | The active involvement of citizens in the public life of their communities and nation.                             | Voting and volunteering are both important forms of **civic participation**.                 |
| **Mutual trust and respect**               | Sự tin tưởng và tôn trọng lẫn nhau    | The belief in each other's reliability and the admiration for each other's qualities.                              | **Mutual trust and respect** are the foundations of a strong and cohesive community.         |
| **A tolerant and inclusive society**       | Một xã hội khoan dung và hòa nhập     | A society that accepts and welcomes people from all different backgrounds and lifestyles.                          | The ultimate aim is to build a **tolerant and inclusive society** for all.                   |
