### 🏛️ **Trách nhiệm & Can thiệp của Chính phủ (Government Responsibility & Intervention)**

| Collocation                                 | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                          | Short English Meaning                                                                     | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                          |
| :------------------------------------------ | :---------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **To safeguard public health**              | Bảo vệ sức khỏe cộng đồng                 | To protect the health of the general population.                                          | It is the fundamental duty of a government **to safeguard public health**.                                |
| **To have a duty / an obligation to...**    | Có nghĩa vụ / bổn phận phải...            | To have a moral or legal responsibility to do something.                                  | Governments **have an obligation to** ensure all citizens have access to basic healthcare.                |
| **Government intervention**                 | Sự can thiệp của chính phủ                | Action taken by a government to influence a situation, often in the economy or society.   | Some believe that **government intervention** is necessary to combat the obesity epidemic.                |
| **To play a pivotal role in...**            | Đóng một vai trò then chốt trong...       | To have a very important and central part in something.                                   | The state **plays a pivotal role in** funding medical research and health education.                      |
| **A pressing public health issue**          | Một vấn đề y tế công cộng cấp bách        | A health problem affecting a large part of the population that requires urgent attention. | Rising rates of obesity have become a **pressing public health issue**.                                   |
| **To allocate public funds for...**         | Phân bổ công quỹ cho...                   | To assign government money for a specific purpose.                                        | More **public funds should be allocated for** preventive healthcare rather than solely for treatment.     |
| **To prioritize public health initiatives** | Ưu tiên các sáng kiến y tế công cộng      | To give special importance to plans and strategies designed to improve public health.     | A responsible government will always **prioritize public health initiatives** in its budget.              |
| **The state's responsibility**              | Trách nhiệm của nhà nước                  | The duties and obligations of the government.                                             | There is ongoing debate about the extent of **the state's responsibility** for individual health choices. |
| **To legislate on public health matters**   | Lập pháp về các vấn đề y tế công cộng     | To make or enact laws related to public health.                                           | Parliaments have the power **to legislate on public health matters**, such as smoking bans.               |
| **To be within the government's purview**   | Nằm trong phạm vi quyền hạn của chính phủ | To be within the scope of the government's responsibilities and authority.                | Protecting citizens from pandemics is clearly **within the government's purview**.                        |

### 📜 **Chính sách & Biện pháp Cụ thể (Specific Policies & Measures)**

| Collocation                                             | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                                              | Short English Meaning                                                                             | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                   |
| :------------------------------------------------------ | :------------------------------------------------------------ | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **To launch a nationwide public health campaign**       | Phát động một chiến dịch y tế công cộng toàn quốc             | To start a national campaign to inform and educate the public about a health issue.               | The government **launched a nationwide public health campaign** to promote vaccination.            |
| **To raise public awareness about...**                  | Nâng cao nhận thức cộng đồng về...                            | To make the general population more knowledgeable and concerned about an issue.                   | Schools can help **to raise public awareness about** the importance of a balanced diet.            |
| **To impose a sin tax on...**                           | Áp đặt "thuế tội lỗi" lên...                                  | To introduce a tax on products considered unhealthy or socially harmful, like tobacco or alcohol. | One proposed solution is **to impose a sin tax on** sugary drinks and junk food.                   |
| **To subsidize healthy food options**                   | Trợ giá cho các lựa chọn thực phẩm lành mạnh                  | To give government money to make healthy foods cheaper and more accessible.                       | Governments could **subsidize healthy food options** like fruits and vegetables.                   |
| **To strictly regulate the food and beverage industry** | Quản lý chặt chẽ ngành công nghiệp thực phẩm và đồ uống       | To control the industry with rigorous rules, especially regarding marketing and labeling.         | There is a need **to strictly regulate the food and beverage industry**'s advertising to children. |
| **To make vaccinations mandatory**                      | Bắt buộc tiêm chủng                                           | To make it a legal requirement for people to be vaccinated.                                       | Some countries have **made vaccinations mandatory** for all school-aged children.                  |
| **To invest in public recreational facilities**         | Đầu tư vào các cơ sở vật chất giải trí công cộng              | To put money into building and maintaining parks, sports centers, and playgrounds for public use. | **Investing in public recreational facilities** encourages citizens to be more physically active.  |
| **To implement health education programs in schools**   | Thực hiện các chương trình giáo dục sức khỏe trong trường học | To introduce and run programs in schools that teach children about health and well-being.         | Schools should **implement health education programs** to teach children about nutrition.          |
| **To ban the advertising of harmful products**          | Cấm quảng cáo các sản phẩm có hại                             | To officially forbid the promotion of products like tobacco or junk food.                         | Many support the idea **to ban the advertising of** fast food during children's TV shows.          |
| **To place stringent restrictions on...**               | Đặt ra các hạn chế nghiêm ngặt đối với...                     | To put very strict and precise limits on something.                                               | The government should **place stringent restrictions on** the sale of tobacco products.            |

### 🏥 **Cơ sở hạ tầng & Mục tiêu (Infrastructure & Aims)**

| Collocation                                               | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                               | Short English Meaning                                                                                            | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                        |
| :-------------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **A robust public healthcare system**                     | Một hệ thống y tế công cộng vững mạnh          | A strong, well-funded, and effective healthcare system provided by the government.                               | A **robust public healthcare system** is the cornerstone of a healthy and productive society.           |
| **Universal healthcare / Universal access to healthcare** | Y tế toàn dân / Tiếp cận y tế toàn dân         | A system where all citizens (and often residents) have access to healthcare, regardless of their ability to pay. | The policy aims to achieve **universal access to healthcare**.                                          |
| **To improve access to medical services**                 | Cải thiện khả năng tiếp cận các dịch vụ y tế   | To make it easier for people, especially in remote or poor areas, to get medical care.                           | More clinics should be built in rural areas **to improve access to medical services**.                  |
| **Healthcare provision**                                  | Việc cung cấp dịch vụ chăm sóc sức khỏe        | The supply and availability of healthcare services.                                                              | The quality of **healthcare provision** varies greatly between urban and rural areas.                   |
| **To improve public health outcomes**                     | Cải thiện các kết quả sức khỏe cộng đồng       | To achieve better overall health results for the population, such as lower disease rates.                        | The ultimate goal of these policies is **to improve public health outcomes**.                           |
| **To reduce the burden on the healthcare system**         | Giảm bớt gánh nặng cho hệ thống y tế           | To decrease the pressure and costs placed on a country's healthcare services.                                    | By preventing diseases, we can **reduce the long-term burden on the healthcare system**.                |
| **To combat / tackle the obesity epidemic**               | Chống lại / giải quyết đại dịch béo phì        | To take strong measures to fight against the widespread problem of obesity.                                      | A multi-faceted approach is needed **to combat the obesity epidemic**.                                  |
| **To increase life expectancy**                           | Tăng tuổi thọ trung bình                       | To raise the average number of years a person is expected to live.                                               | Public health initiatives over the last century have significantly **increased life expectancy**.       |
| **To foster a healthier population**                      | Nuôi dưỡng một dân số khỏe mạnh hơn            | To create the conditions for a population to be in better health.                                                | Investing in public health leads to a more productive workforce and **fosters a healthier population**. |
| **To lower long-term healthcare costs**                   | Giảm chi phí y tế dài hạn                      | To reduce the amount of money a country spends on healthcare over many years.                                    | Preventive measures are a cost-effective way **to lower long-term healthcare costs**.                   |
| **To encourage healthy behaviors**                        | Khuyến khích các hành vi lành mạnh             | To motivate and make it easier for people to adopt healthy habits.                                               | The campaign's purpose is **to encourage healthy behaviors** like regular exercise.                     |
| **To prevent the spread of infectious diseases**          | Ngăn chặn sự lây lan của các bệnh truyền nhiễm | To stop contagious illnesses from moving from person to person.                                                  | Vaccination is the most effective way **to prevent the spread of infectious diseases**.                 |
