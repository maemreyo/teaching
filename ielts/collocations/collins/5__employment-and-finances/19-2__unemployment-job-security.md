### 📉 **Tình trạng Thất nghiệp & Nguyên nhân (Unemployment & Its Causes)**

| Collocation                              | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                            | Short English Meaning                                                                                             | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                      |
| :--------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------ | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **The unemployment rate**                | Tỷ lệ thất nghiệp                           | The percentage of the labor force that is jobless and actively seeking employment.                                | The government's main economic goal is to reduce the **unemployment rate**.                           |
| **An economic downturn / recession**     | Một cuộc suy thoái kinh tế                  | A period of reduced economic activity, often leading to job losses.                                               | During an **economic downturn**, companies are often forced to lay off staff.                         |
| **To be made redundant / laid off**      | Bị cho thôi việc, sa thải (do thừa nhân sự) | To lose your job because your employer no longer needs you, often for economic reasons.                           | Hundreds of workers **were made redundant** when the company downsized.                               |
| **Corporate restructuring / downsizing** | Tái cấu trúc / thu hẹp quy mô doanh nghiệp  | The process of reorganizing a company to make it more efficient, often involving layoffs.                         | The company announced a major **corporate restructuring** that will result in 200 job losses.         |
| **Structural unemployment**              | Thất nghiệp cơ cấu                          | Unemployment resulting from a mismatch between the skills workers have and the skills employers need.             | The shift from manufacturing to a service economy has caused significant **structural unemployment**. |
| **Cyclical unemployment**                | Thất nghiệp chu kỳ                          | Unemployment that rises during economic downturns and falls when the economy improves.                            | **Cyclical unemployment** is a direct result of the natural business cycle.                           |
| **Long-term unemployment**               | Thất nghiệp dài hạn                         | A situation where individuals have been out of work for an extended period, typically over a year.                | **Long-term unemployment** can lead to skills erosion and decreased employability.                    |
| **Youth unemployment**                   | Thất nghiệp ở người trẻ                     | The situation of young people (typically 15-24) not being in education or employment.                             | **Youth unemployment** is a pressing social issue in many developed countries.                        |
| **Technological disruption**             | Sự gián đoạn công nghệ                      | The change that occurs when new technologies and business models affect the value of existing goods and services. | **Technological disruption** from AI and automation is expected to displace many workers.             |
| **Deindustrialization**                  | Phi công nghiệp hóa                         | The decline of traditional manufacturing industries in a region or country.                                       | **Deindustrialization** in the region led to mass unemployment in the 1980s.                          |

### 🛡️ **Sự đảm bảo Việc làm (Job Security)**

| Collocation                      | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                                | Short English Meaning                                                                                                             | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                        |
| :------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Job security**                 | Sự đảm bảo, an toàn trong công việc             | The assurance that an employee has a stable and continuing job.                                                                   | People in government jobs often enjoy a high degree of **job security**.                                |
| **A permanent / stable job**     | Một công việc lâu dài / ổn định                 | A job with a long-term contract and consistent employment.                                                                        | Many young people today value flexibility more than a **stable job**.                                   |
| **Contract-based employment**    | Việc làm dựa trên hợp đồng                      | Work that is based on a fixed-term contract rather than being permanent.                                                          | **Contract-based employment** is common in fields like IT and creative industries.                      |
| **The gig economy**              | Nền kinh tế tạm thời / hợp đồng                 | A labor market characterized by short-term contracts or freelance work.                                                           | The rise of **the gig economy** has transformed the nature of work for millions.                        |
| **Precarious employment**        | Tình trạng việc làm bấp bênh                    | Work that is poorly paid, insecure, and unprotected.                                                                              | Many workers in the gig economy face **precarious employment** with no benefits.                        |
| **Zero-hours contracts**         | Hợp đồng không giờ                              | A type of contract where an employer is not obliged to provide any minimum working hours.                                         | **Zero-hours contracts** are controversial due to the lack of job security they offer.                  |
| **Underemployment**              | Tình trạng thiếu việc làm                       | A situation in which a worker is employed but not in the desired capacity, e.g., working part-time when they want full-time work. | **Underemployment** is a hidden problem, as these individuals are not officially counted as unemployed. |
| **Atypical work arrangements**   | Các hình thức sắp xếp công việc không điển hình | Employment relationships that differ from standard, open-ended, full-time jobs.                                                   | Freelancing and temporary contracts are examples of **atypical work arrangements**.                     |
| **A lack of statutory benefits** | Sự thiếu hụt các phúc lợi theo luật định        | Not receiving legally required benefits like sick pay, holiday pay, or pensions.                                                  | A major downside of freelance work is often **a lack of statutory benefits**.                           |
| **Employability**                | Khả năng được tuyển dụng                        | The skills, knowledge, and attributes that make a person able to get and keep a job.                                              | Continuous learning is key to maintaining high **employability** in a changing job market.              |

### ផលប៉ះពាល់ **Tác động & Hậu quả (Impacts & Consequences)**

| Collocation                                   | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                         | Short English Meaning                                                                   | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                    |
| :-------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Financial hardship**                        | Khó khăn về tài chính                    | A state of serious difficulty with money.                                               | The families of the unemployed often suffer from severe **financial hardship**.                     |
| **To fall below the poverty line**            | Rơi xuống dưới mức nghèo khổ             | To have an income level that is too low to support a basic standard of living.          | Without government support, many unemployed individuals could **fall below the poverty line**.      |
| **To rely on state benefits**                 | Sống dựa vào trợ cấp nhà nước            | To depend on money provided by the government when you are unemployed or in need.       | He had **to rely on state benefits** while searching for a new job.                                 |
| **A detrimental impact on mental health**     | Tác động có hại đến sức khỏe tinh thần   | A damaging effect on a person's psychological well-being.                               | Unemployment can have a **detrimental impact on mental health**, leading to anxiety and depression. |
| **A loss of self-esteem**                     | Sự mất đi lòng tự trọng                  | A decline in one's confidence in their own worth or abilities.                          | Being unemployed for a long time can cause a significant **loss of self-esteem**.                   |
| **Skills erosion / deskilling**               | Sự xói mòn / mai một kỹ năng             | The process by which a person's skills become outdated or are lost through lack of use. | Long-term unemployment often leads to **skills erosion**, making it harder to find a job.           |
| **Social exclusion / marginalization**        | Sự bị loại trừ / bên lề xã hội           | The process of being shut out from the social, economic, and political life of society. | Unemployment can be a major cause of **social exclusion**.                                          |
| **To lead to social unrest**                  | Dẫn đến bất ổn xã hội                    | To cause public dissatisfaction, protests, and potential disorder in society.           | High levels of youth unemployment can sometimes **lead to social unrest**.                          |
| **A strain on public finances**               | Gây áp lực lên tài chính công            | To be a significant burden on the government's budget.                                  | Mass unemployment puts a **strain on public finances** due to increased welfare payments.           |
| **An increased burden on the welfare system** | Gánh nặng gia tăng lên hệ thống phúc lợi | A greater demand on government services that provide for citizens' well-being.          | A recession causes an **increased burden on the welfare system**.                                   |

### ✅ **Giải pháp & Biện pháp của Chính phủ (Solutions & Government Measures)**

| Collocation                               | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                              | Short English Meaning                                                                        | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                          |
| :---------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **To tackle / combat unemployment**       | Giải quyết / chống lại tình trạng thất nghiệp | To take decisive action to deal with the problem of unemployment.                            | The government has launched a new initiative **to tackle youth unemployment**.                            |
| **To stimulate economic growth**          | Kích thích tăng trưởng kinh tế                | To encourage the economy to grow through government policies.                                | The central bank lowered interest rates **to stimulate economic growth**.                                 |
| **Fiscal stimulus**                       | Gói kích thích tài khóa                       | Government spending or tax cuts designed to boost the economy.                               | The government announced a **fiscal stimulus** package to create jobs.                                    |
| **Public works projects**                 | Các dự án công trình công cộng                | Large-scale projects financed by the government to create jobs and improve infrastructure.   | Investing in **public works projects** is a traditional way to combat unemployment during a recession.    |
| **To invest in education and training**   | Đầu tư vào giáo dục và đào tạo                | To spend money on improving the education system and job skills training.                    | A long-term solution is **to invest in education and training** to prepare the workforce for future jobs. |
| **Upskilling and reskilling initiatives** | Các sáng kiến nâng cao và đào tạo lại kỹ năng | Programs designed to teach workers new, advanced skills or to train them for different jobs. | **Upskilling and reskilling initiatives** are crucial for workers displaced by automation.                |
| **Employment subsidies**                  | Các khoản trợ cấp việc làm                    | Financial assistance given to businesses to encourage them to hire more employees.           | The scheme provides **employment subsidies** to companies that hire long-term unemployed individuals.     |
| **To support entrepreneurship**           | Hỗ trợ tinh thần khởi nghiệp                  | To provide support and incentives for people to start their own businesses.                  | The government offers loans and mentorship **to support entrepreneurship**.                               |
| **To strengthen the social safety net**   | Củng cố mạng lưới an sinh xã hội              | To improve the system of government support that protects vulnerable citizens.               | It is important **to strengthen the social safety net** to protect the unemployed from poverty.           |
| **Labor market reforms**                  | Các cải cách thị trường lao động              | Changes to laws and regulations governing the labor market to improve efficiency.            | **Labor market reforms** can make it easier for businesses to hire new staff.                             |
