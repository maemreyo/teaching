### 🏢 **Môi trường làm việc Hiện đại (The Modern Workplace)**

| Collocation                         | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                | Short English Meaning                                                                                | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                       |
| :---------------------------------- | :------------------------------ | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Remote working / Telecommuting**  | Làm việc từ xa                  | The practice of working from home or another remote location, making use of the internet.            | **Remote working** has become a mainstream practice, offering unprecedented flexibility.               |
| **Flexible working arrangements**   | Sắp xếp công việc linh hoạt     | A system where employees have some choice over their working hours and location.                     | Offering **flexible working arrangements** can help attract and retain top talent.                     |
| **A collaborative workspace**       | Không gian làm việc hợp tác     | A workspace designed to encourage teamwork and open communication.                                   | Co-working spaces provide a **collaborative workspace** for freelancers and startups.                  |
| **A corporate culture**             | Văn hóa doanh nghiệp            | The shared values, beliefs, and practices that shape the behavior of a company's employees.          | A positive **corporate culture** that fosters trust and innovation is essential for long-term success. |
| **A hierarchical structure**        | Cấu trúc phân cấp               | A traditional organizational structure with clear levels of authority.                               | Many startups are moving away from a rigid **hierarchical structure** to a flatter model.              |
| **A flat organizational structure** | Cấu trúc tổ chức phẳng          | An organizational structure with few or no levels of middle management.                              | A **flat organizational structure** can empower employees and speed up decision-making.                |
| **The gig economy**                 | Nền kinh tế tạm thời / hợp đồng | A labor market characterized by short-term contracts or freelance work as opposed to permanent jobs. | The rise of **the gig economy** has created both opportunities and challenges for workers.             |
| **A digital nomad**                 | Người du mục kỹ thuật số        | A person who uses technology to work remotely while traveling to different locations.                | As a **digital nomad**, she can run her online business from anywhere in the world.                    |
| **Automation of labor**             | Sự tự động hóa lao động         | The use of technology and machines to do jobs previously done by people.                             | The **automation of labor** is transforming industries from manufacturing to customer service.         |
| **An open-plan office**             | Văn phòng không gian mở         | An office with few or no dividing walls between employees.                                           | **Open-plan offices** are designed to encourage collaboration, but they can also be distracting.       |

### 😊 **Sự hài lòng & Động lực trong Công việc (Job Satisfaction & Motivation)**

| Collocation                                | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                        | Short English Meaning                                                                                       | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                |
| :----------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Job satisfaction**                       | Sự hài lòng trong công việc             | The level of contentment an employee feels with their job.                                                  | High **job satisfaction** is linked to higher productivity and lower staff turnover.            |
| **A sense of fulfillment**                 | Cảm giác mãn nguyện                     | A deep feeling of happiness and satisfaction from doing something meaningful.                               | Helping others gives nurses a profound **sense of fulfillment**.                                |
| **To feel valued and appreciated**         | Cảm thấy được trân trọng và công nhận   | To feel that your contributions are respected and acknowledged by the organization.                         | Employees are more motivated when they **feel valued and appreciated**.                         |
| **Career progression / trajectory**        | Lộ trình / Quỹ đạo thăng tiến sự nghiệp | The path of advancement an individual takes through their career within an organization.                    | The company offers a clear **career trajectory** for ambitious graduates.                       |
| **Professional development opportunities** | Các cơ hội phát triển chuyên môn        | Opportunities to learn new skills and improve one's abilities for career advancement.                       | Access to **professional development opportunities** is a key motivator for many employees.     |
| **A competitive salary**                   | Một mức lương cạnh tranh                | A salary that is as good as, or better than, what other companies in the industry offer.                    | To attract top talent, businesses need to offer a **competitive salary**.                       |
| **Fringe benefits / Perks**                | Các phúc lợi phụ / Đặc quyền            | Extra benefits given to an employee in addition to their salary, such as a company car or health insurance. | **Fringe benefits** like comprehensive health insurance are highly valued by job seekers.       |
| **Intrinsic motivation**                   | Động lực nội tại                        | Motivation that comes from within, such as personal satisfaction or a sense of purpose.                     | **Intrinsic motivation** is often more powerful and sustainable than external rewards.          |
| **Extrinsic motivation**                   | Động lực ngoại lai                      | Motivation that comes from external factors, such as money, bonuses, or praise.                             | While a bonus provides **extrinsic motivation**, it may not lead to long-term job satisfaction. |
| **Employee morale**                        | Tinh thần của nhân viên                 | The overall confidence, satisfaction, and outlook of employees at a workplace.                              | Good management and a positive culture are crucial for maintaining high **employee morale**.    |

### ⚖️ **Cân bằng & Sức khỏe tại nơi làm việc (Work-Life Balance & Well-being)**

| Collocation                       | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                       | Short English Meaning                                                                             | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                   |
| :-------------------------------- | :------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Work-life balance**             | Cân bằng giữa công việc và cuộc sống   | The optimal balance between an individual's work responsibilities and their personal life.        | Many people are prioritizing **work-life balance** over higher salaries.                           |
| **Employee well-being**           | Sức khỏe và hạnh phúc của nhân viên    | The overall mental, physical, and emotional health of employees.                                  | Companies are increasingly investing in **employee well-being** programs to reduce stress.         |
| **To avoid burnout**              | Tránh tình trạng kiệt sức vì công việc | To prevent a state of chronic physical and emotional exhaustion caused by excessive stress.       | Setting clear boundaries between work and personal time is essential **to avoid burnout**.         |
| **A heavy workload**              | Khối lượng công việc lớn               | A large amount of work that an individual is expected to do.                                      | A consistently **heavy workload** is a primary cause of work-related stress.                       |
| **Work-related stress**           | Căng thẳng liên quan đến công việc     | Stress caused by pressures and demands at the workplace.                                          | **Work-related stress** is a significant public health issue with serious consequences.            |
| **A supportive work environment** | Một môi trường làm việc có tính hỗ trợ | A workplace where employees feel safe, respected, and supported by their colleagues and managers. | A **supportive work environment** is crucial for employee mental health.                           |
| **Mental health support**         | Hỗ trợ sức khỏe tinh thần              | Resources and services provided by an employer to help employees with their mental health.        | Offering confidential **mental health support** is a key part of a modern wellness program.        |
| **The right to disconnect**       | Quyền được ngắt kết nối                | The right of employees to not engage in work-related communications outside of working hours.     | Some countries have introduced legislation to protect **the right to disconnect**.                 |
| **Digital detox**                 | "Cai" kỹ thuật số                      | A period of time during which a person refrains from using electronic devices.                    | Taking a **digital detox** during vacation can help prevent burnout.                               |
| **To work long hours**            | Làm việc nhiều giờ                     | To work for more hours than is standard or healthy on a regular basis.                            | **Working long hours** consistently can be detrimental to one's health and personal relationships. |

### 📉 **Thách thức & Sự không hài lòng (Challenges & Dissatisfaction)**

| Collocation                    | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                         | Short English Meaning                                                                                 | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                |
| :----------------------------- | :--------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A dead-end job**             | Một công việc không có tương lai         | A job with no opportunities for career advancement or professional development.                       | He felt he was stuck in a **dead-end job** and decided it was time for a change.                |
| **A mundane / repetitive job** | Một công việc nhàm chán / lặp đi lặp lại | A job that is boring and involves doing the same tasks repeatedly.                                    | Automation is particularly effective at replacing **mundane and repetitive jobs**.              |
| **Job insecurity**             | Sự bất an, không đảm bảo trong công việc | The feeling of uncertainty about the future of one's job.                                             | The gig economy has led to a rise in **job insecurity** for many freelance workers.             |
| **A toxic work environment**   | Một môi trường làm việc độc hại          | A workplace that is characterized by conflict, negativity, and bullying, harming employee well-being. | A **toxic work environment** is a leading cause of high staff turnover.                         |
| **Micromanagement**            | Quản lý vi mô                            | A management style where a manager closely observes or controls the work of their employees.          | **Micromanagement** can stifle creativity and lead to a lack of autonomy.                       |
| **A lack of autonomy**         | Sự thiếu tự chủ                          | Not having the freedom and trust to manage one's own work and make decisions.                         | A **lack of autonomy** is a common reason for low job satisfaction among skilled professionals. |
| **The glass ceiling**          | Rào cản vô hình ("trần kính")            | An unacknowledged barrier to advancement in a profession, especially affecting women and minorities.  | Many organizations are actively working to break **the glass ceiling**.                         |
| **High staff turnover**        | Tỷ lệ nhân viên nghỉ việc cao            | A high rate at which employees leave a company and are replaced.                                      | A **high staff turnover** rate can be costly and indicates underlying problems in the company.  |
| **To be stuck in a rut**       | Bị mắc kẹt trong một lối mòn             | To be in a boring situation or job that is difficult to change.                                       | After ten years in the same role, she felt she was **stuck in a rut**.                          |
| **The skills gap**             | Khoảng trống về kỹ năng                  | The disparity between the skills employers need and the skills the workforce possesses.               | Rapid technological change is widening **the skills gap** in many industries.                   |
