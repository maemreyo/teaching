### 💰 **Quản lý Tài chính & Tiết kiệm (Financial Management & Saving)**

| Collocation                         | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                       | Short English Meaning                                                                               | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                                    |
| :---------------------------------- | :------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Personal financial management**   | Quản lý tài chính cá nhân              | The practice of budgeting, saving, and investing one's own money.                                   | **Personal financial management** is a critical life skill that should be taught in schools.                        |
| **Financial planning**              | Lập kế hoạch tài chính                 | The process of setting financial goals and creating a plan to achieve them.                         | Effective **financial planning** involves creating a budget and tracking your expenses.                             |
| **To set clear financial goals**    | Đặt ra các mục tiêu tài chính rõ ràng  | To define specific objectives for your money, such as buying a house or retiring early.             | **Setting clear financial goals** can provide the motivation needed to save consistently.                           |
| **To live within one's means**      | Sống trong giới hạn tài chính của mình | To spend no more money than you earn.                                                               | The cornerstone of financial stability is the ability **to live within your means**.                                |
| **Practicing frugality**            | Thực hành lối sống tiết kiệm           | The quality of being economical with money or food; thriftiness.                                    | **Practicing frugality** doesn't mean being cheap; it means being resourceful.                                      |
| **To build an emergency fund**      | Xây dựng một quỹ khẩn cấp              | To save money specifically for unexpected financial emergencies.                                    | Financial advisors recommend having enough money in your **emergency fund** to cover 3-6 months of living expenses. |
| **To invest for the long term**     | Đầu tư dài hạn                         | To put money into assets like stocks or real estate with the expectation of growth over many years. | Young people have the advantage of time when it comes to **investing for the long term**.                           |
| **To have a diversified portfolio** | Có một danh mục đầu tư đa dạng         | To have a variety of investments to reduce risk.                                                    | A financial advisor can help you build a **diversified portfolio** that matches your risk tolerance.                |
| **To plan for retirement**          | Lên kế hoạch cho việc nghỉ hưu         | To save and invest money to provide an income for yourself after you stop working.                  | It is never too early **to start planning for retirement**.                                                         |
| **Financial discipline**            | Kỷ luật tài chính                      | The ability to control your spending and stick to a financial plan.                                 | Building wealth requires patience and strong **financial discipline**.                                              |

### 💳 **Chi tiêu & Nợ nần (Spending & Debt)**

| Collocation                    | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                  | Short English Meaning                                                                 | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                        |
| :----------------------------- | :-------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **To make ends meet**          | Kiếm đủ sống (trang trải chi phí) | To have just enough money to pay for your basic needs.                                | With rising inflation, many families are struggling **to make ends meet**.              |
| **To live beyond one's means** | Sống vượt quá khả năng tài chính  | To spend more money than you earn, often by using credit.                             | **Living beyond one's means** is a primary cause of personal debt.                      |
| **To accumulate debt**         | Tích lũy nợ nần                   | To build up an amount of money that you owe over time.                                | It is easy **to accumulate debt** if you consistently overspend.                        |
| **To be heavily in debt**      | Nợ nần chồng chất                 | To owe a very large amount of money.                                                  | After years of financial mismanagement, he found himself **heavily in debt**.           |
| **High-interest debt**         | Nợ lãi suất cao                   | Debt on which a high rate of interest is charged, such as credit card debt.           | It's financially wise to prioritize paying off **high-interest debt** first.            |
| **Student loan debt**          | Nợ vay sinh viên                  | Money owed to pay for the costs of one's education.                                   | Many graduates enter the workforce **burdened with** significant **student loan debt**. |
| **To be burdened with debt**   | Bị gánh nặng nợ nần               | To have a large amount of debt that causes significant stress and financial problems. | An entire generation is **burdened with debt** from housing and education costs.        |
| **To pay off a debt**          | Trả hết một khoản nợ              | To finish paying back all the money that you owe.                                     | My main financial goal for this year is **to pay off my** credit card **debt**.         |
| **Impulse buying**             | Mua sắm tùy hứng                  | The act of buying things without prior planning.                                      | Supermarkets place items near the checkout to encourage **impulse buying**.             |
| **To cut back on spending**    | Cắt giảm chi tiêu                 | To reduce the amount of money you spend.                                              | We need **to cut back on spending** on non-essential items to save for a deposit.       |

### 🛍️ **Chủ nghĩa Tiêu dùng & Chủ nghĩa Vật chất (Consumerism & Materialism)**

| Collocation                             | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                                 | Short English Meaning                                                                               | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                    |
| :-------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A consumer society**                  | Một xã hội tiêu dùng                             | A society where the buying and selling of goods is the most important social and economic activity. | We live in a **consumer society** that constantly encourages us to buy more.                        |
| **Consumerism**                         | Chủ nghĩa tiêu dùng                              | The belief that it is good for people to spend a lot of money on goods and services.                | Critics argue that **consumerism** leads to environmental degradation and social inequality.        |
| **Materialism / To be materialistic**   | Chủ nghĩa vật chất / Là người coi trọng vật chất | The belief that having money and possessions is the most important thing in life.                   | A person who is **materialistic** often judges their own and others' success by their possessions.  |
| **Material possessions**                | Của cải, tài sản vật chất                        | The things that a person owns.                                                                      | Some philosophies suggest that true happiness cannot be found in **material possessions**.          |
| **A throw-away culture**                | Văn hóa "dùng một lần rồi vứt"                   | A society that is characterized by the excessive consumption and disposal of single-use items.      | Our **throw-away culture** is a major contributor to the global plastic pollution crisis.           |
| **Planned obsolescence**                | Lỗi thời có kế hoạch                             | The practice of designing products to break or become outdated after a short period.                | **Planned obsolescence** in electronics forces consumers to upgrade their devices frequently.       |
| **Conspicuous consumption**             | Tiêu dùng phô trương                             | Spending money on luxury goods to publicly display economic power and social status.                | The purchase of supercars and designer watches are classic examples of **conspicuous consumption**. |
| **A status symbol**                     | Một biểu tượng của địa vị                        | A possession that is intended to indicate its owner's high social or economic status.               | In some cultures, a luxury car is a powerful **status symbol**.                                     |
| **To keep up with the Joneses**         | Đua đòi, cố gắng bằng bạn bằng bè                | The social pressure to buy the same expensive things as one's neighbors or peers.                   | The pressure **to "keep up with the Joneses"** can lead people into debt.                           |
| **Advertising and marketing campaigns** | Các chiến dịch quảng cáo và tiếp thị             | Organized series of advertisements and promotions designed to influence consumer behavior.          | Sophisticated **advertising campaigns** can create artificial needs and desires.                    |

### 🎓 **Kiến thức & Các vấn đề Tài chính (Financial Literacy & Issues)**

| Collocation                                  | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                             | Short English Meaning                                                                                   | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                         |
| :------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Financial literacy**                       | Kiến thức tài chính                          | The knowledge and skills to make effective and informed financial decisions.                            | A lack of **financial literacy** is a widespread problem that affects people of all income levels.       |
| **To make informed financial decisions**     | Đưa ra các quyết định tài chính có hiểu biết | To make choices about money based on accurate information and a clear understanding of the options.     | The goal of financial education is to empower people **to make informed financial decisions**.           |
| **Financial insecurity**                     | Sự bất an về tài chính                       | A state of constant worry about one's financial situation.                                              | **Financial insecurity** is a major source of stress and anxiety in modern society.                      |
| **Income inequality**                        | Bất bình đẳng thu nhập                       | The unequal distribution of income between the richest and poorest members of society.                  | **Income inequality** is a growing concern in many developed nations.                                    |
| **The poverty cycle / The cycle of poverty** | Vòng đói nghèo                               | A situation where poor families become trapped in poverty for generations.                              | Breaking **the cycle of poverty** requires investment in education and social services.                  |
| **To live paycheck to paycheck**             | Sống kiểu "làm đồng nào xào đồng nấy"        | To have just enough income to cover living expenses with no money left over for savings.                | Many low-wage workers have **to live paycheck to paycheck**, making them vulnerable to financial shocks. |
| **Disposable income**                        | Thu nhập khả dụng                            | The amount of money that a household has available for spending and saving after income taxes.          | The amount of **disposable income** a family has significantly affects their quality of life.            |
| **A primary source of income**               | Nguồn thu nhập chính                         | The main way in which a person or household earns money.                                                | For most people, their job is their **primary source of income**.                                        |
| **Building wealth**                          | Xây dựng sự giàu có                          | The process of accumulating assets and investments over time.                                           | **Building wealth** is a long-term process that involves saving, investing, and financial discipline.    |
| **To achieve financial independence**        | Đạt được sự độc lập về tài chính             | To have enough income to pay for your living expenses for the rest of your life without having to work. | The ultimate goal for many is **to achieve financial independence**.                                     |
