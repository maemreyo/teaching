### 📝 **Tìm kiếm & Nộp đơn (The Job Search & Application)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **To seek employment / to look for a job** | Tìm kiếm việc làm | To be actively searching for a job. | Many graduates are **seeking employment** in a competitive job market. |
| **To browse job listings / vacancies** | Xem các tin đăng tuyển dụng / vị trí trống | To look through online or printed advertisements for jobs. | I spend an hour each morning **browsing job listings** on LinkedIn. |
| **A job seeker / applicant / candidate** | Người tìm việc / ứng viên | A person who is looking for a job or has applied for one. | The workshop provides valuable advice for first-time **job seekers**. |
| **To tailor one's resume/CV to the job description** | Điều chỉnh sơ yếu lý lịch cho phù hợp với mô tả công việc | To customize your resume to highlight the skills and experience relevant to a specific job. | It is crucial **to tailor your resume to each job description**. |
| **To write a compelling cover letter** | Viết một lá thư xin việc hấp dẫn | To write an introductory letter that captures the employer's interest. | A **compelling cover letter** can make your application stand out. |
| **To submit an application** | Nộp đơn ứng tuyển | To formally send your application for a job. | The deadline **to submit an application** is this Friday. |
| **To meet the minimum requirements** | Đáp ứng các yêu cầu tối thiểu | To have the basic qualifications and experience needed for a job. | Candidates who do not **meet the minimum requirements** will not be shortlisted. |
| **To be shortlisted for an interview** | Được chọn vào vòng phỏng vấn | To be selected from a larger group of applicants for an interview. | She was thrilled **to be shortlisted for an interview** with the prestigious law firm. |
| **The recruitment process** | Quy trình tuyển dụng | The entire process of finding, screening, and hiring a new employee. | **The recruitment process** can often take several weeks from start to finish. |
| **A headhunter / recruitment agency** | Chuyên viên săn đầu người / công ty tuyển dụng | A person or company whose job is to find suitable people for senior jobs in other companies. | The company used a **recruitment agency** to find a new CEO. |

### 🎤 **Phỏng vấn & Tuyển dụng (The Interview & Hiring Process)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **To attend a job interview** | Tham dự một buổi phỏng vấn xin việc | To go to a formal meeting to be asked questions for a potential job. | It's normal to feel nervous before you **attend a job interview**. |
| **To make a good first impression** | Tạo ấn tượng ban đầu tốt | To make people have a positive opinion of you when they first meet you. | Dressing professionally is important **to make a good first impression**. |
| **To demonstrate one's skills and abilities** | Thể hiện các kỹ năng và khả năng của mình | To show that you have the required skills and capabilities for the job. | The interview is your opportunity **to demonstrate your skills and abilities**. |
| **To ask insightful questions** | Đặt những câu hỏi sâu sắc | To ask questions that show you have a deep understanding and are genuinely interested. | **Asking insightful questions** at the end of an interview shows your engagement. |
| **A panel interview** | Một cuộc phỏng vấn hội đồng | An interview where a candidate is questioned by a group of people. | I have a **panel interview** tomorrow with three department heads. |
| **A technical assessment** | Một bài kiểm tra kỹ thuật | A test designed to evaluate a candidate's technical skills for a specific role. | The next stage of the process is a two-hour **technical assessment**. |
| **To check a candidate's references** | Kiểm tra người tham chiếu của ứng viên | To contact people who have previously worked with a candidate to verify their qualifications. | Employers almost always **check a candidate's references** before making an offer. |
| **To receive a job offer** | Nhận được một lời mời làm việc | To be formally offered a job by a company. | After a successful final interview, she was delighted **to receive a job offer**. |
| **To negotiate a salary** | Đàm phán lương | To discuss and agree on the amount of money you will be paid for a job. | Don't be afraid **to negotiate your salary** based on your experience and market rates. |
| **The onboarding process** | Quy trình hội nhập nhân viên mới | The process of integrating a new employee into an organization. | A smooth **onboarding process** helps new hires feel welcome and become productive quickly. |

### 📈 **Phát triển & Thăng tiến Sự nghiệp (Career Development & Progression)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Career development / advancement** | Phát triển / thăng tiến sự nghiệp | The process of making progress in your career through training and promotions. | The company invests heavily in employee **career development**. |
| **To climb the career ladder** | Leo lên nấc thang sự nghiệp | To get promoted to higher and higher positions within a company or field. | Ambitious employees are always looking for opportunities **to climb the career ladder**. |
| **To get a promotion / to be promoted** | Được thăng chức | To be moved to a more senior job. | She was **promoted** to Senior Manager after only two years with the company. |
| **To acquire new skills** | Tiếp thu các kỹ năng mới | To learn new abilities that can help you in your work. | The training program helped him **to acquire new skills** in project management. |
| **Professional development** | Phát triển chuyên môn | The process of learning and improving skills related to your job. | Attending workshops and conferences is a key part of **professional development**. |
| **To have a promising career** | Có một sự nghiệp đầy hứa hẹn | To have good future opportunities in your profession. | As a talented young lawyer, she **has a promising career** ahead of her. |
| **To gain valuable experience** | Tích lũy kinh nghiệm quý báu | To get knowledge and skills from doing something that will be useful in the future. | An internship is a great way **to gain valuable experience** in a competitive field. |
| **To take on more responsibility** | Đảm nhận thêm trách nhiệm | To accept more duties and have more control over decisions. | He is ready **to take on more responsibility** in his role. |
| **A career path / trajectory** | Một con đường / quỹ đạo sự nghiệp | The sequence of jobs that make up a person's career. | The company offers a clear **career path** for graduates, from junior associate to partner. |
| **To reach a career plateau** | Đạt đến điểm chững lại trong sự nghiệp | To get to a stage in your career where you are no longer progressing. | After a decade in the same position, he felt he had **reached a career plateau**. |
