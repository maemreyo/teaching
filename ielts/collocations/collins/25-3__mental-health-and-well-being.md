### 🧠 **Khái niệm & Trạng thái Tinh thần (Core Concepts & Mental State)**

| Collocation                                | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                          | Short English Meaning                                                               | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                  |
| :----------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Mental health and well-being**           | Sức khỏe và an sinh tinh thần             | A person's condition with regard to their psychological and emotional well-being.   | Prioritizing **mental health and well-being** is as important as maintaining physical health.     |
| **Emotional and psychological well-being** | An sinh về mặt cảm xúc và tâm lý          | The state of being happy, healthy, and able to cope with the stresses of life.      | A strong support network is crucial for one's **emotional and psychological well-being**.         |
| **A positive mental attitude / outlook**   | Một thái độ / cái nhìn tinh thần tích cực | A way of thinking that is optimistic and focuses on the good in a situation.        | Cultivating a **positive mental attitude** can help individuals build resilience.                 |
| **A sense of well-being**                  | Cảm giác hạnh phúc, an yên                | A general feeling of being happy, healthy, and comfortable.                         | Regular exercise is known to promote a general **sense of well-being**.                           |
| **One's state of mind**                    | Trạng thái tinh thần của một người        | A person's mood and the way they are thinking and feeling.                          | Happiness is often said to be a **state of mind** rather than a result of external circumstances. |
| **Mental health conditions / disorders**   | Các bệnh lý / rối loạn sức khỏe tâm thần  | Illnesses that affect a person's thinking, feeling, mood, or behavior.              | It is important to reduce the stigma associated with **mental health disorders**.                 |
| **Sound mental health**                    | Sức khỏe tinh thần tốt, vững vàng         | A state of good psychological and emotional health.                                 | **Sound mental health** is the foundation for a productive and fulfilling life.                   |
| **Emotional resilience**                   | Sức bật / khả năng phục hồi cảm xúc       | The ability to adapt to stressful situations and cope with life's adversities.      | Developing **emotional resilience** is key to navigating the ups and downs of life.               |
| **Cognitive health**                       | Sức khỏe nhận thức                        | The health of the brain and its functions, such as memory, thinking, and reasoning. | Lifelong learning can play a role in maintaining good **cognitive health** in old age.            |
| **A spectrum of mental health**            | Một phổ sức khỏe tinh thần                | The idea that mental health exists on a continuum, from good to poor.               | Everyone falls somewhere on the **spectrum of mental health**.                                    |

### 🌪️ **Nguyên nhân & Áp lực (Causes & Pressures)**

| Collocation                                               | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                                | Short English Meaning                                                           | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                |
| :-------------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **To suffer from chronic stress, anxiety, or depression** | Bị căng thẳng, lo âu hoặc trầm cảm mãn tính     | To experience these negative mental states over a long period.                  | A growing number of people are **suffering from chronic stress** due to work pressure.          |
| **To feel overwhelmed by pressure**                       | Cảm thấy quá tải bởi áp lực                     | To feel that the demands of a situation are too much to cope with.              | It is common for students **to feel overwhelmed by** academic **pressure**.                     |
| **The relentless pressures of modern life**               | Những áp lực không ngừng của cuộc sống hiện đại | The constant and unforgiving stresses of contemporary living.                   | **The relentless pressures of modern life** can take a toll on a person's mental health.        |
| **A high-pressure, competitive environment**              | Một môi trường cạnh tranh, áp lực cao           | A situation where there is intense competition and a strong demand to succeed.  | Working in a **high-pressure, competitive environment** can be a major source of stress.        |
| **Social isolation and loneliness**                       | Sự cô lập xã hội và sự cô đơn                   | The state of having minimal contact with others and the feeling of being alone. | **Social isolation** is a significant risk factor for depression, especially among the elderly. |
| **A poor work-life balance**                              | Sự cân bằng công việc-cuộc sống kém             | An unhealthy imbalance between professional and personal life.                  | A **poor work-life balance** is a leading cause of employee burnout and mental health issues.   |
| **To face constant and intense pressure**                 | Đối mặt với áp lực liên tục và dữ dội           | To be subjected to a continuous and powerful force of expectation.              | Athletes often **face constant and intense pressure** to perform at their best.                 |
| **An excessive workload**                                 | Khối lượng công việc quá mức                    | An amount of work that is more than what is reasonable or manageable.           | An **excessive workload** and long working hours are detrimental to mental well-being.          |
| **Financial instability**                                 | Sự bất ổn tài chính                             | A situation in which one's financial situation is not secure or predictable.    | **Financial instability** can be a significant source of chronic anxiety.                       |
| **Traumatic life events**                                 | Các sự kiện chấn thương trong cuộc sống         | Highly stressful events that are shocking and emotionally overwhelming.         | **Traumatic life events**, such as the loss of a loved one, can trigger mental health problems. |

### 📉 **Tác động & Hậu quả (Impacts & Consequences)**

| Collocation                                          | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                                        | Short English Meaning                                                               | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                |
| :--------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------ | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **To have a detrimental effect on one's well-being** | Có ảnh hưởng bất lợi/gây hại cho sức khỏe của một người | To have a harmful impact on a person's overall health and happiness.                | Chronic stress can **have a detrimental effect on one's** physical and mental **well-being**.   |
| **To impair cognitive function**                     | Làm suy giảm chức năng nhận thức                        | To weaken or damage the brain's ability to think, learn, and remember.              | Sleep deprivation is known **to impair cognitive function**, such as memory and concentration.  |
| **A significant loss of productivity**               | Sự sụt giảm năng suất đáng kể                           | A large reduction in the efficiency and amount of work that is done.                | Poor mental health in the workplace can lead to a **significant loss of productivity**.         |
| **A decline in physical health**                     | Sự suy giảm sức khỏe thể chất                           | A worsening of the body's physical condition.                                       | Mental health issues are often intrinsically linked to a **decline in physical health**.        |
| **To strain interpersonal relationships**            | Gây căng thẳng cho các mối quan hệ cá nhân              | To put pressure on and potentially damage one's relationships with other people.    | Depression can negatively **strain interpersonal relationships** with family and friends.       |
| **To lead to emotional and physical burnout**        | Dẫn đến tình trạng kiệt sức về cảm xúc và thể chất      | To cause a state of complete exhaustion resulting from prolonged stress.            | Constant pressure and long hours can eventually **lead to emotional and physical burnout**.     |
| **The pervasive stigma surrounding mental illness**  | Sự kỳ thị sâu rộng xung quanh bệnh tâm thần             | The widespread negative and unfair beliefs that society holds about mental illness. | **The pervasive stigma surrounding mental illness** can prevent people from seeking help.       |
| **A severely reduced quality of life**               | Chất lượng cuộc sống bị suy giảm nghiêm trọng           | A much lower standard of health, comfort, and happiness.                            | Untreated mental health conditions can lead to a **severely reduced quality of life**.          |
| **A vicious cycle**                                  | Một vòng luẩn quẩn                                      | A chain of negative events that reinforce each other.                               | Poor sleep can lead to stress, and stress can lead to poor sleep, creating a **vicious cycle**. |
| **To take a toll on...**                             | Gây tổn hại cho...                                      | To have a bad effect on something or someone over a long period.                    | The constant worry has started **to take a toll on** his health.                                |
