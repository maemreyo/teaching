| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A sense of community** | Tinh thần/ý thức cộng đồng | A feeling of belonging and connection to a group of people. | Living in a neighborhood with a strong **sense of community** can improve well-being. |
| **Community spirit** | Tinh thần cộng đồng | A feeling of being part of a community and wanting to help it. | Volunteering for local events is a great way to show **community spirit**. |

### 📉 **Sự Suy tàn & Thách thức (Decline & Challenges)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A loss of community spirit** | Sự mất đi tinh thần cộng đồng | A decline in the feeling of community. | The rise of individualism has led to a **loss of community spirit** in many places. |
| **Social isolation / alienation** | Sự cô lập / xa lánh xã hội | The state of being separate from other people. | A lack of community interaction can lead to feelings of **social isolation**, especially among the elderly. |
| **A breakdown of community** | Sự tan rã của cộng đồng | The failure or collapse of a community. | Critics argue that the modern lifestyle has caused a **breakdown of community**. |
| **A lack of interaction** | Sự thiếu tương tác | Not enough communication or contact between people. | The design of some modern apartment buildings leads to a **lack of interaction** between residents. |
| **To feel disconnected from** | Cảm thấy mất kết nối với | To feel that you are not connected to something or someone. | Many young people who move to big cities **feel disconnected from** their local community. |
| **The anonymity of city life** | Sự ẩn danh của cuộc sống thành thị | The state of being unknown in a city. | **The anonymity of city life** means that people can feel lonely even when surrounded by millions. |
| **To keep to oneself** | Sống khép kín, không giao du | To not talk to or interact with other people. | In many urban neighborhoods, it is common for people **to keep to themselves**. |

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Social cohesion** | Sự gắn kết xã hội | The way people in a society are united and work together. | A multicultural society must work hard to maintain **social cohesion**. |
| **To live in harmony** | Sống hòa thuận | To live together peacefully. | The goal is to create a society where people from all backgrounds can **live in harmony**. |
| **To promote social inclusion** | Thúc đẩy sự hòa nhập xã hội | To encourage the involvement of all people in society. | Public libraries play an important role in **promoting social inclusion**. |
