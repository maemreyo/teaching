### 🧬 **Khái niệm & Kỹ thuật Cốt lõi (Core Concepts & Techniques)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Genetic engineering** | Kỹ thuật di truyền | The deliberate modification of the characteristics of an organism by manipulating its genetic material. | **Genetic engineering** involves the direct manipulation of an organism's genes. |
| **To alter the genetic makeup of...** | Thay đổi cấu trúc di truyền của... | To change the genes of an organism. | Scientists have learned **to alter the genetic makeup of** plants to make them more resistant to disease. |
| **DNA sequencing** | Giải trình tự DNA | The process of determining the precise order of nucleotides within a DNA molecule. | **DNA sequencing** allows us to read the genetic code of an organism. |
| **Gene therapy** | Liệu pháp gen | The transplantation of normal genes into cells in place of missing or defective ones in order to correct genetic disorders. | **Gene therapy** has the potential to cure many inherited genetic disorders. |
| **To clone an animal / plant** | Nhân bản một động vật / thực vật | To create a genetically identical copy of an animal or plant. | The successful cloning of Dolly the sheep in 1996 was a major scientific breakthrough. |
| **A controversial field of science** | Một lĩnh vực khoa học gây tranh cãi | A field of science that causes a lot of public disagreement. | Genetic engineering remains a highly **controversial field of science**. |

### 🍎 **Thực phẩm Biến đổi Gen (Genetically Modified - GM Foods)**

Đây là một trong những ứng dụng gây tranh cãi nhất của kỹ thuật di truyền.

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Genetically modified (GM) crops / foods** | Cây trồng / thực phẩm biến đổi gen | Crops or foods whose genetic material has been altered. | **Genetically modified crops** are engineered to have desirable traits like higher yields. |
| **To be resistant to pests and diseases** | Kháng sâu bệnh và dịch bệnh | To be able to withstand pests and diseases. | Many GM plants have been made **resistant to common pests and diseases**. |
| **To increase crop yields** | Tăng năng suất cây trồng | To produce more crops from the same area of land. | One of the primary aims of developing GM crops is **to increase crop yields**. |
| **To improve the nutritional value** | Cải thiện giá trị dinh dưỡng | To make food more nutritious. | "Golden rice" is a GM crop designed **to improve the nutritional value** of rice. |
| **To have a longer shelf life** | Có thời hạn sử dụng lâu hơn | To be able to last longer before spoiling. | Some genetically modified fruits have been engineered **to have a longer shelf life**. |
| **To address food shortages** | Giải quyết tình trạng thiếu lương thực | To deal with the problem of not having enough food. | Proponents argue that GM technology can help **to address global food shortages**. |
| **To be safe for human consumption** | An toàn cho con người tiêu thụ | To be safe for people to eat. | There is an ongoing debate about whether GM foods are completely **safe for human consumption**. |
| **The long-term effects on human health** | Các tác động lâu dài lên sức khỏe con người | The effects on health over a long period of time. | Critics are concerned about **the potential long-term effects of GM foods on human health**. |

### ⚖️ **Quan ngại Đạo đức & Rủi ro (Ethical Concerns & Risks)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ethical implications / concerns** | Các hàm ý / quan ngại về đạo đức | Moral questions or worries. | The possibility of human cloning raises profound **ethical implications**. |
| **To play God** | Đóng vai trò của Chúa | To act as if one has the right to make decisions about life and death. | Opponents of genetic engineering argue that scientists are "playing God". |
| **Unforeseen / unintended consequences** | Hậu quả không lường trước được / không mong muốn | Effects that were not expected or planned. | Altering ecosystems could have disastrous and **unforeseen consequences**. |
| **To disrupt the natural ecosystem** | Phá vỡ hệ sinh thái tự nhiên | To disturb the balance of the natural world. | Releasing genetically modified organisms into the wild could **disrupt the natural ecosystem**. |
| **The long-term impact on the environment** | Tác động lâu dài lên môi trường | The effects on the environment over a long period. | The **long-term impact of GM crops on the environment** is still unknown. |
| **To create "designer babies"** | Tạo ra "những đứa trẻ được thiết kế" | To use genetic engineering to choose the characteristics of a baby. | A major fear is that genetic engineering could be used **to create "designer babies"** with specific traits. |
| **A slippery slope** | Một con dốc trơn trượt (một chuỗi sự kiện khó tránh khỏi, thường dẫn đến kết quả tiêu cực) | A course of action that is hard to stop and will lead to something bad. | Some people believe that allowing gene editing for diseases is a **slippery slope** to creating designer babies. |
| **To require strict regulation** | Yêu cầu sự quản lý chặt chẽ | To need to be controlled by strict rules. | This powerful technology must **require strict government regulation** to prevent misuse. |
| **A moral dilemma** | Một vấn đề nan giải về đạo đức | A situation where you have to make a difficult choice between two things that are both morally wrong. | The question of whether to edit human embryos presents a profound **moral dilemma**. |
