### 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 **Cấu trúc & Vai trò Gia đình (Family Structures & Roles)**

| Collocation                               | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                             | Short English Meaning                                                                        | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                           |
| :---------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **The nuclear family**                    | Gia đình hạt nhân (bố mẹ và con cái)         | A family unit consisting of two parents and their children.                                  | **The nuclear family** became the dominant family structure in many Western societies in the 20th century. |
| **The extended family**                   | Gia đình đa thế hệ (nhiều thế hệ sống chung) | A family that extends beyond the nuclear family to include grandparents and other relatives. | In many cultures, **the extended family** plays a central role in raising children.                        |
| **A single-parent household**             | Hộ gia đình đơn thân                         | A family in which one parent raises a child or children alone.                               | The number of **single-parent households** has been increasing in recent decades.                          |
| **The primary breadwinner**               | Người trụ cột kinh tế chính                  | The family member who earns the main income.                                                 | Traditionally, the father was considered **the primary breadwinner**.                                      |
| **The primary caregiver**                 | Người chăm sóc chính                         | The person who has the main responsibility for caring for a child or dependent adult.        | In many families, mothers still take on the role of **the primary caregiver**.                             |
| **To shoulder the responsibility for...** | Gánh vác trách nhiệm về...                   | To accept the duty or burden of something.                                                   | Parents must **shoulder the responsibility for** their children's upbringing.                              |
| **The division of household labor**       | Sự phân chia lao động trong gia đình         | The way in which tasks and chores are shared among family members.                           | Modern couples strive for a more equitable **division of household labor**.                                |
| **To provide for one's family**           | Chu cấp cho gia đình                         | To earn enough money to support one's family.                                                | He works two jobs **to provide for his family**.                                                           |
| **Family obligations / duties**           | Bổn phận / nghĩa vụ gia đình                 | The responsibilities and commitments one has towards one's family.                           | Many people feel strong **family obligations** to care for their elderly parents.                          |
| **Sibling rivalry**                       | Sự ganh đua giữa anh chị em                  | The natural competition and jealousy between brothers and sisters.                           | **Sibling rivalry** is a normal part of family dynamics.                                                   |

### 👶 **Nuôi dạy & Ảnh hưởng (Parenting & Upbringing)**

| Collocation                                      | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                             | Short English Meaning                                                                                   | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                            |
| :----------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Parenting styles**                             | Các phong cách nuôi dạy con                  | The different strategies and approaches that parents use to raise their children.                       | Research has identified various **parenting styles**, such as authoritative, authoritarian, and permissive. |
| **Child-rearing practices**                      | Các phương pháp nuôi dạy con                 | The methods and customs used to raise children within a particular culture or family.                   | **Child-rearing practices** vary significantly across different cultures.                                   |
| **To shape a child's character and development** | Định hình tính cách và sự phát triển của trẻ | To have a profound influence on how a child's personality and abilities grow.                           | A child's early experiences play a crucial role in **shaping their character and development**.             |
| **A formative influence**                        | Một sự ảnh hưởng có tính định hình           | An influence that strongly affects a person's character during their formative years.                   | A supportive family environment is a powerful **formative influence** on a child.                           |
| **To set clear boundaries**                      | Thiết lập giới hạn, quy tắc rõ ràng          | To establish and communicate clear rules, limits, and expectations for behavior.                        | It is important for parents **to set clear boundaries** for their children.                                 |
| **To instill values in a child**                 | Truyền thụ, thấm nhuần các giá trị cho trẻ   | To teach a child fundamental beliefs and principles so that they become a core part of their character. | One of the primary roles of a parent is **to instill values** like honesty and compassion **in a child**.   |
| **To pass on values to the next generation**     | Truyền lại các giá trị cho thế hệ tiếp theo  | To teach and ensure that core beliefs and principles are continued by younger family members.           | Grandparents often play a key role in **passing on cultural values to the next generation**.                |
| **Unconditional love and support**               | Tình yêu thương và sự hỗ trợ vô điều kiện    | Love and support that is given without any limitations or conditions.                                   | Children thrive when they receive **unconditional love and support** from their parents.                    |
| **Parental involvement in education**            | Sự tham gia của phụ huynh vào giáo dục       | The active participation of parents in their children's schooling and learning.                         | **Parental involvement in education** is strongly linked to higher academic achievement.                    |
| **Overprotective parenting**                     | Sự nuôi dạy con quá bao bọc                  | A parenting style where parents try to protect their children from any harm or disappointment.          | **Overprotective parenting** can hinder a child's ability to develop resilience and independence.           |

### 💔 **Xung đột & Giải quyết (Conflict & Resolution)**

| Collocation                             | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                             | Short English Meaning                                                                                    | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                            |
| :-------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Family disputes / conflicts**         | Tranh chấp / xung đột gia đình               | Serious disagreements or arguments within a family.                                                      | Financial issues are a common cause of **family disputes**.                                 |
| **A breakdown in communication**        | Sự đổ vỡ trong giao tiếp                     | A failure to exchange information or feelings effectively.                                               | A **breakdown in communication** is often the root cause of marital problems.               |
| **To resolve a conflict peacefully**    | Giải quyết một xung đột một cách hòa bình    | To find a solution to a disagreement without anger or violence.                                          | It is always better **to resolve conflicts peacefully** through open and honest discussion. |
| **To seek family counseling / therapy** | Tìm đến tư vấn / trị liệu gia đình           | To get professional help from a therapist to resolve problems within a family.                           | The couple decided **to seek family counseling** to improve their communication.            |
| **Irreconcilable differences**          | Những khác biệt không thể hòa giải           | Disagreements between a couple that are too profound to be resolved, often cited in divorce.             | They filed for divorce, citing **irreconcilable differences**.                              |
| **A bitter custody battle**             | Một cuộc chiến giành quyền nuôi con cay đắng | An angry and hostile legal fight between divorcing parents over who will have custody of their children. | A **bitter custody battle** can have a lasting negative impact on children.                 |
| **The generation gap**                  | Khoảng cách thế hệ                           | A difference in attitudes, values, and understanding between one generation and another.                 | **The generation gap** can be a source of conflict between teenagers and their parents.     |
| **To mediate a dispute**                | Hòa giải một cuộc tranh chấp                 | To intervene in a disagreement in order to help the opposing sides reach an agreement.                   | An older sibling sometimes has **to mediate a dispute** between younger ones.               |
| **Marital breakdown**                   | Sự đổ vỡ hôn nhân                            | The failure and end of a marriage.                                                                       | The high divorce rate is indicative of a wider trend of **marital breakdown**.              |
| **Domestic abuse**                      | Bạo hành gia đình                            | Physical, emotional, or sexual abuse that occurs within a home.                                          | **Domestic abuse** is a serious crime with devastating consequences for victims.            |

### societal **Gia đình & Xã hội (Family & Society)**

| Collocation                                           | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                                | Short English Meaning                                                                                        | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                   |
| :---------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **The breakdown of the traditional family structure** | Sự tan rã của cấu trúc gia đình truyền thống    | The decline of the nuclear family model as the dominant form.                                                | Sociologists have written extensively about **the breakdown of the traditional family structure**. |
| **The changing role of women in the family**          | Vai trò đang thay đổi của phụ nữ trong gia đình | The transformation of women's traditional roles as homemakers to include careers and financial independence. | This essay will discuss **the changing role of women in the family**.                              |
| **To maintain family traditions and customs**         | Duy trì truyền thống và phong tục gia đình      | To continue practicing the customs and rituals passed down through a family.                                 | Globalization makes it harder for some families **to maintain their cultural traditions**.         |
| **To balance work and family commitments**            | Cân bằng giữa công việc và các cam kết gia đình | To give the right amount of time and energy to both one's job and one's family responsibilities.             | Many working parents find it difficult **to balance work and family commitments**.                 |
| **Family values**                                     | Các giá trị gia đình                            | The moral and ethical principles that a family believes are important.                                       | Some politicians emphasize a return to traditional **family values**.                              |
| **The decline of the extended family**                | Sự suy giảm của gia đình đa thế hệ              | The trend of fewer families living in multi-generational households.                                         | Urbanization and mobility have contributed to **the decline of the extended family**.              |
| **The fundamental unit of society**                   | Đơn vị cơ bản của xã hội                        | The most basic and important building block of a society.                                                    | The family is often described as **the fundamental unit of society**.                              |
| **Social mobility**                                   | Sự dịch chuyển xã hội                           | The ability of an individual or family to move up or down the social and economic ladder.                    | A stable family background can have a significant impact on a child's future **social mobility**.  |
| **Maternity and paternity leave policies**            | Các chính sách nghỉ thai sản cho mẹ và bố       | Government or company policies that allow parents to take paid time off work after the birth of a child.     | Generous **maternity and paternity leave policies** can promote gender equality at home.           |
| **Childcare facilities**                              | Các cơ sở chăm sóc trẻ em                       | Places such as nurseries and daycare centers that provide care for young children.                           | A lack of affordable **childcare facilities** is a major barrier for working mothers.              |
