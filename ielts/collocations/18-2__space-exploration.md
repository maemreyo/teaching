### 🚀 **Sứ mệnh & Hành trình (The Mission & Journey)**

| Collocation                         | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                    | Short English Meaning                                       | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                        |
| :---------------------------------- | :---------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Space exploration**               | Sự thám hiểm không gian             | The exploration of outer space.                             | **Space exploration** has captivated the human imagination for centuries.                               |
| **A manned / unmanned mission**     | Một sứ mệnh có / không có người lái | A space mission with / without people on board.             | **Unmanned missions** using robotic probes are often safer and less expensive than **manned missions**. |
| **To launch a rocket / spacecraft** | Phóng một tên lửa / tàu vũ trụ      | To send a rocket or spacecraft into space.                  | The space agency plans **to launch a new spacecraft** to Mars next year.                                |
| **To go into orbit**                | Đi vào quỹ đạo                      | To begin to circle a planet or star.                        | The satellite successfully **went into orbit** around the Earth.                                        |
| **An international space station**  | Một trạm không gian quốc tế         | A large spacecraft in orbit where astronauts live and work. | Astronauts from various countries collaborate on the **International Space Station**.                   |
| **A fleet of satellites**           | Một đội vệ tinh                     | A group of satellites working together.                     | A **fleet of satellites** provides us with global communication and weather forecasting.                |
| **To land on the moon / Mars**      | Đổ bộ lên mặt trăng / sao Hỏa       | To arrive on the surface of the moon or Mars.               | Humanity first **landed on the moon** in 1969.                                                          |
| **A space agency**                  | Một cơ quan vũ trụ                  | An organization that is in charge of space exploration.     | NASA is the most famous **space agency** in the world.                                                  |

### ✨ **Khám phá & Lợi ích (Discoveries & Benefits)**

| :---------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A scientific discovery**                | Một khám phá khoa học                            | A new and important finding in science.                                                   | The Hubble Space Telescope has been responsible for numerous major **scientific discoveries**.         |
| **Technological spin-offs**               | Các sản phẩm công nghệ phái sinh                 | Useful products that are developed as a result of technology created for another purpose. | GPS technology is a well-known **technological spin-off** from the space program.                      |
| **To advance our understanding of...**    | Nâng cao hiểu biết của chúng ta về...            | To increase our knowledge of something.                                                   | Manned missions to Mars could significantly **advance our understanding of** the solar system.         |
| **To gather data**                        | Thu thập dữ liệu                                 | To collect information.                                                                   | Robotic probes are sent **to gather data** about the atmosphere and geology of other planets.          |

### 💰 **Thách thức & Phản biện (Challenges & Criticisms)**

| Collocation                               | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                             | Short English Meaning                                          | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                         |
| :---------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **The exorbitant / prohibitive cost**     | Chi phí quá đắt đỏ / cắt cổ                  | A cost that is extremely or unreasonably high.                 | Critics argue that **the exorbitant cost** of manned space missions is not justified.                    |
| **A waste of financial resources**        | Một sự lãng phí tài nguyên tài chính         | A bad use of money.                                            | Some people view space exploration as a **waste of financial resources**.                                |
| **To divert funding from...**             | Chuyển hướng tài trợ từ...                   | To use money for a different purpose than originally intended. | It is argued that money for space programs could **be diverted from** more pressing issues like poverty. |
| **To address pressing problems on Earth** | Giải quyết các vấn đề cấp bách trên Trái Đất | To deal with urgent problems on our planet.                    | Governments should prioritize **addressing pressing problems on Earth** before investing in space.       |
| **The inherent risks / dangers**          | Những rủi ro / nguy hiểm vốn có              | The risks or dangers that are a natural part of something.     | Astronauts are well aware of **the inherent risks** associated with space travel.                        |
| **To overcome technical challenges**      | Vượt qua các thách thức kỹ thuật             | To solve difficult technical problems.                         | Sending humans to Mars would require **overcoming immense technical challenges**.                        |
| **The hostile environment of space**      | Môi trường khắc nghiệt của không gian        | The dangerous and difficult conditions in space.               | Humans must be protected from **the hostile environment of space**.                                      |
| **Space debris / junk**                   | Mảnh vỡ / rác không gian                     | Unwanted objects in space.                                     | **Space debris** in Earth's orbit poses a threat to active satellites.                                   |
| **Ethical considerations**                | Các xem xét về mặt đạo đức                   | Moral issues that need to be considered.                       | The colonization of other planets raises complex **ethical considerations**.                             |
