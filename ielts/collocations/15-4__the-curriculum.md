### 📚 **Các môn học Cốt lõi & Học thuật (Core & Academic Subjects)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Core subjects** | Các môn học cốt lõi | The most important subjects that all students must study. | **Core subjects** like mathematics and language are compulsory in most educational systems. |
| **Academic disciplines** | Các ngành học thuật | Different areas of study in a university. | History and philosophy are important **academic disciplines** that teach critical thinking. |
| **To gain a solid grounding in...** | Có được nền tảng vững chắc về... | To get a good basic knowledge of a subject. | A good primary education should help children **gain a solid grounding in** literacy and numeracy. |
| **A broad and balanced curriculum** | Một chương trình học rộng và cân bằng | A curriculum that includes a wide variety of subjects. | Educators argue that a **broad and balanced curriculum** is essential for a child's development. |
| **To memorize facts and figures** | Ghi nhớ các sự kiện và số liệu | To learn facts and numbers by heart. | Some people believe education has become too focused on **memorizing facts and figures** for exams. |
| **Theoretical knowledge** | Kiến thức lý thuyết | Knowledge based on ideas, not practical experience. | A university education often emphasizes **theoretical knowledge** over practical skills. |
| **To master the fundamentals** | Nắm vững những kiến thức cơ bản | To learn the basic principles of a subject completely. | Before moving to advanced topics, it is crucial **to master the fundamentals**. |

### 🛠️ **Kỹ năng Thực tiễn & Hướng nghiệp (Practical & Vocational Skills)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Practical skills / Life skills** | Kỹ năng thực tế / kỹ năng sống | Skills that are useful in everyday life. | Many argue that schools should teach **practical skills** such as financial management. |
| **Vocational training / courses** | Đào tạo / khóa học nghề | Training for a specific job. | **Vocational courses** prepare students for specific trades like plumbing or hairdressing. |
| **Hands-on experience** | Kinh nghiệm thực hành | Practical experience of doing something. | **Hands-on experience** is often more valuable to employers than theoretical knowledge. |
| **Financial literacy / management** | Kiến thức / quản lý tài chính | The knowledge and skill of managing your money. | A lack of **financial literacy** can lead to debt and poor financial decisions in adulthood. |
| **To prepare students for the real world** | Chuẩn bị cho học sinh bước vào đời thực | To get students ready for life after school. | The curriculum should be designed **to prepare students for the real world**. |
| **To equip students with...** | Trang bị cho học sinh... | To provide students with the necessary skills or tools. | The goal is **to equip students with** the skills they need to succeed in the 21st-century workplace. |
| **Real-world application** | Ứng dụng trong thế giới thực | The use of something in real-life situations. | Students are more motivated when they can see the **real-world application** of what they are learning. |

### 🎨 **Tranh luận: Nghệ thuật & Nhân văn vs. Khoa học (The Debate: Arts & Humanities vs. STEM)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **STEM subjects** | Các môn học STEM (Khoa học, Công nghệ, Kỹ thuật, Toán) | Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics. | Many governments are investing more in **STEM subjects** to drive economic innovation. |
| **The arts and humanities** | Các môn nghệ thuật và nhân văn | Subjects like art, music, literature, and history. | **The arts and humanities** are crucial for fostering creativity and cultural understanding. |
| **To foster creativity and self-expression** | Nuôi dưỡng sự sáng tạo và thể hiện bản thân | To encourage people to be creative and express themselves. | Music and drama classes help **to foster creativity and self-expression**. |
| **To develop analytical and logical thinking** | Phát triển tư duy phân tích và logic | To develop the ability to think in a logical and analytical way. | Science and maths are excellent subjects **to develop analytical and logical thinking**. |
| **A well-rounded education** | Một nền giáo dục toàn diện | An education that is balanced and covers many areas. | A **well-rounded education** should include a balance of both STEM and arts subjects. |
| **To place more emphasis on...** | Chú trọng hơn vào... | To give more importance to something. | Some argue that schools should **place more emphasis on** physical education to combat obesity. |
| **To be undervalued in the curriculum** | Bị đánh giá thấp trong chương trình học | To be considered less important in the school curriculum. | Subjects like art and music are often **undervalued in the school curriculum**. |
| **Art appreciation** | Sự cảm thụ nghệ thuật | The ability to understand and enjoy art. | **Art appreciation** helps students to understand different cultures and perspectives. |

### 📜 **Thiết kế & Mục tiêu Chương trình học (Curriculum Design & Goals)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The national curriculum** | Chương trình giáo dục quốc gia | The official curriculum for all state schools in a country. | **The national curriculum** sets out the subjects that all state-funded schools are required to teach. |
| **Curriculum development** | Phát triển chương trình học | The process of creating or improving a curriculum. | **Curriculum development** should involve input from teachers, parents, and industry experts. |
| **To be part of the syllabus** | Là một phần của giáo trình | To be included in the plan of what is to be studied. | History of art should **be part of the official school syllabus**. |
| **Extracurricular activities** | Các hoạt động ngoại khóa | Activities done outside of regular school classes. | **Extracurricular activities** like sports and clubs are vital for developing social skills. |
| **A holistic approach to education** | Một cách tiếp cận toàn diện đối với giáo dục | An approach that considers all aspects of a child's development. | A **holistic approach to education** considers a child's physical, emotional, and social well-being. |
| **To meet the needs of a changing society** | Đáp ứng nhu cầu của một xã hội đang thay đổi | To be suitable for the demands of modern society. | The curriculum must be updated regularly **to meet the needs of a changing society**. |
| **To tailor the curriculum to...** | Điều chỉnh chương trình học cho phù hợp với... | To adapt the curriculum for a specific need or group. | It is difficult **to tailor the curriculum to** the individual needs of each student. |
