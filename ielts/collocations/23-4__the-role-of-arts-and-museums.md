### 🎨 **Nghệ thuật & Sáng tạo (The Arts & Creative Expression)**

| Collocation                              | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                                 | Short English Meaning                                                                                         | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                                          |
| :--------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **The performing arts**                  | Nghệ thuật biểu diễn                             | Arts such as music, dance, and drama which are performed for an audience.                                     | **The performing arts**, such as theatre and opera, can be a powerful form of storytelling.                               |
| **The visual arts**                      | Nghệ thuật thị giác                              | Arts created primarily for visual perception, such as painting, sculpture, and photography.                   | **The visual arts** allow artists to communicate complex ideas and emotions without words.                                |
| **A work of art / A masterpiece**        | Một tác phẩm nghệ thuật / Một kiệt tác           | A single piece of art / a work of outstanding artistry, skill, or workmanship.                                | The Mona Lisa is considered Leonardo da Vinci's **masterpiece**.                                                          |
| **Artistic expression and creativity**   | Sự thể hiện và sáng tạo nghệ thuật               | The use of imagination and skill to create works that can be appreciated for their beauty or emotional power. | The arts provide a vital outlet for **artistic expression and creativity**.                                               |
| **A profound form of self-expression**   | Một hình thức thể hiện bản thân sâu sắc          | A deep and meaningful way of showing one's personality, thoughts, and feelings.                               | For many, painting is a **profound form of self-expression**.                                                             |
| **Traditional and indigenous art forms** | Các hình thức nghệ thuật truyền thống và bản địa | Art forms that are part of the long-standing culture and heritage of a particular community.                  | It is important to preserve **traditional and indigenous art forms** as they are part of our identity.                    |
| **Contemporary and avant-garde art**     | Nghệ thuật đương đại và tiên phong               | The art of today and new, experimental ideas in art.                                                          | **Contemporary art** often challenges viewers, while **avant-garde art** pushes the boundaries of what is considered art. |
| **An aspiring artist**                   | Một nghệ sĩ đầy tham vọng, khao khát             | Someone who is working hard to become a successful artist.                                                    | The city's art galleries provide a platform for **aspiring artists** to showcase their work.                              |
| **Aesthetic value**                      | Giá trị thẩm mỹ                                  | The value of an object based on its beauty or its ability to evoke an emotional response.                     | While some art has a political message, other works are created purely for their **aesthetic value**.                     |
| **The creative process**                 | Quá trình sáng tạo                               | The series of thoughts and actions that lead to the creation of a new work of art.                            | The artist's sketchbook provides a fascinating insight into her **creative process**.                                     |

### 🏛️ **Vai trò & Chức năng của Bảo tàng & Nghệ thuật (The Role & Function of Museums & Arts)**

| Collocation                                         | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                                       | Short English Meaning                                                                    | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                         |
| :-------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **To preserve and display cultural artifacts**      | Gìn giữ và trưng bày các hiện vật văn hóa              | To protect and exhibit objects of cultural or historical significance.                   | The primary role of a museum is **to preserve and display cultural artifacts** for public education.     |
| **To educate the public**                           | Giáo dục công chúng                                    | To provide knowledge and information to the general population.                          | Museums play a vital role in **educating the public** about history, science, and art.                   |
| **To connect us with our past and heritage**        | Kết nối chúng ta với quá khứ và di sản                 | To help us feel a link to our history and cultural inheritance.                          | Historical objects in museums help **to connect us with our past and heritage**.                         |
| **To foster creativity and critical thinking**      | Nuôi dưỡng sự sáng tạo và tư duy phản biện             | To encourage the development of imaginative and analytical skills.                       | Exposure to the arts can **foster creativity and critical thinking** in children.                        |
| **To provide a source of inspiration**              | Cung cấp một nguồn cảm hứng                            | To be something that gives people new ideas and motivation.                              | Nature has always been a powerful **source of inspiration** for artists.                                 |
| **To enrich our lives and communities**             | Làm phong phú thêm cuộc sống và cộng đồng của chúng ta | To make our lives and communities better, more interesting, and more meaningful.         | Art and music can **enrich our lives and communities** in profound ways.                                 |
| **To reflect the values and concerns of a society** | Phản ánh các giá trị và mối quan tâm của một xã hội    | To show the beliefs, principles, and worries of a society at a particular time.          | The art produced in an era often **reflects the values and concerns of that society**.                   |
| **To challenge our perceptions and assumptions**    | Thách thức nhận thức và giả định của chúng ta          | To make us question the way we see things and what we believe to be true.                | Provocative art can **challenge our perceptions and assumptions** about the world.                       |
| **To promote cultural understanding and tolerance** | Thúc đẩy sự thấu hiểu và khoan dung văn hóa            | To encourage understanding and acceptance between different cultures.                    | By showcasing art from around the world, galleries can **promote cultural understanding and tolerance**. |
| **To serve as a repository of collective memory**   | Đóng vai trò là một kho lưu trữ ký ức tập thể          | To act as a place where the shared memories and history of a group of people are stored. | National museums **serve as a repository of our collective memory**.                                     |

### 💰 **Tài trợ & Hỗ trợ (Funding & Support)**

| Collocation                                      | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                            | Short English Meaning                                                           | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                                 |
| :----------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------ | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Public / Government funding for the arts**     | Tài trợ công / của chính phủ cho nghệ thuật | Financial support for the arts provided by the state.                           | The debate continues over the level of **public funding for the arts**.                                          |
| **To be a waste of taxpayers' money**            | Là một sự lãng phí tiền thuế của dân        | To be a bad use of money collected from the public through taxes.               | Some argue that funding abstract art is a **waste of taxpayers' money**.                                         |
| **To allocate public funds to...**               | Phân bổ công quỹ cho...                     | To assign government money to a specific purpose.                               | Opponents believe the government should **allocate public funds to** essential services like healthcare instead. |
| **To rely on private sponsorship and donations** | Dựa vào sự tài trợ và quyên góp của tư nhân | To depend on financial support from private companies and individuals.          | Many museums and galleries **rely on private sponsorship and donations** to operate.                             |
| **To charge an admission fee**                   | Thu phí vào cửa                             | To require people to pay money to enter a place.                                | **Charging an admission fee** can make museums inaccessible to low-income families.                              |
| **To be a justifiable use of public funds**      | Là một cách sử dụng công quỹ chính đáng     | To be a good and reasonable way to spend government money.                      | Proponents argue that supporting the arts is a **justifiable use of public funds**.                              |
| **To be commercially viable**                    | Có khả năng tồn tại về mặt thương mại       | To be able to make a profit without external financial support.                 | Not all forms of art are **commercially viable**, so they often require subsidies to survive.                    |
| **To be an essential public service**            | Là một dịch vụ công thiết yếu               | To be a very important service for the public that should be accessible to all. | Proponents argue that museums are an **essential public service**, just like libraries.                          |
| **Corporate sponsorship**                        | Tài trợ của doanh nghiệp                    | Financial support from a company, often in exchange for advertising.            | The exhibition was made possible by generous **corporate sponsorship**.                                          |
| **Philanthropic donations**                      | Các khoản quyên góp từ thiện                | Money given by wealthy individuals or foundations to support a cause.           | The new wing of the museum was funded by a large **philanthropic donation**.                                     |

### 🖼️ **Tiếp cận & Tương tác (Access & Engagement)**

| Collocation                                | Nghĩa tiếng Việt                                    | Short English Meaning                                                                                 | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh                                                                                       |
| :----------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **To be accessible to the general public** | Mọi người dân đều có thể tiếp cận                   | To be open and available for everyone in society to use or experience.                                | National museums should be free of charge to ensure they **are accessible to the general public**.     |
| **To democratize art**                     | Dân chủ hóa nghệ thuật                              | To make art accessible and available to all people, not just the elite.                               | Street art is a powerful way **to democratize art** by taking it out of the gallery.                   |
| **To engage with art**                     | Tương tác, tham gia với nghệ thuật                  | To interact with, think about, and respond to a work of art.                                          | Interactive installations encourage visitors **to engage with art** in a more active and personal way. |
| **Art appreciation and education**         | Sự cảm thụ và giáo dục nghệ thuật                   | The ability to understand and enjoy art, and the process of teaching it.                              | Schools should do more to foster **art appreciation and education**.                                   |
| **A vital cultural institution**           | Một thiết chế văn hóa trọng yếu                     | An organization that is extremely important to the cultural life of a society.                        | Museums and theatres are **vital cultural institutions** in any city.                                  |
| **An exhibition / A curated collection**   | Một cuộc triển lãm / một bộ sưu tập được tuyển chọn | A public display of works of art / a group of artworks carefully chosen and organized by a curator.   | The museum is hosting a major **exhibition** of the artist's work.                                     |
| **To broaden one's cultural horizons**     | Mở rộng tầm hiểu biết văn hóa                       | To expand one's knowledge and experience of different arts, cultures, and ideas.                      | Visiting art galleries is a wonderful way **to broaden one's cultural horizons**.                      |
| **To cater to a niche audience**           | Phục vụ cho một nhóm khán giả riêng biệt            | To provide for the interests of a small, specialized group of people.                                 | The gallery is known for **catering to a niche audience** of contemporary art enthusiasts.             |
| **To have a low barrier to entry**         | Có rào cản gia nhập thấp (dễ tiếp cận)              | To be easy for people to access or get involved in, without needing special skills or a lot of money. | Street performances have a **low barrier to entry** for both artists and audiences.                    |
| **Interactive exhibits**                   | Các khu trưng bày có tính tương tác                 | Museum displays that allow visitors to touch, use, or interact with them.                             | **Interactive exhibits** are particularly effective for engaging young children in museums.            |
