### 🏛️ **Vai trò & Chức năng của Bảo tàng & Nghệ thuật (The Role & Function of Museums & Arts)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **To preserve and display artifacts** | Gìn giữ và trưng bày các hiện vật | To protect and show historical objects. | The primary role of a museum is **to preserve and display artifacts** of historical significance. |
| **To educate the public** | Giáo dục công chúng | To teach the general population. | Museums play a vital role in **educating the public** about history, science, and art. |
| **To connect us to our past** | Kết nối chúng ta với quá khứ | To help us feel a connection to history. | Historical objects in museums help **to connect us to our past**. |
| **To foster creativity and imagination** | Nuôi dưỡng sự sáng tạo và trí tưởng tượng | To encourage creative and imaginative thinking. | Exposure to the arts can **foster creativity and imagination** in children. |
| **To provide a source of inspiration** | Cung cấp một nguồn cảm hứng | To be something that gives people new ideas. | Nature has always been a powerful **source of inspiration** for artists. |
| **To enrich our lives** | Làm phong phú thêm cuộc sống của chúng ta | To make our lives better and more interesting. | Art and music can **enrich our lives** in ways that material possessions cannot. |
| **To reflect the values of a society** | Phản ánh các giá trị của một xã hội | To show the beliefs and principles of a society. | The art produced in a particular era often **reflects the values of that society**. |
| **To challenge our perceptions** | Thách thức nhận thức của chúng ta | To make us question the way we see things. | Abstract art can **challenge our perceptions** and encourage us to think differently. |
| **To promote cultural understanding** | Thúc đẩy sự thấu hiểu văn hóa | To encourage understanding between different cultures. | By showcasing art from around the world, galleries can **promote cultural understanding**. |

### 💰 **Tài trợ & Hỗ trợ (Funding & Support)**

| Collocation | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Short English Meaning | Ví dụ / Bối cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Government / State / Public funding** | Tài trợ của chính phủ / nhà nước / công | Money from the government. | The debate continues over whether the arts should receive **public funding**. |
| **A waste of public money** | Một sự lãng phí tiền công | A bad use of government money. | Some people argue that spending on modern art is a **waste of public money**. |
| **To allocate resources to...** | Phân bổ nguồn lực cho... | To assign money or resources to something. | Opponents believe the government should **allocate resources to** essential services like healthcare instead. |
| **To rely on private sponsorship** | Dựa vào sự tài trợ tư nhân | To depend on money from private companies or individuals. | Many museums and galleries **rely on private sponsorship** and donations to operate. |
| **To charge an admission fee** | Thu phí vào cửa | To make people pay to enter. | **Charging an admission fee** can make museums inaccessible to people with low incomes. |
| **Financial support for the arts** | Hỗ trợ tài chính cho nghệ thuật | Money to help the arts. | **Financial support for the arts** is often one of the first things to be cut in a recession. |
| **To be commercially viable** | Có khả năng tồn tại về mặt thương mại | To be able to make a profit. | Not all forms of art are **commercially viable**, so they require subsidies. |
| **To be an essential public service** | Là một dịch vụ công thiết yếu | To be a very important service for the public. | Proponents argue that museums are an **essential public service**, just like libraries. |
