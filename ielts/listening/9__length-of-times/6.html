Mental Processing Optimization

🧮 Advanced Mental Math

Lightning Calculation Methods:

Time Addition Shortcuts

Example: 9:45 + 2h 25m

Method: 9:45 + 2:00 = 11:45, + 25 = 12:10

Trick: Add hours first, then minutes

Cross-Hour Calculations

Example: 10:40 + 35 minutes

Method: 10:40 + 20 = 11:00, + 15 = 11:15

Trick: Bridge to next hour, then add remainder

Format Conversion Mastery:

12h ↔ 24h Speed Conversion

PM times: Add 12 (except 12 PM = 12:xx)

AM times: Keep same (except 12 AM = 00:xx)

Quick check: 24h > 12 = PM time

Spoken Time Recognition

Quarter past: :15

Half past: :30

Quarter to: Previous hour :45

Ten past/to: :10 or :50
