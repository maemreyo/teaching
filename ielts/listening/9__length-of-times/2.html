Duration vs Time Point Distinction

Critical Concept: Duration ≠ Time Point

→ Duration = "How long" • Time Point = "When exactly"

⏳ Duration Characteristics

Measurement Focus

Question patterns: "How long does it take?"

Answer patterns: "It takes 2 hours"

Key concept: Measures span of time

Common Contexts

• Course length: "The course runs for 10 weeks"

• Activity duration: "Each session lasts 90 minutes"

• Process time: "Registration takes 15 minutes"

Expression Varieties

Standard: 2 hours, 45 minutes, 3 weeks

Conversational: A couple of hours, About an hour

Fractional: Half an hour, Quarter of an hour

🕐 Time Point Characteristics

Specific Moment Focus

Question patterns: "What time does it start?"

Answer patterns: "It starts at 9:30"

Key concept: Pinpoints exact moment

Schedule Contexts

• Start time: "Classes begin at 2:15 PM"

• End time: "The session finishes at 4:30"

• Appointment: "Your slot is at 10:45"

Format Variations

Digital: 9:30, 14:15, 23:45

Spoken: Half past nine, Quarter past two

Relative: In 15 minutes, At noon

🔍 Recognition Strategy

Duration Triggers:

  • • "How long..." questions
  • • "for [time period]" structures
  • • "takes/lasts/runs" verbs
  • • "duration/length" nouns

Time Point Triggers:

  • • "What time..." questions
  • • "at [specific time]" structures
  • • "starts/begins/ends" verbs
  • • Clock/calendar references

Duration Calculation Mastery

Strategic Importance: Many Part 1 questions require calculating duration from given start/end times, or vice versa. Mental math skills are essential.

🧮 Basic Calculations

Duration from Times

Example: Start 9:30 AM, End 11:45 AM

Method:

  • • 9:30 to 10:00 = 30 minutes
  • • 10:00 to 11:00 = 60 minutes
  • • 11:00 to 11:45 = 45 minutes
  • • Total: 30 + 60 + 45 = 135 minutes = 2h 15m

End Time from Duration

Example: Start 2:15 PM, Duration 1h 50m

Method:

  • • 2:15 + 1:00 = 3:15
  • • 3:15 + 0:50 = 4:05
  • • End time: 4:05 PM

⚡ Quick Methods

Hour Boundary Method

Break calculations at hour boundaries (10:00, 11:00, etc.) for easier mental math

15-Minute Chunks

Use quarter-hours (15, 30, 45) as building blocks for complex time calculations

Rounding Strategy

Round to nearest 5 minutes first, then adjust for precise calculations

🎯 Common Scenarios

Class Schedule Context

Scenario: "Morning session 9:30-12:15"

Question: "How long is the morning session?"

Calculation: 12:15 - 9:30 = 2h 45m

Answer: "2 hours 45 minutes" or "Nearly 3 hours"

Break Time Context

Scenario: "Class 9:00-10:30, break, resume 10:45-12:00"

Question: "How long is the break?"

Calculation: 10:45 - 10:30 = 15 minutes

Answer: "15 minutes" or "Quarter of an hour"

🔄 Cross-Verification

Logic Checking

Example verification:

If start = 1:30, duration = 2h 30m, end = 4:00

Check: 1:30 + 2:30 = 4:00 ✓

Reverse check: 4:00 - 1:30 = 2:30 ✓

Extended Time Periods

📅 Days, Weeks, Months

Daily Patterns

Single day:

  • • "All day" = full day
  • • "Half day" = ~4 hours
  • • "Morning" = ~4 hours
  • • "Afternoon" = ~4 hours

Multiple days:

  • • "3 days" = 72 hours
  • • "A week" = 7 days
  • • "Weekend" = 2 days

Weekly Patterns

Single week:

  • • "A week" = 7 days
  • • "Weekdays" = Mon-Fri (5 days)
  • • "Work week" = Mon-Fri

Multiple weeks:

  • • "Fortnight" = 2 weeks
  • • "A month" = ~4 weeks
  • • "6 weeks" = 1.5 months

Monthly Patterns

Academic terms:

  • • "Semester" = ~4 months
  • • "Term" = ~3 months
  • • "Quarter" = 3 months

Calendar periods:

  • • "Academic year" = 9 months
  • • "Calendar year" = 12 months
  • • "Season" = 3 months

🎓 Academic Context Applications

Course Duration Contexts

Short courses:

  • • "Weekend workshop" = 2 days
  • • "Intensive week" = 5-7 days
  • • "Summer course" = 2-6 weeks

Standard courses:

  • • "10-week course" = 1 semester
  • • "Full term" = 12-14 weeks
  • • "Academic year" = 36 weeks

Schedule Pattern Recognition

Weekly patterns:

  • • "Twice a week" = 2 sessions/week
  • • "Every other day" = 3-4 times/week
  • • "Daily except weekends" = 5 times/week

Total calculations:

  • • 2×/week × 10 weeks = 20 sessions
  • • 3×/week × 8 weeks = 24 sessions
  • • Daily × 5 days × 6 weeks = 30 sessions

🔄 Frequency and Repetition

Frequency Expression Types:

Regular frequency:

  • • Daily = every day
  • • Weekly = once a week
  • • Monthly = once a month
  • • Annually = once a year

Multiple frequency:

  • • Twice daily = 2 times/day
  • • Three times a week = 3×/week
  • • Every other day = alternate days
  • • Fortnightly = every 2 weeks

Irregular frequency:

  • • Occasionally = sometimes
  • • Regularly = consistent schedule
  • • Frequently = often but not daily
  • • Rarely = seldom
