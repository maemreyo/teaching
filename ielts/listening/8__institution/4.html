Giai đoạn 1: Pre-listening (Lợi thế 30 giây)

🎯 Strategic Objectives

Transform từ "blank page anxiety" sang "confident readiness". 30 seconds để build complete mental framework cho entire conversation.

1 Đọc Hướng Dẫn (5 giây)

Check ngay lập tức:

  • • Word/number limit: "NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS AND/OR A NUMBER"
  • • Question type: Form completion, table, notes
  • • Special instructions: Any specific format requirements

Common Limits:

• ONE WORD ONLY

• ONE WORD AND/OR A NUMBER

• NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS

• NO MORE THAN THREE WORDS

2 Quét Biểu Mẫu (10 giây)

Strategic Reading Order:

Form Title

Context identification

Section Headings

Conversation flow prediction

Pre-filled Info

Context clues for blanks

Example Analysis:

"Westley Public Library - Membership Application"

Context: Library membership signup

Expected flow: Personal details → Service preferences → Costs

Information types: Names, address, phone, membership type, fees

3 Gạch Chân Keywords (10 giây)

Keywords to Mark:

  • • Labels: Name, Address, Phone, etc.
  • • Context words: Words around blanks
  • • Specific terms: Course, membership, facility names
  • • Numbers/dates: Pre-filled timing information

Keyword Example:

First name: _______

Address: 24 Green Street, _______ (city)

Monthly cost: £_______

Keywords: First name, Address + 24 Green Street, city, Monthly cost + £

4 Dự Đoán Loại Câu Trả Lời (5 giây)

Personal Data

"Name:" → Proper noun

"Address:" → Street/number

"Phone:" → Numbers

"Postcode:" → Alphanumeric

Service Info

"Type:" → Category name

"Facility:" → Equipment name

"Course:" → Subject title

"Requirement:" → Condition

Numerical Data

"Cost:" → Price/number

"Date:" → Day/month

"Time:" → Hour/minute

"Duration:" → Time period

✅ Pre-listening Success Indicators

  • ✓ Clear understanding of context
  • ✓ Word limits memorized
  • ✓ Keywords identified and marked
  • ✓ Expected information types predicted
  • ✓ Conversation flow anticipated
  • ✓ Mental readiness for distractors

Giai đoạn 2: Active Listening Framework

🎧 Core Philosophy

Execute systematic techniques during audio playback. Balance between following conversation flow và maintaining accuracy focus.

1 Theo Dõi (Track)

Tracking Techniques:

  • • Visual focus: Eyes follow current question
  • • Keyword matching: Listen for marked keywords
  • • Section awareness: Know which form section is active
  • • Flow prediction: Anticipate next question topic

Warning Signs:

• Eyes wandering to other questions

• Lost track of conversation topic

• Panic about previous missed answer

• Not recognizing section transitions

2 Lắng Nghe Toàn Bộ Câu Trả Lời

Complete Answer Strategy:

Don't write immediately

Wait for speaker to finish complete thought

Listen for signal words

Correction markers, confirmation phrases

Process full context

Understand complete information before deciding

Example Application:

"The cost is £95... actually, sorry, that's the old price. It's £85 now."

❌ Passive: Writes "95" immediately

✅ Active: Waits, hears correction, writes "85"

3 Sử Dụng Chữ Viết Tắt

Quick Writing System:

Names: Write first few letters clearly

Numbers: Use digits, not words

Long words: Abbreviate middle, keep start/end

Corrections: Cross out, write new version

Examples:

Hear: "Environmental Studies"

Write: "Envir Studies"

Hear: "Membership"

Write: "Member" or "Mbr"

Remember: This is rough notes phase. Perfect spelling comes in transfer phase. Focus on capturing information accurately, not beautiful handwriting.

4 Đừng Hoảng Sợ

Recovery Strategy:

  • • Missed answer: Move immediately to next question
  • • Don't dwell: Obsessing over one = missing multiple
  • • Stay with flow: Audio continues regardless
  • • Smart guessing: Use context clues after audio ends

Panic Prevention:

Mindset: "I can afford to miss 1-2 answers"

Reality: Missing one won't ruin your score

Focus: Maximize remaining opportunities

Breathing: Stay calm and focused

Giai đoạn 3: Post-listening (Chuyển giao và Kiểm soát chất lượng)

⚡ Time-Critical Operations

Paper-based test: 10 minutes transfer time
Computer-based test: 2 minutes only

Every second counts. Systematic approach prevents errors và time waste.

1 Chuyển Giao Cẩn Thận

Transfer Process:

One answer at a time

Don't batch transfer - do sequentially

Double-check question numbers

Ensure answer matches correct question

Read your handwriting

Make sure you can interpret your notes

Common Transfer Errors:

• Answer in wrong question box

• Skipping question numbers

• Misreading own handwriting

• Forgetting to transfer final answers

2 Kiểm Soát Chất Lượng

Spelling Check:

Common words:

vegetarian, membership, facilities, environment

Proper nouns:

Names, places, course titles

Grammar Check:

Singular/Plural:

"facility" vs "facilities"

Article usage:

a, an, the (if within word limit)

Number Check:

Clarity:

7 vs 1, 6 vs 0, 5 vs 8

Format:

Phone: no spaces, Prices: decimal points

3 Chiến Lược Viết Hoa

ALL CAPS Advantage:

  • • Officially accepted: IELTS allows full capitalization
  • • Eliminates errors: No wrong capitalization possible
  • • Clear writing: More legible than mixed case
  • • Time saving: No thinking about caps rules

Implementation:

Practice examples:

ENVIRONMENTAL STUDIES

VEGETARIAN

MEMBERSHIP

BADMINTON COURT

Pro tip: Use ALL CAPS từ giai đoạn note-taking để consistent throughout

✅ Post-listening Quality Indicators

  • ✓ All answers transferred to correct positions
  • ✓ Spelling checked for common errors
  • ✓ Word limits strictly observed
  • ✓ Numbers written clearly and accurately
  • ✓ Capitalization consistent (ALL CAPS recommended)
  • ✓ No blank answers (educated guessing completed)
  • ✓ Final review completed within time limit
  • ✓ Confidence in accuracy of transferred answers

Tích hợp ba giai đoạn: Thực hành có mục tiêu

🎯 Systematic Practice Approach

Master each phase separately, then integrate. Don't attempt full integration until individual phases are automatic.

Week 1-2: Pre-listening

  • • Practice form analysis
  • • Speed reading exercises
  • • Prediction accuracy drills
  • • Keyword identification

Week 3-4: Active Listening

  • • Distractor recognition training
  • • Signal word drills
  • • Note-taking efficiency
  • • Recovery strategies

Week 5-6: Post-listening

  • • Transfer speed drills
  • • Quality control checklists
  • • Time management
  • • Error pattern analysis
