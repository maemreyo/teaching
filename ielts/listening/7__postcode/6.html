🎵 Phonetic Discrimination Training

Minimal Pairs Practice

B vs P

D vs T

G vs J

M vs N

Number-Letter Confusion

3 vs E

2 vs Z

0 vs O

1 vs I

Vowel Distinction

/æ/ vs /ʌ/

/i:/ vs /ɪ/

/u:/ vs /ʊ/

/ɔ:/ vs /ɒ/

Recommended Practice Method:

  1. Listen to minimal pair audio 10 times without looking
  2. Write down what you hear for each pair
  3. Check accuracy and identify specific weaknesses
  4. Focus extra practice on problematic sound pairs
  5. Practice with postcode context: "B2G 4JT" vs "P2J 4GT"

🎯 Distractor Pattern Training

Common Distractor Scripts

Pattern 1: False Start

"It's BT23... no wait, BT32DF"

Pattern 2: Correction Chain

"BT23... actually BT33... sorry, BT32DF"

Pattern 3: Alternative Options

"Is it 3 or 8? It's 3. So BT32DF"

Recognition Training

Key Signal Words:

Danger Signals:

  • • "Actually..."
  • • "Wait..."
  • • "Sorry..."
  • • "I mean..."

Confidence Signals:

  • • "That's..."
  • • "So it's..."
  • • "Right, so..."
  • • Spelling out

🧠 Working Memory Training

Level 1: Single Version

Practice holding one complete postcode:

BT32DF

Duration: 10 seconds before writing

Level 2: Dual Version

Hold two versions simultaneously:

BT23DF (wrong)

BT32DF (correct)

Duration: 15 seconds before deciding

Level 3: Multi Version

Track 3+ versions + final decision:

V1: BT23DF

V2: BT33DF

V3: BT32DF ✓

Duration: 20 seconds complex tracking

📅 4-Week Training Program

Week 1: Foundation

  • • Day 1-2: Format recognition (UK, AU, CA)
  • • Day 3-4: Basic phonetic discrimination
  • • Day 5-6: Simple distractor patterns
  • • Day 7: Assessment + review

Target: 60% accuracy on Cambridge 15

Week 2: Development

  • • Day 1-2: Advanced phonetic pairs
  • • Day 3-4: Multiple distractor tracking
  • • Day 5-6: Working memory challenges
  • • Day 7: Assessment + strategy refinement

Target: 75% accuracy on Cambridge 14

Week 3: Mastery

  • • Day 1-2: P.O.S.T.C.O.D.E strategy integration
  • • Day 3-4: Accent variation practice
  • • Day 5-6: Complex scenario simulation
  • • Day 7: Full Cambridge 16 test

Target: 85% accuracy on Cambridge 16

Week 4: Perfection

  • • Day 1-2: Cambridge 17 challenge tests
  • • Day 3-4: Speed variation drills
  • • Day 5-6: Final exam simulation
  • • Day 7: Comprehensive assessment

Target: 95% accuracy on Cambridge 17

📊 Progress Tracking

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

Overall Accuracy Target: 95%+
First-attempt Success Target: 80%+
Distractor Resistance Target: 90%+
Format Recognition Speed Target: <3sec

Weekly Self-Assessment

Red Flags (Need immediate attention):

  • • <50% accuracy on current week's target
  • • Consistent confusion on same phonetic pairs
  • • Unable to track 2+ versions simultaneously
  • • Taking >5 seconds for format recognition

Green Indicators (On track):

  • • Meeting or exceeding weekly targets
  • • Improving speed without losing accuracy
  • • Successfully handling new distractor patterns
  • • Confident with P.O.S.T.C.O.D.E strategy
