City Rubbish

Câu hỏi thường gặp & Trả lời mẫu

Do you think your city is clean or not?

"To be honest, I think the city I'm living in is not particularly clean. Trash like used water bottles and candy wrappers is all over the place, which makes me feel quite uncomfortable."

How do you feel when you see people throw garbage on the street?

"I find it really disturbing. In my opinion, people should care more about the environment, especially when bins are nearby."

What do you do with garbage on the street?

"I always try to find a recycling bin to dispose of it properly. If I can't find one, I'll carry it with me until I get home."

Ngân hàng từ vựng

  • Nouns: water bottles, candy wrappers, recycling bin.
  • Verbs: to dispose of, to throw away.
  • Adjectives: disturbing, disgusting, uncomfortable.

Staying Up Late

Câu hỏi thường gặp & Trả lời mẫu

Do you often stay up late?

"Yes, definitely. As a student, I have so much work to do these days, so I often have to burn the midnight oil to finish my deadlines."

How do you feel after staying up late?

"Honestly, I feel quite grumpy and unproductive the next morning. That's why I know I'm more of an early bird than a night owl."

Thành ngữ "ăn điểm"

  • Night owl: người thức khuya ("cú đêm")
  • Early bird: người dậy sớm
  • Burn the midnight oil: làm việc/học bài đến khuya
  • Grumpy (adj): gắt gỏng, cáu kỉnh

Wild Animals

Câu hỏi thường gặp & Trả lời mẫu

What wild animals do you like?

"I absolutely love lions. I think they look so awesome and powerful, especially since they are known as the king of the jungle."

Do you think it is necessary to protect wild animals?

"In my opinion, it is absolutely vital to protect wild animals and their habitats. Doing that can help maintain the natural balance of all life on Earth."

Ngân hàng từ vựng

  • Adjectives: adorable, awesome, unique, fluffy, vital.
  • Nouns: habitat (môi trường sống), king of the jungle.
  • Key Phrases: maintain the natural balance.
