Nắm Vững Ngữ Pháp Nguyên Nhân - Kết Quả

Cách diễn đạt logic và chính xác trong IELTS Writing

Because of

Dùng để chỉ NGUYÊN NHÂN

Cấu trúc:

Because of + Noun / V-ing, S + V.

S + V + because of + Noun / V-ing.

Ví dụ:

Because of social isolation during the pandemic, people could not maintain their monthly income.

Many individuals experienced quarantine because of being exposed to COVID-19.

As a result of

Dùng để chỉ KẾT QUẢ

Cấu trúc:

As a result of + Noun / V-ing, S + V.

Ví dụ:

As a result of the COVID-19 pandemic, schools switched to online learning.

Many people did not want to return to the office as a result of working from home for too long.

So that

Dùng để chỉ MỤC ĐÍCH

Cấu trúc:

S + V + so that + S + V.

Ví dụ:

People wear masks during the COVID-19 pandemic so that viruses are not spread out.

Từ vựng cần nhớ (Key Vocabulary)

  • Pandemic (n): Đại dịch
  • Quarantine (n): Sự cách ly
  • Social isolation (n): Sự cách ly xã hội
  • Expose (v): Tiếp xúc với
  • Maintain income: Duy trì thu nhập

Lỗi sai thường gặp & Cách sửa

She does exercising regularly so that losing weight.

Sửa: She exercises regularly so that she can lose weight.

Lý do: Sau `so that` phải là một mệnh đề (S+V).

The flight was delayed because of the weather was bad.

Sửa: The flight was delayed because of the bad weather.

Lý do: Sau `because of` là danh từ/cụm danh từ, không phải mệnh đề.
